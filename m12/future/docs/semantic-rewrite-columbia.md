# Harvard Turns an Early Deficit into a 20-Point Win

**Final:** Harvard 75, Columbia 55  
**Date:** Friday, Feb. 27, 2032  
**Location:** Cambridge, Mass. | Lavietes Pavilion  
**Game:** Ivy League

Columbia entered the second quarter one point ahead. Harvard then produced its biggest sequence of the night: 21 consecutive points, turning a 22-15 deficit into a 36-22 halftime lead.

Columbia opened the third with a 19-5 burst, cutting the margin to 43-41. Harvard answered with 11 straight points, and a 12-1 run in the final period extended the lead to 70-48. The 75-55 result gave the staff a chance to use the bench.

## How it Happened

The visitors edged ahead, 16-15, after the opening quarter. Their six-point run made it 22-15 early in the second, but Harvard took over from there, scoring 21 unanswered points before halftime.

Columbia's third-quarter push made it a two-point game. Harvard responded with an 11-0 stretch, then put the result out of reach with a 12-1 run in the fourth.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard won by 20 after trailing in the second quarter.
- A 21-0 run turned a seven-point deficit into a 14-point halftime lead.
- Columbia pulled within two in the third before Harvard answered with 11 straight points.
- The final margin grew to 22 before Harvard closed out the 75-55 win.
