A new-look Harvard women's basketball team wasted no time making a first impression. The Crimson (1-0) opened the 2030-31 season with a 73-47 rout of Seton Hall on Thursday night at Lavietes Pavilion, pulling away with a dominant third quarter in front of 1,123 fans.

Less than eight months removed from a 28-4 season that ended in the NCAA Tournament's second round, Harvard took the floor without four seniors from that team, including captains Brielle Mendoza and Isla Marchetti. Three sophomores who were first-years on that team carried the opener, as Chloe Ferreira, Esme Ellison, and Jordan Delgado all reached double figures, and all 15 players on the roster saw the court. Harvard outrebounded the Pirates 40-28 and held them to 32.0% shooting.

"There's a lot of new in this group, and I wanted to see how we'd respond when things got loose early. We guarded, we rebounded, and our young players didn't play like young players. It's a good start, and now we get to work," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Ellison scored the first points of the season at the free throw line, and Harvard jumped out to a 7-0 lead. Seton Hall answered with nine straight points to go up 9-7, but the Crimson closed the quarter on an 11-2 spurt to lead 19-12 after 10 minutes.

An 8-1 run early in the second quarter, capped by a Ferreira jumper, stretched the lead to 30-17. The Pirates chipped away with a 9-2 run of their own, and Harvard took a 32-26 lead into halftime.

The third quarter decided it. Harvard ripped off a 19-1 burst, capped by first-year Reese Kearns's bucket inside, to turn a tight game into a 55-34 lead. The Crimson outscored Seton Hall 25-10 in the frame and carried a 57-36 advantage into the fourth.

Ferreira, senior captain Mira Hightower, and Ellison keyed a 12-4 run down the stretch that pushed the lead to its high-water mark of 26 at the final buzzer. Seton Hall never got closer than 18 in the fourth quarter, and first-years Naomi Prescott, Natalie Olsen, and Wren Farrow all got into their first collegiate game.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira led Harvard with a career-high 14 points (5-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), three rebounds, and three assists.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison added 13 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and a team-high eight rebounds.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi scored 12 points with three 3-pointers and four assists.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado scored 11 points and shared the team lead with four assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower went 5-of-6 at the free throw line for seven points.
- First-years Naomi Prescott (four points) and Reese Kearns (two points) scored in their collegiate debuts.
- Harvard outrebounded Seton Hall 40-28 and outscored the Pirates 25-10 in the third quarter.
