Harvard women's basketball dug itself a 15-point hole in the first 10 minutes on Friday night at Lavietes Pavilion and spent the rest of the evening trying to climb out. Princeton held on for a 61-53 win, completing a sweep of the season series and keeping its perfect Ivy League record intact.

The Crimson (15-11, 6-6 Ivy) has now dropped three straight, following road losses at Cornell and Brown, and slipped to .500 in league play. Harvard forced 14 turnovers and knocked down eight 3-pointers, but the Tigers' defense held the Crimson to 27.4% shooting overall. Inside the arc, Harvard made just 12 of its 55 attempts.

"Princeton is the standard in this league right now, and they showed why in that first quarter. I loved how we fought back. We cut it to five in the fourth, but we dug too deep a hole and couldn't make enough shots inside the arc," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before junior Mackenzie Takahashi drilled a three for Harvard's first points. The teams were tied at 5 when the Tigers took off on a 19-4 run, and Princeton led 24-9 after one quarter.

Harvard found its footing in the second quarter. Chloe Ferreira, Jordan Delgado, and Mira Hightower keyed a 12-2 run that cut the deficit to 29-23, and the Crimson won the frame 16-10 to trail 34-25 at halftime.

Harvard scored the first six points of the second half to get within 34-31, the closest it had been since the first quarter. Princeton responded with an 11-0 run that pushed its lead to 49-35, and that was the score after three quarters.

The Crimson made one more charge. Harvard outscored the Tigers 11-2 to open the fourth quarter and pulled within 51-46 with 6:12 to play, but the Tigers outscored the Crimson 10-7 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 15-11 overall and 6-6 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-4 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Princeton swept the season series, following a 69-64 win at Jadwin Gymnasium on Jan. 31.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 18 points (6-of-18 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT).
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira added 12 points (4-of-18 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), seven rebounds, and a team-high four assists in 36 minutes.
- Senior Mira Hightower recorded nine points, 10 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi scored eight points with two 3-pointers and two steals.
- Harvard outscored Princeton 44-37 over the final three quarters and recorded nine steals.
