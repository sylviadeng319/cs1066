Harvard women's basketball (13-8, 4-3 Ivy) dropped a 70-63 decision to Penn on Saturday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

The Quakers shot 51.9% from the field, while Harvard was held to 45.8%.

## How it Happened

Penn scored first before sophomore Esme Ellison scored inside for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 6-2 deficit into a 10-6 lead. A 19-4 Penn spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 12-8 deficit into a 27-16 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's bucket inside, which cut the deficit to 33-27. The Quakers countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that extended the lead to 39-27. Penn took a 39-32 lead into the break.

The Quakers went on an 18-4 run midway through the quarter, which extended the lead to 60-42. The Crimson faced a 60-45 deficit going into the final frame.

Harvard couldn't string together enough stops to mount a comeback.

## Harvard Highlights
