Harvard women's basketball (15-9, 6-4 Ivy) let a 15-point lead slip away on Saturday in a 63-60 loss to Cornell at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

"We didn't do enough of the things that make us good. We'll regroup and get back to work," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Big Red scored first before sophomore Chloe Ferreira scored on a driving layup for Harvard's first points. Valentina Okonkwo, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Ferreira keyed a 19-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 6-2 deficit into a 21-6 lead. A 10-2 Cornell spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 23-16. It stayed that way after one quarter.

The Big Red countered with a 22-9 run in the quarter that flipped a 23-16 deficit into a 38-32 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 38-33.

Cornell went on a 12-0 run in the quarter, which extended the lead to 50-37. Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 50-43. The Crimson faced a 52-44 deficit going into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's putback, which cut the deficit to 59-57. Cornell led 59-57 in the final minutes. The Big Red hit a jumper with 2:50 left to stretch the lead to 61-57. Cornell converted a layup with 2:35 left to stretch the lead to 63-57, but Ellison answered when she added a free throw to pull Harvard within 63-58. With 43 seconds left, Ellison banked in a turnaround to pull Harvard within 63-60. The Crimson's final attempt to tie it came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 15-9 overall and 6-4 in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison led the way for Harvard with 15 points (5-of-11 FG, 5-of-7 FT) and 10 rebounds for a double-double.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi finished with 10 points (4-of-14 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and four steals.
- Senior Mira Hightower grabbed nine rebounds.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado dished out a team-high seven assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 17 of its 23 field goals.
