Harvard women's basketball (7-4) never trailed on Tuesday, beating Fairfield, 83-73, at Leo D. Mahoney Arena in Fairfield, Conn.

"Everybody who checked in made an impact. That kind of depth is something we've been building toward," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as sophomore Esme Ellison scored inside. Harvard opened the game on a 16-5 run. The Crimson took a 20-9 lead into the second quarter.

A 25-9 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Thea Sutherland's putback, stretched the lead to 45-18. Fairfield countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 45-24. That was the score at halftime.

The Stags went on a 20-4 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 51-44. Jasmine Danforth, Chloe Ferreira, and Mira Hightower keyed a 10-0 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 61-44. The Crimson carried a 63-51 advantage into the final frame.

The Stags got within 63-all with 6:13 left before Harvard took control. The Crimson strung together a 15-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Ellison's bucket inside, which broke a 63-all tie and put Harvard up 78-65.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-4 overall.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 19 points (7-of-12 FG, 5-of-9 3-PT) and six assists.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison contributed 16 points (6-of-9 FG, 4-of-5 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi chipped in 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), five rebounds, and five assists.
- Jasmine Danforth (10) and Thea Sutherland (10) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
- Harvard's 83 points were its most in a game this season.
