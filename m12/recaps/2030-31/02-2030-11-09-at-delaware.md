Harvard women's basketball (2-0) erased an 11-point deficit and came away with a 63-62 win over Delaware on Saturday at Bob Carpenter Center in Newark, Del.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Blue Hens 51-43.

## How it Happened

The Blue Hens scored first before junior Mackenzie Takahashi hit a pair of free throws for Harvard's first points. Delaware countered with a 12-5 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 9-6 deficit into an 18-14 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Takahashi's free throws, which turned a 23-19 deficit into a 25-23 lead. A 7-0 Delaware spurt late in the quarter flipped a 25-23 deficit into a 30-25 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

Delaware went on a 9-0 run midway through the quarter, which extended the lead to 43-32. A 6-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's putback, cut the deficit to 43-38. The Crimson faced a 46-38 deficit going into the final frame.

Jordan Delgado, Thea Sutherland, and Takahashi keyed a 23-9 run early in the quarter that turned a 46-38 deficit into a 61-55 lead. Harvard held a 61-57 lead in the final minutes. With 2:20 left, Danforth went 2-for-2 at the line to push the lead to 63-57. The Blue Hens converted a layup with 1:56 left to cut the deficit to 63-59. Delaware made a free throw with 37 seconds left to cut the deficit to 63-60. The Blue Hens scored with 28 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with 20 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado contributed 15 points (6-of-16 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five assists.
- Harvard held Delaware to 32.8% shooting from the field.
