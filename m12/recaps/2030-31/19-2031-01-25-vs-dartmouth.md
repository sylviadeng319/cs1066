Harvard women's basketball (13-6, 4-1 Ivy) cruised to a 62-39 victory over Dartmouth on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 55-40. The Crimson shot 35.7% from the field and held Dartmouth to 24.1%.

## How it Happened

The Big Green scored first before junior Mackenzie Takahashi made a free throw for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt late in the quarter that turned an 8-4 deficit into a 10-8 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 12-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's three, which stretched the lead to 22-11. Harvard took a 23-16 lead into halftime.

Chloe Ferreira, Valentina Okonkwo, and Takahashi keyed a 16-7 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 39-25. Harvard took a 43-29 lead into the fourth quarter.

A 19-8 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 62-37. The lead reached 25, allowing Tiana Adeyemi, Wren Farrow, and Naomi Prescott to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-6 overall and 4-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison led Harvard with 21 points (10-of-15 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado added 12 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi contributed 10 points (4-of-10 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT).
- Senior Mira Hightower grabbed 12 rebounds.
- Junior Valentina Okonkwo grabbed 12 rebounds.
