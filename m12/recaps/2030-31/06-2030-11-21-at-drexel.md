Harvard women's basketball (4-2) let a 13-point lead slip away on Thursday in a 64-61 loss to Drexel at Daskalakis Athletic Center in Philadelphia, Pa.

Drexel shot 44.4% from the field, while Harvard was held to 31.8%.

"We have to be more consistent for 40 minutes. We showed flashes, but flashes aren't enough," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Mackenzie Takahashi scored on a driving layup. The Dragons went on a 12-5 run in the quarter, which flipped a 4-2 deficit into a 14-9 lead. The Dragons led 16-15 after one.

Takahashi, Esme Ellison, and Mira Hightower keyed a 21-7 run in the quarter that turned a 16-15 deficit into a 36-23 lead. The Crimson led 36-26 at the break.

A 21-4 Drexel spurt in the quarter flipped a 36-26 deficit into a 47-40 lead. The Crimson strung together an 11-4 burst late in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's free throw, which evened the score at 51. That was the score heading into the fourth.

The Dragons led 62-59 in the final minutes. Chloe Ferreira hit a pull-up jumper with 23 seconds left to make it 62-61. The Dragons converted a layup with 14 seconds left to stretch the lead to 64-61. The Crimson's final attempt to tie it came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 4-2 overall.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 14 points (6-of-14 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT) and three assists.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado added 12 points (3-of-7 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT), five rebounds, and three assists.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira chipped in 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) and six rebounds.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison grabbed 11 rebounds.
