Harvard women's basketball (16-11, 7-6 Ivy) cruised to a 70-48 victory over Penn on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The victory snapped a three-game skid. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Quakers 49-37.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Kendall Lindqvist scored through contact. The Quakers countered with an 11-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 2-0 deficit into an 11-2 lead. Chloe Ferreira, Mira Hightower, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 17-0 run midway through the quarter that turned an 11-2 deficit into a 19-11 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 9-1 Penn spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 27-25. The Crimson led 32-27 at the break.

A 14-3 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Thea Sutherland's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 46-30. Harvard led 54-40 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 16-4 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 70-44. The lead reached 26, allowing Lindqvist, Naomi Prescott, and Emeka Rhodes to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-11 overall and 7-6 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-4 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 15 points (5-of-6 FG, 3-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower recorded a double-double with 13 points (4-of-4 FG, 5-of-8 FT), 12 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira chipped in 13 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado dished out a team-high six assists.
- Harvard held Penn to 29.0% shooting from the field.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 25 field goals.
