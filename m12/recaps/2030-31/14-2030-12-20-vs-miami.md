Harvard women's basketball (9-5) held on for a 73-70 win over Miami on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 59.6% from the field and held Miami to 49.0%.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Mackenzie Takahashi finished a layup in transition. The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Kendall Lindqvist's bucket inside, which broke a 2-all tie and put Harvard up 9-2. A 9-3 Miami spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 14-13. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 23-17.

The Hurricanes went on a 14-6 run in the quarter, which flipped a 28-21 deficit into a 35-34 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 36-35.

A 12-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's three, broke a 36-all tie and put Harvard up 48-38. Miami countered with an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 50-48. Harvard led 52-48 heading into the fourth.

Miami led 69-65 in the final minutes. Mira Hightower banked in a turnaround with 1:24 left to pull Harvard within 69-67. With 1:05 left, Thea Sutherland finished a post move to tie the game at 69. With 16 seconds left, Chloe Ferreira knocked down a jumper to give the Crimson a 71-69 lead. Esme Ellison banked in a turnaround with 12 seconds left to push the lead to 73-69. The Hurricanes scored with 3 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.
