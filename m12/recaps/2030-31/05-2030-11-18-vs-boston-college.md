Harvard women's basketball (4-1) led from start to finish in a 73-63 win over Boston College on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 53.6% from the field and held Boston College to 43.6%.

## How it Happened

Senior Mira Hightower opened the scoring when she finished a post move. Jordan Delgado, Chloe Ferreira, and Thea Sutherland keyed a 12-0 run midway through the quarter that broke a 2-all tie and put Harvard up 14-2. Harvard led 17-9 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 33-16. The score held there at halftime.

The Eagles countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 35-25. A 12-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's layup, stretched the lead to 60-38. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 13-6 burst early in the quarter, capped by Sutherland's layup, which stretched the lead to 73-46. Harvard kept the Eagles at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 10.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-1 overall.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado led the way for Harvard with 15 points (5-of-12 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 2-of-3 FT), six rebounds, and eight assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower contributed 12 points (6-of-7 FG) and seven rebounds.
- First-year Jasmine Danforth finished with 11 points (4-of-5 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- The Crimson assisted on 22 of its 30 field goals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
