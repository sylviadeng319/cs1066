Harvard women's basketball (11-6, 2-1 Ivy) let a 10-point lead slip away on Saturday in a 65-63 loss to Columbia at Lavietes Pavilion.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Mackenzie Takahashi splashed a three from the wing. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 13-8.

Columbia went on a 27-6 run in the quarter, which flipped a 20-10 deficit into a 37-26 lead. Harvard trailed 37-28 at halftime.

A 27-13 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's putback, turned a 37-28 deficit into a 55-50 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

The Lions led 62-61 in the final minutes. With 2:08 left, Chloe Ferreira scored on a driving layup to give the Crimson a 63-62 lead. Columbia scored inside with 1:43 left to go in front 64-63. The Lions made a free throw with 1:11 left to stretch the lead to 65-63. Harvard did not score over the final 2:08. Harvard had one last look in the closing seconds but couldn't get it to fall.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 11-6 overall and 2-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 6-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison finished with 13 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira chipped in 13 points (4-of-18 FG, 1-of-8 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), five rebounds, three assists, and four steals.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado finished with 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower grabbed 13 rebounds.
