Harvard women's basketball (8-4) picked up a 74-60 win over Sacred Heart on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pioneers 44-32.

"That was a complete team effort, and that's what we're going to need as the season goes on," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Mira Hightower opened the scoring when she made a free throw. Harvard put together a 14-2 spurt in the quarter that turned a 2-1 deficit into a 15-4 lead. An 8-2 Sacred Heart spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 17-12. The score held there after one quarter.

The Pioneers went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 19-13 deficit into a 20-19 lead. An 18-5 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's putback, turned a 20-19 deficit into a 37-25 lead. Harvard took a 37-27 lead into halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 24-14 burst in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's layup, which stretched the lead to 61-43. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Hightower, Jordan Delgado, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 72-54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-4 overall. The Crimson is 4-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison finished with a team-high 17 points (7-of-14 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado added 15 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT), four assists, and three steals.
- Junior Valentina Okonkwo grabbed nine rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 29 field goals.
