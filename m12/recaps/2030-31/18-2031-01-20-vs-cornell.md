Behind a strong second quarter, Harvard women's basketball (12-6, 3-1 Ivy) beat Cornell, 66-54, on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 55-40.

"We did a lot of the little things well tonight: boxing out, talking on defense, making the extra pass," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before sophomore Jordan Delgado hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. Delgado, Mira Hightower, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 13-2 run in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into a 13-4 lead. A 7-0 Cornell spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 15-13. Harvard led 18-13 after the first quarter.

Cornell went on a 9-2 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 29-26. Harvard put together an 11-2 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 40-28. It stayed that way at halftime.

The Big Red countered with an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 44-38. The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's free throws, which stretched the lead to 53-41. The score held there heading into the fourth.

A 9-1 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Delgado's bucket, stretched the lead to 64-52.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-6 overall and 3-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado led the way for Harvard with 15 points (6-of-13 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), five rebounds, and nine assists.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison contributed 14 points (6-of-16 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira finished with 14 points (5-of-15 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Mira Hightower grabbed 12 rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 17 of its 26 field goals.
