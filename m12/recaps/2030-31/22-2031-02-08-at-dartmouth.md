The Crimson made quick work of Dartmouth on Saturday, as Harvard women's basketball (14-8, 5-3 Ivy) built a lead as large as 24 on the way to a 79-55 win at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

The victory snapped a two-game skid. The Crimson shot 47.7% from the field and held the Big Green to 38.2%.

"I loved our energy from the opening tip. We defended, we shared the ball, and we played for each other for 40 minutes," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Dartmouth scored first before first-year Naomi Prescott drilled a three for Harvard's first points. Prescott, Mira Hightower, and Tiana Adeyemi keyed a 6-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into a 6-2 lead. Harvard led 15-14 after the first quarter.

A 17-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's bucket inside, turned an 18-17 deficit into a 34-24 lead. The Crimson led 34-26 at the break.

The Crimson strung together an 11-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's 3-pointer, which stretched the lead to 47-33. Harvard led 53-42 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 24-11 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 77-53. With the lead as large as 24, Kendall Lindqvist, Emeka Rhodes, and Adeyemi saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-8 overall and 5-3 in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira finished with a team-high 21 points (9-of-15 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison added 18 points (9-of-11 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT) and eight rebounds.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi finished with 16 points (5-of-9 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and six assists.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded eight steals.
- Ferreira's 21 points were a career high.
