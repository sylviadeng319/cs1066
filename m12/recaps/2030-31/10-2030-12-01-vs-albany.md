Albany handed Harvard women's basketball (6-4) a 70-58 loss on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Great Danes shot 39.3% from the field, while Harvard was held to 31.9%.

## How it Happened

Albany scored first before senior Kendall Lindqvist banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. The Great Danes went on an 8-2 run midway through the quarter, which broke a 5-all tie and pushed the Great Danes ahead 13-7. The score held there after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 9-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's three, which turned a 13-7 deficit into a 16-15 lead. An 18-5 Albany spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 16-15 deficit into a 33-21 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 33-26.

The Great Danes countered with a 26-8 run in the quarter that extended the lead to 59-34. Jordan Delgado, Esme Ellison, and Takahashi keyed a 9-0 run late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 59-43. Albany led 60-44 after three quarters.

Albany went on an 8-2 run midway through the quarter, which extended the lead to 70-55. Harvard couldn't string together enough stops to mount a comeback.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 6-4 overall. The Crimson is 3-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 15 points (5-of-10 FG, 3-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 13 points (6-of-13 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and 12 rebounds.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira came away with four steals.
- Senior Mira Hightower protected the rim with three blocks.
