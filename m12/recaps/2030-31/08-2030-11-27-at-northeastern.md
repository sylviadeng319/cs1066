Behind a strong third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (5-3) beat Northeastern, 68-57, on Wednesday at Cabot Center in Boston, Mass.

The victory snapped a two-game skid. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Huskies 44-31.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Esme Ellison opened the scoring when she scored through contact. An 11-3 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Chloe Ferreira's triple, broke a 4-all tie and put Harvard up 15-7. A 10-2 Northeastern spurt late in the quarter drew level at 17. The score held there after one quarter.

Mira Hightower and Jordan Delgado keyed a 10-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 19-17 deficit into a 27-19 lead. The Huskies countered with an 11-5 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 32-30. That was the score at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 13-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Ellison's free throws, which stretched the lead to 45-30. Harvard led 54-39 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 67-45. The Huskies never got closer than 11 in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-3 overall.
- Senior Mira Hightower finished with a team-high 16 points (6-of-9 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-5 FT) and six rebounds.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado contributed 13 points (4-of-12 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and seven assists.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 12 points (4-of-12 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and 16 rebounds.
- Chloe Ferreira (11) also reached double figures.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi came away with five steals.
- Hightower's 16 points were a career high.
