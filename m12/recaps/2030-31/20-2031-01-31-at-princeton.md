For a quarter and a half at Jadwin Gymnasium, Harvard women's basketball looked ready to hand Princeton its first Ivy League loss. The Tigers had other ideas. Princeton erased a 12-point Crimson lead with a second-quarter surge and made the key shots late for a 69-64 win on Friday night.

It was the first meeting between the teams since Harvard beat Princeton, 75-57, in last season's Ivy League Tournament final, and it lived up to the billing. The Crimson (13-7, 4-2 Ivy) led 28-16 in the second quarter, rallied from seven down in the fourth to tie the game at 59, and pulled within a point inside the final three minutes. Senior Thea Sutherland gave Harvard a lift off the bench all night. In the end, 17 turnovers and a 44-38 Princeton edge on the boards proved too much to overcome, and the Tigers stayed perfect in Ivy League play.

"I'm proud of the way we competed in a tough building. We had them where we wanted them in the second quarter and gave a lot of it back with turnovers, and against a team this good you can't do that. We'll be better for it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before sophomore Jordan Delgado knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. A 12-5 spurt put the Crimson in front 12-9, and Harvard led 14-11 after one quarter.

Mira Hightower, Delgado, and Sutherland keyed a 14-2 run early in the second quarter that broke a 14-all tie and gave Harvard a 28-16 cushion. Princeton answered with a 24-8 run of its own to flip the game to 40-36, and the Tigers scored 30 points in the frame to take a 41-38 lead into halftime.

Harvard punched back in the third quarter. Trailing 46-40, the Crimson strung together nine straight points, capped by a Chloe Ferreira 3-pointer, to go up 49-46. Princeton regained the lead and carried a 52-50 edge into the fourth.

The Tigers pushed the margin to 59-52, but Harvard answered with seven unanswered points, the last on a Ferreira free throw, to tie it at 59. Princeton scored the next four, and Sutherland's 3-pointer with 2:38 left made it 63-62. That was as close as the Crimson would get. Princeton answered with a three 11 seconds later, added a free throw with 1:14 left and a basket with 47 seconds remaining, and Sutherland's putback only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 13-7 overall and 4-2 in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 14 points (4-of-20 FG, 2-of-9 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and two steals.
- Senior Thea Sutherland scored 12 points off the bench on 5-of-6 shooting, including 2-of-2 from 3-point range, and grabbed seven rebounds in 12 minutes.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira added eight points and a team-high five assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower scored eight points on 3-of-5 shooting.
- First-year Wren Farrow went 2-for-2 from the field for four points in two minutes.
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
