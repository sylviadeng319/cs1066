Harvard women's basketball (15-8, 6-3 Ivy) picked up a 72-64 win over Columbia on Friday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Lions scored first before sophomore Chloe Ferreira made a free throw for Harvard's first points. Ferreira, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Jordan Delgado keyed a 24-7 run in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into a 24-9 lead. A 6-0 Columbia spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 24-15. That was the score after one quarter.

An 11-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Thea Sutherland's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 35-17. Columbia countered with an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 35-25. Harvard went into the locker room up 43-28.

The Lions went on a 10-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 48-38. The Crimson carried a 55-45 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard held a 63-62 lead in the final minutes. With 2:31 left, Ferreira knocked down a corner three to push the lead to 66-62. Mira Hightower converted a putback with 2:05 left to push the lead to 68-62. Columbia hit a jumper with 1:31 left to get within 68-64, but Esme Ellison answered when she hit a jumper from the elbow to make it 70-64. Ellison put the game away when she banked in a turnaround with 40 seconds left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-8 overall and 6-3 in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado paced the Crimson with 12 points (5-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi contributed 11 points (3-of-8 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison added 10 points (5-of-10 FG) and nine rebounds.
- Chloe Ferreira (10) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
