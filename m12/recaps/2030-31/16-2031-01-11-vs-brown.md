It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (11-5, 2-0 Ivy) made the plays it needed in a 73-68 win over Brown on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

Junior Mackenzie Takahashi opened the scoring when she knocked down a jumper. A 7-0 Brown spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 7-2 lead. The Crimson strung together a 16-4 burst in the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's layup, which turned a 7-2 deficit into an 18-11 lead. The Crimson took an 18-14 lead into the second quarter.

Harvard put together a 23-14 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 41-28. Harvard went into the locker room up 43-32.

Brown countered with a 17-9 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 54-49. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 66-64 lead in the final minutes. Takahashi hit a triple with 1:51 left to make it 69-64. Thea Sutherland scored through contact with 50 seconds left to make it 71-64. Brown hit a pair at the line with 33 seconds left to cut the deficit to 71-66. The Bears got a basket with 18 seconds left to get within 71-68. Mira Hightower banked in a turnaround with 6 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-5 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 6-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado paced the Crimson with 17 points (7-of-15 FG, 0-of-6 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT), three assists, and three steals.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi contributed 16 points (6-of-19 FG, 3-of-13 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Sophomore Esme Ellison added 12 points (6-of-6 FG) and seven rebounds.
- Chloe Ferreira (11) and Mira Hightower (10) also reached double figures.
