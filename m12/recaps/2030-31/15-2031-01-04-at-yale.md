Harvard women's basketball opened its Ivy League schedule the way it wanted to, by going into New Haven and handling its archrival. The Crimson (10-5, 1-0 Ivy) blew the game open with a 30-point third quarter and beat Yale, 72-61, on Saturday at John J. Lee Amphitheater.

It was a physical, low-percentage afternoon for both teams, and Harvard won it on the glass. The Crimson pulled down 53 rebounds, 17 of them on the offensive end, and held the Bulldogs to 29.6% shooting. Sophomore Esme Ellison controlled the paint with a monster rebounding game, and senior captain Mira Hightower added a double-digit rebounding night of her own. The win is Harvard's second straight and its third in a row over Yale, following a pair of victories over the Bulldogs last season.

"Conference play is a different animal, and you have to be ready to win ugly on the road. We didn't shoot it great in the first half, but we kept going to the glass, and in the third quarter our defense turned into offense," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Junior Valentina Okonkwo scored Harvard's first points of Ivy play on a post move with 9:51 left in the opening quarter, and neither team found much rhythm from there. The Crimson led 11-10 after one.

Tied at 11 early in the second quarter, Harvard put together a 13-3 spurt to go up 24-14. Yale steadied itself before the break, and the Crimson took a 26-19 lead into the locker room.

Then Harvard took over. Hightower, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Kendall Lindqvist keyed a 30-12 run that stretched the margin to 56-31, and the Crimson outscored Yale 30-16 in the third quarter to carry a 56-35 lead into the fourth.

Yale opened the final frame with eight straight points to get within 56-43, but Harvard had the answer. A 16-3 run, capped by a Hightower layup, pushed the lead to 72-46. The Bulldogs scored the game's final 15 points to make the final score respectable, but they never got closer than 11 in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-5 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with 16 points (7-of-19 FG, 1-of-10 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five assists.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira added 14 points (5-of-14 FG, 4-of-10 3-PT), making four of Harvard's seven 3-pointers.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison grabbed 17 rebounds, six of them offensive, matching her season high, and added nine points and three assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower posted 11 rebounds and four assists.
- Senior Kendall Lindqvist scored eight points off the bench, going 4-of-4 at the free throw line.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points, and the Crimson assisted on 19 of its 29 field goals.
- Harvard outrebounded Yale 53-43.
