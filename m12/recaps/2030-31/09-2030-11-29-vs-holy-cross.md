Harvard women's basketball (6-3) pulled away from Holy Cross for a 69-62 victory on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Crusaders 53-43.

"We came in with a clear game plan and our players executed it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board when she dropped in a floater with 9:21 left in the opening quarter. Mackenzie Takahashi, Valentina Okonkwo, and Tiana Adeyemi keyed a 19-10 run in the quarter that broke a 2-all tie and put Harvard up 21-12. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 23-16.

Holy Cross countered with a 14-2 run early in the quarter that flipped a 27-16 deficit into a 30-29 lead. An 18-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's bucket inside, turned a 30-29 deficit into a 47-32 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 9-2 Holy Cross spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 49-41. Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 58-41. Harvard took a 58-46 lead into the fourth quarter.

Holy Cross never got closer than six in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-3 overall. The Crimson is 3-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Chloe Ferreira led the way for Harvard with 20 points (8-of-12 FG, 4-of-5 3-PT), five rebounds, and four assists.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado added 11 points (4-of-15 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi finished with 10 points (3-of-15 FG, 0-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT), seven rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Mira Hightower grabbed 13 rebounds.
- Harvard held Holy Cross to 33.3% shooting from the field.
- Ferreira's 20 points were a career high.
