Senior Day at Lavietes Pavilion had nearly everything Harvard women's basketball could have wanted: a 15-point lead, a starting lineup featuring all three seniors, and a near-perfect shooting day from captain Mira Hightower in her final home game. What it didn't have was the ending. Yale tied the game with 32 seconds left in regulation and pulled away in overtime for a 74-69 win on Saturday.

The loss closes Harvard's season at 16-12 overall and 7-7 in Ivy League play, splitting the season series with the Bulldogs after the Crimson's 72-61 win in New Haven on Jan. 4. Harvard finishes fifth in the league standings, one game behind Brown and Columbia, which claimed the final two spots in the Ivy League Tournament at 8-6.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Hightower, Thea Sutherland, and Kendall Lindqvist. Hightower made the most of the moment, missing just once from the field, and Sutherland knocked down a pair of 3-pointers. Harvard forced 19 turnovers against eight of its own, but Yale shot 49.1% from the field and made the bigger plays at the end.

"I'm heartbroken for our seniors, because they deserved to walk off this floor with a win. Mira, Thea, and Kendall gave everything to this program, through a championship season and through a year when we were young and learning. Our underclassmen are better because of them," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Hightower got Harvard started with a jumper from the elbow with 9:26 left in the first quarter, and Harvard opened on a 19-7 run. Yale answered with seven straight points to get within 21-16, and the Crimson led 23-18 after one.

An 18-9 burst in the second quarter, capped by a Mackenzie Takahashi 3-pointer, pushed the lead to 41-27. Harvard took a 41-32 lead into halftime.

Chloe Ferreira and Valentina Okonkwo keyed a 6-0 run to open the second half, and the Crimson led 47-32. Then the Bulldogs caught fire. Yale outscored Harvard 16-1 to tie the game at 48, and the Crimson carried just a 49-48 lead into the fourth.

Harvard answered with a 10-0 run, capped by a Lindqvist putback, to go back up 59-48. The Crimson still led 65-61 in the final minutes, but Yale scored with 1:53 left and hit a jumper with 32 seconds remaining to tie it at 65, and regulation ended there.

Jordan Delgado scored in transition to open the overtime, but the Bulldogs led 71-69 late and sealed it with a 3-pointer with one second left, winning the extra period 9-4.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard finishes the season 16-12 overall and 7-7 in Ivy League play. The Crimson went 9-5 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Mira Hightower scored 15 points on 7-of-8 shooting (1-of-1 FT) and grabbed eight rebounds, five of them offensive, in her final home game.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado added 15 points (6-of-15 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-3 FT) and a team-high six assists in 35 minutes.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi scored 12 points (5-of-13 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT) off the bench with five rebounds.
- Senior Thea Sutherland scored nine points (2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) in 34 minutes.
- Senior Kendall Lindqvist had a team-high four steals and six rebounds.
- Harvard made six of its 13 3-point attempts and recorded nine steals.
