Harvard women's basketball (3-1) dropped a 61-51 decision to Syracuse on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Syracuse shot 38.1% from the field, while Harvard was held to 29.5%.

"Give Syracuse credit. They executed better than we did today," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board when she connected from deep with 9:31 left in the opening quarter. A 23-4 Syracuse spurt in the quarter flipped a 6-2 deficit into a 25-10 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Orange countered with a 12-2 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 37-12. Harvard trailed 37-16 at halftime.

Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 42-25. The Orange led 47-27 after three quarters.

The Orange controlled the fourth quarter from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 3-1 overall.
- Junior Valentina Okonkwo finished with a team-high 11 points (5-of-5 FG, 1-of-1 FT).
- Sophomore Esme Ellison finished with 10 points (4-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi chipped in 10 points (3-of-13 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), five rebounds, and four assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 12 of its 18 field goals.
