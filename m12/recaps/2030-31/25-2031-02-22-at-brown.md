Harvard women's basketball (15-10, 6-5 Ivy) dropped a 68-61 decision to Brown on Saturday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

"Give Brown credit. They executed better than we did today," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before sophomore Jordan Delgado sank two free throws for Harvard's first points. Brown won the first quarter 21-8. Brown carried a 21-8 lead into the second quarter.

A 10-1 Brown spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 31-9. A 6-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's layup, cut the deficit to 31-15. Harvard trailed 37-20 at halftime.

Brown countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 46-23. The Crimson strung together a 13-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Delgado's triple, which cut the deficit to 46-36. The Bears led 51-40 after three quarters.

Harvard got within 53-49 with 7:35 left, but Brown outscored the Crimson 15-12 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 15-10 overall and 6-5 in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Esme Ellison led the way for Harvard with 17 points (7-of-12 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Mackenzie Takahashi chipped in 11 points (3-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Sophomore Jordan Delgado came away with four steals.
- The Crimson assisted on 16 of its 24 field goals.
