Harvard women's basketball (3-0) never trailed on Tuesday, beating Boston University, 56-43, at Case Gym in Boston, Mass.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Terriers 60-47. The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Jordan Delgado opened the scoring when she drilled a three. Harvard opened the game on a 12-6 run. The Crimson took a 12-8 lead into the second quarter.

Boston University countered with a 7-1 run early in the quarter that drew level at 15. Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt early in the quarter that broke a 15-all tie and put Harvard up 27-15. Harvard went into the locker room up 27-18.

Boston University went on a 6-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 27-24. A 16-3 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Delgado's jumper, stretched the lead to 43-27. The Crimson carried a 43-29 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 9-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Delgado's 3-pointer, which stretched the lead to 52-35.
