Harvard women's basketball (8-5) let an 11-point lead slip away on Sunday in a 61-58 loss to Providence at Alumni Hall in Providence, R.I.

The Friars shot 37.9% from the field, while Harvard was held to 30.9%.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Chloe Ferreira got Harvard on the board when she drilled a three with 9:41 left in the opening quarter. A 9-2 Providence spurt early in the quarter flipped a 3-0 deficit into a 9-5 lead. Harvard put together a 16-1 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 9-5 deficit into a 21-10 lead. Harvard led 21-15 after the first quarter.

A 7-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Mira Hightower's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 28-19. Harvard took a 32-26 lead into halftime.

The Friars countered with an 18-10 run in the quarter that flipped a 32-26 deficit into a 44-42 lead. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Jordan Delgado, Ferreira, and Hightower keyed a 7-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 44-42 deficit into a 49-44 lead. Harvard held a 56-54 lead in the final minutes. Delgado buried a mid-range jumper with 2:41 left to push the lead to 58-54. The Friars drained a 3-pointer with 1:46 left to cut the deficit to 58-57. Providence scored inside with 1:01 left to go in front 59-58. The Friars converted a layup with 52 seconds left to stretch the lead to 61-58. Harvard did not score over the final 2:41. The Crimson's final attempt to tie it came up short.
