A night after a one-point loss at Brown, Harvard women's basketball bounced back in a big way. Junior Isla Marchetti scored a career high, and the Crimson pulled away from Yale for a 78-62 win on Saturday at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn.

The Crimson (20-4, 8-2 Ivy) reached 20 wins for the season and completed a sweep of the Bulldogs for the second year in a row, following a 70-49 win in Cambridge on Jan. 20. Harvard trailed 42-39 midway through the third quarter before an 18-3 run turned the game, and its 78 points were the most it has scored this season. Marchetti was nearly unstoppable, missing only five shots all night.

"After last night, I wanted to see how we'd respond, and I loved our answer. Isla was terrific, and when she gets going like that, everybody else plays with more confidence," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored first, and senior Juniper Pryor answered with a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. The Bulldogs went up 11-6 before an 11-5 Harvard spurt gave the Crimson a 17-16 lead, but Yale led 18-17 after one quarter.

Harvard opened the second quarter with a 14-6 burst, capped by a Marchetti layup, to go up 31-24. Yale answered with an 8-1 run to tie the game at 32, and the Crimson took a 34-32 lead into halftime.

The Bulldogs went back in front 42-39 midway through the third quarter. Marchetti, Jasmine Ramsey, and Celeste Laurent then keyed the 18-3 run that put Harvard ahead 57-45, and the Crimson led 57-47 entering the fourth.

Harvard put the game away early in the final period. A 15-1 run, capped by two Brielle Mendoza free throws, stretched the lead to 72-48.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-4 overall and 8-2 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Isla Marchetti scored a career-high 27 points (11-of-16 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), topping the 24 she scored against Columbia on Jan. 13.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza added 12 points (3-of-7 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT) and nine rebounds, six of them on the offensive end.
- Senior Celeste Laurent scored 12 points and dished out a team-high six assists, and senior Juniper Pryor added five assists.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey had 11 points (4-of-6 FG), nine rebounds, and three assists.
- Harvard's 78 points were its most in a game this season. The Crimson shot 45.3% from the field and outrebounded Yale 42-36.
