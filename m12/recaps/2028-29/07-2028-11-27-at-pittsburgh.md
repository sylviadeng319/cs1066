Harvard women's basketball (5-2) let an 11-point lead slip away on Monday in a 67-64 loss to Pittsburgh at Petersen Events Center in Pittsburgh, Pa.

Pittsburgh shot 50.0% from the field, while Harvard was held to 42.9%.

## How it Happened

The Panthers scored first before senior Jasmine Ramsey hit a jumper from the elbow for Harvard's first points. Pittsburgh opened the game on a 6-0 run. A 19-10 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Juniper Pryor's 3-pointer, turned a 6-0 deficit into a 19-16 lead. The Crimson took a 22-20 lead into the second quarter.

Pittsburgh went on a 6-0 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 29-25 deficit into a 31-29 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 33-32.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 39-37 deficit into a 45-39 lead. A 10-2 Pittsburgh spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 45-39 deficit into a 49-47 lead. The game was tied at 49 after three quarters.

The Crimson strung together a 15-4 burst early in the quarter, capped by Ramsey's 3-pointer, which broke a 49-all tie and put Harvard up 64-53. Harvard held a 64-61 lead in the final minutes. The Panthers made two free throws with 1:21 left to get within 64-63. Pittsburgh scored inside with 44 seconds left to go in front 65-64. The Panthers converted a layup with 33 seconds left to stretch the lead to 67-64. Harvard did not score over the final 6:24. The Crimson's final attempt to tie it came up short.
