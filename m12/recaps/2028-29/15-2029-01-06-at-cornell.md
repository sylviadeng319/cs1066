Harvard women's basketball (13-2, 1-0 Ivy) pulled away from Cornell for a 71-55 victory on Saturday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. The win is Harvard's eighth straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 42-33.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before junior Isla Marchetti added a free throw for Harvard's first points. Cornell opened the game on an 11-3 run. An 8-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's 3-pointer, evened the score at 11. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 12-0 Cornell spurt early in the quarter flipped a 17-11 deficit into a 23-17 lead. The Crimson strung together an 11-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Juniper Pryor's triple, which turned a 23-17 deficit into a 28-25 lead. Harvard took a 30-29 lead into halftime.

The Big Red countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 31-29 deficit into a 36-31 lead. Jasmine Ramsey, Celeste Laurent, and Pryor keyed a 12-3 run in the quarter that turned a 36-31 deficit into a 43-39 lead. Harvard led 45-43 heading into the fourth.

Cornell got within 47-45 with 9:25 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 24-4 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 71-49.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-2 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey led Harvard with 15 points (6-of-10 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT), seven rebounds, five assists, and three steals.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza recorded a double-double with 14 points (6-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- Senior Celeste Laurent added 13 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), three assists, and three steals.
- Juniper Pryor (13) also reached double figures.
