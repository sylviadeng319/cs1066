Syracuse made enough plays down the stretch to edge Harvard women's basketball (5-1), 75-74, on Wednesday at JMA Wireless Dome in Syracuse, N.Y.

The loss snapped Harvard's five-game winning streak. Turnovers were costly, as Harvard gave the ball away 20 times.

"We had our chances. It stings, but we'll learn from it and be better for it," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Orange scored first before junior Niamh Hollis finished a layup in transition for Harvard's first points. Syracuse won the first quarter 31-18. The Orange carried a 31-18 lead into the second quarter.

An 8-2 Syracuse spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 39-20. Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 39-26. Syracuse took a 44-26 lead into the break.

The Crimson strung together a 15-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's jumper, which cut the deficit to 51-43. The Orange countered with a 9-3 run late in the quarter that extended the lead to 60-46. Syracuse led 60-49 after three quarters.

Harvard held a 69-68 lead in the final minutes. The Orange made two free throws with 1:32 left to go in front 70-69, but Mackenzie Takahashi answered when she knocked down a corner three to give the Crimson a 72-70 lead. Laurent went 2-for-2 at the line with 46 seconds left to make it 74-70. Syracuse answered from beyond the arc with 7 seconds left to cut the deficit to 74-73. The Orange scored inside with 5 seconds left to go in front 75-74. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 5-1 overall.
- Senior Celeste Laurent led the way for Harvard with 24 points (6-of-8 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 9-of-9 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Niamh Hollis finished with 13 points (5-of-8 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 30 points.
- The 24 points were a season high for Laurent.
