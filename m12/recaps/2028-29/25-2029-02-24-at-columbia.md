Behind a strong first quarter, Harvard women's basketball (21-4, 9-2 Ivy) beat Columbia, 62-54, on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Niamh Hollis hit a triple. Mackenzie Takahashi, Brielle Mendoza, and Bria Vasquez keyed a 15-7 run in the quarter that turned a 5-3 deficit into an 18-12 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

Columbia went on a 9-3 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 26-21. Harvard went into the locker room up 38-28.

Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 49-35. The Crimson carried a 51-42 advantage into the final frame.

The Lions got within 54-all with 4:47 left before Harvard took control. The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's free throw, which broke a 54-all tie and put Harvard up 62-54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 21-4 overall and 9-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Celeste Laurent finished with 14 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and three assists.
- First-year Bria Vasquez finished with 14 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Junior Isla Marchetti hit three 3-pointers.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard held Columbia to 28.4% shooting from the field.
- Harvard's bench contributed 27 points.
- Vasquez's 14 points were a career high.
