Harvard women's basketball (4-0) pulled away from Albany for a 66-51 victory on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Great Danes scored first before first-year Bria Vasquez converted a putback for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together a 12-3 spurt in the quarter that turned a 7-2 deficit into a 14-10 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 14-12.

The Crimson strung together an 18-2 burst in the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's jumper, which stretched the lead to 32-15. Harvard went into the locker room up 35-21.

Albany countered with an 11-3 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 38-32. Jasmine Ramsey, Brielle Mendoza, and Juniper Pryor keyed a 15-5 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 53-37. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

A 9-3 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 64-44. Albany never got closer than 13 in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-0 overall. The Crimson is 4-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Celeste Laurent paced the Crimson with 18 points (7-of-12 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey contributed 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Isla Marchetti finished with 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
