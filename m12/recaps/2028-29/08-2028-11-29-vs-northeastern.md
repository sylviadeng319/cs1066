Harvard women's basketball (6-2) picked up a 65-58 win over Northeastern on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The victory snapped a two-game skid.

"That was a complete team effort, and that's what we're going to need as the season goes on," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Juniper Pryor hit a pull-up jumper. The Huskies countered with a 10-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 9-2 deficit into a 12-9 lead. Niamh Hollis, Pryor, and Celeste Laurent keyed an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that turned a 12-9 deficit into a 17-12 lead. The Crimson took a 21-18 lead into the second quarter.

Harvard put together a 15-4 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 36-22. The Huskies went on a 9-1 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 37-31. Harvard took a 39-31 lead into halftime.

An 8-0 Northeastern spurt early in the quarter drew level at 39. An 8-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Pryor's layup, broke a 39-all tie and put Harvard up 47-41. Harvard took a 49-45 lead into the fourth quarter.

Northeastern got within 58-56 with 4:13 left, but Harvard outscored the Huskies 7-2 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-2 overall. The Crimson is 5-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Celeste Laurent finished with a team-high 16 points (7-of-16 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT).
- Senior Juniper Pryor added 14 points (7-of-12 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT), five rebounds, and four assists.
- Junior Niamh Hollis contributed 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and two blocks.
- Junior Isla Marchetti came away with five steals.
