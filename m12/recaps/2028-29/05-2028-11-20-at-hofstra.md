Harvard women's basketball (5-0) led from start to finish in a 67-44 win over Hofstra on Monday at Mack Sports Complex in Hempstead, N.Y.

The Crimson shot 50.0% from the field and held Hofstra to 35.3%. The win is Harvard's fifth straight.

## How it Happened

Junior Lauren Wakefield opened the scoring when she hit a jumper from the elbow. Harvard put together a 14-1 spurt in the quarter that broke a 4-all tie and put Harvard up 18-5. Harvard led 20-7 after the first quarter.

Hofstra went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 20-14. The Crimson strung together a 10-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's putback, which stretched the lead to 30-16. Harvard went into the locker room up 32-23.

A 19-4 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Niamh Hollis's layup, stretched the lead to 55-31. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Celeste Laurent, Brielle Mendoza, and Thea Sutherland keyed an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 67-44. The lead reached 25, allowing Wakefield, Sutherland, and Emeka Rhodes to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-0 overall.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza finished with 11 points (3-of-4 FG, 5-of-6 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Isla Marchetti chipped in 11 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT).
- Junior Niamh Hollis added 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT) and three assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 28 points.
