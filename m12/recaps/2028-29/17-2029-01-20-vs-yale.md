Harvard women's basketball stretched its winning streak to 10 games on Saturday, smothering Yale in a 70-49 victory at Lavietes Pavilion to stay unbeaten in Ivy League play.

The Crimson (15-2, 3-0 Ivy) won with defense and rebounding. Yale made just 18 of its 71 shots from the field (25.4%), and Harvard dominated the glass 57-37, grabbing 14 offensive rebounds. Junior Isla Marchetti and senior Celeste Laurent shared team scoring honors, and Marchetti ran the offense as well, leading the team in assists. It was Harvard's fifth straight win over the Bulldogs, a run that dates to the 2026-27 season.

"Our defense was terrific tonight, and we owned the boards. When we defend and rebound like that, we give ourselves a chance every night. I loved that everybody got in and contributed," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored first, but Marchetti answered with a driving layup, and she, Brielle Mendoza, and first-year Valentina Okonkwo keyed a 9-0 run that gave Harvard a 9-2 lead. The Bulldogs responded with nine straight points of their own to go ahead 11-9, but the Crimson scored the last six points of the quarter to lead 15-11.

Harvard took charge in the second quarter with a 12-4 run, capped by a Jasmine Ramsey bucket inside, that pushed the lead to 27-15. The Crimson took a 32-22 advantage into halftime.

Yale made a push in the third quarter, using a 9-2 spurt to cut the deficit to 41-34. Harvard closed the period with a 7-0 burst, capped by a Marchetti 3-pointer, to lead 48-34 entering the fourth.

A 12-3 run early in the fourth quarter stretched the margin to 63-41, and the Bulldogs never threatened again. The lead reached 22, and all 14 players on Harvard's roster saw the floor.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-2 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson has won 10 straight games and is 9-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Isla Marchetti scored 17 points (5-of-7 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) with five rebounds and a team-high seven assists.
- Senior Celeste Laurent also scored 17 points (5-of-13 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey added 14 points (6-of-10 FG, 2-of-2 FT), eight rebounds, and three blocks.
- Senior Juniper Pryor and junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed seven rebounds apiece as Harvard outrebounded Yale 57-37.
- Harvard held Yale to 25.4% shooting, made 15 of its 18 free throws, and assisted on 17 of its 24 field goals.
