It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (9-2) made the plays it needed in a 59-58 win over Drexel on Wednesday at Daskalakis Athletic Center in Philadelphia, Pa.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Dragons 49-41. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

"Those are the games that build a team. We didn't play our best, but we found a way, and that's growth," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Jasmine Ramsey scored through contact. Harvard opened the game on a 21-2 run. The Dragons went on a 10-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 21-12. Harvard led 23-13 after the first quarter.

The teams traded baskets for much of the second quarter. The Crimson led 37-26 at the break.

A 7-0 Drexel spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 37-33. Harvard put together an 11-1 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 48-34. Harvard led 48-38 heading into the fourth.

Drexel countered with a 14-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 53-40 deficit into a 54-53 lead. Drexel led 56-55 in the final minutes. Isla Marchetti buried a mid-range jumper with 2:26 left to put Harvard ahead 57-56. The Dragons converted a layup with 46 seconds left to go in front 58-57, but Celeste Laurent answered when she dropped in a floater to put Harvard ahead 59-58.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-2 overall.
- Senior Celeste Laurent led Harvard with 14 points (5-of-11 FG, 0-of-6 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), six assists, and five steals.
- First-year Mackenzie Takahashi added 12 points (4-of-16 FG, 3-of-12 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Isla Marchetti finished with 12 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT).
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard held Drexel to 31.3% shooting from the field.
