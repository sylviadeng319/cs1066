The Crimson made quick work of Sacred Heart on Thursday, as Harvard women's basketball (12-2) built a lead as large as 26 on the way to a 69-49 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 46.4% from the field and held Sacred Heart to 30.9%. The win is Harvard's seventh straight.

## How it Happened

The Pioneers scored first before junior Isla Marchetti dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 16-15.

The Crimson strung together a 14-2 burst in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's layup, which stretched the lead to 32-19. The Pioneers went on a 6-0 run late in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 32-25. The Crimson led 33-25 at the break.

A 17-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Ramsey's putback, stretched the lead to 50-25. The Crimson carried a 54-31 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together an 8-2 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 64-38. With the lead as large as 26, Valentina Okonkwo, Lauren Wakefield, and Kendall Lindqvist saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-2 overall. The Crimson is 7-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey paced the Crimson with 21 points (9-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Juniper Pryor contributed 10 points (4-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Celeste Laurent hit three 3-pointers.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard outrebounded Sacred Heart 41-31, including 10 offensive boards.
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
- The 21 points were a season high for Ramsey.
