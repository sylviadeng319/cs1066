Harvard women's basketball (18-3, 6-1 Ivy) pulled away from Penn for a 72-57 victory on Saturday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

"We did a lot of the little things well tonight: boxing out, talking on defense, making the extra pass," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

First-year Mackenzie Takahashi opened the scoring when she buried a 3-pointer. Harvard opened the game on a 13-4 run. The Quakers countered with a 12-2 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 13-4 deficit into a 16-15 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

The Quakers went on an 18-10 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 22-20 deficit into a 38-32 lead. That was the score at halftime.

A 26-9 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Brielle Mendoza's three, turned a 38-32 deficit into a 58-47 lead. The Crimson carried a 58-51 advantage into the final frame.

Juniper Pryor, Niamh Hollis, and Mendoza keyed a 14-4 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 72-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-3 overall and 6-1 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Juniper Pryor led the way for Harvard with 20 points (7-of-17 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), five rebounds, three assists, and three steals.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza recorded a double-double with 12 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-3 FT), 14 rebounds, and four assists.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey chipped in 12 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Senior Celeste Laurent dished out a team-high eight assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 28 field goals.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded 10 steals.
- The 20 points were a season high for Pryor.
