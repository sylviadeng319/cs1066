Harvard women's basketball (3-0) held on for a 69-64 win over Seton Hall on Tuesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Isla Marchetti buried a 3-pointer. Harvard opened the game on a 14-2 run. A 15-2 Seton Hall spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 14-2 deficit into a 17-16 lead. The Crimson took a 26-19 lead into the second quarter.

Celeste Laurent, Jasmine Ramsey, and Juniper Pryor keyed a 15-6 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 41-25. Seton Hall went on a 6-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 41-31. The Crimson led 43-32 at the break.

A 15-8 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Marchetti's layup, stretched the lead to 58-40. Seton Hall countered with an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 58-48. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 67-57 lead in the final minutes. The Pirates scored inside with 1:49 left to cut the deficit to 67-59. The Pirates converted a layup with 1:24 left to cut the deficit to 67-61, but Ramsey answered when she scored through contact to push the lead to 69-61. The Pirates scored with 42 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-0 overall.
- Senior Juniper Pryor led the way for Harvard with 18 points (6-of-15 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and nine assists.
- Junior Isla Marchetti added 14 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three steals.
- Senior Celeste Laurent chipped in 12 points (5-of-14 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed 13 rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 27 field goals.
- Marchetti's 14 points were a career high.
