Harvard women's basketball's season ended in heartbreak on its home floor. No. 3 seed Columbia closed the game on an 11-2 run, capped by a layup with two seconds left, to beat No. 2 seed Harvard, 57-56, in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson (23-6) had beaten the Lions in both regular-season meetings and in last year's Ivy Tournament semifinal, and they led 54-46 midway through the fourth quarter. But Harvard did not score over the final 3:29, and Columbia rallied on a night when neither team shot better than 30% from the field. Senior Celeste Laurent did all she could in her final game, scoring a season high and hitting five 3-pointers.

"It hurts right now, and it should, because this group cared so much. Celeste was incredible tonight, and our seniors have meant everything to this program. I couldn't be prouder of this team and what it accomplished this season," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Senior Jasmine Ramsey opened the scoring with a putback with 9:36 left in the first quarter. Harvard led 9-7 late in the period before the Lions closed it on a 6-0 run to lead 13-9.

Laurent, Ramsey, and Isla Marchetti sparked a 17-2 run early in the second quarter that turned that deficit into a 26-15 Harvard lead. The Crimson took a 30-24 lead into halftime.

A 9-0 run midway through the third quarter, capped by a Marchetti layup, pushed the lead to 39-28. Columbia answered with 14 straight points to go in front 42-39, and the game was tied at 42 after three quarters.

The Lions scored first in the fourth for a 44-42 lead, but Harvard responded with a 12-2 burst, capped by a Niamh Hollis bucket, to go up 54-46. The Crimson still led 56-53 in the final minutes. Columbia scored inside with 36 seconds left to make it 56-55, then converted a layup with two seconds remaining for its first lead since early in the fourth. Harvard's last attempt did not fall.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard's season ends with a 23-6 record, including an 11-3 mark in Ivy League play.
- Senior Celeste Laurent led Harvard with a season-high 27 points (9-of-16 FG, 5-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT) and nine rebounds in her final collegiate game.
- Junior Isla Marchetti added 10 points (4-of-16 FG, 2-of-2 FT), a team-high five assists, and two steals.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed a team-high 10 rebounds.
- Junior Niamh Hollis had five points and seven rebounds off the bench, and first-year Mackenzie Takahashi came up with three steals.
- Harvard made 6 of its 13 3-point attempts and committed just 10 turnovers to Columbia's 14.
