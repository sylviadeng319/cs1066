Harvard women's basketball had not lost at Lavietes Pavilion all season, but Princeton came to Cambridge on Saturday and left with a 75-67 win and a sweep of the season series.

The Crimson (22-5, 10-3 Ivy) entered on a three-game winning streak after holding off Penn on Friday night, and they shot 53.2% from the field. But Harvard committed 16 turnovers to Princeton's nine, and the Tigers took 15 more shots, built a 16-point lead, and held off a late Crimson push. Princeton has now won five straight meetings between the teams, including a 77-57 win at Jadwin Gymnasium on Feb. 2.

"I'm proud of the way we fought in the fourth quarter, but we gave them too many extra possessions to dig out of that hole. Princeton is a very good team. We have to be sharper with the ball, and we will be," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before senior Jasmine Ramsey converted a putback for Harvard's first points. The Tigers led 19-15 after one quarter.

Princeton seized control early in the second quarter with an 18-6 run that made it 39-23. Harvard scored the final five points of the half to trail 39-28 at the break.

The Crimson used an 11-4 spurt early in the third quarter to get within 51-43. Princeton answered with an 8-0 run late in the period and led 61-45 entering the fourth.

Harvard kept coming. The Crimson outscored the Tigers 22-14 in the final period, but the deficit was too large to erase, and Princeton closed out the eight-point win.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 22-5 overall and 10-3 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 12-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Celeste Laurent led Harvard with 17 points (6-of-8 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza added 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Niamh Hollis scored nine points on 4-of-5 shooting off the bench, and senior Juniper Pryor added nine points with two 3-pointers.
- First-year Mackenzie Takahashi scored seven points (2-of-3 FG, 3-of-3 FT), and Harvard's bench contributed 21 points.
- Harvard shot 53.2% from the field (25-of-47) and won the fourth quarter 22-14.
