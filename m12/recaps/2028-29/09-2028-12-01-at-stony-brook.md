Down 15 in the third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (7-2) rallied for a 60-58 win over Stony Brook on Friday at Stony Brook Arena in Stony Brook, N.Y.

Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Junior Brielle Mendoza got Harvard on the board when she made a free throw with 8:30 left in the opening quarter. The Seawolves countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 4-0 deficit into a 7-4 lead. The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Niamh Hollis's layup, which turned a 7-4 deficit into a 10-7 lead. Harvard trailed 16-14 after the opening frame.

A 9-0 Stony Brook spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 25-14. Hollis, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Juniper Pryor keyed a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 25-21. Harvard trailed 30-21 at halftime.

Stony Brook went on an 11-0 run midway through the quarter, which extended the lead to 45-30. A 6-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's layup, cut the deficit to 45-36. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Seawolves led 56-51 in the final minutes. With 2:42 left, Hollis connected from deep to make it 56-54. Isla Marchetti scored on a driving layup with 2:02 left to tie the game at 56. With 1:12 left, Hollis knocked down a jumper to put Harvard ahead 58-56. Pryor buried a mid-range jumper with 53 seconds left to push the lead to 60-56. Stony Brook scored with 43 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-2 overall.
- Senior Juniper Pryor finished with 11 points (4-of-15 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), six rebounds, and six assists.
- Junior Niamh Hollis finished with 11 points (5-of-6 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 17 of its 22 field goals.
