It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (11-2) made the plays it needed in a 74-73 win over Bryant on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers. The win is Harvard's sixth straight.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Isla Marchetti sank two free throws. The Crimson strung together a 12-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's layup, which broke a 2-all tie and put Harvard up 14-4. A 9-0 Bryant spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 14-13. The score held there after one quarter.

Harvard put together a 17-4 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 24-21 deficit into a 38-28 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 38-30.

Mackenzie Takahashi, Marchetti, and Brielle Mendoza keyed a 13-1 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 59-44. The Crimson carried a 60-46 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard held a 72-63 lead in the final minutes. Bryant scored inside with 2:24 left to cut the deficit to 72-65. The Bulldogs got a basket with 1:46 left to cut the deficit to 72-67. Bryant drained a 3-pointer with 56 seconds left to get within 72-70, but Laurent answered when she dropped in a floater to make it 74-70. The Bulldogs scored with 22 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-2 overall. The Crimson is 6-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Celeste Laurent finished with a team-high 20 points (7-of-18 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Isla Marchetti finished with 15 points (5-of-17 FG, 3-of-12 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Brielle Mendoza added 11 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT).
- Mackenzie Takahashi (10) also reached double figures.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed nine rebounds.
- Marchetti's 15 points were a career high.
