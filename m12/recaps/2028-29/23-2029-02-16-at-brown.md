Harvard women's basketball (19-4, 7-2 Ivy) came up just short on Friday, falling 55-54 to Brown at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Bears 44-36.

## How it Happened

First-year Bria Vasquez opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Brown went on a 6-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 9-7 deficit into a 13-9 lead. Harvard trailed 13-11 after the opening frame.

Harvard put together a 7-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned an 18-15 deficit into a 22-18 lead. A 6-0 Brown spurt late in the quarter flipped a 26-22 deficit into a 28-26 lead. The score held there at halftime.

A 13-5 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's layup, turned a 28-26 deficit into a 39-33 lead. The Crimson carried a 41-36 advantage into the final frame.

The Bears countered with a 9-0 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 46-40 deficit into a 49-46 lead. Harvard held a 50-49 lead in the final minutes. Brown converted a layup with 2:05 left to go in front 51-50, but Thea Sutherland answered when she scored through contact to put Harvard ahead 52-51. Brielle Mendoza converted a putback with 1:23 left to push the lead to 54-51. The Bears hit a jumper with 1:20 left to get within 54-53. The Bears got a basket with 28 seconds left to go in front 55-54. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 19-4 overall and 7-2 in Ivy League play.

- Senior Jasmine Ramsey chipped in 10 points (5-of-7 FG) and nine rebounds.
