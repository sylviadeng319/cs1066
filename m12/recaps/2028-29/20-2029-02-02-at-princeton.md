Harvard women's basketball's 12-game winning streak came to an end Friday night at Jadwin Gymnasium, where Princeton pulled away for a 77-57 win in a matchup of Ivy League unbeatens.

The Crimson (17-3, 5-1 Ivy) had not lost since Nov. 27, and they briefly led in the first quarter, but the Tigers controlled the game from there. Harvard committed 20 turnovers, and Princeton made 16 of its 18 free throws and knocked down nine 3-pointers. The win was Princeton's fourth straight over Harvard, a stretch that includes last season's Ivy League Tournament final.

"Princeton played a terrific game, and they made us pay for every mistake. We turned it over too much to beat a team that good on its floor. Games like this show you where you need to grow, and we'll take the lessons and move forward," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Junior Isla Marchetti got Harvard on the board with a driving layup with 9:21 left in the opening quarter, and the Crimson jumped out to a 4-0 lead. Princeton answered with a 12-2 run to go up 12-6, and Harvard came back with a 9-2 spurt to retake the lead at 15-14. The Tigers closed the quarter strong and led 22-17 after one.

Princeton stretched the margin in the second quarter, using a 15-7 run to go up 37-28, and that was the score at halftime.

Jasmine Ramsey and first-year Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 6-0 run early in the third quarter that pulled Harvard within 43-36. The Tigers responded with a 12-3 spurt and led 57-41 entering the fourth.

A 15-2 Princeton run early in the final period pushed the lead to 72-49, and the Tigers held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-3 overall and 5-1 in Ivy League play. The defeat snapped a 12-game winning streak.
- Senior Juniper Pryor led Harvard with 11 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Senior Celeste Laurent added nine points (4-of-8 FG) and a team-high four assists.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza had eight points, a team-high seven rebounds, three steals, and two blocks.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey scored eight points on 4-of-6 shooting, and first-year Mackenzie Takahashi added eight off the bench.
- Harvard recorded 10 steals but committed 20 turnovers, and made 10 of 16 free throws to Princeton's 16 of 18.
