Behind a strong fourth quarter, Harvard women's basketball (14-2, 2-0 Ivy) beat Columbia, 70-58, on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 46.3% from the field and held Columbia to 37.9%. The win is Harvard's ninth straight.

## How it Happened

The Lions scored first before senior Celeste Laurent sank two free throws for Harvard's first points. An 18-8 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Juniper Pryor's three, turned a 4-0 deficit into an 18-12 lead. The Crimson took a 19-16 lead into the second quarter.

The Lions countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 23-18 deficit into a 24-23 lead. Isla Marchetti, Niamh Hollis, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 14-7 run in the quarter that turned a 24-23 deficit into a 37-31 lead. That was the score at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 14-7 burst in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's free throws, which stretched the lead to 54-43. Harvard took a 54-48 lead into the fourth quarter.

Columbia got within 54-52 with 9:08 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 10-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 68-56.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-2 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Isla Marchetti led the way for Harvard with 24 points (8-of-13 FG, 4-of-8 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT).
- Senior Juniper Pryor finished with 13 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Celeste Laurent chipped in 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 0-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Mackenzie Takahashi (10) also reached double figures.
- Marchetti's 24 points were a career high.
