Harvard women's basketball (8-2) erased a 10-point deficit and came away with a 62-60 win over Fairfield on Sunday at Leo D. Mahoney Arena in Fairfield, Conn.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

"Our defense turned the game. Once we started getting stops, everything opened up," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

First-year Mackenzie Takahashi got Harvard on the board when she hit a pull-up jumper with 9:48 left in the opening quarter. The Stags went on an 8-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 4-2 deficit into a 10-4 lead. Fairfield led 12-11 after one.

The Crimson strung together a 7-1 burst early in the quarter, capped by Bria Vasquez's layup, which turned a 14-11 deficit into an 18-15 lead. A 15-5 Fairfield spurt midway through the quarter flipped an 18-15 deficit into a 30-23 lead. Harvard trailed 30-26 at halftime.

Brielle Mendoza, Niamh Hollis, and Celeste Laurent keyed an 11-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 30-26 deficit into a 37-30 lead. The Stags countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that drew level at 37. Harvard took a 48-45 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Stags countered with a 13-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 48-45 deficit into a 58-48 lead. The Stags led 60-53 in the final minutes. With 2:48 left, Isla Marchetti sank two free throws to make it 60-55. With 1:36 left, Vasquez hit a triple to pull Harvard within 60-58. With 1:02 left, Laurent buried a mid-range jumper to tie the game at 60. Vasquez went 2-for-2 at the line with 3 seconds left to give the Crimson a 62-60 lead, and the Crimson held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-2 overall.
