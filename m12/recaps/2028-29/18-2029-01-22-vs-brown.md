Harvard women's basketball (16-2, 4-0 Ivy) picked up a 73-64 win over Brown on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 11th straight.

"We came in with a clear game plan and our players executed it," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before senior Jasmine Ramsey finished a post move for Harvard's first points. A 26-12 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's jumper, turned a 4-2 deficit into a 28-16 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

Juniper Pryor, Niamh Hollis, and Ramsey keyed a 6-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 34-18. A 15-2 Brown spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 36-33. Harvard took a 42-37 lead into halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 21-8 burst in the quarter, capped by Mira Hightower's putback, which stretched the lead to 63-48. Harvard took a 63-50 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard kept Brown at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below nine.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-2 overall and 4-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Celeste Laurent finished with a team-high 16 points (6-of-17 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and six assists.
- Junior Isla Marchetti contributed 15 points (6-of-14 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT).
- Junior Brielle Mendoza chipped in 12 points (4-of-7 FG, 4-of-5 FT), seven rebounds, and two blocks.
- Jasmine Ramsey (10) also reached double figures.
