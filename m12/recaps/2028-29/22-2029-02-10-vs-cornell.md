Harvard women's basketball (19-3, 7-1 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Cornell, 74-55, at Lavietes Pavilion.

"That's what it looks like when we're locked in on the defensive end. Our defense created our offense tonight," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Junior Brielle Mendoza got Harvard on the board when she scored inside with 9:26 left in the opening quarter. Harvard outscored the Big Red 29-7 in the first quarter. Harvard led 29-7 after the first quarter.

A 14-4 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Niamh Hollis's bucket, stretched the lead to 48-16. Harvard took a 48-19 lead into halftime.

Isla Marchetti and Mendoza keyed a 6-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 54-22. A 13-0 Cornell spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 54-35. Harvard took a 57-38 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 12-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Hollis's layup, which stretched the lead to 69-46. The lead reached 32, allowing Valentina Okonkwo, Thea Sutherland, and Emeka Rhodes to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-3 overall and 7-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- First-year Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 16 points (7-of-15 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Isla Marchetti contributed 15 points (5-of-13 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Niamh Hollis added 12 points (5-of-8 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT).
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard held Cornell to 31.6% shooting from the field.
- Harvard's bench contributed 34 points.
- Takahashi's 16 points were a career high.
