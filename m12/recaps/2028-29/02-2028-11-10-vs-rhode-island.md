Behind a strong second quarter, Harvard women's basketball (2-0) beat Rhode Island, 74-62, on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

"There's a lot to build on from tonight. I liked our pace and our poise," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Junior Niamh Hollis got Harvard on the board when she knocked down a jumper with 9:44 left in the opening quarter. The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's free throws, which turned a 3-2 deficit into an 8-3 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 17-14.

The Rams went on an 8-2 run midway through the quarter, which drew level at 26. Harvard put together a 15-0 spurt midway through the quarter that broke a 26-all tie and put Harvard up 41-26. Harvard took a 41-28 lead into halftime.

A 9-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Ramsey's free throws, stretched the lead to 58-39. Harvard led 58-42 heading into the fourth.

Ramsey, Juniper Pryor, and Bria Vasquez keyed a 7-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 67-49.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey led Harvard with 18 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 7-of-8 FT) and 10 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Juniper Pryor added 15 points (5-of-15 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT, 2-of-3 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza chipped in 14 points (6-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Mendoza's 14 points were a career high.
