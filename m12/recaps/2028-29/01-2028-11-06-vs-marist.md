A new-look Harvard women's basketball team began the 2028-29 season with a 69-61 win over Marist on Monday night at Lavietes Pavilion, answering a third-quarter Red Foxes rally with a decisive fourth-quarter burst.

Eight months after falling to Princeton in the Ivy League Tournament final, the Crimson (1-0) opened without last year's four seniors, and the new group leaned on its veterans. Senior Jasmine Ramsey led the way with a team-high scoring night, senior captain Juniper Pryor scored the first point of the season, and senior captain Celeste Laurent hit the 3-pointer that capped the decisive run. Marist shot 57.8% from the field, but Harvard forced 21 turnovers and knocked down 10 3-pointers to make up the difference.

"There's plenty to clean up, but that's what November is for. Marist shot the ball well, and our group kept defending, kept making plays, and found a way to finish it. I like the way our seniors set the tone," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Pryor opened the scoring with a free throw, and after Marist went in front 2-1, Harvard answered with an 8-0 spurt for a 9-2 lead. The Red Foxes came back with a 10-2 run to go up 12-11, but the Crimson regained control and led 18-14 after one quarter.

Marist pulled even at 18 early in the second quarter before Harvard took off on a 16-6 run, capped by a Ramsey free throw, that sent the Crimson into halftime ahead 34-24.

The Red Foxes came out of the locker room firing. They scored 22 points in the third quarter and tied the game at 40. Pryor, sophomore Mira Hightower, and junior Isla Marchetti answered with a 12-4 run that put Harvard back on top 52-44, and the Crimson carried a 52-46 lead into the fourth.

Marist got within 52-50 with 9:07 to play, but that was as close as it came. Harvard strung together a 14-2 burst, capped by Laurent's 3-pointer, to stretch the lead to 66-52 and put the game away.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey led Harvard with 18 points (6-of-9 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
- Junior Isla Marchetti added 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT).
- Senior Juniper Pryor scored 10 points (3-of-8 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) with two assists and two steals.
- First-year Mackenzie Takahashi scored eight points (3-of-5 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) off the bench in her collegiate debut, and first-years Bria Vasquez and Valentina Okonkwo also made their debuts.
- Senior Celeste Laurent had seven points, five rebounds, and a team-high four assists.
- Harvard forced 21 turnovers, recorded 10 steals, and made 10 of its 25 3-point attempts.
