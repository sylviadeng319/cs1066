Harvard women's basketball gave its seniors a Senior Day to remember. The Crimson held Dartmouth to six points in the first quarter and cruised to a 72-45 win on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion to close the regular season.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Celeste Laurent, Juniper Pryor, and Jasmine Ramsey, and all three started. Pryor, a captain alongside Laurent, led the team in scoring. The Crimson (23-5, 11-3 Ivy) never trailed, shot 48.3% from the field, and held the Big Green to 29.1% to complete a season sweep after a 71-53 win in Hanover on Jan. 27.

Harvard finishes second in the Ivy League standings and will host the Ivy League Tournament at Lavietes Pavilion as the No. 2 seed.

"These three seniors have given so much to this program, and it was special to see them out there together on this floor one more time. The way everybody played for them, and the way the ball moved, that's what this team is about," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Junior Isla Marchetti scored on a driving layup for the game's first points, and after Dartmouth tied it at 2, Harvard took over. The Crimson put together a 24-4 burst, capped by a Pryor layup, to lead 26-6 after one quarter.

The teams played even in the second quarter, 11-11, and Harvard took a 37-17 lead into halftime.

Dartmouth used a 10-2 run early in the third quarter to cut the lead to 42-27, but the Crimson steadied and carried a 50-30 advantage into the fourth.

With the lead as large as 27, all 14 players on Harvard's roster saw the floor. Marchetti, Pryor, and Niamh Hollis keyed a 10-0 run to close the game.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 23-5 overall and 11-3 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 13-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Juniper Pryor led Harvard with 19 points (8-of-15 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT) in her final regular-season home game.
- Junior Isla Marchetti added 13 points (5-of-11 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT), five rebounds, and four assists.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey scored nine points and grabbed a team-high eight rebounds.
- Senior Celeste Laurent scored eight points on 3-of-4 shooting and dished out a team-high six assists.
- First-year Valentina Okonkwo went 2-of-2 from the field for four points.
- Harvard outrebounded Dartmouth 43-31 and made nine 3-pointers.
