Behind a strong first quarter, Harvard women's basketball (17-2, 5-0 Ivy) beat Dartmouth, 71-53, on Saturday at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

Harvard's defense forced 22 turnovers. The win is Harvard's 12th straight.

## How it Happened

Dartmouth scored first before senior Juniper Pryor hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 26-8 burst in the quarter, capped by Pryor's 3-pointer, which turned a 2-0 deficit into a 26-10 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 32-10. The Crimson led 38-19 at the break.

The Big Green went on a 13-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 38-32. Celeste Laurent, Niamh Hollis, and Pryor keyed a 10-4 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 48-36. Harvard led 48-40 heading into the fourth.

A 19-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Laurent's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 71-50. The lead reached 22, allowing Valentina Okonkwo, Thea Sutherland, and Emeka Rhodes to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-2 overall and 5-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Juniper Pryor led the way for Harvard with 17 points (8-of-10 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT) and six assists.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey added 13 points (5-of-13 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), six rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Celeste Laurent finished with 12 points (4-of-10 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-6 FT), five rebounds, five assists, and four steals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points.
