Harvard women's basketball (10-2) erased an 8-point deficit and came away with a 64-53 win over Providence on Saturday at Alumni Hall in Providence, R.I.

The win is Harvard's fifth straight. The Crimson shot 42.1% from the field and held Providence to 31.7%.

"That's a resilient group in that locker room. Being down didn't change our belief one bit," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Junior Brielle Mendoza opened the scoring when she knocked down a corner three. The Friars went on a 9-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped an 11-6 deficit into a 15-11 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

Celeste Laurent, Isla Marchetti, and Mendoza keyed a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 22-21. The Crimson went into the locker room down 24-21.

A 10-2 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Marchetti's 3-pointer, turned a 31-24 deficit into a 34-33 lead. Harvard trailed 36-35 entering the fourth.

Harvard put together a 29-17 spurt in the quarter that turned a 36-35 deficit into a 64-53 lead.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-2 overall.
- First-year Mackenzie Takahashi finished with a team-high 14 points (5-of-14 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Junior Isla Marchetti contributed 13 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Senior Celeste Laurent chipped in 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Niamh Hollis (10) also reached double figures.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed 12 rebounds.
- Junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 12 rebounds.
- Takahashi's 14 points were a career high.
