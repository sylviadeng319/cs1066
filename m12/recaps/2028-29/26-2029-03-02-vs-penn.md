Harvard women's basketball (22-4, 10-2 Ivy) held on for a 66-62 win over Penn on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before senior Celeste Laurent drilled a three for Harvard's first points. A 19-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Laurent's layup, turned a 1-0 deficit into a 19-1 lead. Penn went on an 11-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 19-12. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 31-14.

Penn countered with an 11-3 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 34-25. Harvard took a 36-25 lead into halftime.

An 18-8 Penn spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 48-43. Harvard took a 51-43 lead into the fourth quarter.

A 15-5 Penn spurt early in the quarter drew level at 58. Harvard held a 60-58 lead in the final minutes. With 2:50 left, Laurent connected from deep to make it 63-58. Laurent splashed a three from the wing with 2:05 left to push the lead to 66-58. The Quakers scored inside with 46 seconds left to get within 66-60. The Quakers scored with less than a second left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 22-4 overall and 10-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 12-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jasmine Ramsey finished with a team-high 17 points (7-of-12 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Celeste Laurent chipped in 16 points (6-of-11 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Junior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 10 rebounds.
