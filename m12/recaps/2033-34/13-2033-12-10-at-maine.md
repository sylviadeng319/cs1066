Harvard women's basketball (11-2) pulled away from Maine for a 55-49 victory on Saturday at Memorial Gym in Orono, Maine.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Black Bears 53-42.

## How it Happened

The Black Bears scored first before junior Bella Chen hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. Maine opened the game on a 7-0 run. The Crimson strung together an 11-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Simone Zamora's 3-pointer, which turned a 7-0 deficit into an 11-8 lead. The Crimson took a 15-12 lead into the second quarter.

An 8-0 Maine spurt early in the quarter flipped a 15-12 deficit into a 20-15 lead. A 9-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's bucket, turned a 20-15 deficit into a 24-22 lead. It was 26-all at the break.

Harvard put together a 10-2 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 30-28 deficit into a 38-32 lead. Harvard took a 38-36 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Black Bears got within 38-all with 9:54 left before Harvard took control. Lainey Rinaldi, Wren Farrow, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 17-7 run in the quarter that broke a 38-all tie and put Harvard up 55-45.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-2 overall.

- Junior Lainey Rinaldi added 11 points (4-of-9 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT).
