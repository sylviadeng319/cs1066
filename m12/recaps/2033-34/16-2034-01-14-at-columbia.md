Harvard women's basketball (14-2, 2-0 Ivy) picked up a 79-63 win over Columbia on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's ninth straight.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Columbia scored first before junior Elise Fennimore splashed a three from the wing for Harvard's first points. A 6-0 Columbia spurt late in the quarter flipped a 12-10 deficit into a 16-12 lead. Columbia carried a 16-13 lead into the second quarter.

The Lions went on a 10-2 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 26-17. Jasmine Danforth, Reese Kearns, and Natalie Olsen keyed a 22-4 run in the quarter that turned a 26-17 deficit into a 39-30 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

Columbia countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 41-36. The Crimson strung together a 10-4 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Naomi Prescott's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 51-40. Harvard led 53-42 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 10-3 spurt late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 79-63.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-2 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Natalie Olsen finished with a team-high 18 points (7-of-11 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), seven rebounds, and five assists.
- Junior Elise Fennimore finished with 15 points (6-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT), nine rebounds, four assists, three steals, and four blocks.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth added 15 points (6-of-12 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and two blocks.
- Junior Bella Chen came away with six steals.
- Olsen's 18 points were a career high.
- Fennimore's 15 points were a career high.
