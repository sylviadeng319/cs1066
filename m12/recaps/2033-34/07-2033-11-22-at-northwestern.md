Harvard women's basketball (5-2) came up just short on Tuesday, falling 76-75 to Northwestern at Welsh-Ryan Arena in Evanston, Ill.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Wildcats 37-27.

## How it Happened

Senior Jasmine Danforth got Harvard on the board when she splashed a three from the wing with 9:04 left in the opening quarter. Northwestern countered with a 22-7 run early in the quarter that flipped a 3-0 deficit into a 22-10 lead. Harvard put together an 11-3 spurt late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 25-21. Harvard trailed 26-21 after the opening frame.

Naomi Prescott, Danforth, and Bella Chen keyed a 16-4 run in the quarter that turned a 26-21 deficit into a 37-30 lead. The Wildcats went on a 9-0 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 37-30 deficit into a 39-37 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 20-8 burst early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's 3-pointer, which turned a 42-37 deficit into a 57-50 lead. An 8-0 Northwestern spurt late in the quarter flipped a 57-50 deficit into a 58-57 lead. Harvard led 60-58 heading into the fourth.

A 7-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Chloe Rousseau's bucket, turned a 67-65 deficit into a 72-67 lead. Harvard held a 72-70 lead in the final minutes. Northwestern got a basket with 2:01 left to tie it at 72. The Wildcats hit a jumper with 1:28 left to go in front 74-72, but Wren Farrow answered when she made a free throw to pull Harvard within 74-73. With 50 seconds left, Chen dropped in a floater to put Harvard ahead 75-74. The Wildcats converted a layup with 18 seconds left to go in front 76-75. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.
