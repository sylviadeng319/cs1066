Down 12 in the third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (23-4, 11-2 Ivy) rallied for a 72-67 win over Brown on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before senior Wren Farrow added a free throw for Harvard's first points. Brown opened the game on a 10-0 run. A 14-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's bucket inside, turned a 10-0 deficit into a 14-10 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 16-13.

Brown countered with a 28-16 run in the quarter that flipped a 16-13 deficit into a 41-32 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 12-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Farrow's bucket inside, which cut the deficit to 51-50. The Bears went on a 9-2 run late in the quarter, which extended the lead to 60-52. Harvard trailed 60-58 entering the fourth.

Naomi Prescott, Natalie Olsen, and Danforth keyed a 14-7 run early in the quarter that turned a 60-58 deficit into a 72-67 lead.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 23-4 overall and 11-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 12-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth paced the Crimson with 22 points (9-of-14 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT).
- Junior Bella Chen added 13 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT).
- Senior Wren Farrow chipped in 12 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-3 FT) and six rebounds.
- Naomi Prescott (11) also reached double figures.
- Senior Natalie Olsen dished out a team-high six assists.
