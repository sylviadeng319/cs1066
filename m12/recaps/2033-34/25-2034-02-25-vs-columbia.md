Harvard women's basketball (21-4, 9-2 Ivy) erased an 8-point deficit and came away with a 58-57 win over Columbia on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Columbia scored first before junior Lainey Rinaldi knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. The Lions opened the game on a 12-4 run. Bella Chen and Reese Kearns keyed a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 12-11. Columbia led 16-11 after one.

An 8-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Chen's bucket, turned an 18-11 deficit into a 19-18 lead. Columbia went on a 9-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 21-20 deficit into a 29-21 lead. Harvard trailed 29-27 at halftime.

A 7-0 Columbia spurt early in the quarter broke a 29-all tie and pushed the Lions ahead 36-29. The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's 3-pointer, which turned a 36-29 deficit into a 37-36 lead. The Crimson carried a 39-38 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 49-44 deficit into a 53-49 lead. Harvard held a 53-51 lead in the final minutes. Columbia got a basket with 1:34 left to tie it at 53. The Lions scored inside with 1:30 left to go in front 55-53. Columbia converted a layup with 1:28 left to stretch the lead to 57-53, but Wren Farrow answered when she banked in a turnaround to pull Harvard within 57-55. Olsen knocked down a corner three with 16 seconds left to put Harvard ahead 58-57, and the Crimson held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 21-4 overall and 9-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Bella Chen finished with a team-high 18 points (7-of-18 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Senior Wren Farrow recorded a double-double with 12 points (4-of-13 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi finished with 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and six rebounds.
- Harvard held Columbia to 33.8% shooting from the field.
