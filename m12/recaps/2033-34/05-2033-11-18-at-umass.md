Down 8 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (4-1) rallied for a 70-61 win over UMass on Friday at Mullins Center in Amherst, Mass.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Minutewomen 37-29. The Crimson shot 49.1% from the field and held UMass to 39.3%.

## How it Happened

The Minutewomen scored first before senior Jasmine Danforth finished a post move for Harvard's first points. A 12-3 UMass spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-1 deficit into a 13-5 lead. The Crimson strung together a 15-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Danforth's putback, which turned a 13-5 deficit into a 20-13 lead. The Crimson took a 20-15 lead into the second quarter.

Harvard put together a 13-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 41-24. Harvard went into the locker room up 43-28.

A 13-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Danforth's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 58-36. Harvard took a 58-40 lead into the fourth quarter.

UMass never got closer than seven in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-1 overall.
- Junior Bella Chen led the way for Harvard with 18 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Senior Jasmine Danforth finished with 16 points (8-of-10 FG).
- Senior Wren Farrow contributed 15 points (7-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT), six rebounds, and two blocks.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi dished out a team-high six assists.
- Farrow's 15 points were a career high.
