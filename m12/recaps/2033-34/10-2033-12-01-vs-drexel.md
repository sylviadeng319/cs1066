Behind a strong second quarter, Harvard women's basketball (8-2) beat Drexel, 67-56, on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 46.2% from the field and held Drexel to 37.3%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Dragons 37-27.

## How it Happened

The Dragons scored first before senior Wren Farrow banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together an 11-5 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 3-0 deficit into an 11-8 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 14-11.

The Dragons went on an 8-1 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 14-11 deficit into a 19-15 lead. The Crimson strung together a 24-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's jumper, which turned a 19-15 deficit into a 39-24 lead. The Crimson led 39-25 at the break.

The Dragons countered with a 17-5 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 46-42. Chen, Farrow, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 9-0 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 55-42. That was the score heading into the fourth.

An 8-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Chen's three, stretched the lead to 67-54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-2 overall. The Crimson is 4-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 14 points (6-of-10 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen chipped in 13 points (5-of-10 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT).
- Senior Wren Farrow contributed 12 points (5-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
