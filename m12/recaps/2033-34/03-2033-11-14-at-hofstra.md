Harvard women's basketball (3-0) erased a 17-point deficit and came away with a 65-64 win over Hofstra on Monday at Mack Sports Complex in Hempstead, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

The Pride scored first before senior Reese Kearns scored through contact for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together an 11-4 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's layup, which turned a 5-0 deficit into an 11-9 lead. The Pride went on a 21-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped an 11-9 deficit into a 30-13 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

Danforth and Wren Farrow keyed an 8-2 run late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 37-26. It stayed that way at halftime.

Harvard put together a 27-8 spurt in the quarter that turned a 37-26 deficit into a 53-45 lead. Harvard took a 53-47 lead into the fourth quarter.

An 8-1 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's triple, stretched the lead to 61-48. Harvard held a 62-58 lead in the final minutes. Hofstra hit a jumper with 2:15 left to get within 62-60. The Pride converted a layup with 1:00 left to tie it at 62, but Danforth answered when she banked in a turnaround to put Harvard ahead 64-62. Danforth made a free throw with 28 seconds left to push the lead to 65-62. The Pride scored with 1 second left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-0 overall.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi led the way for Harvard with 18 points (5-of-6 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT) and five assists.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth finished with 16 points (6-of-12 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT).
- Junior Bella Chen contributed 10 points (4-of-12 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT) and four assists.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded seven steals.
