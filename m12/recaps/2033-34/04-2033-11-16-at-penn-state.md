Harvard women's basketball (3-1) battled Penn State into an extra period on Wednesday but fell, 83-76, at Bryce Jordan Center in University Park, Pa.

## How it Happened

The Lady Lions scored first before junior Elise Fennimore sank two free throws for Harvard's first points. An 11-0 Penn State spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 9-8 deficit into a 19-9 lead. The Lady Lions carried a 21-16 lead into the second quarter.

Fennimore, Wren Farrow, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 20-6 run in the quarter that turned a 25-18 deficit into a 38-31 lead. Penn State countered with a 12-2 run late in the quarter that flipped a 38-31 deficit into a 43-40 lead. That was the score at halftime.

The Lady Lions went on a 14-4 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 57-44. A 13-1 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Danforth's layup, cut the deficit to 58-57. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 12-4 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 58-57 deficit into a 69-62 lead. Harvard held a 69-64 lead in the final minutes. Penn State scored inside with 2:37 left to get within 69-66. The Lady Lions converted a layup with 1:14 left to get within 69-68, but Farrow answered when she scored through contact to make it 71-68. Penn State hit a three with 25 seconds left to tie it at 71. Regulation ended 71-all, and the game went to an extra period.

The Lady Lions scored first in overtime. The Lady Lions led 78-75 late in the extra period. Penn State answered from beyond the arc with 56 seconds left to stretch the lead to 81-75, but Danforth answered when she made a free throw to make it 81-76. The Lady Lions made two free throws with 7 seconds left to stretch the lead to 83-76. Penn State won the extra period 12-5.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 3-1 overall.

- Junior Lainey Rinaldi added 12 points (4-of-7 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and three assists.
