Harvard women's basketball is headed to the Sweet 16 for the first time in program history. The No. 12 seed Crimson (28-4) trailed No. 13 seed Kansas State by seven entering the fourth quarter on Sunday, then held the Wildcats to nine points in the final frame for a 58-57 win in the NCAA Tournament Second Round at Colonial Life Arena in Columbia, S.C.

Kansas State had reached the second round by upsetting the No. 4 seed on its home floor, and it was the Wildcats who ended Harvard's 2030 NCAA Tournament run in the second round. This time the Crimson flipped the script. Harvard came up with 12 steals, won the rebounding battle 45-41, and closed the game on a 15-7 run. The win is Harvard's ninth straight, and its 28 victories match the program's total from 2029-30.

"Our players believed they belonged on this stage, and they played like it. We were down seven going into the fourth, and they just kept defending, one possession at a time. To be the first team in Harvard history to make the Sweet 16 is something these players will carry with them forever," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Kansas State opened the game with seven straight points before senior Wren Farrow went 2-for-2 at the line for Harvard's first points. Farrow, Elise Fennimore, and Bella Chen keyed a 16-8 Harvard run that gave the Crimson a 16-15 lead after one quarter.

The Wildcats answered with a 10-2 spurt early in the second quarter to go up 25-18. Harvard responded with a 13-2 run, capped by a Lainey Rinaldi jumper, to take a 31-27 lead, but Kansas State closed the half with seven straight points and led 34-31 at the break.

The game was tied at 36 in the third quarter before the Wildcats ran off seven unanswered points to go up 43-36. Kansas State carried a 48-41 lead into the fourth.

The Wildcats still led 50-43 early in the final frame when Harvard took over. The Crimson put together a 15-4 run to turn the seven-point deficit into a 58-54 lead. Kansas State scored with 1:42 left to make it a one-point game, but the Harvard defense didn't allow another point the rest of the way, and the celebration began at the final buzzer.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the NCAA Tournament Sweet 16 for the first time in program history and improves to 28-4, extending its winning streak to nine games.
- Junior Bella Chen led Harvard with 15 points (5-of-15 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), four assists, and a team-high five steals.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth added 12 points (6-of-15 FG) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Wren Farrow posted nine points and a team-high 11 rebounds.
- Junior Elise Fennimore scored six points and grabbed six rebounds off the bench.
- Senior Natalie Olsen and Chen each handed out four assists, and the Crimson assisted on 15 of its 23 field goals.
- Harvard held Kansas State to nine fourth-quarter points and forced 15 turnovers.
