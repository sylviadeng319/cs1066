Harvard women's basketball's 10-game winning streak and its perfect start at home both came to an end on Monday night. Princeton held the Crimson (15-3, 3-1 Ivy) to 16 first-half points and pulled away for a 56-44 win at Lavietes Pavilion, handing Harvard its first Ivy League loss of the season.

Playing two days after an 88-80 win over Penn, the Crimson never found a rhythm offensively. Harvard shot 29.3% from the field, attempted only three 3-pointers, and turned the ball over 15 times against eight for the Tigers. The Crimson trailed by as many as 21 before winning the fourth quarter 18-12.

"Princeton is a terrific team, and they made us pay for every mistake tonight. We didn't do enough of the things that make us good. We'll learn from it, and we'll see them again," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Tigers scored first before junior Bella Chen knocked down a mid-range jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson nudged ahead 7-6, but Princeton closed the first quarter with eight straight points to lead 14-7.

The Tigers took control in the second quarter. A 15-3 run stretched the margin to 29-10, and Harvard went into halftime trailing 31-16.

Princeton scored the first six points of the second half to go up 37-16. The Crimson battled through the rest of the third quarter but still faced a 44-26 deficit entering the fourth.

Harvard's offense finally came to life in the final frame. After a 6-0 Princeton run made it 52-31, the Crimson closed the game on a 13-4 burst, but the deficit was too large to overcome.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 15-3 overall and 3-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- The loss snapped Harvard's 10-game winning streak, which began after a Nov. 22 loss at Northwestern.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 16 points (5-of-13 FG, 6-of-8 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Elise Fennimore and junior Bella Chen each grabbed eight rebounds, and Fennimore added two blocks.
- Senior Wren Farrow pulled down seven rebounds and blocked three shots.
- Harvard outrebounded Princeton 46-44, including 13 offensive rebounds, and scored 18 points in the fourth quarter.
