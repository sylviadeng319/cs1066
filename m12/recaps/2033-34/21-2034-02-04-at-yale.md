For the second night in a row, Harvard women's basketball won by a single point on the road. The Crimson (18-3, 6-1 Ivy) trailed rival Yale by 10 in the fourth quarter on Saturday, then scored 15 of the game's final 19 points for a 60-59 win at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn.

The victory completed a weekend sweep that began with Friday's 68-67 win at Brown and extended Harvard's winning streak to three games. The Crimson won it on the glass and on defense, outrebounding the Bulldogs 44-37 with 16 offensive boards and forcing 18 turnovers.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time. Two one-point wins on the road in two nights says a lot about how much this group trusts each other," head coach Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Junior Bella Chen got the Crimson started with a layup in transition, and Harvard raced out to an 18-7 lead. Yale scored the last three points of the quarter, and the Crimson led 18-10 after 10 minutes.

The Bulldogs flipped the game in the second quarter. A 20-5 Yale run turned that eight-point deficit into a 30-23 lead, and Harvard trailed 30-27 at halftime.

Yale pushed its advantage to 40-31 midway through the third quarter before the Crimson answered with 10 straight points to go back in front 41-40. The Bulldogs responded again and carried a 47-41 lead into the fourth.

An 8-0 Yale spurt early in the final frame made it 55-45, and the Crimson's streak looked to be in trouble. Instead, Harvard locked down defensively and scored the next 10 points to tie the game at 55. Wren Farrow's putback with 2:48 left gave the Crimson the lead, and after Yale tied it at 57, Farrow made a free throw to put Harvard back in front. Jasmine Danforth then knocked down a jumper from the elbow with 1:16 left for a 60-57 lead. Yale scored with 36 seconds remaining, but Harvard's defense came up with the stops it needed to close it out.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-3 overall and 6-1 in Ivy League play and has won three straight, all on the road.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 13 points (4-of-10 FG, 5-of-6 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen added 10 points (4-of-15 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Natalie Olsen grabbed eight rebounds, and senior Wren Farrow scored seven points, including Harvard's go-ahead putback and free throw in the final three minutes.
- Senior Reese Kearns gave Harvard six points on 3-of-4 shooting, four rebounds, and two steals off the bench, and first-year Juniper Haddad scored four points in three minutes.
- Harvard's bench contributed 18 points, and the Crimson assisted on 16 of its 23 field goals.
