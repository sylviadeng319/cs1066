Harvard women's basketball (7-2) picked up a 80-73 win over UMass Lowell on Tuesday at Costello Athletic Center in Lowell, Mass.

Harvard knocked down 12 3-pointers. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the River Hawks 43-33.

"We came in with a clear game plan and our players executed it," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

UMass Lowell scored first before senior Natalie Olsen connected from deep for Harvard's first points. A 29-14 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Reese Kearns's bucket inside, turned a 2-0 deficit into a 29-16 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 31-18.

The River Hawks went on a 16-2 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 38-36. Harvard went into the locker room up 41-38.

Naomi Prescott, Olsen, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 13-2 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 54-40. The River Hawks countered with an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 54-48. The Crimson carried a 59-52 advantage into the final frame.

A 15-4 UMass Lowell spurt early in the quarter flipped a 61-52 deficit into a 67-65 lead. Harvard held a 73-69 lead in the final minutes. UMass Lowell hit a pair at the line with 2:15 left to cut the deficit to 73-71, but Malia Moreau answered when she knocked down a jumper to make it 75-71. The River Hawks converted a layup with 49 seconds left to cut the deficit to 75-73, but Bella Chen answered when she scored on a driving layup to make it 77-73. Chen put the game away when she knocked down a corner three with 18 seconds left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-2 overall.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth finished with a team-high 24 points (10-of-16 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen finished with 15 points (5-of-16 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three steals.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi added 11 points (3-of-11 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), nine rebounds, and seven assists.
- Junior Elise Fennimore grabbed nine rebounds.
- Danforth's 24 points were a career high.
- Harvard's 80 points were its most in a game this season.
