Down 13 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (17-3, 5-1 Ivy) rallied for a 68-67 win over Brown on Friday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Junior Bella Chen opened the scoring when she knocked down a jumper. A 9-0 Brown spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 9-2 lead. Jasmine Danforth, Elise Fennimore, and Lainey Rinaldi keyed a 14-3 run midway through the quarter that turned a 9-2 deficit into a 16-12 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 16-15.

Brown countered with a 14-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 16-15 deficit into a 29-16 lead. The Crimson strung together a 23-10 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's three, which evened the score at 41. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 10-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Naomi Prescott's layup, turned a 43-41 deficit into a 51-43 lead. The Bears went on a 13-5 run in the quarter, which drew level at 56. The score held there heading into the fourth.

An 8-0 Brown spurt early in the quarter flipped a 58-56 deficit into a 64-58 lead. The game was tied at 66 in the final minutes. With 1:06 left, Danforth scored through contact to put Harvard ahead 68-66. The Bears scored with 6 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-3 overall and 5-1 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Bella Chen led Harvard with 17 points (6-of-16 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), four assists, and three steals.
- Senior Natalie Olsen finished with 13 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 26 field goals.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded 11 steals.
