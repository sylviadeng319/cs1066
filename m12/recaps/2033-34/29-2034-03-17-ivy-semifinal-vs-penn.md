The defending champions will get a chance to repeat. No. 2 seed Harvard women's basketball (25-4) erased a 13-point second-quarter deficit and edged No. 3 seed Penn, 56-55, in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals on Friday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y., as junior Bella Chen's jumper with 25 seconds left provided the winning points.

On a night when shots were hard to come by, the Crimson won with defense and ball security. Harvard shot just 28.6% from the field but committed only seven turnovers, held the Quakers to 32.3% shooting, and made 13 of its 17 free throws. The win is Harvard's sixth straight and its third over Penn this season, after an 88-80 victory in Cambridge on Jan. 21 and a 65-63 win at the Palestra on Feb. 18.

"Tournament basketball is about surviving and advancing, and that's exactly what we did. We didn't shoot it well, but we kept defending, we kept taking care of the ball, and we made the plays at the end. I'm really proud of how tough this group was," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Senior Jasmine Danforth opened the scoring with a putback, and Harvard jumped out to a 4-0 lead. Penn answered with a 17-9 burst and led 17-13 after one quarter.

The Quakers pushed the margin to 26-13 with a 9-0 run early in the second quarter. Harvard steadied itself with six straight points, and Penn took a 28-21 lead into the break.

Wren Farrow, Chen, and Danforth keyed a 13-2 Harvard run early in the third quarter that tied the game at 34. Penn responded with six unanswered points to go back up 40-34, and the Quakers led 42-39 entering the fourth.

The Quakers still led 53-49 in the final minutes when Natalie Olsen buried a 3-pointer with 2:46 left to make it a one-point game. Penn went 2-for-2 at the line with 1:11 to go for a 55-52 lead, but Danforth answered with a basket inside. Then Chen knocked down a jumper with 25 seconds remaining to put Harvard ahead 56-55, and the Crimson defense held over the final possessions to send Harvard back to the championship game.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game for the second straight season and improves to 25-4.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 13 points (4-of-7 FG, 5-of-7 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Wren Farrow posted eight points and a team-high 11 rebounds, five of them on the offensive glass.
- Junior Bella Chen scored nine points, including the game-winning jumper, with four assists and two steals.
- Senior Natalie Olsen added eight points (3-of-7 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT), including the 3-pointer that pulled Harvard within one in the final three minutes.
- Junior Elise Fennimore grabbed seven rebounds and added two steals off the bench.
- Harvard turned the ball over just seven times and forced 13 Penn turnovers.
