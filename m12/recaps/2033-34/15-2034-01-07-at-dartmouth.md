Harvard women's basketball (13-2, 1-0 Ivy) cruised to a 76-49 victory over Dartmouth on Saturday at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 47-29. Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers.

"We talked about being the aggressor, and our players took that to heart from the first possession," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Dartmouth scored first before sophomore Malia Moreau drilled a three for Harvard's first points. Jasmine Danforth, Wren Farrow, and Bella Chen keyed a 9-0 run midway through the quarter that turned a 6-5 deficit into a 14-6 lead. Harvard led 17-9 after the first quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 16-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's three, which stretched the lead to 38-21. The Crimson led 38-25 at the break.

A 19-7 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Chen's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 57-36. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 17-8 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 74-44. The lead reached 30, allowing Chloe Rousseau, Quinn Castellanos, and Simone Zamora to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-2 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Bella Chen led the way for Harvard with 17 points (6-of-15 FG, 4-of-10 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Senior Jasmine Danforth chipped in 15 points (6-of-11 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi contributed 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Natalie Olsen (12) also reached double figures.
- Senior Reese Kearns protected the rim with three blocks.
- Harvard held Dartmouth to 31.6% shooting from the field.
