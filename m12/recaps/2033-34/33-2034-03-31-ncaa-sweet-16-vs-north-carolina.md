The best season of this era of Harvard women's basketball ended on the biggest stage the program has ever reached. No. 1 seed North Carolina used a dominant middle two quarters to pull away from the No. 12 seed Crimson, 78-64, in the NCAA Tournament Sweet 16 on Friday at Moda Center in Portland, Ore., in front of 9,432 fans.

Harvard (28-5) shot a better percentage from the field than the Tar Heels, 39.3% to 37.8%, but North Carolina forced 15 turnovers, came up with 12 steals, and outscored the Crimson 49-32 in the second and third quarters. Harvard never stopped competing, winning the fourth quarter 20-14 after trailing by as many as 28. The loss snapped a nine-game winning streak and closed the careers of seniors Jasmine Danforth, Wren Farrow, Reese Kearns, Natalie Olsen, and Naomi Prescott, who finish with 90 wins over four seasons.

"We ran into a great team today, but I'm incredibly proud of the way we competed and the season we had. This group won an Ivy League championship, won two games in the NCAA Tournament, and took this program somewhere it had never been. Our seniors have set a new standard for everyone who comes after them," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

North Carolina scored first before senior Wren Farrow got Harvard on the board with a putback. The Crimson went ahead 8-7, but the Tar Heels answered with a 6-0 run and led 15-12 after one quarter.

North Carolina took control in the second quarter. A 14-3 Tar Heels run made it 31-17, and North Carolina scored 26 points in the frame to take a 41-27 lead into halftime.

The Tar Heels opened the third quarter with 14 straight points to go up 55-27. Harvard kept fighting and closed the period with eight unanswered points of its own, capped by another Farrow putback, but still trailed 64-44 entering the fourth.

The Crimson won the final frame 20-14 behind its seniors, cutting the final margin to 14, and the Harvard faithful in Portland stood to salute a team that made history.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard's season ends in the NCAA Tournament Sweet 16 with a 28-5 record, an Ivy League Tournament championship, and the first two-win NCAA Tournament run in program history.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 17 points (7-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds in her final collegiate game.
- Junior Bella Chen added 13 points on 6-of-9 shooting.
- Senior Wren Farrow scored 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-2 FT) with six rebounds and two blocks.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi hit two 3-pointers and handed out a team-high four assists.
- Sophomore Ciara Bergstrom scored six points in three minutes off the bench, going 4-of-4 at the free throw line.
- Harvard made 16 of its 21 free throws.
