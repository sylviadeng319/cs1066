Princeton made its case as the team to beat in the Ivy League on Friday night. The Tigers outscored Harvard women's basketball 31-9 in the third quarter and rolled to an 83-53 win at Jadwin Gymnasium, completing a season sweep of the Crimson (19-4, 7-2 Ivy) and snapping Harvard's four-game winning streak.

Harvard had fought back within four in the second quarter and trailed by just eight at halftime. But Princeton shot 47.8% from the field, assisted on 22 of its 32 baskets, made all 12 of its free throws, and won the battle on the boards 41-30. Both of the Crimson's Ivy League losses this season have come against the Tigers, who also won the first meeting, 56-44, on Jan. 23 in Cambridge.

"Princeton is a really good team, and they showed it tonight. That third quarter got away from us, and against a team like that you can't let it happen. We have to learn from this one and be better the next time we see them," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before senior Wren Farrow got Harvard on the board with a basket inside. Points were hard to find for the Crimson in the opening 10 minutes, and the Tigers led 15-5 after one quarter.

Harvard came alive in the second quarter. A 10-0 spurt pulled the Crimson within 23-19, and Harvard scored 23 points in the frame, its best quarter of the night. Princeton answered with a 6-0 run late in the half to go up 36-26, and the Tigers led 36-28 at the break.

The third quarter decided it. Princeton opened the second half on a 31-6 run that pushed its lead to 67-34, and the Tigers carried a 67-37 advantage into the fourth.

The Crimson kept competing and played the final frame even with Princeton, 16-16, with all 16 players on the roster seeing the floor, but the margin was too much to overcome.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 19-4 overall and 7-2 in Ivy League play. Both of Harvard's Ivy losses have come against Princeton.
- Senior Wren Farrow led Harvard with 16 points (7-of-13 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and four rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen added 11 points (4-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT).
- Senior Naomi Prescott grabbed a team-high seven rebounds and added two steals off the bench.
- First-year Callie Winslow and junior Lainey Rinaldi each dished out three assists.
- Harvard shot 40.7% from the field but went 5-of-12 at the free throw line.
