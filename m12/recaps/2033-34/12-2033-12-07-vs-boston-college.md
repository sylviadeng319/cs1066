Harvard women's basketball (10-2) erased a 12-point deficit and came away with a 65-64 win over Boston College on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fifth straight.

"We didn't flinch. At halftime we talked about getting back to who we are, and our players responded," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Jasmine Danforth opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Harvard outscored the Eagles 22-9 in the first quarter. Harvard led 22-9 after the first quarter.

A 26-4 Boston College spurt in the quarter flipped a 26-11 deficit into a 37-30 lead. Boston College took a 37-33 lead into the break.

Boston College countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 44-33. The Crimson strung together an 11-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's jumper, which cut the deficit to 46-44. Harvard trailed 50-46 entering the fourth.

Boston College went on a 12-4 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 62-50. The Eagles led 64-58 in the final minutes. With 2:53 left, Natalie Olsen knocked down a corner three to pull Harvard within 64-61. Bella Chen finished a layup in transition with 1:15 left to pull Harvard within 64-63. Wren Farrow hit a jumper from the elbow with 35 seconds left to put Harvard ahead 65-64, and the Crimson held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-2 overall. The Crimson is 6-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi paced the Crimson with 17 points (6-of-11 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT), nine rebounds, and six assists.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth chipped in 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 3-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen finished with 11 points (4-of-14 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and five steals.
- Senior Natalie Olsen dished out a team-high six assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 17 of its 25 field goals.
