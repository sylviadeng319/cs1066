A month after needing a late rally to escape New Haven by a point, Harvard women's basketball left no doubt in the rematch. The Crimson (22-4, 10-2 Ivy) forced 21 turnovers, came up with 15 steals, and beat Yale 68-51 on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion to complete a season sweep of its rival.

Harvard held the Bulldogs to six points in the second quarter and broke the game open with a 20-7 burst in the third, building a lead that reached 27 in the fourth. The win is the Crimson's third straight, with two regular-season games left to play.

"That was a complete team effort, and that's what we're going to need as the season goes on. Our defense set the tone, and we took care of the basketball. When we do those two things, we're hard to beat," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored first, and junior Lainey Rinaldi answered with a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. The Bulldogs led 7-3 before the Crimson put together a 9-2 run, capped by a Wren Farrow three, to go up 12-9. Harvard led 16-14 after one quarter.

The Crimson defense took over in the second quarter. Rinaldi, Bella Chen, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 10-1 run that broke a 16-all tie and gave Harvard a 26-17 lead, and the Crimson took a 28-20 advantage into halftime.

Harvard put the game away coming out of the break. A 20-7 surge, capped by a pair of Farrow free throws, pushed the lead to 48-27, and the Crimson led 48-31 entering the fourth.

Yale scored 20 points in the final frame, but Harvard matched it. A 15-5 Crimson spurt early in the fourth stretched the margin to 67-40, and the Bulldogs never got closer than 17 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 22-4 overall and 10-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Harvard swept the season series with Yale, following a 60-59 win in New Haven on Feb. 4.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth recorded a double-double with a team-high 23 points (9-of-19 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT) and 12 rebounds, and added three steals.
- Junior Bella Chen scored 13 points (5-of-20 FG, 2-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and grabbed eight rebounds.
- Senior Wren Farrow added 11 points (3-of-10 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and a team-high four steals.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi dished out a team-high six assists without committing a turnover.
- Harvard held Yale to 32.7% shooting from the field and outrebounded the Bulldogs 49-41.
