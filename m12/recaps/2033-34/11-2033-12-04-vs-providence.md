Down 9 in the fourth quarter, Harvard women's basketball (9-2) rallied for a 62-57 win over Providence on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Friars 50-35. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Friars scored first before junior Bella Chen hit a triple for Harvard's first points. Providence went on a 10-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped an 8-5 deficit into a 15-8 lead. Harvard trailed 15-12 after the opening frame.

Harvard put together an 18-3 spurt in the quarter that turned a 15-12 deficit into a 30-18 lead. Harvard took a 30-21 lead into halftime.

The Friars countered with a 22-5 run in the quarter that flipped a 33-21 deficit into a 43-38 lead. The score held there heading into the fourth.

The Friars led 57-52 in the final minutes. With 2:58 left, Chen hit a pair of free throws to make it 57-54. Wren Farrow scored inside with 2:26 left to pull Harvard within 57-56. Chen buried a 3-pointer with 1:07 left to put Harvard ahead 59-57. Lainey Rinaldi made a free throw with 39 seconds left to push the lead to 60-57. Jasmine Danforth hit a jumper from the elbow with 26 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-2 overall. The Crimson is 5-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Bella Chen led the way for Harvard with 16 points (5-of-13 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Wren Farrow finished with 11 points (5-of-12 FG, 1-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth added 11 points (5-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 FT), nine rebounds, and three assists.
