Harvard women's basketball is going back to the NCAA Tournament. Down 13 to top-seeded Princeton in the fourth quarter, the No. 2 seed Crimson (26-4) closed on a 16-2 run and won the Ivy League Tournament championship, 63-62, on Saturday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y., as senior Jasmine Danforth's putback in the final seconds provided the winning points.

The title is Harvard's second straight, after last season's championship game win over the same Princeton program, and it came against a team that had beaten the Crimson twice in the regular season by a combined 42 points. Princeton finished first in the league standings at 13-1, but Harvard shot 50.0% from the field and forced 16 turnovers to earn the league's automatic bid. The win is the Crimson's seventh straight.

"I'm so happy for these players. Princeton beat us twice this year, and nobody would have blamed us for hanging our heads when we were down 13 in the fourth quarter. This group never did. They believed in each other, they kept getting stops, and they made the plays to win a championship," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Junior Bella Chen opened the scoring with a 3-pointer, and Harvard raced out to an 18-6 lead. Princeton answered with a 14-3 run to close the first quarter, and the Crimson led just 21-20 after one.

Neither team found much separation in a second quarter that Harvard won 10-8, and the Crimson took a 31-28 lead into halftime.

Harvard built its advantage to 45-37 in the third quarter before the Tigers closed the frame with six straight points to get within 45-43.

Princeton seized control early in the fourth. A 17-2 Tigers run turned that two-point deficit into a 60-47 lead. The Crimson didn't flinch. Harvard scored nine straight points to get within 60-56, Lainey Rinaldi added a free throw with 1:41 left, and Elise Fennimore finished a post move with 1:35 remaining to make it 60-59. Fennimore then scored through contact with 23 seconds left to put the Crimson ahead 61-60, capping a 14-0 run. Princeton answered with a layup two seconds later to go back in front, but Danforth converted a putback on the other end for a 63-62 lead, and Harvard held on to cut down the nets.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard wins its second straight Ivy League Tournament title, earns the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament, and improves to 26-4 with seven straight wins.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 20 points (9-of-14 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), including the game-winning putback, and surpassed 1,000 career points.
- Senior Wren Farrow added 14 points (6-of-12 FG, 2-of-4 FT) and two steals.
- Junior Bella Chen scored 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) with four assists.
- Junior Elise Fennimore scored nine points on 4-of-5 shooting off the bench, including the two baskets that pulled Harvard within one and then put it ahead in the final 1:35.
- Harvard shot 26-of-52 (50.0%) from the field and forced 16 Princeton turnovers.
