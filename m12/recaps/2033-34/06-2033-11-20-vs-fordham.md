Harvard women's basketball (5-1) led from start to finish in a 71-53 win over Fordham on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 47.5% from the field and held Fordham to 38.6%.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Bella Chen knocked down a corner three. The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Wren Farrow's bucket inside, which broke a 12-all tie and put Harvard up 21-12. That was the score after one quarter.

Juniper Haddad, Lainey Rinaldi, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 14-3 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 38-21. Harvard took a 40-26 lead into halftime.

The third quarter was a back-and-forth affair. The Crimson carried a 54-42 advantage into the final frame.

A 17-9 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's free throws, stretched the lead to 71-51. With the lead as large as 20, Chloe Rousseau, Quinn Castellanos, and Simone Zamora saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-1 overall.
- Senior Wren Farrow paced the Crimson with 17 points (8-of-9 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT).
- Junior Bella Chen added 14 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-9 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Natalie Olsen dished out a team-high six assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 28 field goals.
- Farrow's 17 points were a career high.
