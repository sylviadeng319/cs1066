Down 12 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (2-0) rallied for a 71-63 win over Sacred Heart on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pioneers 37-28.

## How it Happened

Junior Elise Fennimore opened the scoring when she finished a post move. Harvard opened the game on an 8-2 run. An 11-2 Sacred Heart spurt midway through the quarter flipped an 8-2 deficit into a 13-10 lead. Sacred Heart led 15-12 after one.

The Pioneers went on a 19-7 run in the quarter, which broke a 16-all tie and pushed the Pioneers ahead 35-23. The Crimson went into the locker room down 35-25.

The Crimson strung together a 20-3 burst in the quarter, capped by Wren Farrow's free throws, which turned a 35-25 deficit into a 45-38 lead. Harvard led 47-42 heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 64-60 lead in the final minutes. Fennimore converted a putback with 1:28 left to make it 66-60. Sacred Heart drained a 3-pointer with 58 seconds left to get within 66-63, but Lainey Rinaldi answered when she hit a pair of free throws to make it 68-63. Jasmine Danforth added a free throw with 27 seconds left to make it 69-63. Farrow scored inside with 15 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 19 points (8-of-9 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-5 FT), five rebounds, four assists, and three steals.
- Junior Bella Chen contributed 15 points (6-of-15 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT).
- Senior Wren Farrow added 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- Natalie Olsen (10) also reached double figures.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 28 field goals.
