Harvard women's basketball (19-3, 7-1 Ivy) pulled away from Dartmouth for a 66-57 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 48-40.

## How it Happened

The Big Green scored first before junior Elise Fennimore scored inside for Harvard's first points. A 13-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's triple, turned a 3-0 deficit into a 13-3 lead. Dartmouth countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 13-9. The Crimson took a 16-10 lead into the second quarter.

Chloe Rousseau, Bella Chen, and Rinaldi keyed a 15-5 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 31-18. Harvard went into the locker room up 37-26.

The Big Green went on a 14-6 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 45-40. The Crimson strung together a 7-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Rinaldi's three, which stretched the lead to 52-41. The Crimson carried a 56-45 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together a 10-4 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 66-51.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-3 overall and 7-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Bella Chen led the way for Harvard with 25 points (9-of-18 FG, 5-of-13 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi finished with 11 points (3-of-10 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Natalie Olsen dished out a team-high six assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 18 of its 24 field goals.
