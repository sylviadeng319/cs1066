It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (20-4, 8-2 Ivy) made the plays it needed in a 65-63 win over Penn on Saturday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

"Winning close games is a skill, and tonight our players showed they have it," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before junior Bella Chen splashed a three from the wing for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Elise Fennimore's putback, which turned a 4-0 deficit into an 8-4 lead. A 9-0 Penn spurt midway through the quarter flipped an 8-4 deficit into a 13-8 lead. It was 15-all after the first quarter.

Penn countered with a 9-2 run in the quarter that flipped a 16-15 deficit into a 24-18 lead. Lainey Rinaldi, Wren Farrow, and Natalie Olsen keyed a 10-2 run late in the quarter that turned a 24-18 deficit into a 28-26 lead. Harvard trailed 29-28 at halftime.

A 21-10 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Farrow's bucket inside, turned a 29-28 deficit into a 49-39 lead. Harvard took a 49-44 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 59-47. The game was tied at 61 in the final minutes. The Quakers converted a layup with 1:39 left to go in front 63-61, but Rinaldi answered when she hit a pull-up jumper to tie the game at 63. Jasmine Danforth scored inside with 39 seconds left to put Harvard ahead 65-63, and the Crimson held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-4 overall and 8-2 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Bella Chen led Harvard with 14 points (5-of-12 FG, 4-of-7 3-PT).
- Senior Wren Farrow contributed 10 points (5-of-8 FG).
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi dished out a team-high seven assists.
