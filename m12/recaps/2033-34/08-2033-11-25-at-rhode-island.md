Harvard women's basketball (6-2) erased a 13-point deficit and came away with a 72-63 win over Rhode Island on Friday at Ryan Center in Kingston, R.I.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Rams 46-36.

"That's a resilient group in that locker room. Being down didn't change our belief one bit," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Rams scored first before senior Natalie Olsen buried a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. The Rams opened the game on a 12-4 run. The Crimson strung together an 11-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Naomi Prescott's putback, which turned a 12-4 deficit into a 15-13 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

Rhode Island countered with a 15-2 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 24-17 deficit into a 32-26 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 7-0 Rhode Island spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 39-26. Wren Farrow, Jasmine Danforth, and Prescott keyed a 13-2 run early in the quarter that cut the deficit to 41-39. The Rams led 51-48 after three quarters.

Rhode Island got within 55-48 with 8:06 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 24-8 spurt in the quarter that turned a 55-48 deficit into a 72-63 lead.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-2 overall.
- Senior Natalie Olsen finished with a team-high 15 points (5-of-12 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen contributed 13 points (6-of-9 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT) and three steals.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi dished out a team-high seven assists.
- Olsen's 15 points were a career high.
