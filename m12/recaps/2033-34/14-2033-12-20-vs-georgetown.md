Behind a strong fourth quarter, Harvard women's basketball (12-2) beat Georgetown, 72-54, on Tuesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Hoyas 43-29. The win is Harvard's seventh straight.

## How it Happened

Senior Natalie Olsen got Harvard on the board when she added a free throw with 9:49 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on an 11-0 run. Georgetown countered with a 15-2 run midway through the quarter that flipped an 11-0 deficit into a 15-13 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 17-15.

Harvard put together a 13-2 spurt early in the quarter that turned an 18-17 deficit into a 30-20 lead. Georgetown went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 30-27. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 7-0 Georgetown spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 35-32 deficit into a 39-35 lead. An 11-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Reese Kearns's putback, turned a 39-35 deficit into a 46-39 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 25-9 burst in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's three, which stretched the lead to 71-48. With the lead as large as 23, Juniper Haddad, Simone Zamora, and Callie Winslow saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-2 overall. The Crimson is 7-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth paced the Crimson with 22 points (9-of-10 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-3 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen contributed 15 points (4-of-12 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT), five rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Natalie Olsen chipped in 13 points (3-of-8 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
