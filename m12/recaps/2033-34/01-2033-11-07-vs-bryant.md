The defending Ivy League Tournament champions picked up right where they left off. Harvard women's basketball (1-0) opened the 2033-34 season with a 75-50 win over Bryant on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion, pulling away with a 44-21 second half after a back-and-forth opening 20 minutes.

Junior Lainey Rinaldi set the pace with a career high in scoring, and the Crimson dominated the glass, outrebounding the Bulldogs 48-28. Harvard shot 45.8% from the field, made 11 3-pointers, and went a perfect 10-for-10 at the free throw line, while holding Bryant to 28.8% shooting. All 16 players on the roster saw the floor, including all four first-years in their collegiate debuts.

"Openers are about setting the tone, and I thought we set a good one. We turned it over more than we'd like, but the way we rebounded and defended in the second half is who we want to be," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Bryant scored the first basket of the season before senior Natalie Olsen buried a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. The Crimson ran from there, putting together a 16-3 burst capped by a Rinaldi triple to go up 16-5. The Bulldogs answered with eight straight points to get within 16-13, and Harvard led 18-15 after one quarter.

The teams traded baskets for most of the second quarter, and the Crimson held a slim 31-29 lead at the break.

Bryant scored early in the third quarter to go in front 33-31, but that was the last time the Bulldogs led. Harvard answered with an 18-4 spurt to take a 49-37 lead and carried a 53-41 advantage into the fourth.

Elise Fennimore, Rinaldi, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 22-6 run in the final frame that put the game away. Bryant never got closer than nine in the fourth quarter, and the margin reached 25 at the final buzzer, giving the Crimson's bench and newcomers extended minutes down the stretch.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi led Harvard with a career-high 20 points (7-of-16 FG, 4-of-11 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Bella Chen added 15 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT).
- Senior Jasmine Danforth opened her final season with a double-double, posting 13 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- Junior Elise Fennimore scored six points and grabbed nine rebounds off the bench, and senior Wren Farrow added eight rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points. First-year Callie Winslow hit a 3-pointer in her collegiate debut, and sophomore Simone Zamora scored five points in four minutes.
- Harvard outrebounded Bryant 48-28, including 11 offensive boards, and made all 10 of its free throws.
