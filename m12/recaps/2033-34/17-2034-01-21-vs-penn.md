Harvard women's basketball (15-2, 3-0 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Penn, 88-80, at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 10th straight.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Elise Fennimore banked in a turnaround. Harvard opened the game on a 14-6 run. The Quakers went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 16-15. The Crimson took a 26-19 lead into the second quarter.

Jasmine Danforth, Wren Farrow, and Naomi Prescott keyed an 8-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 36-21. The Quakers countered with a 12-4 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 40-33. Harvard took a 49-40 lead into halftime.

A 9-0 Penn spurt early in the quarter drew level at 49. A 16-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Danforth's putback, broke a 49-all tie and put Harvard up 65-49. Harvard led 71-57 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 84-72.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-2 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led the way for Harvard with 32 points (13-of-20 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT).
- Senior Wren Farrow chipped in 18 points (8-of-13 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), seven rebounds, and six steals.
- Junior Elise Fennimore contributed 10 points (4-of-6 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen dished out a team-high eight assists.
- Farrow's 18 points were a career high.
- Danforth's 32 points were a career high.
- Harvard's 88 points were its most in a game this season.
