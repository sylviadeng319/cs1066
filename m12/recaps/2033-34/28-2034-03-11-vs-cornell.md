Harvard women's basketball sent its seniors out in style. With all five members of the senior class in the starting lineup, the Crimson (24-4, 12-2 Ivy) never trailed in a 76-44 rout of Cornell on Saturday, closing the regular season with a fifth straight win in front of a sellout crowd of 2,195 at Lavietes Pavilion.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Jasmine Danforth, Wren Farrow, Reese Kearns, Natalie Olsen, and Naomi Prescott. Danforth, a captain, then hit a 3-pointer for the game's first points and didn't miss a shot from the field all game. The class arrived for a 2030-31 season that ended without an Ivy League Tournament berth. Four years later, it has won 86 games, an Ivy League Tournament title last March, and now the No. 2 seed for this year's tournament at Cornell's Newman Arena.

"This group has meant so much to our program. They came in when we were building, and they've helped turn us into a team that expects to play for championships. To see all five of them on the floor together to start the game, and to play the way we did, was really special," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Danforth's three from the wing with 9:19 left in the first quarter started a 14-2 Harvard run, and the Crimson led 17-9 after one.

Danforth, Olsen, and Prescott keyed a 12-5 run early in the second quarter that pushed the lead to 31-18, and the Crimson took that 13-point margin into halftime.

Harvard blew it open in the third quarter. A 23-4 run, capped by a Bella Chen free throw, stretched the lead to 54-22, and the Crimson outscored Cornell 27-12 in the frame to lead 58-30 entering the fourth.

A 10-1 burst early in the final frame, capped by a Danforth layup, made it 68-34. The lead reached 34, and every player on the Harvard roster got into the game before the final buzzer.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard finishes the regular season 24-4 overall and 12-2 in Ivy League play, second in the league standings, and earns the No. 2 seed in the Ivy League Tournament. The Crimson finished 13-1 at Lavietes Pavilion.
- Harvard swept the season series with Cornell, following a 64-62 win in Ithaca on Jan. 28.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 18 points on perfect shooting (6-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and grabbed nine rebounds.
- Senior Natalie Olsen added 14 points (4-of-7 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Bella Chen scored 10 points off the bench with five assists and three steals.
- Senior Naomi Prescott scored eight points, and senior Wren Farrow added eight points and three steals.
- Harvard held Cornell to 27.5% shooting from the field, forced 19 turnovers, and outrebounded the Big Red 46-35.
