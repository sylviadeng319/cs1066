A year after its NCAA Tournament stay ended in the first round, Harvard women's basketball made sure this one would last longer. The No. 12 seed Crimson (27-4) built a 17-point first-half lead, survived a fourth-quarter charge, and knocked off No. 5 seed West Virginia, 65-64, in the NCAA Tournament First Round on Friday at Colonial Life Arena in Columbia, S.C.

The win is Harvard's first in the NCAA Tournament since a first-round victory over Ohio State in 2030, and its eighth straight overall. West Virginia erased a nine-point deficit early in the fourth quarter, but junior Bella Chen's mid-range jumper broke a 62-all tie after the Mountaineers pulled even with 1:08 left, and the Crimson made just enough plays from there.

"This is a special moment for Harvard and for this program. We knew West Virginia was going to make a run, and when they took the lead, nobody on our bench panicked. Our players stayed together, and they made winning plays at the end. I'm thrilled for them," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

West Virginia scored the game's first four points before Chen got Harvard on the board at the free throw line. The Crimson took off from there, ripping off a 22-7 run, capped by a Natalie Olsen basket, to lead 22-11 after one quarter.

Olsen's 3-pointer capped an 8-2 Harvard spurt early in the second quarter that pushed the lead to 32-15. The Mountaineers answered with a 16-6 run, and the Crimson took a 38-31 lead into halftime.

Harvard pulled away again in the third quarter, using an 11-3 burst to go up 49-34. West Virginia scored seven straight to get within 49-41, and the Crimson led 55-46 entering the fourth.

The Mountaineers made their move early in the final frame, going on a 13-1 run to take a 59-56 lead. Harvard regrouped and led 62-60 in the final minutes before West Virginia tied it with a basket at the 1:08 mark. Chen answered with a mid-range jumper to put the Crimson back in front 64-62, and Wren Farrow added a free throw with three seconds left for a 65-62 cushion. The Mountaineers scored with one second remaining, but it only trimmed the final margin, and the Crimson celebrated a trip to the second round.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the NCAA Tournament Second Round and improves to 27-4, extending its winning streak to eight games.
- Senior Natalie Olsen led Harvard with 15 points on efficient shooting (6-of-9 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT).
- Junior Lainey Rinaldi added 12 points (5-of-14 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT), eight rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth scored 11 points (2-of-5 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 6-of-8 FT) and grabbed eight rebounds.
- Senior Wren Farrow pulled down a team-high 12 rebounds and added nine points, including a free throw with three seconds left that made it a three-point game.
- Junior Bella Chen scored eight points, including the go-ahead jumper.
- Harvard outrebounded West Virginia 47-43 and held the Mountaineers to 34.7% shooting from the field.
