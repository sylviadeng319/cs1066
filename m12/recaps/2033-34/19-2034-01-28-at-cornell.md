Harvard women's basketball (16-3, 4-1 Ivy) erased an 11-point deficit and came away with a 64-62 win over Cornell on Saturday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 50-41.

## How it Happened

The Big Red scored first before senior Wren Farrow sank two free throws for Harvard's first points. A 16-5 Cornell spurt in the quarter broke a 2-all tie and pushed the Big Red ahead 18-7. The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's 3-pointer, which cut the deficit to 18-13. The score held there after one quarter.

The Big Red went on a 16-4 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 22-20 deficit into a 36-26 lead. Farrow, Simone Zamora, and Jasmine Danforth keyed an 8-0 run late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 36-34. That was the score at halftime.

A 7-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Danforth's bucket inside, evened the score at 41. The Crimson faced a 49-45 deficit going into the final frame.

The Big Red led 58-57 in the final minutes. Chen hit a triple with 1:53 left to give the Crimson a 60-58 lead. Chen dropped in a floater with 1:52 left to push the lead to 62-58. Cornell got a basket with 1:09 left to cut the deficit to 62-60. The Big Red scored inside with 13 seconds left to tie it at 62, but Elise Fennimore answered when she scored through contact to put Harvard ahead 64-62.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-3 overall and 4-1 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Bella Chen paced the Crimson with 23 points (8-of-27 FG, 5-of-20 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), seven rebounds, and six assists.
- Senior Jasmine Danforth recorded a double-double with 11 points (4-of-12 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- Junior Elise Fennimore added 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Wren Farrow grabbed nine rebounds.
