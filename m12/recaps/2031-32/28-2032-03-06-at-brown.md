Harvard women's basketball (19-9, 7-7 Ivy) erased a 10-point deficit and came away with a 48-43 win over Brown on Saturday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

"That's a resilient group in that locker room. Being down didn't change our belief one bit," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Brown scored first before junior Jordan Delgado knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. The Bears countered with a 12-1 run in the quarter that flipped a 3-2 deficit into a 14-4 lead. Harvard trailed 14-6 after the opening frame.

Mackenzie Takahashi, Jasmine Danforth, and Delgado keyed a 12-0 run in the quarter that turned a 15-6 deficit into an 18-15 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 20-18.

The Crimson strung together a 13-3 burst in the quarter, capped by Danforth's free throws, which turned a 26-24 deficit into a 37-29 lead. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Bears went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 38-37. Harvard held a 43-38 lead in the final minutes. With 2:43 left, Valentina Okonkwo converted a putback to make it 45-38. The Bears scored inside with 2:28 left to get within 45-40, but Chloe Ferreira answered when she drilled a three to push the lead to 48-40. Brown converted a layup with 1:27 left to cut the deficit to 48-42. The Bears scored with 1:22 left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-9 overall and 7-7 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Esme Ellison finished with 11 points (4-of-12 FG, 3-of-4 FT) and 13 rebounds for a double-double.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth recorded a double-double with 11 points (3-of-6 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and 12 rebounds.
- Junior Jordan Delgado finished with 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT) and six assists.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard held Brown to 24.1% shooting from the field.
