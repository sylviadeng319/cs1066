Harvard women's basketball (11-2) erased a 9-point deficit and came away with a 64-56 win over Hofstra on Sunday at Mack Sports Complex in Hempstead, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's third straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pride 48-38.

## How it Happened

The Pride scored first before senior Valentina Okonkwo hit a pair of free throws for Harvard's first points. Hofstra opened the game on an 11-2 run. Jordan Delgado, Jasmine Danforth, and Chloe Ferreira keyed a 14-6 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 17-16. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 19-11 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's triple, turned a 17-16 deficit into a 35-28 lead. The score held there at halftime.

The third quarter was a back-and-forth affair. Harvard led 53-44 heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 9-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Danforth's putback, which stretched the lead to 62-48.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-2 overall.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth paced the Crimson with 15 points (6-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) and 10 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo added 12 points (5-of-13 FG, 2-of-2 FT), nine rebounds, and three blocks.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira chipped in 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and three assists.
