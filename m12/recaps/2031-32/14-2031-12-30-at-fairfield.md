Behind a strong third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (12-2) beat Fairfield, 74-58, on Tuesday at Leo D. Mahoney Arena in Fairfield, Conn.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Stags scored first before junior Jordan Delgado dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. Fairfield countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 6-2 deficit into an 8-6 lead. Harvard put together a 12-1 spurt in the quarter that turned an 8-6 deficit into an 18-9 lead. The Crimson took a 20-16 lead into the second quarter.

Esme Ellison, Chloe Ferreira, and Natalie Olsen keyed a 13-4 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 38-28. Harvard went into the locker room up 38-30.

A 27-7 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 65-39. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard kept Fairfield at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 14.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-2 overall.
- Junior Esme Ellison finished with a team-high 19 points (6-of-12 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 5-of-6 FT) and 13 rebounds for a double-double.
- Junior Jordan Delgado contributed 12 points (3-of-6 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 6-of-6 FT).
- Junior Chloe Ferreira finished with 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT).
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
