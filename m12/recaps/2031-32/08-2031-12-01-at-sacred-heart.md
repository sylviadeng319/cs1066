Harvard women's basketball (7-1) cruised to a 81-58 victory over Sacred Heart on Monday at William H. Pitt Center in Fairfield, Conn.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pioneers 51-33. The win is Harvard's seventh straight.

"We talked about being the aggressor, and our players took that to heart from the first possession," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Sacred Heart scored first before senior Mackenzie Takahashi knocked down a jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 13-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's triple, which turned a 3-0 deficit into a 13-6 lead. Harvard led 17-11 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together an 11-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 28-11. A 20-9 Sacred Heart spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 37-31. Harvard took a 39-33 lead into halftime.

Sacred Heart countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that drew level at 40. An 8-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Emeka Rhodes's 3-pointer, broke a 43-all tie and put Harvard up 51-43. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Jasmine Danforth, Jordan Delgado, and Takahashi keyed a 16-0 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 81-58. The lead reached 23, allowing Wren Farrow, Reese Kearns, and Naomi Prescott to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-1 overall.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira finished with 15 points (6-of-14 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT), eight rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi added 15 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT) and seven assists.
- Junior Jordan Delgado finished with 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and four assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 21 of its 31 field goals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 32 points.
