Harvard women's basketball gave its three seniors the sendoff they wanted. On Senior Day at Lavietes Pavilion, the Crimson (18-9, 6-7 Ivy) beat Cornell, 73-57, on Saturday, avenging a one-point loss in Ithaca last month and winning its third straight game.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored captains Valentina Okonkwo and Mackenzie Takahashi along with Emeka Rhodes, and all three were in the starting lineup. For Rhodes, a fellow Montclair, N.J., native alongside Okonkwo, it was her first start of the season. Takahashi, who passed 1,000 career points at Penn on Feb. 13, capped two of Harvard's big runs with 3-pointers, while junior Esme Ellison and sophomore Jasmine Danforth did the heavy lifting inside as Harvard took care of the ball, committing just nine turnovers while forcing 18.

"Mackenzie, Valentina, and Emeka have given so much to this program, through some really good days and some hard ones. Today was about them, and I loved the way everyone on our team played for them. They deserved to go out at home with a win," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored the first three points of the game before Danforth got Harvard going at the free throw line. Her two foul shots started an 11-0 run that Takahashi capped with a 3-pointer for an 11-3 lead, and the Crimson led 17-10 after one quarter.

Ellison, Bella Chen, and Danforth keyed a 14-5 run in the second quarter that made it 33-22, and Harvard took a 33-24 lead into halftime.

The Crimson broke the game open after the break. A 12-0 run, capped by another Takahashi 3-pointer, pushed the lead to 45-28. Cornell answered with six straight points, but Harvard outscored the Big Red 23-16 in the quarter and led 56-40 heading into the fourth.

Cornell made one last push, trimming the margin to seven at 58-51. Harvard answered with a 14-2 spurt to go up 72-53, and the Crimson closed out the win from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-9 overall and 6-7 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Esme Ellison led Harvard with 23 points (9-of-16 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), matching her season high, and grabbed eight rebounds.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth scored a career-high 22 points (9-of-13 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and pulled down nine rebounds.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi added 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and three assists in her final regular-season home game.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo grabbed a team-high 10 rebounds.
- Senior Emeka Rhodes played 30 minutes in her first start of the season, finishing with five rebounds and four assists.
- Harvard avenged a 58-57 loss at Cornell on Jan. 17.
