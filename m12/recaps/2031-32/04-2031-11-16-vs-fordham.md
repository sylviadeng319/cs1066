Harvard women's basketball (3-1) held on for a 69-66 win over Fordham on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers. The win is Harvard's third straight.

"Credit to Fordham; they made it hard on us all night. Our players just kept believing," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Fordham scored first before junior Jordan Delgado scored on a driving layup for Harvard's first points. The Rams went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which broke a 7-all tie and pushed the Rams ahead 14-7. The Crimson strung together a 19-6 burst late in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's putback, which turned a 14-7 deficit into a 26-20 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

A 16-3 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 42-23. The Rams countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 42-29. Harvard went into the locker room up 43-29.

The teams traded baskets for much of the third quarter. The Crimson carried a 59-48 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 69-57. Harvard held a 69-57 lead in the final minutes. Fordham drained a 3-pointer with 1:58 left to cut the deficit to 69-60. The Rams scored inside with 1:53 left to get within 69-62. Fordham hit a jumper with 56 seconds left to get within 69-64. The Rams scored with 52 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-1 overall. The Crimson is 3-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 15 points (6-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira added 13 points (5-of-9 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT), six rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo chipped in 13 points (4-of-6 FG, 5-of-5 FT).
- Jordan Delgado (11) also reached double figures.
