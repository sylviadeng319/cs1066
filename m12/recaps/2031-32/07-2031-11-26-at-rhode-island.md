Harvard women's basketball (6-1) picked up a 65-54 win over Rhode Island on Wednesday at Ryan Center in Kingston, R.I.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Rams 41-32.

## How it Happened

Junior Chloe Ferreira opened the scoring when she buried a 3-pointer. A 6-0 Rhode Island spurt early in the quarter flipped a 5-0 deficit into a 6-5 lead. Valentina Okonkwo, Bella Chen, and Esme Ellison keyed a 12-5 run midway through the quarter that turned a 6-5 deficit into a 17-11 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Rams countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 22-18. Harvard put together a 10-3 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 32-21. The Crimson led 34-26 at the break.

The Rams went on an 11-2 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 43-39. Harvard led 45-41 heading into the fourth.

A 15-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Ellison's layup, stretched the lead to 60-41.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-1 overall.
- Junior Esme Ellison finished with 12 points (5-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth finished with 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT).
- Junior Jordan Delgado chipped in 12 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo recorded a double-double with 11 points (4-of-6 FG, 3-of-5 FT) and 12 rebounds.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded nine steals.
