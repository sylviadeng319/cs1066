Harvard women's basketball fought its way back to the Ivy League Tournament this season. On Friday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, top-seeded Princeton ended the run. The No. 1 seed Tigers pulled away in the second quarter and held off a late Crimson push for a 65-55 win in the semifinals, closing No. 4 seed Harvard's season at 19-10.

The Crimson arrived on a four-game winning streak, having won its last four regular-season games to climb from 3-7 in league play to 7-7 and claim a tournament berth one year after missing the field. But Princeton, which went 14-0 in the Ivy League for the second straight season, beat Harvard for the third time this year. The Crimson shot 31.7% from the field and made nine of its 14 free throws, and it never recovered from a 20-9 Tigers run before halftime. The loss ends the collegiate careers of seniors Valentina Okonkwo, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Emeka Rhodes.

"I'm so proud of this group. A lot of people counted us out in February, and these players kept showing up and kept believing in each other. Princeton is a terrific team and they earned it tonight, but our seniors have left a real mark on this program, and the rest of our players know what it takes now," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first and went up 9-4 before Harvard answered with a 9-3 spurt to take a 13-12 lead, which held through the end of the first quarter.

The Crimson still led 16-12 early in the second when the Tigers took control. Princeton outscored Harvard 20-9 the rest of the half to go into the locker room ahead 32-25.

The teams traded baskets for much of the third quarter, and Harvard couldn't make up any ground. Princeton carried a 45-36 lead into the fourth.

The Tigers pushed the margin to 63-49 with a 7-0 spurt midway through the final period. Harvard kept fighting, scoring 19 points in the fourth quarter, and outscored the Tigers 6-2 the rest of the way to make the final 65-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard's season ends in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals with a 19-10 record and a 7-7 mark in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth posted a double-double with 13 points (6-of-14 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT) and 10 rebounds.
- Junior Jordan Delgado added 13 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Junior Esme Ellison scored eight points and grabbed seven rebounds in 36 minutes.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo pulled down seven rebounds in her final collegiate game, and senior Mackenzie Takahashi finished her career with five points and two assists.
- First-year Bella Chen scored six points with four rebounds and two assists off the bench.
- Princeton swept the season series, 3-0, after wins of 60-56 in Cambridge on Jan. 30 and 67-63 in Princeton on Feb. 14.
