Harvard women's basketball (9-2) pulled away from Vermont for a 64-49 victory on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Catamounts 50-40.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Valentina Okonkwo finished a post move. A 17-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's 3-pointer, turned a 3-2 deficit into a 19-5 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

A 7-0 Vermont spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 21-12. Delgado, Esme Ellison, and Bella Chen keyed a 15-2 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 36-14. The score held there at halftime.

Harvard put together a 13-3 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 51-20. Harvard took a 53-25 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Catamounts never got closer than 15 in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-2 overall. The Crimson is 5-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth led the way for Harvard with 16 points (7-of-14 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT), 13 rebounds, and three steals for a double-double.
- Junior Jordan Delgado contributed 15 points (6-of-17 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT), seven assists, and three steals.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo recorded a double-double with 12 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-2 FT), 13 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi came away with four steals.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded 15 steals.
- Harvard held Vermont to 31.1% shooting from the field.
- Danforth's 16 points were a career high.
