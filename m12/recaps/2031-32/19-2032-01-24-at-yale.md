Harvard women's basketball did nearly everything it could on the glass at John J. Lee Amphitheater on Saturday. It could not make enough shots. The Crimson (13-6, 1-4 Ivy) built an 11-point second-quarter lead over Yale, then shot just 24.4% from the field for the game in a 58-53 loss to its rival.

Harvard outrebounded the Bulldogs 56-46 and pulled down 24 offensive rebounds, and the Crimson went a perfect 10-for-10 at the free throw line. But Harvard made only 19 of its 78 field goal attempts, and Yale won the third quarter 20-14 to take control. The loss was Harvard's fourth straight after a 13-2 start, a stretch that began with a home defeat against Brown and continued with one-point losses at Cornell and Columbia.

"We got the looks we wanted and we went and got the ball back over and over again. It just wouldn't go down tonight. I'm not going to fault the effort, but we have to find a way to win games like this, and we will," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Valentina Okonkwo opened the scoring with a jumper from the elbow, but Yale answered with an 11-5 spurt to go in front 11-7. Harvard pulled even, and the teams were tied at 11 after one quarter.

Trailing 16-14 in the second, the Crimson put together its best stretch of the afternoon, a 15-2 run that made it 29-18. Yale closed the half with seven straight points, though, and Harvard's lead was down to 29-25 at the break.

The Bulldogs took over in the third quarter. Harvard still led 34-29 when Yale went on a 16-6 run to go ahead 45-40, and the Crimson trailed 45-43 heading into the fourth.

Points stayed hard to come by for Harvard in the final frame. Yale led 56-51 in the closing minutes and scored with 2:49 left to push the margin to seven. Mackenzie Takahashi answered with a pull-up jumper to make it 58-53, but neither team scored again.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 13-6 overall and 1-4 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo posted a double-double with 14 points (5-of-7 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and a team-high 17 rebounds, 10 of them on the offensive end.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth grabbed 12 rebounds, including seven offensive boards.
- First-year Bella Chen went 3-of-4 from 3-point range for nine points off the bench, and junior Chloe Ferreira added nine points and six rebounds as a reserve.
- Junior Esme Ellison scored nine points with eight rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points, and the Crimson assisted on 13 of its 19 field goals.
