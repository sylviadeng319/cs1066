Harvard women's basketball (16-9, 4-7 Ivy) cruised to a 68-44 victory over Dartmouth on Saturday at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

The victory snapped a two-game skid. The Crimson shot 39.7% from the field and held Dartmouth to 25.0%.

## How it Happened

The Big Green scored first before senior Valentina Okonkwo scored inside for Harvard's first points. Okonkwo, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Tiana Adeyemi keyed a 25-8 run in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into a 25-10 lead. The Crimson took a 25-13 lead into the second quarter.

A 16-3 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's bucket, stretched the lead to 41-18. Harvard went into the locker room up 41-19.

The Crimson strung together a 9-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's three, which stretched the lead to 54-29. The Crimson carried a 54-34 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together an 11-3 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 65-37. The lead reached 28, allowing Emeka Rhodes, Wren Farrow, and Elise Fennimore to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-9 overall and 4-7 in Ivy League play.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth led Harvard with 12 points (4-of-7 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Junior Chloe Ferreira contributed 10 points (3-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT), five rebounds, and three assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 29 points.
