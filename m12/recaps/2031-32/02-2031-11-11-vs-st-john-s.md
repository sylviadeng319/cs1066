Harvard women's basketball (1-1) never trailed on Tuesday, beating St. John's, 70-60, at Lavietes Pavilion.

## How it Happened

Junior Esme Ellison opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Harvard opened the game on an 18-2 run. The Red Storm countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 21-11. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 9-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's triple, stretched the lead to 30-16. St. John's went on a 13-5 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 35-29. Harvard went into the locker room up 37-29.

Neither team could create much separation in the third quarter, which ended 16-13 in Harvard's favor. The Crimson carried a 53-42 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard held a 63-58 lead in the final minutes. The Red Storm converted a layup with 2:07 left to cut the deficit to 63-60, but Chloe Ferreira answered when she sank two free throws to push the lead to 65-60. With 1:03 left, Jordan Delgado made a free throw to make it 66-60. With 56 seconds left, Valentina Okonkwo converted a putback to make it 68-60. Ellison put the game away when she finished a post move with 55 seconds left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 1-1 overall.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo finished with 13 points (4-of-16 FG, 5-of-5 FT) and 13 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi finished with 13 points (4-of-15 FG, 1-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Jordan Delgado added 13 points (4-of-9 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Esme Ellison (12) and Chloe Ferreira (10) also reached double figures.
- The Crimson assisted on 15 of its 23 field goals.
