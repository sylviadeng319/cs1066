Brown handed Harvard women's basketball (13-3, 1-1 Ivy) a 59-51 loss on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The loss snapped Harvard's five-game winning streak.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before senior Mackenzie Takahashi added a free throw for Harvard's first points. Brown opened the game on an 8-1 run. The Bears led 9-5 after one.

The Crimson strung together a 13-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's putback, which turned a 15-8 deficit into a 21-15 lead. The Bears went on a 13-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 21-15 deficit into a 28-21 lead. Harvard trailed 28-27 at halftime.

An 18-6 Brown spurt in the quarter extended the lead to 46-33. The Crimson faced a 46-36 deficit going into the final frame.

Harvard got within 55-49 with 54 seconds left, but Brown outscored the Crimson 4-2 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 13-3 overall and 1-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Esme Ellison led the way for Harvard with 16 points (6-of-9 FG, 4-of-5 FT).
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth contributed 14 points (6-of-9 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-3 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo grabbed 10 rebounds.
