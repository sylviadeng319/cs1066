Harvard women's basketball gave unbeaten Princeton all it could handle on Friday night at Lavietes Pavilion, carrying a lead into halftime and outshooting the Tigers from the field. The Ivy League leaders made the plays that mattered in the second half and escaped with a 60-56 win.

The Crimson (13-7, 1-5 Ivy) shot 40.4% to Princeton's 32.8%, and the teams split the rebounds 40-40. The difference came in the small margins. Harvard committed 17 turnovers to the Tigers' 12, and Princeton made 13 of its 16 free throws while the Crimson got to the line just eight times. The loss extended Harvard's skid to five games, all of them in league play.

"That's the best team in our league, and for a long stretch tonight we were the better team. We have to take care of the ball and finish possessions, but I saw a group that competed for 40 minutes, and that's what we need to build on," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Neither team could create much separation in a tight first quarter, and Harvard led 13-12 after 10 minutes.

Princeton strung together an 11-4 burst early in the second quarter to turn a 15-12 deficit into a 23-19 lead and still led 25-21 late in the half. The Crimson closed the period on a 12-4 run, capped by a Valentina Okonkwo layup, to go into the locker room ahead 33-29.

The Tigers came out of the break with a 14-2 run that flipped a 36-31 Harvard lead into a 45-38 Princeton advantage. Harvard fought back to within 47-44 by the end of the third.

The Crimson briefly regained the lead at 48-47 early in the fourth, but Princeton answered with an 11-2 run to go up 58-50. Harvard kept coming. Mackenzie Takahashi hit a pull-up jumper with 2:48 left to make it 60-55, and Esme Ellison's free throw with 1:50 to play pulled the Crimson within 60-56. Harvard couldn't get any closer.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 13-7 overall and 1-5 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with 13 points (6-of-14 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT), six rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Esme Ellison scored 10 points (3-of-8 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and grabbed eight rebounds.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth pulled down a team-high 10 rebounds.
- First-year Bella Chen gave Harvard a lift off the bench with eight points on 3-of-5 shooting, including two 3-pointers, in 10 minutes.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo went 3-of-4 from the field and blocked three shots, and junior Jordan Delgado dished out a team-high five assists.
