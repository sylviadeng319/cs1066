Down 14 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (4-1) rallied for a 72-63 win over Northwestern on Tuesday at Welsh-Ryan Arena in Evanston, Ill.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Wildcats scored first before sophomore Natalie Olsen added a free throw for Harvard's first points. A 19-8 Northwestern spurt in the quarter broke a 3-all tie and pushed the Wildcats ahead 22-11. The score held there after one quarter.

Mackenzie Takahashi and Jordan Delgado keyed a 7-0 run early in the quarter that cut the deficit to 24-20. The Wildcats countered with a 10-0 run midway through the quarter that extended the lead to 36-22. Northwestern took a 36-25 lead into the break.

The Crimson strung together a 26-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's layup, which turned a 38-25 deficit into a 51-43 lead. Harvard led 51-44 heading into the fourth.

The Wildcats went on a 12-2 run midway through the quarter, which drew level at 58. Harvard held a 63-61 lead in the final minutes. With 2:37 left, Takahashi knocked down a corner three to make it 66-61. Northwestern got a basket with 2:20 left to get within 66-63, but Esme Ellison answered when she finished a post move to push the lead to 68-63. With 1:04 left, Olsen scored on a driving layup to make it 70-63. Valentina Okonkwo put the game away when she hit a jumper from the elbow with 3 seconds left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-1 overall.
- Junior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 25 points (10-of-17 FG, 5-of-12 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and nine assists.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira contributed 11 points (5-of-14 FG, 1-of-8 3-PT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Esme Ellison grabbed nine rebounds.
- Delgado's 25 points were a career high.
