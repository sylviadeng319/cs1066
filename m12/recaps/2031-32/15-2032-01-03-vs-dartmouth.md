The Crimson made quick work of Dartmouth on Saturday, as Harvard women's basketball (13-2, 1-0 Ivy) built a lead as large as 33 on the way to a 72-50 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. The win is Harvard's fifth straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 59-40.

"Everybody who checked in made an impact. That kind of depth is something we've been building toward," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Junior Esme Ellison got Harvard on the board when she hit a jumper from the elbow with 9:34 left in the opening quarter. The Crimson strung together a 20-5 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Ellison's free throws, which turned a 5-4 deficit into a 24-10 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 9-0 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 34-14. Harvard went into the locker room up 38-21.

Harvard put together a 12-3 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 54-29. The Crimson carried a 54-32 advantage into the final frame.

Natalie Olsen, Chloe Ferreira, and Ellison keyed a 13-2 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 67-34. The Big Green never got closer than 22 in the fourth quarter. With the lead as large as 33, Tiana Adeyemi, Emeka Rhodes, and Wren Farrow saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-2 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Esme Ellison led Harvard with 14 points (6-of-13 FG, 2-of-4 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo added 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Jordan Delgado dished out a team-high six assists.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth grabbed 11 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 25 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 21 of its 30 field goals.
