Behind a strong first quarter, Harvard women's basketball (8-1) beat Northeastern, 76-59, on Wednesday at Cabot Center in Boston, Mass.

The win is Harvard's eighth straight.

## How it Happened

The Huskies scored first before junior Tiana Adeyemi hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 23-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's free throws, which turned a 4-0 deficit into a 23-9 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 13-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's three, stretched the lead to 36-12. A 12-0 Northeastern spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 36-24. The Crimson led 48-29 at the break.

The Huskies went on an 11-3 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 54-40. Natalie Olsen, Takahashi, and Valentina Okonkwo keyed a 10-2 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 64-42. The Crimson carried a 64-46 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard kept the Huskies at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 14.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-1 overall.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 14 points (5-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), six rebounds, and five steals.
- Junior Esme Ellison chipped in 12 points (6-of-9 FG), five rebounds, and two blocks.
- First-year Bella Chen hit three 3-pointers off the bench.
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 34 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 21 of its 30 field goals.
