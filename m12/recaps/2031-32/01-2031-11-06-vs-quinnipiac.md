Harvard women's basketball opened the 2031-32 season with a lesson in finishing. The Crimson (0-1) led Quinnipiac by 14 in the fourth quarter on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion, but the Bobcats closed on a 27-6 surge and left Cambridge with a 66-59 win.

It was a frustrating start for a team eager to move past a 16-12 season that ended without a trip to the Ivy League Tournament. Harvard did plenty right, outrebounding Quinnipiac 41-32 and grabbing 12 offensive boards, but 18 turnovers and a 30-point fourth quarter from the Bobcats undid a strong middle of the game.

"We played 30 good minutes and then we stopped doing the things that built the lead. We turned it over, we didn't get stops, and a good Quinnipiac team made us pay. That's on all of us, and it's early. We'll learn from this one," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Junior Esme Ellison put the first point of the season on the board at the free throw line, but Quinnipiac answered with a 13-3 run to go up 13-4. The Crimson trailed 15-8 after one quarter.

Harvard flipped the game in the second. Down 17-8, the Crimson ripped off a 19-4 run keyed by Valentina Okonkwo, Ellison, and Mackenzie Takahashi to take a 27-21 lead, and Harvard went to the locker room ahead 29-23 after outscoring the Bobcats 21-8 in the quarter.

A 12-3 spurt early in the third stretched the margin to 41-31. Quinnipiac cut it to 41-36 by the end of the period, but Harvard opened the fourth with 12 of the first 15 points to build its biggest lead of the night at 53-39.

That's when the Bobcats took over. Quinnipiac scored 27 of the next 31 points to turn a 14-point deficit into a 66-57 lead, and the Crimson couldn't string together the stops it needed to answer. A late Harvard basket made the final 66-59.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard opens the season 0-1.
- Junior Esme Ellison led Harvard with 13 points (5-of-8 FG, 3-of-4 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi added 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT).
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo scored nine points and grabbed nine rebounds.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira hit two 3-pointers and scored eight points off the bench.
- Junior Jordan Delgado dished out a team-high six assists and made both of her 3-point attempts.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth pulled down seven rebounds, four on the offensive end, and had three steals.
