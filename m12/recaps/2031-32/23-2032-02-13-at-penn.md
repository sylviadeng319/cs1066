Penn made enough plays down the stretch to edge Harvard women's basketball (15-8, 3-6 Ivy), 61-59, on Friday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before first-year Bella Chen sank two free throws for Harvard's first points. Penn opened the game on a 12-4 run. The Quakers carried a 14-9 lead into the second quarter.

Harvard put together an 11-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 14-9 deficit into a 20-14 lead. A 13-0 Penn spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 20-14 deficit into a 27-20 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

Penn countered with a 14-3 run in the quarter that extended the lead to 41-23. The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's triple, which cut the deficit to 41-30. The Quakers led 43-30 after three quarters.

Penn led 58-52 in the final minutes. With 2:10 left, Chen connected from deep to pull Harvard within 58-55. Valentina Okonkwo converted a putback with 1:48 left to pull Harvard within 58-57. With 53 seconds left, Okonkwo scored through contact to put Harvard ahead 59-58. Penn made a free throw with 25 seconds left to tie it at 59. Penn scored inside with 5 seconds left to go in front 61-59. Harvard had one last look in the closing seconds but couldn't get it to fall.
