The Crimson made quick work of Drexel on Thursday, as Harvard women's basketball (2-1) built a lead as large as 38 on the way to a 87-56 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Dragons 47-35. The Crimson shot 48.6% from the field and held Drexel to 34.4%.

"We challenged ourselves to play to our standard regardless of the opponent, and I thought we did that," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

First-year Bella Chen got Harvard on the board when she scored on a driving layup with 9:48 left in the opening quarter. The Crimson strung together an 18-1 burst early in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's putback, which turned a 3-2 deficit into a 20-4 lead. An 8-1 Drexel spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 21-12. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 29-14.

Mackenzie Takahashi, Esme Ellison, and Chloe Ferreira keyed a 22-2 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 51-18. Harvard took a 56-24 lead into halftime.

Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 62-24. Drexel countered with a 13-5 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 67-37. Harvard led 73-41 heading into the fourth.

A 10-4 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's layup, stretched the lead to 83-48. The lead reached 38, allowing Naomi Prescott, Reese Kearns, and Lainey Rinaldi to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-1 overall.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with 17 points (6-of-8 FG, 4-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira chipped in 16 points (6-of-11 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 14 points (5-of-8 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and 13 rebounds.
- Jordan Delgado (12) and Bella Chen (11) also reached double figures.
