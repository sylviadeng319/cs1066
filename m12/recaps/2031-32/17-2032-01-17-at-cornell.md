Harvard women's basketball (13-4, 1-2 Ivy) came up just short on Saturday, falling 58-57 to Cornell at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

The Big Red shot 43.1% from the field, while Harvard was held to 28.4%.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before junior Tiana Adeyemi hit a triple for Harvard's first points. Cornell went on a 10-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 5-4 deficit into a 14-5 lead. The Big Red carried a 15-10 lead into the second quarter.

An 8-0 Cornell spurt in the quarter extended the lead to 29-17. The Big Red took a 29-20 lead into the break.

Cornell countered with an 8-0 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 37-23. Mackenzie Takahashi, Chloe Ferreira, and Lainey Rinaldi keyed a 13-3 run early in the quarter that cut the deficit to 40-36. Harvard trailed 45-40 entering the fourth.

The game was tied at 54 in the final minutes. Bella Chen hit a pull-up jumper with 2:12 left to put Harvard ahead 56-54. Cornell scored inside with 37 seconds left to tie it at 56, but Esme Ellison answered when she made a free throw to put Harvard ahead 57-56. The Big Red converted a layup with 13 seconds left to go in front 58-57. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.

## Harvard Highlights
