Harvard women's basketball (13-5, 1-3 Ivy) let a 10-point lead slip away on Monday in a 72-71 loss to Columbia at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Lions 44-36.

## How it Happened

The Lions scored first before junior Esme Ellison added a free throw for Harvard's first points. A 9-0 Columbia spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 5-2 deficit into an 11-5 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

The Lions went on an 11-0 run midway through the quarter, which broke a 16-all tie and pushed the Lions ahead 27-16. The Crimson strung together a 14-3 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's triple, which evened the score at 30. Harvard trailed 40-34 at halftime.

A 21-5 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's bucket inside, turned a 40-34 deficit into a 55-45 lead. Harvard took a 63-53 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Lions countered with a 16-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 63-53 deficit into a 69-63 lead. Columbia led 69-63 in the final minutes. Mackenzie Takahashi drilled a three with 1:48 left to pull Harvard within 69-66. The Lions hit a three with 1:43 left to stretch the lead to 72-66, but Chloe Ferreira answered when she splashed a three from the wing to pull Harvard within 72-69. Takahashi hit a pull-up jumper with 16 seconds left to pull Harvard within 72-71. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 13-5 overall and 1-3 in Ivy League play.
