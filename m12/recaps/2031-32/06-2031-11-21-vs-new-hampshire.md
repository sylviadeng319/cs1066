Harvard women's basketball (5-1) cruised to a 81-59 victory over New Hampshire on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Wildcats 47-38. Harvard knocked down 11 3-pointers.

## How it Happened

The Wildcats scored first before junior Esme Ellison finished a post move for Harvard's first points. A 13-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's bucket inside, turned a 3-0 deficit into a 13-3 lead. A 6-0 New Hampshire spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 13-9. The score held there after one quarter.

Ellison, Bella Chen, and Emeka Rhodes keyed a 9-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 22-9. The Wildcats went on a 13-5 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 29-24. It stayed that way at halftime.

Harvard put together a 27-6 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 56-30. The Wildcats countered with an 11-2 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 58-41. Harvard took a 65-41 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 9-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Chloe Ferreira's three, which stretched the lead to 74-43. The Wildcats never got closer than 22 in the fourth quarter. With the lead as large as 31, Naomi Prescott, Lainey Rinaldi, and Reese Kearns saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-1 overall. The Crimson is 4-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 17 points (7-of-11 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT) and five assists.
- Junior Chloe Ferreira finished with 13 points (5-of-12 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Esme Ellison contributed 12 points (6-of-8 FG) and nine rebounds.
- Bella Chen (11) also reached double figures.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 36 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 21 of its 30 field goals.
