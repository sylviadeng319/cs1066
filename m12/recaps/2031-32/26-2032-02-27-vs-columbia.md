The Crimson made quick work of Columbia on Friday, as Harvard women's basketball (17-9, 5-7 Ivy) built a lead as large as 22 on the way to a 75-55 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 53.6% from the field and held Columbia to 34.5%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Lions 40-26.

## How it Happened

First-year Lainey Rinaldi opened the scoring when she buried a 3-pointer. The Lions led 16-15 after one.

Columbia went on a 6-0 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 22-15. Chloe Ferreira, Jordan Delgado, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 21-0 run midway through the quarter that turned a 22-15 deficit into a 36-22 lead. That was the score at halftime.

Columbia countered with a 19-5 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 43-41. Harvard put together an 11-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 54-41. The score held there heading into the fourth.

A 12-1 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Takahashi's layup, stretched the lead to 70-48. The lead reached 22, allowing Reese Kearns, Naomi Prescott, and Wren Farrow to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-9 overall and 5-7 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi finished with a team-high 16 points (5-of-8 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 6-of-6 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Jordan Delgado added 12 points (6-of-8 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT) and four assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 27 points.
