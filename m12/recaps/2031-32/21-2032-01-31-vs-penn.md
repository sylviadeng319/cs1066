Harvard women's basketball (14-7, 2-5 Ivy) pulled away from Penn for a 74-61 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The victory snapped a five-game skid. The Crimson shot 54.7% from the field and held Penn to 37.1%.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before junior Esme Ellison went 2-for-2 at the line for Harvard's first points. The Quakers opened the game on a 12-4 run. Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt in the quarter that turned a 12-4 deficit into a 16-12 lead. The Crimson took a 16-14 lead into the second quarter.

Ellison, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 16-9 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 32-23. A 10-2 Penn spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 34-33. Harvard took a 42-36 lead into halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 16-6 burst early in the quarter, capped by Danforth's layup, which stretched the lead to 58-42. Harvard led 58-45 heading into the fourth.

A 12-4 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's layup, stretched the lead to 70-52.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-7 overall and 2-5 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Esme Ellison finished with a team-high 14 points (5-of-7 FG, 4-of-6 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth contributed 13 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Chloe Ferreira chipped in 13 points (5-of-7 FG, 3-of-3 3-PT).
- Valentina Okonkwo (10) and Mackenzie Takahashi (10) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
