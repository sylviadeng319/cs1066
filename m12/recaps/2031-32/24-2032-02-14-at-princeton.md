For a half at Jadwin Gymnasium, Harvard women's basketball looked like the team that could finally trip up unbeaten Princeton. Then came the third quarter. The Tigers scored 26 points in the period, erasing a 12-point Harvard lead on the way to a 67-63 win on Saturday that completed a sweep of the regular-season series.

The Crimson (15-9, 3-7 Ivy) played the second game of a road weekend one night after a 61-59 loss at Penn, and it brought plenty of fight to New Jersey. Harvard shot 48.1% from the field, forced 18 turnovers, and came up with 15 steals. But the Crimson made just four of its 19 attempts from 3-point range, and a 24-4 Princeton surge after halftime proved too much to overcome despite a fourth-quarter push that got Harvard within two.

"I'm proud of the way we played on both ends for most of this game. We had a stretch in the third quarter where we lost our composure a little, and against a team this good, that's all it takes. We'll be back, and we'll be better for it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored the game's first basket before senior Mackenzie Takahashi answered with a jumper, starting a 7-0 run that she capped with a layup for a 7-3 Harvard lead. The Tigers countered with seven straight of their own, but the Crimson regrouped to lead 13-10 after one quarter.

Harvard took control in the second. A 14-3 run, capped by a Jordan Delgado basket, stretched the lead to 29-17, and the Crimson took a 33-26 advantage into the break.

The Tigers flipped the game early in the third. Down 35-26, Princeton reeled off a 24-4 run to go ahead 50-39, and it led 52-43 heading into the fourth.

Harvard refused to go quietly. A 12-4 spurt in the fourth quarter pulled the Crimson within 61-59, and the margin was still three at 64-61 in the final minutes. Princeton made two free throws with 2:34 left and another at 2:15 to go up 67-61. Delgado dropped in a floater to make it a four-point game, but the Crimson couldn't get any closer.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 15-9 overall and 3-7 in Ivy League play. Princeton won both regular-season meetings, following its 60-56 win at Lavietes Pavilion on Jan. 30.
- Junior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 16 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
- Junior Esme Ellison added 15 points (7-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Mackenzie Takahashi scored 13 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) with six rebounds and a team-high five steals.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth chipped in seven points and three steals.
- Harvard recorded 15 steals and forced 18 turnovers.
