Two weeks after a frustrating afternoon in New Haven, Harvard women's basketball made sure there would be no drama in the rematch. The Crimson (15-7, 3-5 Ivy) never trailed against Yale on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion, poured in 27 points in the second quarter, and rolled to a 77-57 win.

The victory evened the season series after Yale's 58-53 win on Jan. 24, and it gave Harvard a measure of payback for last season's finale, when the Bulldogs beat the Crimson in overtime on this same floor. Harvard took care of the ball, committing just seven turnovers, went 7-for-7 at the free throw line, and assisted on 19 of its 31 field goals. It was the Crimson's second straight win after a five-game slide, and all 15 players on the roster saw action.

"We talked all week about playing with an edge, and I thought we did that from the opening tip. We shared the ball, we took care of it, and we defended. When we play like that, we're a tough team to beat," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Valentina Okonkwo scored through contact with 9:16 left in the first quarter for the game's opening basket, and Harvard jumped out to a 12-4 lead. Yale answered with an 8-2 run to get within 14-12, but the Crimson finished the quarter strong to lead 20-12 after 10 minutes.

The Bulldogs pulled within 29-22 early in the second quarter. Then Harvard took control. Esme Ellison, Okonkwo, and Bella Chen keyed an 18-4 run that pushed the lead to 47-26, and that was the score at halftime.

Yale won a back-and-forth third quarter by two points, but Harvard still carried a 58-39 cushion into the fourth. The Bulldogs never got closer than 15 the rest of the way, and with the lead as large as 21, Carrie Moore went deep into her bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-7 overall and 3-5 in Ivy League play, and the Crimson is 9-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season. The teams split their regular-season series.
- Junior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 18 points (7-of-15 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Valentina Okonkwo added 14 points (7-of-10 FG) and six rebounds.
- First-year Bella Chen scored 10 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and handed out four assists off the bench.
- Sophomore Wren Farrow scored five points in six minutes, going 3-of-3 at the line.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points, and the Crimson committed just seven turnovers while forcing 13.
