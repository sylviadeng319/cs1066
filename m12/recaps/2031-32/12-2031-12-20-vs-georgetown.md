Harvard women's basketball (10-2) led from start to finish in a 71-59 win over Georgetown on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

## How it Happened

Junior Esme Ellison opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Harvard opened the game on an 11-4 run. The Hoyas countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that drew level at 11. It stayed that way after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 17-7 burst early in the quarter, capped by Chloe Ferreira's three, which broke an 11-all tie and put Harvard up 28-18. Harvard took a 34-24 lead into halftime.

Georgetown went on an 11-4 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 40-37. Harvard put together a 15-4 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 55-41. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard kept the Hoyas at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 11.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-2 overall. The Crimson is 6-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Esme Ellison finished with a team-high 23 points (11-of-13 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Jordan Delgado added 15 points (5-of-12 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-7 FT) and five assists.
- Sophomore Jasmine Danforth finished with 11 points (4-of-5 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), seven rebounds, three assists, and four steals.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 28 field goals.
