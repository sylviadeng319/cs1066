Harvard women's basketball (17-7, 8-2 Ivy) pulled away from Columbia for a 74-68 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

Columbia scored first before senior Jada Nkemelu scored through contact for Harvard's first points. A 20-11 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Nkemelu's 3-pointer, turned a 7-2 deficit into a 22-18 lead. The Crimson took a 24-22 lead into the second quarter.

Addison Garrison, Kira Bianchi, and Celeste Laurent keyed a 20-4 run early in the quarter that broke a 24-all tie and put Harvard up 44-28. Columbia countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 44-35. Harvard took a 49-37 lead into halftime.

Columbia went on a 6-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 51-43. Harvard put together a 10-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 61-43. Harvard took a 65-51 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Bianchi's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 73-55. Columbia never got closer than six in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-7 overall and 8-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Kira Bianchi led Harvard with 18 points (9-of-12 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT).
- Senior Addison Garrison chipped in 13 points (4-of-11 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu added 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Fatima Quintero (10) also reached double figures.
