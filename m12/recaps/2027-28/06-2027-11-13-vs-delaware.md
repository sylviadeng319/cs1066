Harvard women's basketball (6-0) pulled away from Delaware for a 64-53 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight.

## How it Happened

The Blue Hens scored first before senior Kira Bianchi finished a post move for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 9-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's free throw, which turned a 7-4 deficit into a 13-9 lead. The teams were knotted at 14 after one.

Ramsey, Bianchi, and Jada Nkemelu keyed a 9-2 run early in the quarter that broke a 14-all tie and put Harvard up 23-16. An 8-0 Delaware spurt early in the quarter flipped a 23-16 deficit into a 24-23 lead. It was 25-all at the break.

Harvard put together a 23-10 spurt in the quarter that turned a 27-25 deficit into a 48-37 lead. Harvard led 51-41 heading into the fourth.

An 8-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Nkemelu's triple, stretched the lead to 59-41. Harvard kept Delaware at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below six.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-0 overall. The Crimson is 5-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Fatima Quintero led the way for Harvard with 15 points (6-of-16 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), eight rebounds, three assists, and three steals.
- Junior Jasmine Ramsey contributed 13 points (4-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison finished with 12 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), nine rebounds, and three steals.
- Senior Kira Bianchi recorded a double-double with 11 points (4-of-8 FG, 3-of-3 FT), 13 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Junior Celeste Laurent dished out a team-high seven assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 18 of its 24 field goals.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded 10 steals.
