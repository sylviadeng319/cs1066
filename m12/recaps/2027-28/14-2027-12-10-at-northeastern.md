The Crimson made quick work of Northeastern on Friday, as Harvard women's basketball (9-5) built a lead as large as 26 on the way to a 79-53 win at Cabot Center in Boston, Mass.

The Crimson shot 56.6% from the field and held Northeastern to 42.2%.

## How it Happened

The Huskies scored first before senior Kira Bianchi finished a post move for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together a 22-5 spurt in the quarter that turned a 6-2 deficit into a 24-11 lead. Harvard led 24-13 after the first quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 10-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Fatima Quintero's triple, which stretched the lead to 36-18. A 7-0 Northeastern spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 36-25. Harvard took a 40-29 lead into halftime.

Northeastern countered with a 10-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 48-44. An 8-1 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's layup, stretched the lead to 56-45. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Niamh Hollis, Thea Sutherland, and Ramsey keyed a 23-8 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 79-53. With the lead as large as 26, Hollis and Lauren Wakefield saw time off the bench.
