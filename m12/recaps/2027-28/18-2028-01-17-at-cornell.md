Harvard women's basketball (12-6, 3-1 Ivy) never trailed on Monday, beating Cornell, 69-51, at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 53-44.

## How it Happened

Senior Jada Nkemelu got Harvard on the board when she sank two free throws with 7:43 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 13-5 run. A 6-0 Cornell spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 13-11. That was the score after one quarter.

Addison Garrison, Fatima Quintero, and Kira Bianchi keyed a 33-11 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 46-22. It stayed that way at halftime.

The third quarter was a back-and-forth affair. The Crimson carried a 57-30 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 10-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Garrison's triple, which stretched the lead to 69-44. The lead reached 27, allowing Lauren Wakefield and Kendall Lindqvist to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-6 overall and 3-1 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu led the way for Harvard with 18 points (7-of-13 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with 11 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison added 10 points (3-of-14 FG, 2-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-3 FT), six rebounds, and three assists.
- Harvard held Cornell to 29.7% shooting from the field.
