Harvard women's basketball (13-6, 4-1 Ivy) erased a 14-point deficit and came away with a 73-63 win over Brown on Saturday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

Harvard's defense forced 22 turnovers.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Senior Jada Nkemelu opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Brown went on a 19-3 run in the quarter, which flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 19-5 lead. The Bears led 21-11 after one.

Harvard put together a 16-2 spurt midway through the quarter that evened the score at 31. Brown countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that broke a 31-all tie and pushed the Bears ahead 38-31. Brown took a 38-33 lead into the break.

Addison Garrison, Fatima Quintero, and Nkemelu keyed a 26-11 run in the quarter that turned a 40-33 deficit into a 59-51 lead. The score held there heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together an 8-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Kira Bianchi's free throw, which stretched the lead to 69-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-6 overall and 4-1 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu paced the Crimson with 18 points (8-of-12 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT).
- Senior Addison Garrison chipped in 15 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT), six assists, and four steals.
- Senior Kira Bianchi contributed 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 FT).
