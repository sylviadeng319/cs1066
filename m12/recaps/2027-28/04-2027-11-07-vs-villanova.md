Harvard women's basketball (4-0) held on for a 62-59 win over Villanova on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Wildcats scored first before senior Addison Garrison hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst in the quarter, capped by Fatima Quintero's free throw, which turned a 4-2 deficit into a 12-4 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 14-9.

A 16-6 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 39-24. Harvard took a 39-26 lead into halftime.

Villanova went on a 19-6 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 52-47. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 62-52 lead in the final minutes. Villanova answered from beyond the arc with 2:05 left to cut the deficit to 62-55. The Wildcats converted a layup with 36 seconds left to get within 62-57. Villanova scored with 11 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-0 overall.
- Junior Jasmine Ramsey led Harvard with 15 points (7-of-13 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Addison Garrison added 14 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-7 FT), seven rebounds, and four assists.
- Senior Kira Bianchi grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard held Villanova to 32.4% shooting from the field.
