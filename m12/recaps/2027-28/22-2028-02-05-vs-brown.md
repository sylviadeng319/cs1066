Harvard women's basketball (15-7, 6-2 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Brown, 74-51, at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Bears 51-39. The Crimson shot 41.8% from the field and held Brown to 30.9%.

## How it Happened

Senior Addison Garrison opened the scoring when she hit a triple. Harvard opened the game on a 30-9 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 30-11.

Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 41-18. The Crimson led 45-24 at the break.

Garrison, Jada Nkemelu, and Isla Marchetti keyed a 10-2 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 62-34. Harvard took a 63-39 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Fatima Quintero's bucket, which stretched the lead to 74-49. With the lead as large as 28, Niamh Hollis, Brielle Mendoza, and Mira Hightower saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-7 overall and 6-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Addison Garrison paced the Crimson with 18 points (5-of-13 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 5-of-8 FT), seven rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu added 14 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with 10 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Fatima Quintero (10) also reached double figures.
- Junior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points.
