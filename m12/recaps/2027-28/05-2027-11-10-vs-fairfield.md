Harvard women's basketball (5-0) picked up a 71-61 win over Fairfield on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 50.9% from the field and held Fairfield to 34.4%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Stags 40-32.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Senior Kira Bianchi got Harvard on the board when she scored inside with 9:13 left in the opening quarter. Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that broke a 4-all tie and put Harvard up 12-4. An 8-2 Fairfield spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 14-12. The Crimson took a 15-12 lead into the second quarter.

Addison Garrison, Jasmine Ramsey, and Jada Nkemelu keyed a 26-10 run in the quarter that turned an 18-17 deficit into a 43-28 lead. That was the score at halftime.

The teams traded baskets for much of the third quarter. The Crimson carried a 55-41 advantage into the final frame.

Fairfield never got closer than seven in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-0 overall. The Crimson is 4-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Addison Garrison finished with 15 points (6-of-14 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Kira Bianchi added 15 points (7-of-9 FG, 1-of-1 FT), seven rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu chipped in 13 points (6-of-9 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT) and seven rebounds.
- Fatima Quintero (12) also reached double figures.
