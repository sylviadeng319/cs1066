## How it Happened

The Minutewomen scored first before senior Fatima Quintero knocked down a jumper for Harvard's first points. Jasmine Ramsey and Kira Bianchi keyed a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that turned a 9-4 deficit into a 10-9 lead. The Minutewomen countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 10-9 deficit into a 16-10 lead. Harvard trailed 18-15 after the opening frame.

UMass went on an 8-2 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 26-19. The Crimson strung together a 12-4 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Ramsey's three, which turned a 26-19 deficit into a 31-30 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 33-32.

A 13-4 UMass spurt early in the quarter flipped a 34-33 deficit into a 46-38 lead. Harvard put together a 12-2 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 46-38 deficit into a 50-48 lead. The Crimson carried a 51-50 advantage into the final frame.

The Minutewomen led 66-64 in the final minutes. UMass got a basket with 1:15 left to stretch the lead to 68-64. The Minutewomen hit a jumper with 54 seconds left to stretch the lead to 70-64. The Minutewomen hit a three with 15 seconds left to stretch the lead to 73-64. Harvard did not score over the final 3:19.
