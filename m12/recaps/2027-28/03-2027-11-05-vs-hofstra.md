Behind a strong second quarter, Harvard women's basketball (3-0) beat Hofstra, 64-47, on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pride 48-39. The win is Harvard's third straight.

"We did a lot of the little things well tonight: boxing out, talking on defense, making the extra pass," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Junior Jasmine Ramsey got Harvard on the board when she made a free throw with 9:48 left in the opening quarter. Hofstra countered with an 8-2 run early in the quarter that flipped a 1-0 deficit into an 8-3 lead. The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Jada Nkemelu's free throws, which turned an 8-3 deficit into a 12-8 lead. The Crimson took a 13-12 lead into the second quarter.

Isla Marchetti, Kira Bianchi, and Nkemelu keyed a 22-9 run in the quarter that turned a 17-14 deficit into a 36-26 lead. The score held there at halftime.

A 15-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Bianchi's layup, stretched the lead to 51-32. Harvard took a 51-34 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 63-34. Harvard kept the Pride at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 17.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-0 overall.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu finished with a team-high 19 points (7-of-19 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and 10 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Kira Bianchi recorded a double-double with 17 points (7-of-14 FG, 3-of-3 FT), 11 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Senior Fatima Quintero dished out a team-high six assists.
- Harvard held Hofstra to 31.0% shooting from the field.
- The Crimson assisted on 18 of its 24 field goals.
