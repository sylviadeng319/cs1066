Harvard women's basketball (7-2) let a 9-point lead slip away on Tuesday in a 67-58 loss to Maine at Memorial Gym in Orono, Maine.

The Black Bears shot 48.0% from the field, while Harvard was held to 37.5%.

## How it Happened

First-year Thea Sutherland got Harvard on the board when she finished a post move with 9:58 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 9-0 run. A 24-2 Maine spurt in the quarter flipped a 9-0 deficit into a 24-11 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

Harvard put together a 16-5 spurt midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 31-29. Harvard trailed 35-33 at halftime.

Maine went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 42-33. A 7-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Addison Garrison's jumper, cut the deficit to 42-40. Harvard trailed 51-45 entering the fourth.

Harvard got within 53-50 with 8:25 left, but Maine outscored the Crimson 14-8 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 7-2 overall.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with 12 points (4-of-8 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Fatima Quintero added 12 points (4-of-14 FG, 3-of-11 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), four assists, and three steals.
