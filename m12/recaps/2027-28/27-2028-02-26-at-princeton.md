Harvard women's basketball arrived at Jadwin Gymnasium riding a five-game winning streak and looking for a split of the season series with the league leaders. Princeton had other ideas, pulling away late for a 69-57 win on Saturday in Princeton, N.J.

The Crimson (19-8, 10-3 Ivy), playing a night after a wire-to-wire win at Penn, were far more competitive than in the first meeting, a 56-44 loss at Lavietes Pavilion on Jan. 29. Harvard shot 40.4% from the field, made seven 3-pointers to Princeton's three, and cut the deficit to one in the third quarter. But 22 turnovers and an 18-of-22 night at the free throw line for the Tigers were too much to overcome, and Princeton completed a regular-season sweep, a year after Harvard won 84-79 on this floor.

"We fought. We cut it to one in the third quarter and we were right there with six minutes to go. But you can't turn it over 22 times against a team this good and expect to win. That's the lesson, and we've got to take it with us into March," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before senior Fatima Quintero hit a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points, and Quintero and Addison Garrison keyed a 6-0 run for a 6-2 lead. The Tigers answered with a 14-2 burst and led 20-14 after the first quarter.

Harvard battled back in the second. An 11-5 run, capped by a Jada Nkemelu 3-pointer, tied the game at 27, but Princeton responded with a 13-2 spurt to go up 40-29. The Crimson scored the last four points of the half to trail 40-33 at the break.

The third quarter brought Harvard's best stretch. A 9-1 burst, capped by a Kira Bianchi bucket inside, cut the deficit to 47-46, but the Tigers scored the next six points and carried a 53-46 lead into the fourth.

Harvard still had a chance late. The Crimson got within 55-51 with 6:53 left, but Princeton outscored Harvard 14-6 the rest of the way to pull away.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 19-8 overall and 10-3 in Ivy League play. The loss snapped a five-game winning streak.
- Senior Kira Bianchi led Harvard with 19 points (8-of-12 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu added 12 points (4-of-7 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison scored nine points on 3-of-5 shooting, including 2-of-2 from 3-point range, and grabbed nine rebounds.
- Junior Juniper Pryor dished out a team-high four assists off the bench.
- Princeton swept the regular-season series after a 56-44 win at Lavietes Pavilion on Jan. 29.
