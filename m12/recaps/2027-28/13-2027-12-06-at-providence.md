Harvard women's basketball (8-5) dropped a 69-59 decision to Providence on Monday at Alumni Hall in Providence, R.I.

The Friars shot 39.3% from the field, while Harvard was held to 28.8%.

"Give Providence credit. They executed better than we did today," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Providence scored first before senior Addison Garrison finished a layup in transition for Harvard's first points. The Friars countered with a 19-8 run in the quarter that flipped a 2-1 deficit into a 20-10 lead. Providence led 20-12 after one.

Jada Nkemelu, Celeste Laurent, and Isla Marchetti keyed an 8-0 run early in the quarter that evened the score at 20. A 17-6 Providence spurt midway through the quarter broke a 20-all tie and pushed the Friars ahead 37-26. That was the score at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 17-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Marchetti's free throw, which turned a 37-26 deficit into a 43-39 lead. The Friars went on an 11-2 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 43-39 deficit into a 50-45 lead. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Harvard got within 50-47 with 8:56 left, but Providence outscored the Crimson 19-12 the rest of the way.
