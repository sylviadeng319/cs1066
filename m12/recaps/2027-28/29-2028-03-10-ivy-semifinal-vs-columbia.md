For the second straight year, Harvard women's basketball will play for the Ivy League championship. The No. 2 seed Crimson (21-8) rallied from a seven-point third-quarter deficit to beat No. 3 seed Columbia, 74-58, in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals on Friday at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn.

The win settled the rubber match of a season series the teams had split. Columbia won the first meeting, 67-64 in overtime, on Jan. 15 in New York, and Harvard answered with a 74-68 win at Lavietes Pavilion on Feb. 12. This one belonged to the Crimson after halftime: Harvard outscored the Lions 45-24 over the final two quarters and dominated the glass, 44-31, behind a monster night from senior captain Kira Bianchi.

"Columbia beat us in overtime in January, and they gave us everything again tonight. We didn't panic when we were down at half. Our seniors took over, and that's what they've done all year. Tournament basketball is about surviving and advancing, and now we get a chance to play for a championship," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Senior Jada Nkemelu opened the scoring with a 3-pointer, and Harvard led 7-4 before Columbia answered with an 11-5 run. The Lions led 17-14 after the first quarter.

Columbia stretched its advantage early in the second. A 12-3 spurt put the Lions up 29-17. Harvard responded with a 12-2 run of its own to pull within 31-29, and Columbia took a 34-29 lead into the break.

The Lions scored first after halftime to go up 36-29, and then the Crimson took over. Fatima Quintero, Celeste Laurent, and Bianchi keyed a 22-9 run that turned the seven-point deficit into a 51-45 lead after three quarters.

Columbia pulled within 51-48 with 9:45 to play, but that was as close as it got. A 20-4 Harvard run, capped by a Quintero layup, made it 71-52 and sent the Crimson into Saturday's final against No. 1 seed Princeton.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game and improves to 21-8 overall. The Crimson won the season series with Columbia, 2-1.
- Senior Kira Bianchi led Harvard with 21 points (8-of-9 FG, 5-of-7 FT), 14 rebounds, and three blocks for a double-double.
- Senior Fatima Quintero added 17 points (6-of-13 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT), six rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Addison Garrison scored 16 points (5-of-10 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and had three steals.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu grabbed nine rebounds, and junior Celeste Laurent dished out a team-high four assists.
- Harvard recorded 10 steals and five blocks and grabbed 14 offensive rebounds.
