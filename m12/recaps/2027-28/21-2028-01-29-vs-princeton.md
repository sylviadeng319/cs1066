Less than 24 hours after grinding out a win over Penn, Harvard women's basketball ran into a Princeton defense that gave it almost nothing. The Tigers held the Crimson to 26.7% shooting and handed Harvard a 56-44 loss on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The defeat snapped a three-game winning streak for the Crimson (14-7, 5-2 Ivy), and the 44 points were Harvard's fewest of the season. Princeton controlled the glass, 48-38, and turned 14 offensive rebounds into extra chances. Harvard forced 16 turnovers and used a third-quarter push to cut the deficit to nine, but the Tigers had an answer each time.

"Princeton is the standard in this league right now, and they showed why tonight. We got good looks that didn't fall, but they beat us on the boards and we can't let that happen. We'll see them again, and we'll be ready," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first, and junior Celeste Laurent answered with a corner 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. The Crimson held a brief 4-2 edge before the Tigers took over with a 12-3 run, and Princeton led 18-12 after the first quarter.

The second quarter belonged to the visitors. A 13-4 Princeton run early in the period pushed the lead to 31-16, and the Tigers took a 35-23 advantage into halftime.

Harvard came out of the break with more energy. A 10-4 run, capped by a free throw from senior Addison Garrison, cut the deficit to 42-33, and the Crimson trailed 46-35 entering the fourth.

Princeton answered every push. A 7-0 spurt midway through the final quarter made it 53-38, and Harvard couldn't string together enough stops to mount a comeback.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 14-7 overall and 5-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Addison Garrison led Harvard with 11 points (3-of-11 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Kira Bianchi added nine points and six rebounds and made all three of her free throws.
- Junior Celeste Laurent scored seven points and had two steals.
- Junior Jasmine Ramsey grabbed a team-high seven rebounds off the bench.
- Senior Fatima Quintero and sophomore Isla Marchetti each recorded three steals as Harvard forced 16 Princeton turnovers.
