For the second time in 10 months, Harvard women's basketball had to survive a late Yale charge at John J. Lee Amphitheater. And for the second time, the Crimson held on, edging the Bulldogs, 70-68, on Saturday in New Haven, Conn.

Harvard (11-5, 2-0 Ivy) led by 13 in the third quarter and took a 60-49 advantage into the final frame, but Yale outscored the Crimson 19-10 in the fourth and scored three times in the final 2:10 to keep the outcome in doubt. It was a familiar script: last March, Harvard closed the regular season with a 75-73 win in the same building after the Bulldogs scored the game's final eight points. The win is Harvard's third straight and keeps the Crimson unbeaten in league play.

"Yale is going to play hard against us every time, especially in this building. We made enough plays at the end, and our bench was terrific. That's how you win on the road in this league," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Kira Bianchi scored on a post move for the game's first basket, and Harvard opened on an 11-2 run. Yale answered with 16 straight points to go in front 18-11, and the Bulldogs led 25-18 after the first quarter.

The Crimson flipped the game in the second quarter. Addison Garrison, Jada Nkemelu, and sophomore Isla Marchetti keyed a 14-2 run that turned the seven-point deficit into a 32-27 lead, and Harvard's defense held Yale to nine points in the period to take a 39-34 lead into halftime.

Harvard kept pushing early in the third. A 12-2 run, capped by a jumper from junior Juniper Pryor, made it 51-38. Yale got within 53-47 with a 9-2 spurt, but the Crimson led 60-49 heading into the fourth.

The Bulldogs chipped away from there. Harvard still led 68-62 in the final minutes, but Yale scored with 2:10 left and again with 1:29 left to make it 68-66. First-year Thea Sutherland answered by banking in a turnaround for a 70-66 lead, and a Yale basket with 31 seconds to go only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-5 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Kira Bianchi led Harvard with 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Juniper Pryor scored 11 points off the bench in nine minutes, going a perfect 4-of-4 from the field, 1-of-1 from 3-point range, and 2-of-2 at the line.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu added 11 points (5-of-11 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Fatima Quintero scored 10 points with a team-high seven rebounds and five assists.
- First-year Thea Sutherland had two points and three steals in eight minutes, and her turnaround in the final 1:29 gave Harvard a 70-66 lead.
- Harvard's bench contributed 25 points, and the Crimson recorded 11 steals while forcing 15 Yale turnovers.
