Harvard women's basketball (18-7, 9-2 Ivy) led from start to finish in a 71-45 win over Dartmouth on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight. The Crimson shot 48.3% from the field and held the Big Green to 35.6%.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Kira Bianchi banked in a turnaround. Harvard opened the game on a 22-2 run. A 9-3 Dartmouth spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 25-11. The score held there after one quarter.

A 12-1 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Bianchi's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 37-12. Dartmouth countered with an 8-2 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 39-20. Harvard went into the locker room up 41-20.

Neither team could create much separation in the third quarter. Harvard led 55-35 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 67-35. With the lead as large as 32, Lauren Wakefield, Kendall Lindqvist, and Thea Sutherland saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-7 overall and 9-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Addison Garrison led Harvard with 16 points (5-of-13 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT), five rebounds, and five steals.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with 14 points (6-of-8 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Fatima Quintero contributed 11 points (4-of-13 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT) and four steals.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded 17 steals.
- Harvard outrebounded Dartmouth 38-24, including 12 offensive boards.
