Harvard women's basketball (16-7, 7-2 Ivy) cruised to a 63-36 victory over Cornell on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

"I loved our energy from the opening tip. We defended, we shared the ball, and we played for each other for 40 minutes," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Senior Fatima Quintero got Harvard on the board when she added a free throw with 9:56 left in the opening quarter. Harvard put together a 9-2 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 2-1 deficit into a 10-4 lead. Harvard led 12-6 after the first quarter.

Quintero, Celeste Laurent, and Jada Nkemelu keyed a 22-8 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 36-17. That was the score at halftime.

Cornell countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 40-24. The Crimson carried a 46-28 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 12-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's free throw, which stretched the lead to 63-36. The lead reached 27, allowing Mira Hightower, Brielle Mendoza, and Niamh Hollis to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-7 overall and 7-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Celeste Laurent led the way for Harvard with 17 points (7-of-18 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu chipped in 11 points (5-of-12 FG, 1-of-2 FT).
- Senior Addison Garrison contributed 10 points (3-of-13 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and five rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 18 of its 25 field goals.
- Harvard held Cornell to 24.1% shooting from the field.
