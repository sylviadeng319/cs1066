Harvard women's basketball took a lead into the fourth quarter of the Ivy League championship game, 10 minutes from a second straight NCAA Tournament bid. No. 1 seed Princeton took it back with a 22-7 run, and the Tigers beat the No. 2 seed Crimson, 74-67, on Saturday at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn.

The loss ends Harvard's season at 21-9 and closes the careers of a senior class of Addison Garrison, Kira Bianchi, Jada Nkemelu, and Fatima Quintero, who helped the Crimson cut down the nets at Jadwin Gymnasium a year ago with a 50-42 win over the Tigers in this same game. This time Princeton, which went 13-1 in league play, completed a 3-0 sweep of the season series. Harvard led 51-48 entering the final frame and pushed the margin to six, but the Tigers scored 26 points in the fourth quarter, and Harvard committed 16 turnovers to Princeton's 10.

"I'm heartbroken for our seniors, because they deserved to go out with a trophy. They've won a championship, they've played in the NCAA Tournament, and they've set the standard for everyone who comes after them. Princeton made the big plays in the fourth quarter, and we have to give them credit. This season doesn't define our players, but it says a lot about who they are," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Princeton came out firing, opening the game on a 13-5 run, with junior Jasmine Ramsey's two free throws accounting for Harvard's first points. The Crimson trailed 13-8 after one quarter.

Harvard found its footing in the second quarter. A 13-5 burst, capped by a Bianchi layup, gave the Crimson a 21-18 lead before Princeton answered with a 12-3 run to go up 30-24. Harvard closed the half strong, and the Tigers took only a 31-30 lead into the locker room.

The third quarter was a back-and-forth battle. Harvard went up 43-39 before a 7-0 Princeton run put the Tigers ahead 46-43, but the Crimson answered with eight straight points late in the period for a 51-46 lead and carried a 51-48 edge into the fourth.

Harvard stretched the lead to 54-48 early in the final quarter. Then Princeton delivered the decisive stretch, a 22-7 run that turned a six-point deficit into a 70-61 lead. The Crimson kept fighting, but the Tigers held firm down the stretch.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard finishes the season 21-9 overall after an 11-3 Ivy League record that placed second in the league standings.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu led Harvard with 16 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 5-of-7 FT) in her final collegiate game.
- Senior Kira Bianchi added 10 points on 4-of-5 shooting and grabbed eight rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison scored 10 points (4-of-13 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Junior Jasmine Ramsey scored nine points off the bench, going 6-of-6 from the free throw line, and added three assists.
- Junior Celeste Laurent dished out a team-high four assists and had two steals.
- Princeton won all three meetings this season, following a 56-44 win at Lavietes Pavilion on Jan. 29 and a 69-57 win at Jadwin Gymnasium on Feb. 26.
