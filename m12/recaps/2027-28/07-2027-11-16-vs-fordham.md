Harvard women's basketball (6-1) let a 10-point lead slip away on Tuesday in a 65-54 loss to Fordham at Lavietes Pavilion.

The loss snapped Harvard's six-game winning streak. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Rams 51-38.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Fatima Quintero went 2-for-2 at the line. Harvard opened the game on an 11-1 run. Fordham countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 11-8. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 13-8.

The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's bucket inside, which broke a 13-all tie and put Harvard up 21-13. Fordham went on a 17-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 21-13 deficit into a 30-23 lead. The Rams took a 32-29 lead into the break.

An 11-4 Fordham spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 43-33. Kira Bianchi and Ramsey keyed a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 43-39. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard got within 48-44 with 8:19 left, but Fordham outscored the Crimson 17-10 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 6-1 overall. The Crimson is 5-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu paced the Crimson with 9 points (3-of-9 FG, 3-of-4 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Kira Bianchi grabbed 14 rebounds.
