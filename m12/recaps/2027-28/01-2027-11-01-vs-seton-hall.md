The defending Ivy League Tournament champions picked up right where they left off. Harvard women's basketball opened the 2027-28 season with an 80-60 win over Seton Hall on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion, scoring 28 points in the first quarter and never letting the Pirates back within single digits after halftime.

It was the first game since last March's 65-62 NCAA Tournament loss at No. 4 Maryland, and the Crimson (1-0) looked sharp from the start. Harvard shot 54.4% from the field, held Seton Hall to 34.9%, won the rebounding battle 41-33, and assisted on 17 of its 31 field goals. All 14 players on the roster saw the floor, including first-years Thea Sutherland, Kendall Lindqvist, and Mira Hightower.

"We have four seniors who know what it takes to win in March, and they set the tone tonight. There's plenty to clean up, but that's what November is for, and I liked the way we shared the ball," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Senior captain Addison Garrison opened the season's scoring at the free throw line, and the early minutes were a trade of punches. Seton Hall used a 9-2 run to turn an 8-5 deficit into a 14-10 lead before Harvard answered with an 18-5 spurt to close the quarter ahead 28-19.

The Crimson took control in the second quarter. A 12-0 burst, capped by a Kira Bianchi bucket inside, stretched the lead to 41-23. The Pirates countered with seven straight points, but Harvard carried a 47-32 lead into the locker room.

Harvard came out of halftime with another 12-0 run, this one keyed by sophomore Isla Marchetti, Bianchi, and Garrison, to go up 59-32. Seton Hall closed the third quarter on a 9-0 spurt to trail 61-43, but the Crimson shut the door early in the fourth. A 10-0 run, capped by a Garrison 3-pointer, made it 73-47, and the Pirates never got closer than 16 the rest of the way. The lead reached 27, giving the Harvard bench extended minutes down the stretch.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Senior Addison Garrison led all scorers with 23 points (9-of-12 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 4-of-8 FT) and added three assists in 24 minutes.
- Senior Kira Bianchi added 16 points on 7-of-9 shooting from the field.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu posted a double-double with 10 points (4-of-5 FG) and 10 rebounds, eight of them on the defensive end.
- Sophomore Isla Marchetti scored nine points off the bench on 4-of-6 shooting.
- Junior Celeste Laurent added nine points and three assists, and senior Fatima Quintero dished out three assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 17 points, and first-year Thea Sutherland recorded a block in her debut.
