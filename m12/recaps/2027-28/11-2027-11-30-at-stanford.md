Stanford pulled away from Harvard women's basketball (7-4) on Tuesday, handing the Crimson a 66-47 defeat at Maples Pavilion in Stanford, Calif.

Turnovers were costly, as Harvard gave the ball away 20 times.

"We didn't defend the way we're capable of. That's on all of us, starting with me," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Cardinal scored first before senior Jada Nkemelu banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. Stanford opened the game on a 17-5 run. The Cardinal carried a 17-7 lead into the second quarter.

Stanford countered with a 17-3 run in the quarter that extended the lead to 34-10. Harvard trailed 36-14 at halftime.

A 12-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Kira Bianchi's bucket inside, cut the deficit to 36-26. An 11-0 Stanford spurt midway through the quarter extended the lead to 47-26. Stanford led 49-32 after three quarters.

The Cardinal went on a 13-0 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 62-32. Stanford controlled the fourth quarter from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 7-4 overall.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with a team-high 10 points (5-of-11 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT) and eight rebounds.
