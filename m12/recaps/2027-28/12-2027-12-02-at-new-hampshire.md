Down 8 in the third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (8-4) rallied for a 71-53 win over New Hampshire on Thursday at Lundholm Gym in Durham, N.H.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Wildcats 45-37. The victory snapped a three-game skid.

## How it Happened

The Wildcats scored first before senior Kira Bianchi banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. Bianchi, Fatima Quintero, and Jasmine Ramsey keyed a 9-1 run early in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into a 9-3 lead. Harvard led 9-7 after the first quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 13-6 burst early in the quarter, capped by Bianchi's free throw, which broke an 11-all tie and put Harvard up 24-17. The Wildcats countered with a 12-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 30-23 deficit into a 35-30 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 35-31.

A 15-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Mira Hightower's free throw, turned a 39-31 deficit into a 46-41 lead. Harvard took a 46-43 lead into the fourth quarter.

New Hampshire got within 46-all with 8:52 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 25-7 spurt in the quarter that broke a 46-all tie and put Harvard up 71-53. The lead reached 18, allowing Brielle Mendoza, Niamh Hollis, and Lauren Wakefield to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-4 overall.
- Senior Addison Garrison led the way for Harvard with 16 points (6-of-9 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Senior Fatima Quintero chipped in 14 points (5-of-13 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), seven rebounds, and six steals.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded 14 steals.
