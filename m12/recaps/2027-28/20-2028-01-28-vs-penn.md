It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (14-6, 5-1 Ivy) made the plays it needed in a 61-56 win over Penn on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Jasmine Ramsey finished a post move. Harvard put together a 21-3 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 5-2 deficit into a 23-8 lead. The Crimson took a 25-11 lead into the second quarter.

The Quakers went on a 9-2 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 32-21. The Crimson led 38-27 at the break.

A 9-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Kira Bianchi's putback, stretched the lead to 50-30. An 8-0 Penn spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 50-38. Harvard led 52-38 heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 57-51 lead in the final minutes. The Quakers hit a pair at the line with 2:43 left to get within 57-53, but Juniper Pryor answered when she buried a 3-pointer to push the lead to 60-53. Penn made a free throw with 1:43 left to cut the deficit to 60-54. The Quakers hit a jumper with 1:16 left to cut the deficit to 60-56. Celeste Laurent added a free throw with 1:15 left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-6 overall and 5-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Celeste Laurent led Harvard with 11 points (4-of-13 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Kira Bianchi added 10 points (5-of-10 FG) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison finished with 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT).
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points.
