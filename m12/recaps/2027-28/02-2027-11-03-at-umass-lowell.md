Harvard women's basketball (2-0) led from start to finish in a 92-64 win over UMass Lowell on Wednesday at Costello Athletic Center in Lowell, Mass.

Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Kira Bianchi added a free throw. Harvard opened the game on an 18-5 run. An 18-6 UMass Lowell spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 24-23. Harvard led 27-23 after the first quarter.

Fatima Quintero, Jada Nkemelu, and Addison Garrison keyed a 26-5 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 53-28. The River Hawks countered with a 9-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 53-37. Harvard went into the locker room up 55-37.

The River Hawks went on an 8-2 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 57-45. A 16-8 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Garrison's bucket, stretched the lead to 73-53. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 12-2 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 85-57. With the lead as large as 28, Thea Sutherland, Lauren Wakefield, and Brielle Mendoza saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
- Senior Fatima Quintero led the way for Harvard with 18 points (6-of-11 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Junior Celeste Laurent contributed 17 points (5-of-13 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), four assists, and three steals.
