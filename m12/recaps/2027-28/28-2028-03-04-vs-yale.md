Harvard women's basketball sent its seniors out of Lavietes Pavilion in style. On a Senior Day that honored Fatima Quintero, Addison Garrison, Jada Nkemelu, and Kira Bianchi, the Crimson never trailed in a 78-51 rout of Yale on Saturday to close the regular season.

The win completed a season sweep of the Bulldogs, following a 70-68 decision in New Haven on Jan. 8, and gave Harvard (20-8, 11-3 Ivy) sole possession of second place in the final league standings. It was also a strong response to last weekend's loss at Princeton. The Crimson shot 49.2% from the field, held Yale to 32.7%, won the glass 38-29, and got 28 points from its bench. Twelve different players scored.

"These four seniors have given this program everything, and today was about them. I loved how we defended for them. That's what it looks like when we're locked in, and that's who we need to be next weekend," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Junior Juniper Pryor scored the first basket of the game with a mid-range jumper, and after Yale tied it at 2, Harvard used a 15-4 spurt to go up 17-6. The Bulldogs answered with nine straight points to make it 17-15, and the Crimson led 19-15 after one quarter.

Then Harvard put the game away. A 28-6 run in the second quarter, capped by junior Jasmine Ramsey's bucket inside, pushed the lead to 47-21, and the Crimson outscored Yale 30-11 in the period to take a 49-26 lead into halftime.

Yale used a 7-0 run early in the third quarter to get within 53-35, but Harvard answered with six straight points, the last on a Bianchi free throw, and led 59-38 entering the fourth.

First-year Thea Sutherland, Bianchi, and Celeste Laurent keyed a 16-3 run in the final quarter that stretched the lead to 78-49, and the Bulldogs never got closer than 16 the rest of the way. With the lead as large as 29, the Crimson emptied its bench, and every player on the roster got into the game.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard finishes the regular season 20-8 overall and 11-3 in Ivy League play, second in the league, and will be the No. 2 seed in the Ivy League Tournament at Yale's John J. Lee Amphitheater. The Crimson went 12-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Harvard swept the season series with Yale and has won four straight meetings with the Bulldogs, including both games of the 2026-27 series.
- Senior Fatima Quintero led Harvard with 16 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Senior Kira Bianchi added 12 points (5-of-7 FG, 2-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Celeste Laurent scored 11 points and dished out a team-high four assists.
- Junior Jasmine Ramsey and sophomore Isla Marchetti each scored seven points off the bench, and first-year Thea Sutherland added five.
- Senior Addison Garrison chipped in seven points and four rebounds, and senior Jada Nkemelu added four points and four rebounds.
