Harvard women's basketball (7-1) picked up a 71-63 win over Merrimack on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Warriors 34-22.

## How it Happened

Junior Celeste Laurent opened the scoring when she hit a pull-up jumper. Merrimack went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 4-0 deficit into a 7-4 lead. Harvard put together a 21-8 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 7-4 deficit into a 25-15 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

Neither team could create much separation in the second quarter, which ended 10-9 in Harvard's favor. Harvard went into the locker room up 35-24.

Merrimack countered with a 14-3 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 42-38. An 11-5 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's jumper, stretched the lead to 53-43. The score held there heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Ramsey's putback, which stretched the lead to 65-47.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-1 overall. The Crimson is 6-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Kira Bianchi led Harvard with 24 points (9-of-13 FG, 6-of-6 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison finished with 14 points (7-of-11 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT) and three steals.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu grabbed 11 rebounds.
- The 24 points were a season high for Bianchi.
