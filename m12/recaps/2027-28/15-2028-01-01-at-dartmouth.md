The Crimson made quick work of Dartmouth on Saturday, as Harvard women's basketball (10-5, 1-0 Ivy) built a lead as large as 26 on the way to a 63-41 win at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 63-50.

## How it Happened

Junior Celeste Laurent opened the scoring when she scored on a driving layup. Harvard put together a 15-2 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 4-2 deficit into a 17-6 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 21-10.

Dartmouth went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 25-17. The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Addison Garrison's layup, which stretched the lead to 33-17. The Crimson led 33-19 at the break.

An 11-4 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Garrison's free throws, stretched the lead to 44-23. An 8-0 Dartmouth spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 44-31. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Kira Bianchi, Garrison, and Fatima Quintero keyed a 19-6 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 63-37. With the lead as large as 26, Niamh Hollis, Lauren Wakefield, and Mira Hightower saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-5 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with a team-high 17 points (8-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and 14 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Addison Garrison finished with 14 points (5-of-17 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Celeste Laurent dished out a team-high six assists.
- Senior Jada Nkemelu grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard held Dartmouth to 23.9% shooting from the field.
