Harvard women's basketball (19-7, 10-2 Ivy) led from start to finish in a 66-53 win over Penn on Friday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

The Crimson shot 49.2% from the field and held Penn to 33.9%. The win is Harvard's fifth straight.

"We talked about being the aggressor, and our players took that to heart from the first possession," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Senior Kira Bianchi opened the scoring when she scored inside. Harvard opened the game on a 16-5 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 20-11.

A 9-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Jada Nkemelu's layup, stretched the lead to 29-11. A 6-0 Penn spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 31-19. Harvard took a 36-19 lead into halftime.

The Quakers went on a 12-3 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 39-31. Bianchi and Jasmine Ramsey keyed an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 47-31. Harvard took a 47-38 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 19-8 burst in the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's layup, which stretched the lead to 66-46.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-7 overall and 10-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with a team-high 16 points (7-of-10 FG, 2-of-3 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Fatima Quintero added 15 points (7-of-11 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Addison Garrison finished with 10 points (3-of-9 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Celeste Laurent dished out a team-high six assists.
