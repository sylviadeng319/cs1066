Harvard women's basketball (11-6, 2-1 Ivy) battled Columbia into an extra period on Saturday but fell, 67-64, at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

The Lions shot 41.3% from the field, while Harvard was held to 34.3%.

## How it Happened

Columbia scored first before junior Celeste Laurent splashed a three from the wing for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into an 8-2 lead. The Crimson took an 8-5 lead into the second quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Kira Bianchi's putback, which turned a 14-12 deficit into a 19-14 lead. A 10-4 Columbia spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 19-14 deficit into a 24-23 lead. Harvard took a 25-24 lead into halftime.

Columbia countered with an 11-1 run early in the quarter that flipped a 25-24 deficit into a 35-26 lead. A 12-3 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Addison Garrison's bucket, evened the score at 38. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Columbia went on a 12-4 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 43-40 deficit into a 52-47 lead. The Lions led 54-50 in the final minutes. Bianchi scored through contact with 2:48 left to pull Harvard within 54-52. With 1:00 left, Bianchi banked in a turnaround to tie the game at 54. Jasmine Ramsey hit a jumper from the elbow with 34 seconds left to put Harvard ahead 56-54. Columbia hit a jumper with 25 seconds left to tie it at 56. The teams were tied at 56 at the end of regulation, sending the game to overtime.

Garrison buried a mid-range jumper to open the scoring in overtime. The Lions led 62-58 late in the extra period. With 58 seconds left, Bianchi hit a jumper from the elbow to pull Harvard within 62-60. Fatima Quintero sank two free throws with 16 seconds left to tie the game at 62. Columbia drained a 3-pointer with 15 seconds left to go in front 65-62. The Lions converted a layup with 11 seconds left to stretch the lead to 67-62, but Bianchi answered when she finished a post move to pull Harvard within 67-64. Columbia won the extra period 11-8.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 11-6 overall and 2-1 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Kira Bianchi finished with a team-high 21 points (10-of-16 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and 14 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Addison Garrison contributed 15 points (4-of-12 FG, 2-of-9 3-PT, 5-of-6 FT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Fatima Quintero came away with five steals.
