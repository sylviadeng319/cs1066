Harvard women's basketball opened the 2035-36 season the hard way. The Crimson (1-0) trailed Hofstra 54-52 with 1:04 left, then scored the game's final five points for a 57-54 win on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

It was not a pretty night on offense. Harvard shot 29.3% from the field and made just five 3-pointers, but it outrebounded the Pride 53-44, grabbed 23 offensive rebounds, and committed only nine turnovers. Senior captain Ciara Bergstrom set a career high in points and posted a double-double in the first game since last season ended with a 58-54 loss at Princeton in the Ivy League Tournament final.

"Opening nights are rarely smooth, and this one wasn't. We missed a lot of shots, but we kept going to the glass, and when we needed stops at the end, we got them. That tells me a lot about this group," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Hofstra scored first before junior Chloe Rousseau hit a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. Trailing 6-3, the Crimson answered with a 12-3 burst, capped by a Bergstrom basket inside, to take a 15-9 lead, and that was the score after one quarter.

Rousseau, Bergstrom, and sophomore Bria Jefferson keyed an 11-5 run in the second quarter that stretched the advantage to 26-14. Harvard held Hofstra to 17 first-half points and led 26-17 at the break.

The Pride found its range after halftime. A 17-10 Hofstra run cut the deficit to 41-36 in the third quarter, and the Crimson carried a 43-36 lead into the fourth.

Hofstra kept coming. A 17-8 run flipped the game and gave the Pride a 53-51 lead in the final minutes. Jefferson made a free throw with 1:16 left, and Hofstra answered with one of its own at 1:04. Senior Quinn Castellanos tied the game at 54 with a pair of free throws, Rousseau knocked down a jumper with 26 seconds left to put Harvard ahead, and Bergstrom sealed it at the line with three seconds to play.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom posted a double-double with a career-high 16 points (6-of-15 FG, 4-of-6 FT) and a team-high 14 rebounds, seven of them on the offensive end.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau scored 14 points (6-of-18 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT), handed out four assists, and hit the go-ahead jumper with 26 seconds left.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos grabbed 11 rebounds, and sophomore Bria Jefferson added seven points and nine rebounds.
- Junior Callie Winslow dished out a team-high six assists in 12 minutes off the bench.
- Harvard held Hofstra to 33.3% shooting from the field and scoreless over the final 64 seconds.
