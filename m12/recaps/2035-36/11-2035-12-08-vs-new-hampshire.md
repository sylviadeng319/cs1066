New Hampshire made enough plays down the stretch to edge Harvard women's basketball (10-1), 66-64, on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The loss snapped Harvard's 10-game winning streak. The Wildcats shot 45.3% from the field, while Harvard was held to 37.5%.

## How it Happened

New Hampshire scored first before junior Juniper Haddad buried a mid-range jumper for Harvard's first points. The Wildcats opened the game on an 18-9 run. Harvard trailed 18-11 after the opening frame.

The Wildcats went on a 15-6 run in the quarter, which extended the lead to 33-21. The Crimson strung together a 10-2 burst late in the quarter, capped by Haddad's three, which cut the deficit to 35-31. The Crimson went into the locker room down 39-31.

A 12-3 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Chloe Rousseau's bucket, turned a 39-31 deficit into a 43-42 lead. New Hampshire countered with a 9-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 43-42 deficit into a 51-43 lead. The Crimson carried a 52-51 advantage into the final frame.

New Hampshire led 61-57 in the final minutes. New Hampshire hit a jumper with 2:34 left to stretch the lead to 63-57, but Bria Jefferson answered when she converted a putback to pull Harvard within 63-59. The Wildcats answered from beyond the arc with 2:07 left to stretch the lead to 66-59, but Jefferson answered when she hit a triple to make it 66-62. With 1:25 left, Rousseau buried a mid-range jumper to make it 66-64. Harvard had one last look in the closing seconds but couldn't get it to fall.
