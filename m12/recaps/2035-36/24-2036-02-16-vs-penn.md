Harvard women's basketball (18-6, 6-4 Ivy) held on for a 51-50 win over Penn on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The victory snapped a three-game skid.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before junior Chloe Rousseau finished a layup in transition for Harvard's first points. Rousseau, Quinn Castellanos, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed an 18-7 run in the quarter that turned a 3-0 deficit into an 18-10 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

Harvard put together a 10-3 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 28-13. The Quakers countered with an 8-1 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 29-21. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 7-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Callie Winslow's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 36-24. Harvard led 40-32 heading into the fourth.

A 15-4 Penn spurt early in the quarter drew level at 47. Harvard held a 51-48 lead in the final minutes. The Quakers scored with 1:33 left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-6 overall and 6-4 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Juniper Haddad led Harvard with 11 points (4-of-13 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom grabbed 14 rebounds.
- Harvard held Penn to 28.8% shooting from the field.
- Harvard's bench contributed 25 points.
