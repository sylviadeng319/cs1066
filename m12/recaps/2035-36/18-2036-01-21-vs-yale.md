Yale never got a lead to hold. Harvard women's basketball scored 31 points in the first quarter, never trailed, and beat the Bulldogs, 87-75, on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion for its sixth straight win.

Two days after a win over Brown, the Crimson (16-2, 4-0 Ivy) stayed unbeaten in league play behind one of its sharpest offensive nights of the season. Harvard shot 54.0% from the field, made 12 3-pointers, and forced 20 Yale turnovers. It was the Crimson's third straight win in the rivalry after a season sweep of the Bulldogs last winter.

"Yale shot the ball well tonight, so our defense had to show up in other ways, and it did. We shared the ball, and every time they made a run, we had an answer," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Junior Hailey Brennan opened the scoring with a driving layup, and Harvard raced out to a 28-14 lead before taking a 31-17 advantage after the first quarter.

A 6-0 run capped by a Chloe Rousseau 3-pointer pushed the margin to 40-21 in the second quarter. Yale responded with an 18-3 run to pull within 45-41, but Harvard steadied itself and went into halftime up 47-41.

The Crimson took control for good after the break. A 19-1 burst, capped by another Rousseau triple, stretched the lead to 66-44. Yale closed the third quarter with an 11-2 spurt to trail 68-55 entering the fourth.

Callie Winslow and Rousseau keyed an 8-0 run midway through the final frame that made it 83-63, and the Bulldogs never got within single digits in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-2 overall and 4-0 in Ivy League play and extends its winning streak to six games.
- Junior Hailey Brennan led Harvard with 16 points (6-of-13 FG, 4-of-11 3-PT), four assists, and two steals.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau scored 15 points (6-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT).
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson added 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Callie Winslow scored 11 points and handed out a team-high five assists off the bench, and junior Juniper Haddad went 4-of-4 from the field for nine points.
- Harvard's bench contributed 26 points, and the Crimson recorded 10 steals and 18 assists.
