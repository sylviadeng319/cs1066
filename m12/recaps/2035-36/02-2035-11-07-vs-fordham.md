Harvard women's basketball (2-0) erased a 24-point deficit and came away with a 68-62 win over Fordham on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

"Our defense turned the game. Once we started getting stops, everything opened up," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Rams scored first before junior Hailey Brennan knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. A 28-8 Fordham spurt in the quarter flipped a 4-2 deficit into a 30-12 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

Fordham went on a 6-0 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 36-12. A 13-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Ciara Bergstrom's putback, cut the deficit to 38-25. The score held there at halftime.

Harvard put together a 25-10 spurt in the quarter that turned a 39-25 deficit into a 50-49 lead. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Rams got within 54-53 with 6:56 left before Harvard took control. Brennan, Bergstrom, and Quinn Castellanos keyed a 10-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 64-53.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau led the way for Harvard with 16 points (7-of-18 FG, 2-of-10 3-PT) and five steals.
- Junior Hailey Brennan contributed 15 points (6-of-13 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT, 0-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom added 14 points (6-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson grabbed nine rebounds.
- Rousseau's 16 points were a career high.
- Brennan's 15 points were a career high.
