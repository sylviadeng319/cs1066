Harvard women's basketball (13-2, 1-0 Ivy) cruised to a 81-61 victory over Cornell on Saturday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 37-27. The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

The Big Red scored first before senior Ciara Bergstrom scored through contact for Harvard's first points. Cornell opened the game on a 20-7 run. The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Hailey Brennan's jumper, which cut the deficit to 20-13. It stayed that way after one quarter.

Cornell countered with an 8-0 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 28-17. An 18-4 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Bergstrom's free throw, turned a 28-17 deficit into a 35-32 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 35-34.

Harvard put together a 31-5 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 66-39. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Quinn Castellanos, Brennan, and Bria Jefferson keyed an 11-3 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 79-47. The Big Red never got closer than 20 in the fourth quarter. With the lead as large as 32, Sage Navarro, Isla Underwood, and Fatima Sorensen saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-2 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos led the way for Harvard with 19 points (7-of-10 FG, 5-of-5 FT), five rebounds, and five assists.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom contributed 16 points (7-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 21 of its 32 field goals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 25 points.
- Castellanos's 19 points were a career high.
