Down 9 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (19-6, 7-4 Ivy) rallied for a 63-55 win over Columbia on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Lions scored first before junior Chloe Rousseau hit a triple for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 13-1 burst in the quarter, capped by Bria Jefferson's free throws, which turned a 5-0 deficit into a 13-6 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 13-11.

The Lions went on an 11-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 13-11 deficit into a 22-13 lead. Ciara Bergstrom and Malia Moreau keyed a 9-0 run midway through the quarter that evened the score at 22. The Crimson went into the locker room down 25-22.

Columbia countered with a 10-3 run early in the quarter that flipped a 27-25 deficit into a 35-30 lead. It was 37-all heading into the fourth.

An 8-0 Columbia spurt early in the quarter flipped a 42-39 deficit into a 47-42 lead. Harvard held a 55-53 lead in the final minutes. With 1:02 left, Hailey Brennan hit a pair of free throws to push the lead to 57-53. With 51 seconds left, Rousseau knocked down a jumper to make it 59-53. Bergstrom scored through contact with 35 seconds left to push the lead to 61-53. Quinn Castellanos hit a pair of free throws with 16 seconds left to push the lead to 63-53. The Lions scored with 7 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-6 overall and 7-4 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau led the way for Harvard with 14 points (5-of-8 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), six rebounds, and three steals.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom finished with 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson chipped in 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), eight rebounds, and three assists.
