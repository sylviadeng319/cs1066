Harvard women's basketball found itself down 10 at John J. Lee Amphitheater early in the second half on Friday. Then the Crimson ripped off a 24-2 run that turned a tight rivalry game into a 73-53 win over Yale.

Harvard (20-6, 8-4 Ivy) reached 20 wins for the season, won its third straight game, and completed a season sweep of the Bulldogs after an 87-75 victory at Lavietes Pavilion on Jan. 21. The Crimson outscored Yale 45-19 after halftime, shot 50.0% from the field, and assisted on 22 of its 28 field goals.

"We didn't love how we started, and we talked at halftime about trusting each other and moving the ball. The way they responded in the third quarter, on the road, in a rivalry game, says a lot about this group," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored first and opened the game on a 10-3 run before Harvard answered with a 9-2 spurt, capped by a Callie Winslow 3-pointer, to tie it at 12. The Bulldogs led 15-12 after the first quarter.

Harvard grabbed the lead with a 12-2 burst early in the second quarter, capped by a Chloe Rousseau layup, to go up 24-17. Yale responded with 15 straight points to take a 32-24 lead and went into the break ahead 34-28.

The Bulldogs pushed the margin to 42-32 in the third quarter before the Crimson took over. Harvard's 24-2 run turned that deficit into a 56-44 lead, and the score held there heading into the fourth. Harvard outscored Yale 28-10 in the quarter.

Rousseau, sophomore Bria Jefferson, and first-year Avery Ashworth keyed a 15-2 run early in the fourth that pushed the lead to 71-48, and Yale never got back within 10. With the lead as large as 23, every player on the Harvard roster got into the game.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-6 overall and 8-4 in Ivy League play and sweeps the season series with Yale.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau led Harvard with 15 points (6-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) in 18 minutes.
- Junior Juniper Haddad scored 14 points (6-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) off the bench with four assists and three steals.
- First-year Avery Ashworth went 4-of-5 from the field for eight points, and sophomore Bria Jefferson added eight points and four assists.
- Junior Callie Winslow dished out a team-high six assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 35 points, and all 16 players saw action.
