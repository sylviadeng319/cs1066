No. 13 seed Harvard women's basketball built a 22-point lead on Ohio State's home floor, then survived a frantic fourth quarter to knock off the No. 4 seed Buckeyes, 66-63, in the NCAA Tournament First Round on Friday at Value City Arena in Columbus, Ohio.

The Crimson (24-7) made 10 3-pointers and carried a 56-36 lead into the fourth quarter in front of 6,689 fans. Ohio State outscored Harvard 27-10 over the final 10 minutes but ran out of time. The win is Harvard's fourth straight and its second NCAA Tournament victory over Ohio State, following a 71-62 first-round win over the Buckeyes in 2030.

"A lot of people didn't give us much of a chance, but our players believed they belonged here from the opening tip. Ohio State is a terrific team, and they made a big run at the end, but we made just enough plays. I'm so proud of them," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Bria Jefferson opened the scoring with a jumper from the elbow, and Harvard ran out to an 8-0 lead. The Crimson led 23-15 after the first quarter.

A 12-0 run early in the second quarter, capped by a pair of Ciara Bergstrom free throws, pushed the lead to 35-15. Ohio State closed the half with eight straight points, but Harvard still led 37-25 at the break.

Harvard answered in the third quarter. A 15-5 burst, capped by a Hailey Brennan 3-pointer, made it 52-30, and the Crimson took a 56-36 lead into the fourth.

Then the Buckeyes came charging, cutting the deficit to 63-57 in the final minutes. Two Ohio State free throws with 2:59 left made it 63-59, but Brennan answered with a free throw and then a mid-range jumper with 2:54 left for a 66-59 lead. The Buckeyes scored with 2:34 remaining to pull within 66-61, and the Harvard defense held them without a point until a basket with one second left trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the NCAA Tournament Second Round and improves to 24-7 with its fourth straight win.
- Junior Callie Winslow scored a career-high 14 points (5-of-11 FG, 4-of-8 3-PT) off the bench and added four assists.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson scored 11 points (5-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 FT) with five rebounds and three assists.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau added 11 points (3-of-5 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three steals.
- Junior Hailey Brennan scored nine points, including Harvard's final three, and handed out four assists.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos grabbed 10 rebounds, and Harvard's bench contributed 23 points.
