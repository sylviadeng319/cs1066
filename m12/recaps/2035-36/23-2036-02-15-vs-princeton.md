Harvard women's basketball led Princeton after one quarter and battled back to within three late in the third, but the Tigers had an answer each time and left Lavietes Pavilion with a 68-58 win on Friday.

The loss completed a regular-season sweep for Princeton, which also won 66-59 at Jadwin Gymnasium on Feb. 2, and extended Harvard's skid to three games after a 71-65 loss at Dartmouth on Feb. 9. Senior Ciara Bergstrom was a force inside for the Crimson (17-6, 5-4 Ivy). As a team, though, Harvard shot 36.7% from the field, went 10-of-17 from the free throw line, and was outrebounded 47-41.

"We put ourselves in position to win this game. We cut it to three going into the fourth quarter, and then we didn't get enough stops or make enough shots. That's on all of us, and we'll be better for it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Quinn Castellanos got Harvard on the board with a turnaround bank shot with 9:04 left in the opening quarter, and the Crimson led 16-11 after 10 minutes.

Harvard stretched its lead to 22-15 with a 6-0 spurt early in the second quarter before Princeton took over. The Tigers went on a 16-3 run to take a 31-25 lead, and that was the score at halftime.

A 17-9 Princeton spurt early in the third quarter pushed the margin to 50-36. The Crimson responded with its best stretch of the night, an 11-0 run capped by a Bergstrom putback, to pull within 50-47 heading into the fourth.

Princeton scored the first three points of the final frame to make it 53-47 with 9:51 left, and Harvard never got any closer. The Crimson managed 11 points in the quarter as the Tigers pulled away.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-6 overall and 5-4 in Ivy League play. Princeton swept the regular-season series.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom led Harvard with 18 points (6-of-9 FG, 6-of-9 FT), eight rebounds, and two blocks.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson grabbed a team-high 10 rebounds and recorded three steals.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos added nine points and eight rebounds.
- Junior Callie Winslow scored seven points on 3-of-5 shooting and added three assists off the bench.
- Princeton won the battle on the offensive glass, 14-9.
