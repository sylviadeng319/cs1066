Harvard women's basketball (17-3, 5-1 Ivy) erased a 12-point deficit and came away with a 68-61 win over Penn on Friday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Quakers 39-31.

## How it Happened

Penn scored first before junior Chloe Rousseau dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. The Quakers opened the game on an 8-0 run. Penn led 14-11 after one.

The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Thea Dalton's bucket inside, which turned a 20-14 deficit into a 22-20 lead. The Quakers went on a 15-3 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 22-20 deficit into a 35-25 lead. That was the score at halftime.

A 13-5 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Juniper Haddad's bucket, cut the deficit to 48-44. The Crimson faced a 53-46 deficit going into the final frame.

The game was tied at 59 in the final minutes. With 2:25 left, Bria Jefferson hit a jumper from the elbow to put Harvard ahead 61-59. With 1:37 left, Ciara Bergstrom finished a post move to push the lead to 63-59. Penn got a basket with 1:24 left to get within 63-61, but Hailey Brennan answered when she drilled a three to push the lead to 66-61. Rousseau finished a layup in transition with 27 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-3 overall and 5-1 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau finished with a team-high 17 points (6-of-10 FG, 3-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-6 FT).
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom chipped in 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Hailey Brennan contributed 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and three assists.
- Quinn Castellanos (10) also reached double figures.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson grabbed nine rebounds.
