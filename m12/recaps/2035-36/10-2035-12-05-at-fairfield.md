It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (10-0) made the plays it needed in a 67-62 win over Fairfield on Wednesday at Leo D. Mahoney Arena in Fairfield, Conn.

The win is Harvard's 10th straight.

"We had some stretches we'd like back, but when it mattered we were connected and tough," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Stags scored first before senior Quinn Castellanos banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. A 12-5 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Hailey Brennan's triple, turned a 4-0 deficit into a 12-9 lead. The Crimson took a 12-11 lead into the second quarter.

Bria Jefferson, Chloe Rousseau, and Callie Winslow keyed a 20-11 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 32-22. The Stags countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 34-31. The Crimson led 36-31 at the break.

A 9-1 Fairfield spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 45-41. The Crimson strung together a 14-6 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Brennan's 3-pointer, which stretched the lead to 59-47. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 67-54 lead in the final minutes. Fairfield hit a three with 1:41 left to cut the deficit to 67-57. The Stags answered from beyond the arc with 1:35 left to get within 67-60. Fairfield scored with 1 second left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-0 overall.
- Junior Hailey Brennan finished with a team-high 24 points (9-of-22 FG, 6-of-14 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau added 17 points (6-of-15 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three steals.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos finished with 10 points (5-of-8 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT), six rebounds, and four blocks.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom grabbed 15 rebounds.
- Brennan's 24 points were a career high.
