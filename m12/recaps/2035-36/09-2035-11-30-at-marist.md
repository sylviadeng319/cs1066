Harvard women's basketball (9-0) led from start to finish in a 76-48 win over Marist on Friday at McCann Arena in Poughkeepsie, N.Y.

The Crimson shot 43.5% from the field and held the Red Foxes to 30.6%. The win is Harvard's ninth straight.

"That's what it looks like when we're locked in on the defensive end. Our defense created our offense tonight," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Junior Hailey Brennan got Harvard on the board when she added a free throw with 9:49 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 16-8 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 21-13.

A 14-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Ciara Bergstrom's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 40-20. Harvard took a 42-24 lead into halftime.

Bria Jefferson, Quinn Castellanos, and Thea Dalton keyed a 17-9 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 62-38. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 71-41. With the lead as large as 30, Reagan Cardenas, Fatima Sorensen, and Cara Valdez saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-0 overall.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau paced the Crimson with 22 points (7-of-12 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 6-of-7 FT).
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom contributed 11 points (4-of-9 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
- Harvard outrebounded Marist 45-35, including 13 offensive boards.
- Rousseau's 22 points were a career high.
