Columbia handed Harvard women's basketball (16-3, 4-1 Ivy) a 67-57 loss on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

The Lions shot 41.4% from the field, while Harvard was held to 29.4%. The loss snapped Harvard's six-game winning streak.

"We didn't do enough of the things that make us good. We'll regroup and get back to work," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Lions scored first before senior Quinn Castellanos hit a jumper from the elbow for Harvard's first points. A 7-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Hailey Brennan's layup, turned a 5-0 deficit into a 7-5 lead. A 10-1 Columbia spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 9-7 deficit into a 17-10 lead. The Lions carried a 17-12 lead into the second quarter.

Columbia countered with a 13-3 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 30-15. Harvard put together a 7-0 spurt late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 40-32. It stayed that way at halftime.

Columbia went on a 12-5 run in the quarter, which extended the lead to 52-42. Harvard trailed 54-45 entering the fourth.

Harvard got within 54-49 with 9:51 left, but Columbia outscored the Crimson 13-8 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 16-3 overall and 4-1 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Hailey Brennan led Harvard with 14 points (5-of-15 FG, 4-of-10 3-PT) and three steals.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom added 12 points (6-of-10 FG), eight rebounds, and three blocks.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau finished with 10 points (4-of-12 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT).
- Senior Quinn Castellanos protected the rim with three blocks.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson grabbed 10 rebounds.
