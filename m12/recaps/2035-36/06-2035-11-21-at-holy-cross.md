Harvard women's basketball (6-0) held on for a 63-62 win over Holy Cross on Wednesday at Hart Center in Worcester, Mass.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Crusaders 37-24.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Callie Winslow sank two free throws. Harvard opened the game on an 8-0 run. Holy Cross went on a 9-2 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 10-9. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 21-15.

Bria Jefferson, Chloe Rousseau, and Savannah Lachance keyed a 9-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 31-17. A 13-4 Holy Cross spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 35-30. That was the score at halftime.

A 16-7 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Lachance's putback, stretched the lead to 51-37. The Crimson carried a 51-42 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard held a 59-54 lead in the final minutes. Jefferson scored through contact with 1:57 left to push the lead to 61-54. Holy Cross answered from beyond the arc with 1:47 left to get within 61-57. The Crusaders hit a pair at the line with 1:31 left to cut the deficit to 61-59. The Crusaders drained a 3-pointer with 34 seconds left to go in front 62-61, but Juniper Haddad answered when she buried a mid-range jumper to give the Crimson a 63-62 lead.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-0 overall.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom led the way for Harvard with 15 points (5-of-12 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 5-of-6 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos finished with 14 points (7-of-11 FG, 0-of-3 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Hailey Brennan dished out a team-high eight assists.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson grabbed nine rebounds.
