The rubber match wasn't close. No. 2 seed Harvard women's basketball broke open a tight first quarter, got 41 points from its bench, and beat No. 3 seed Columbia, 77-54, in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals on Friday at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn.

The Crimson (22-7) and the Lions split their regular-season meetings, with Columbia winning 67-57 in New York on Jan. 26 and Harvard taking a 63-55 decision at Lavietes Pavilion on Feb. 23. The win sends Harvard to the tournament final for the second straight season, a year after a 58-54 loss at Princeton in the title game. Back on the floor where it beat Yale two weeks ago, Harvard shot 52.5% from the field, held Columbia to 32.3%, and led by as many as 25.

"We knew Columbia would be ready after the way the first two games went, so we wanted to be the aggressor. Everybody who came off the bench gave us something tonight. Now we get a chance to play for a championship, and that's all you can ask for," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Columbia scored first before senior Ciara Bergstrom answered with a post move. The Lions used a 9-1 run to turn a 6-2 deficit into an 11-7 lead, but Harvard responded with 12 straight points, capped by a Quinn Castellanos layup, to go up 19-11. The Crimson led 24-20 after one quarter.

The second quarter belonged to Harvard's defense. A 14-0 burst, capped by a pair of Hailey Brennan free throws, made it 38-23. The Crimson held Columbia to 12 points in the period and led 44-32 at halftime.

Harvard's reserves took over after the break. Sophomore Thea Dalton, first-year Cara Valdez, and junior Juniper Haddad keyed a 12-0 run early in the third quarter that stretched the lead to 56-32. Columbia answered with six straight points, but the Crimson carried a 63-45 lead into the fourth.

A 14-7 Harvard spurt early in the final frame pushed the lead to 77-52, and the Lions never got closer than 20 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game and improves to 22-7. The Crimson won two of three meetings with Columbia this season.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos led Harvard with 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 FT) and 12 rebounds for a double-double.
- Junior Callie Winslow scored 10 points (3-of-11 FG, 2-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and added four assists off the bench.
- Sophomore Thea Dalton went 4-of-4 from the field for eight points in seven minutes.
- Senior Malia Moreau scored seven points in four minutes, and sophomore Sage Navarro dished out four assists in seven minutes.
- Harvard's bench contributed 41 points, and the Crimson outrebounded Columbia 42-33.
