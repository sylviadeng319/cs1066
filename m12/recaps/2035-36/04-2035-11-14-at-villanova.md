Behind a strong first quarter, Harvard women's basketball (4-0) beat Villanova, 59-51, on Wednesday at Finneran Pavilion in Villanova, Pa.

The Crimson shot 38.1% from the field and held Villanova to 26.9%. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

The Wildcats scored first before sophomore Savannah Lachance hit a jumper from the elbow for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 17-6 burst in the quarter, capped by Lachance's layup, which turned a 6-2 deficit into a 19-12 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

A 7-0 Villanova spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 21-19. Harvard put together a 15-4 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 36-23. It stayed that way at halftime.

The Wildcats countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 42-30. An 11-3 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Chloe Rousseau's jumper, stretched the lead to 53-33. Harvard took a 53-35 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard kept Villanova at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below eight.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-0 overall.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos paced the Crimson with 14 points (6-of-11 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau contributed 12 points (5-of-12 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), five rebounds, and four assists.
- Junior Hailey Brennan finished with 12 points (5-of-14 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT), five rebounds, and three assists.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson recorded a double-double with 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT), 10 rebounds, and four assists.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom grabbed 10 rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 24 field goals.
- Castellanos's 14 points were a career high.
