Down 11 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (11-2) rallied for a 77-61 win over Siena on Saturday at Alumni Recreation Center in Loudonville, N.Y.

The victory snapped a two-game skid. The Crimson shot 46.7% from the field and held Siena to 37.5%.

## How it Happened

Siena scored first before junior Chloe Rousseau went 2-for-2 at the line for Harvard's first points. The Saints opened the game on a 22-11 run. The Saints led 22-17 after one.

Siena countered with an 11-3 run early in the quarter that broke a 24-all tie and pushed the Saints ahead 35-27. A 9-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Ciara Bergstrom's free throws, cut the deficit to 37-36. It stayed that way at halftime.

Harvard put together an 18-4 spurt in the quarter that turned a 37-36 deficit into a 54-41 lead. Harvard took a 54-45 lead into the fourth quarter.

Bergstrom, Avery Ashworth, and Callie Winslow keyed a 22-9 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 76-54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-2 overall.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom paced the Crimson with 19 points (5-of-8 FG, 9-of-13 FT), eight rebounds, and three blocks.
- Sophomore Bria Jefferson finished with 14 points (6-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Hailey Brennan chipped in 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT) and four assists.
- Quinn Castellanos (10) and Callie Winslow (10) also reached double figures.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 28 field goals.
- Bergstrom's 19 points were a career high.
- Jefferson's 14 points were a career high.
