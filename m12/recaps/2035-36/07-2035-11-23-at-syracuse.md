Harvard women's basketball (7-0) picked up a 57-46 win over Syracuse on Friday at JMA Wireless Dome in Syracuse, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's seventh straight. The Crimson shot 37.1% from the field and held the Orange to 27.9%.

## How it Happened

The Orange scored first before junior Hailey Brennan hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Callie Winslow's 3-pointer, which turned a 3-0 deficit into an 8-3 lead. Harvard led 13-11 after the first quarter.

Syracuse countered with a 13-4 run early in the quarter that flipped an 18-15 deficit into a 28-22 lead. Syracuse took a 28-26 lead into the break.

Harvard put together a 17-5 spurt in the quarter that turned a 28-26 deficit into a 43-33 lead. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard kept Syracuse at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below six.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-0 overall.
- Junior Hailey Brennan finished with a team-high 17 points (7-of-15 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau added 10 points (4-of-14 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Brennan's 17 points were a career high.
