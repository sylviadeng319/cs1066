Harvard women's basketball (17-5, 5-3 Ivy) let a 13-point lead slip away on Saturday in a 71-65 loss to Dartmouth at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

Dartmouth shot 42.9% from the field, while Harvard was held to 31.4%.

## How it Happened

The Big Green scored first before junior Hailey Brennan hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt late in the quarter that turned a 13-9 deficit into a 17-13 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

Chloe Rousseau, Quinn Castellanos, and Callie Winslow keyed a 13-4 run late in the quarter that turned a 22-21 deficit into a 34-26 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 10-4 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Castellanos's free throw, stretched the lead to 46-33. The Big Green countered with a 15-4 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 50-48. That was the score heading into the fourth.

A 17-3 Dartmouth spurt early in the quarter flipped a 50-48 deficit into a 65-53 lead. Dartmouth led 68-56 in the final minutes. With 2:29 left, Ciara Bergstrom converted a putback to pull Harvard within 68-58. Brennan buried a 3-pointer with 1:18 left to pull Harvard within 68-61. Bria Jefferson splashed a three from the wing with 1:12 left to make it 68-64. With 28 seconds left, Bergstrom made a free throw to make it 68-65. The Big Green drained a 3-pointer with 28 seconds left to stretch the lead to 71-65.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-5 overall and 5-3 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Hailey Brennan finished with a team-high 15 points (6-of-16 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT) and four steals.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom recorded a double-double with 10 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 15 of its 22 field goals.
