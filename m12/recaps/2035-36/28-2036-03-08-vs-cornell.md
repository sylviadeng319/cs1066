Harvard women's basketball's seniors made their last regular-season game at Lavietes Pavilion count. Captains Ciara Bergstrom and Quinn Castellanos each scored in double figures, and fellow senior Malia Moreau made a Senior Day start as the Crimson beat Cornell, 65-57, on Saturday to close the regular season.

The win came a week after a 62-57 loss at Brown in which Harvard let a 15-point lead slip away, and it completed a season sweep of the Big Red following an 81-61 win in Ithaca on Jan. 5. Harvard (21-7, 9-5 Ivy) dominated the glass, outrebounding Cornell 60-47, and finished the regular season in a three-way tie for second place with Columbia and Penn. The Crimson will be the No. 2 seed at next weekend's Ivy League Tournament at Yale.

"These seniors have given so much to this program, and I'm so glad they got to go out on this floor with a win. They've been through a lot together, and they're not done yet. That's exactly how they wanted it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before Bergstrom made a pair of free throws for Harvard's first points. The Crimson answered with a 16-5 burst, capped by a Castellanos putback, to lead 16-7 and was up 21-12 after the first quarter.

Sophomore Bria Jefferson, Bergstrom, and junior Chloe Rousseau keyed a 15-2 run early in the second quarter that made it 38-17. Cornell scored six straight to get within 38-23, but Harvard held the Big Red to 11 points in the quarter and led 44-23 at the break.

Cornell came out firing in the third quarter. The Big Red used a 17-5 run to cut the deficit to 49-40, as Harvard managed just five points in the period.

The Crimson regained control in the fourth. A 14-2 spurt early in the quarter restored a 63-42 lead, and Cornell got no closer than the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard finishes the regular season 21-7 overall and 9-5 in Ivy League play and earns the No. 2 seed in the Ivy League Tournament. The Crimson went 11-3 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom posted a double-double with 14 points (4-of-9 FG, 6-of-8 FT) and 14 rebounds, seven of them offensive.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos added 14 points (6-of-11 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Malia Moreau made the start and finished with six points, six rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau scored 11 points with three steals, and junior Hailey Brennan grabbed 12 rebounds.
- Harvard outrebounded Cornell 25-12 on the offensive glass.
