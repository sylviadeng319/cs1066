One year after watching Princeton celebrate a league title on its home floor, Harvard women's basketball got its turn. The No. 2 seed Crimson (23-7) beat No. 1 seed Princeton, 62-52, on Saturday at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn., to win the Ivy League Tournament and earn the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament.

The Tigers went 13-1 in league play, beat Harvard in both regular-season meetings, and had won five straight games in the series, including a 58-54 win over the Crimson in last year's title game at Jadwin Gymnasium. This time Harvard did not trail over the final three quarters, shot 47.1% from the field, and forced 16 turnovers to win its first Ivy League Tournament title since 2033-34. The win is Harvard's third straight.

"A year ago we sat in that locker room at Princeton and talked about how badly we wanted another chance. This group earned it. Princeton is a great team, and they had our number all season, so to beat them with a championship on the line is really special," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before junior Hailey Brennan buried a mid-range jumper. The teams traded 6-0 runs in a tight opening quarter, and Harvard led 10-9 after 10 minutes.

The Crimson built a 16-9 lead in the second quarter before the Tigers answered with six straight points to pull within one. A 9-2 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by a Juniper Haddad layup, made it 25-17, and the Crimson took a 25-19 lead into halftime.

Callie Winslow, Chloe Rousseau, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed a 7-0 run to open the second half that pushed the lead to 32-19. Princeton answered with a 16-5 run to get within 39-37, but Harvard steadied itself and led 44-38 after three quarters.

The Crimson delivered the decisive blow early in the fourth. A 15-6 spurt stretched the lead to 59-46, and Princeton never got closer than four in the final quarter. When the horn sounded, the Crimson had the trophy and a ticket to the NCAA Tournament.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard wins the Ivy League Tournament and earns the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament. The Crimson improves to 23-7 and has won three straight.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau led Harvard with 15 points (7-of-15 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT) and four steals.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom scored 13 points on 5-of-6 shooting from the field (3-of-4 FT) and grabbed five rebounds.
- Junior Hailey Brennan added 10 points (4-of-12 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT).
- Junior Juniper Haddad went 3-of-3 from the field and 2-of-2 from the line for eight points off the bench.
- Harvard recorded eight steals and held Princeton to 2-of-6 shooting from 3-point range.
