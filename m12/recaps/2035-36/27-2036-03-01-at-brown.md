Harvard women's basketball (20-7, 8-5 Ivy) let a 15-point lead slip away on Saturday in a 62-57 loss to Brown at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

The Bears shot 44.6% from the field, while Harvard was held to 31.2%.

"Give Brown credit. They executed better than we did today," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Brown scored first before junior Juniper Haddad splashed a three from the wing for Harvard's first points. A 6-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Malia Moreau's triple, turned a 1-0 deficit into a 6-1 lead. An 11-4 Brown spurt in the quarter flipped a 6-1 deficit into a 12-10 lead. The Bears led 15-13 after one.

Brown went on a 12-5 run in the quarter, which extended the lead to 29-21. Harvard trailed 29-26 at halftime.

Harvard put together a 24-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 35-26 deficit into a 50-35 lead. Brown countered with a 16-0 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 50-35 deficit into a 51-50 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Brown countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 54-53 deficit into a 60-54 lead. The Bears led 60-56 in the final minutes. With 2:54 left, Ciara Bergstrom added a free throw to make it 60-57. Brown hit a jumper with 7 seconds left to stretch the lead to 62-57. Harvard did not score over the final 2:54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 20-7 overall and 8-5 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Hailey Brennan led Harvard with 13 points (4-of-8 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom recorded a double-double with 12 points (3-of-9 FG, 6-of-7 FT) and 11 rebounds.
