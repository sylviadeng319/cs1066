A night after rallying to win at Penn, Harvard women's basketball nearly pulled off a second comeback in as many days. The Crimson cut a 15-point fourth-quarter deficit to five in the final minute, but Princeton held on for a 66-59 win on Saturday at Jadwin Gymnasium in Princeton, N.J.

The loss was Harvard's second in league play, following a Jan. 26 defeat at Columbia, and it extended Princeton's winning streak in the series to four games after the Tigers beat the Crimson three times last season. The two teams shot nearly identical percentages from the field, but Princeton forced 17 Harvard turnovers, grabbed 15 offensive rebounds, and took 12 more shots.

"I liked the fight at the end, but you can't spot a team like Princeton 15 points on its own floor and expect to get away with it. We turned it over too many times, and they made us pay," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first before junior Hailey Brennan drilled a 3-pointer, and the Crimson ran off nine straight points for a 9-1 lead, capped by a Bria Jefferson free throw. The Tigers answered with a 16-2 run of their own and led 17-11 after the first quarter.

A 9-0 Princeton spurt early in the second quarter made it 28-17, and Harvard trailed 35-24 at halftime.

Jefferson, Callie Winslow, and Quinn Castellanos keyed a 9-0 run early in the third quarter that cut the deficit to 42-35, but Princeton answered with an 8-2 run to go back up 50-37 and led 52-42 entering the fourth.

A 7-0 Tigers run early in the final frame pushed the lead to 62-47. Harvard didn't fold. The Crimson outscored Princeton 12-2 over the next stretch and got within 64-59 with 46 seconds left, but the Tigers added the game's final two points to close it out.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-4 overall and 5-2 in Ivy League play. The teams meet again Feb. 15 at Lavietes Pavilion.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom led Harvard with 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Hailey Brennan scored 11 points (4-of-12 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT) and dished out a team-high five assists.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos added nine points and four assists, and sophomore Bria Jefferson grabbed seven rebounds.
- First-year Avery Ashworth pulled down five rebounds and blocked two shots in 12 minutes off the bench.
- Harvard assisted on 15 of its 22 field goals.
