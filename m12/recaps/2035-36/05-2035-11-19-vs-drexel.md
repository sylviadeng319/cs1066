Harvard women's basketball (5-0) held on for a 60-55 win over Drexel on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fifth straight.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Bria Jefferson opened the scoring when she converted a putback. Harvard opened the game on a 9-0 run. The Dragons went on an 8-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 9-8. Harvard led 18-14 after the first quarter.

Quinn Castellanos, Chloe Rousseau, and Juniper Haddad keyed a 7-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 25-16. The Crimson led 27-23 at the break.

A 13-3 Drexel spurt in the quarter flipped a 33-24 deficit into a 37-36 lead. Harvard led 41-40 heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together an 11-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Rousseau's 3-pointer, which stretched the lead to 56-46. Harvard held a 57-49 lead in the final minutes. The Dragons made a free throw with 1:57 left to cut the deficit to 57-50. Drexel hit a jumper with 1:42 left to cut the deficit to 57-52, but Jefferson answered when she drilled a three to push the lead to 60-52. The Dragons converted a layup with 23 seconds left to cut the deficit to 60-54. Drexel scored with 10 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.
