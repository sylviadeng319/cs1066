Harvard women's basketball (8-0) led from start to finish in a 74-64 win over Bryant on Sunday at Chace Athletic Center in Smithfield, R.I.

The win is Harvard's eighth straight.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Chloe Rousseau buried a 3-pointer. Harvard opened the game on a 24-8 run. The Bulldogs went on a 9-0 run late in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 24-17. The Crimson took a 26-17 lead into the second quarter.

A 6-0 Bryant spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 29-25. Harvard went into the locker room up 34-30.

Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 43-36. Bryant countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that drew level at 45. Harvard took a 53-48 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 10-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Rousseau's jumper, which stretched the lead to 63-51.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-0 overall.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau paced the Crimson with 21 points (8-of-15 FG, 5-of-9 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos contributed 13 points (3-of-7 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 7-of-8 FT), seven rebounds, and four assists.
- Junior Hailey Brennan chipped in 13 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 28 field goals.
- Rousseau's 21 points were a career high.
