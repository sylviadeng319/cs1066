Harvard women's basketball (3-0) never trailed on Saturday, beating Sacred Heart, 88-62, at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 48.1% from the field and held Sacred Heart to 38.1%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pioneers 47-34.

"We challenged ourselves to play to our standard regardless of the opponent, and I thought we did that," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Junior Hailey Brennan got Harvard on the board when she knocked down a corner three with 9:54 left in the opening quarter. Harvard outscored the Pioneers 22-11 in the first quarter. The Crimson took a 22-11 lead into the second quarter.

Sacred Heart countered with an 8-0 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 28-21. Harvard put together a 12-3 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 43-27. The score held there at halftime.

A 16-2 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Brennan's layup, stretched the lead to 63-37. Harvard led 68-42 heading into the fourth.

The Pioneers never got closer than 23 in the fourth quarter. The lead reached 30, allowing Sage Navarro, Fatima Sorensen, and Reagan Cardenas to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-0 overall.
- Junior Juniper Haddad led Harvard with 28 points (11-of-12 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Hailey Brennan finished with 15 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom added 12 points (5-of-8 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Quinn Castellanos (11) also reached double figures.
- Junior Callie Winslow dished out a team-high seven assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 42 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 28 of its 37 field goals.
- Haddad's 28 points were a career high.
