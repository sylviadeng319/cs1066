Down 9 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (12-2) rallied for a 73-68 win over Merrimack on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Warriors 40-28.

## How it Happened

Merrimack scored first before junior Hailey Brennan knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. An 11-2 Merrimack spurt in the quarter broke a 5-all tie and pushed the Warriors ahead 16-7. Harvard trailed 18-14 after the opening frame.

A 17-5 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Avery Ashworth's layup, turned a 22-15 deficit into a 32-27 lead. The Crimson led 36-34 at the break.

Callie Winslow, Bria Jefferson, and Brennan keyed a 17-0 run early in the quarter that broke a 38-all tie and put Harvard up 55-38. Merrimack went on a 10-0 run late in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 55-48. Harvard led 59-50 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 69-54. Harvard held a 71-64 lead in the final minutes. Ciara Bergstrom scored inside with 2:00 left to make it 73-64. The Warriors got a basket with 1:46 left to cut the deficit to 73-66. Merrimack scored with 50 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-2 overall. The Crimson is 5-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau finished with 16 points (5-of-16 FG, 1-of-8 3-PT, 5-of-6 FT) and four assists.

- Sophomore Bria Jefferson chipped in 11 points (4-of-5 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
