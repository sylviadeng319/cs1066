Harvard women's basketball's March run came to an end on Sunday. No. 5 seed North Carolina used a big first-quarter run to pull away from the No. 13 seed Crimson for a 79-59 win in the NCAA Tournament Second Round at Value City Arena in Columbus, Ohio.

Two days after upsetting No. 4 seed Ohio State on the same floor, Harvard (24-8) jumped out to a 5-0 lead before the Tar Heels answered with a 26-6 surge. The Crimson outscored North Carolina 34-33 in the second half but could not climb out of a 46-25 halftime hole. For the second time in three seasons, the Tar Heels ended Harvard's NCAA Tournament run, after top-seeded North Carolina beat the Crimson in the 2034 Sweet 16.

The loss ended the collegiate careers of seniors Ciara Bergstrom, Quinn Castellanos, and Malia Moreau, a class that won three Ivy League Tournament titles and was part of the program's first Sweet 16 team.

"It hurts right now, because this group wanted more. But I told them in the locker room how proud I am of them. They won a championship, they won on one of the biggest stages in the country, and our seniors leave this program in a great place," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Junior Hailey Brennan got Harvard on the board with a pull-up jumper with 7:10 left in the first quarter, and the Crimson led 5-0. North Carolina answered with a 26-6 run that carried it to a 26-11 lead after one quarter.

The Tar Heels kept pushing in the second quarter. An 18-7 run made it 46-22, and Harvard trailed 46-25 at halftime.

Harvard came out swinging after the break. An 18-9 run in the third quarter cut the deficit to 57-43, but North Carolina responded with eight straight points and led 65-44 after three quarters.

The Tar Heels scored seven straight early in the fourth to go up 72-48. The Crimson kept competing to the final buzzer and won the quarter 15-14.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard's season ends in the NCAA Tournament Second Round with a 24-8 record, an Ivy League Tournament championship, and a first-round win over No. 4 seed Ohio State.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau led Harvard with 10 points (4-of-12 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) and two steals.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos scored eight points (2-of-3 FG, 3-of-4 FT) and grabbed six rebounds in her final collegiate game.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom added eight points (4-of-9 FG), and junior Hailey Brennan scored eight with two 3-pointers.
- Sophomore Sage Navarro made two of her three 3-point attempts for six points off the bench, and senior Malia Moreau logged four minutes in her final collegiate game.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points, and the Crimson shot 9-of-24 (37.5%) from 3-point range.
