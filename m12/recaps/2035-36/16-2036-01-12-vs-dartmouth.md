Harvard women's basketball (14-2, 2-0 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Dartmouth, 84-61, at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 35-27. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

Junior Chloe Rousseau opened the scoring when she hit a pull-up jumper. Harvard opened the game on a 17-8 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 17-11.

The Crimson strung together a 15-3 burst late in the quarter, capped by Thea Dalton's three, which stretched the lead to 44-31. Harvard took a 44-33 lead into halftime.

Hailey Brennan, Bria Jefferson, and Sage Navarro keyed a 14-2 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 58-35. A 16-3 Dartmouth spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 61-51. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 19-5 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 80-56. The lead reached 24, allowing Isla Underwood, Cara Valdez, and Malia Moreau to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-2 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 6-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Quinn Castellanos finished with a team-high 15 points (4-of-4 FG, 7-of-8 FT).
- Junior Juniper Haddad finished with 14 points (6-of-12 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom added 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Hailey Brennan hit three 3-pointers.
- Harvard's bench contributed 30 points.
