Harvard women's basketball (15-2, 3-0 Ivy) pulled away from Brown for a 66-52 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fifth straight.

## How it Happened

Senior Ciara Bergstrom opened the scoring when she went 2-for-2 at the line. A 9-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Bergstrom's free throw, turned a 12-9 deficit into an 18-14 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Juniper Haddad's free throws, which stretched the lead to 25-16. Brown went on an 11-4 run in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 29-27. The Crimson led 32-27 at the break.

The Bears countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 32-27 deficit into a 33-32 lead. Quinn Castellanos, Chloe Rousseau, and Hailey Brennan keyed a 17-5 run in the quarter that turned a 33-32 deficit into a 49-38 lead. Harvard took a 49-40 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together a 16-5 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 65-45.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-2 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Chloe Rousseau led the way for Harvard with 16 points (6-of-12 FG, 4-of-7 3-PT), five rebounds, and five steals.
- Senior Ciara Bergstrom finished with 15 points (5-of-10 FG, 5-of-7 FT) and two blocks.
- Junior Hailey Brennan grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard held Brown to 30.6% shooting from the field.
