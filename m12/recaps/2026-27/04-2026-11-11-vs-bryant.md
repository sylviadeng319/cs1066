Harvard women's basketball (3-1) erased a 10-point deficit and came away with a 58-48 win over Bryant on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Bulldogs 38-30.

## How it Happened

Bryant scored first before junior Jada Nkemelu hit a jumper from the elbow for Harvard's first points. The Bulldogs opened the game on a 16-6 run. Bryant carried a 16-8 lead into the second quarter.

The Bulldogs countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 23-13. Kira Bianchi, Nkemelu, and Fatima Quintero keyed a 9-0 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 23-22. The Crimson went into the locker room down 27-22.

The Crimson strung together a 14-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Nkemelu's layup, which turned a 27-22 deficit into a 36-29 lead. Harvard took a 38-35 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Bulldogs got within 44-43 with 4:41 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together an 11-0 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 44-43 deficit into a 54-44 lead.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-1 overall.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis finished with a team-high 17 points (7-of-16 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Addison Garrison chipped in 14 points (5-of-12 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Junior Jada Nkemelu grabbed nine rebounds.
