Harvard women's basketball flew across the country to open the 2026-27 season and came home with a statement win. The Crimson (1-0) outlasted California, 62-57, on Tuesday at Haas Pavilion in Berkeley, Calif., holding off a fourth-quarter charge from the Golden Bears in front of 6,529 fans.

Harvard won it with efficiency and muscle. The Crimson shot 49.0% from the field, held California to 33.9%, and outrebounded the Golden Bears 42-27, more than enough to overcome 24 turnovers on a night when ball security was far from sharp.

"Winning on the road against a team like California is a great way to start. We turned it over way too much, and we'll clean that up, but I loved how we defended and rebounded, and how we kept our poise when they made their run," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

California scored the first basket of the season, but senior Cara Kavanagh answered with a pull-up jumper and Harvard took off from there. A 17-6 spurt turned a 2-0 deficit into a 17-8 lead before the Golden Bears closed the quarter strong to trail 17-13 after 10 minutes.

An 8-1 Harvard run, capped by a bucket from Addison Garrison, put the Crimson up 25-18 in the second quarter. California answered with seven straight points to tie the game at 25, but Harvard regrouped to take a 32-27 lead into halftime.

The Crimson pushed the margin back up early in the third quarter. A 12-4 burst, capped by a Jada Nkemelu putback, made it 46-36, and Harvard carried a 47-39 advantage into the final frame.

California made its move in the fourth, outscoring Harvard 18-15 in the quarter and pulling within 57-54 in the final minutes. Fatima Quintero steadied the Crimson with a pull-up jumper with 55 seconds left for a 59-54 lead. After a California free throw, Nkemelu knocked down a jumper from the elbow to make it 61-55, and Quintero added a free throw with two seconds left to close out the win.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard opens the season 1-0 with a road win over a power-conference opponent.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with 21 points (9-of-11 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), 10 rebounds, and three blocks for a double-double in the season opener.
- Junior Addison Garrison added 10 points (4-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 FT), six rebounds, and four steals.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh scored 10 points and dished out a team-high six assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi pulled down 11 rebounds as Harvard won the battle on the glass 42-27.
- Junior Fatima Quintero and junior Jada Nkemelu each scored eight points and combined for Harvard's final five points of the game.
