Harvard women's basketball won the first chapter of this season's rivalry with Yale, pulling away for a 65-57 victory on Saturday in front of a sold-out crowd at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson (13-3, 2-0 Ivy) weathered a 14-0 Yale run in the third quarter that turned a 17-point lead into a one-possession game, then answered every Bulldogs push down the stretch. The win is Harvard's sixth straight and keeps the Crimson unbeaten in Ivy League play.

"Yale is always going to give us their best shot, and when they made that run in the third, I loved that nobody panicked. We got back to defending, we got the ball inside, and our seniors made big plays," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Chloe Petrakis opened the scoring with a turnaround bank shot, but points were scarce for both teams in the opening 10 minutes. Yale used a 7-0 run to go up 7-3 and carried a 9-8 lead into the second quarter.

The Bulldogs pushed their lead to 16-10 early in the second quarter before Harvard found its rhythm. The Crimson ripped off a 23-6 run, capped by a Petrakis layup, and went into the locker room ahead 33-22 after outscoring Yale 25-13 in the frame.

Petrakis, Kira Bianchi, and Anika Pemberton keyed a 10-0 run early in the third quarter that pushed the margin to 47-30. Yale didn't go away. Harvard still led 49-32 when the Bulldogs answered with 14 unanswered points, and the Crimson's lead was down to 49-46 heading into the fourth.

Harvard regained control in the final frame and led 59-52 inside the last three minutes. Yale hit a three with 2:56 left to cut it to 59-55, but Petrakis answered with a jumper from the elbow. After another Yale basket, Bianchi scored inside to restore a six-point lead at 63-57, and Petrakis hit one more jumper with 34 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-3 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play, extending its winning streak to six games. The Crimson is 7-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with a season-high 27 points (12-of-19 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and grabbed 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Junior Kira Bianchi posted a double-double of her own with 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and a team-high 15 rebounds.
- Junior Fatima Quintero added 13 points (5-of-10 FG, 3-of-4 FT).
- Senior Cara Kavanagh dished out a team-high seven assists.
- Harvard held Yale to 33.8% shooting from the field and won the rebounding battle 44-40.
- The crowd of 2,195 was a sellout at Lavietes Pavilion.
