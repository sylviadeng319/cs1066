Harvard women's basketball fell behind by 14 in the first quarter at Jadwin Gymnasium on Friday, then spent the next 30 minutes rewriting the night. The Crimson (15-5, 4-2 Ivy) rallied for an 84-79 win over Princeton, handing the Tigers their first Ivy League loss of the season.

In an offensive duel between two of the league's best, Harvard shot 53.6% from the field, made nine 3-pointers, and knocked down 15 of its 18 free throws. The win snapped a two-game skid and was Harvard's highest-scoring game of the season.

"We got punched early, and I told our players after the first quarter that we had 30 minutes left to play our game. That's a great Princeton team, and to come in here and win says a lot about this group's toughness," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton came out firing. After Chloe Petrakis answered the Tigers' first basket with two free throws, Princeton ripped off a 19-4 run to take a 23-9 lead. Harvard steadied itself late in the quarter with a 10-4 spurt and trailed 27-19 after one.

The second quarter belonged to the Crimson, who held Princeton to 12 points in the frame. A 21-10 burst capped by a Petrakis basket inside put Harvard back in front at 40-37. The Crimson took a 40-39 lead into halftime.

Princeton pushed back early in the third quarter with a 12-6 run to pull within 52-51. Cara Kavanagh, Petrakis, and Addison Garrison answered with a 9-2 run that made it 61-53, and Harvard carried a 63-55 lead into the fourth.

The Tigers kept coming, scoring 24 points in the final frame, but Harvard had an answer each time. The Crimson led 80-72 when Garrison went 2-for-2 at the line with 1:46 left. Princeton scored five quick points to get within 82-77, but Petrakis scored through contact on the next trip down to push the lead back to seven, and Harvard held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-5 overall and 4-2 in Ivy League play, snapping a two-game losing streak.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis and junior Addison Garrison shared team-high honors with 19 points apiece. Petrakis went 7-of-10 from the field and 5-of-6 at the line with seven rebounds, while Garrison went 6-of-11 from the field, 3-of-6 from 3-point range, and 4-of-4 at the line.
- Junior Fatima Quintero added 17 points (7-of-11 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT), and senior Cara Kavanagh scored 11 points with five assists.
- Sophomore Celeste Laurent handed out four assists in 13 minutes off the bench.
- Four Harvard players reached double figures, and the Crimson's 84 points were its most in a game this season.
- Harvard erased a 14-point first-quarter deficit to win at Jadwin Gymnasium.
