Harvard women's basketball (9-3) pulled away from Rhode Island for a 73-61 victory on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 49.2% from the field and held the Rams to 38.6%.

"There's a lot to build on from tonight. I liked our pace and our poise," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Addison Garrison knocked down a jumper. A 6-0 Rhode Island spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 6-2 lead. Chloe Petrakis, Cara Kavanagh, and Kira Bianchi keyed an 11-5 run early in the quarter that turned a 6-2 deficit into a 13-11 lead. Rhode Island led 18-17 after one.

The Rams went on a 6-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 19-18 deficit into a 24-19 lead. A 22-2 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Anika Pemberton's putback, turned a 24-19 deficit into a 41-26 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 41-29.

The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Kavanagh's bucket, which stretched the lead to 51-33. The Rams countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 51-39. Harvard took a 56-42 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together a 13-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 73-54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-3 overall. The Crimson is 4-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Addison Garrison finished with a team-high 19 points (7-of-10 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), four assists, and three steals.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis recorded a double-double with 18 points (8-of-12 FG, 2-of-2 FT), 11 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh finished with 12 points (5-of-13 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT), eight assists, and three steals.
