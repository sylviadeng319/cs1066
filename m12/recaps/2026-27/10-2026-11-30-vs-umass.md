Harvard women's basketball (7-3) let a 10-point lead slip away on Monday in a 61-60 loss to UMass at Lavietes Pavilion.

## How it Happened

UMass scored first before sophomore Celeste Laurent finished a layup in transition for Harvard's first points. Laurent, Addison Garrison, and Cara Kavanagh keyed a 23-11 run in the quarter that turned a 4-0 deficit into a 23-15 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

UMass countered with a 23-5 run in the quarter that flipped a 27-17 deficit into a 40-32 lead. The score held there at halftime.

UMass went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 47-32. A 14-2 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Chloe Petrakis's free throws, cut the deficit to 49-46. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Minutewomen led 60-58 in the final minutes. UMass made a free throw with 2:27 left to stretch the lead to 61-58, but Anika Pemberton answered when she scored through contact to make it 61-60. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 7-3 overall.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with 15 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Addison Garrison added 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 0-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh chipped in 11 points (4-of-14 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed nine rebounds.
