Harvard women's basketball (14-5, 3-2 Ivy) came up just short on Saturday, falling 70-68 to Brown at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Senior Chloe Petrakis opened the scoring when she scored inside. Harvard opened the game on a 7-0 run. The Bears went on a 21-7 run in the quarter, which flipped a 7-0 deficit into a 21-14 lead. Brown carried a 23-19 lead into the second quarter.

A 6-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Cara Kavanagh's bucket, cut the deficit to 28-27. The Bears countered with a 14-3 run in the quarter that extended the lead to 42-30. Harvard trailed 47-35 at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 17-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Kavanagh's triple, which cut the deficit to 54-52. The Crimson faced a 59-56 deficit going into the final frame.

A 7-0 Brown spurt early in the quarter flipped a 62-59 deficit into a 66-62 lead. Brown led 68-66 in the final minutes. The Bears got a basket with 2:29 left to stretch the lead to 70-66, but Petrakis answered when she went 2-for-2 at the line to pull Harvard within 70-68. Harvard had one last look in the closing seconds but couldn't get it to fall.

## Harvard Highlights
