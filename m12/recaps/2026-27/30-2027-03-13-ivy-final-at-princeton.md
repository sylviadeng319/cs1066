Harvard women's basketball is going dancing. The No. 2 seed Crimson (23-7) knocked off top-seeded Princeton, 50-42, on the Tigers' home floor at Jadwin Gymnasium on Saturday to win the Ivy League Tournament championship and claim the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament.

It was a defensive battle from the opening tip. Harvard held Princeton, which went 13-1 in league play during the regular season, to 26.6% shooting and just 14 points in the first half. A 12-0 run early in the fourth quarter then gave the Crimson a 43-22 cushion that proved to be more than enough. The win is Harvard's sixth straight.

"I'm so happy for these players. Not many people gave us a chance to come in here and beat a team like Princeton on its home floor, but this group believed in each other all week. To cut down the nets with them is something I'll never forget," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first, and neither offense found much rhythm early. Sophomore Jasmine Ramsey got Harvard on the board at the free throw line, and a 9-2 run capped by an Anika Pemberton layup gave the Crimson a 9-5 lead after one quarter.

The second quarter was a grind. The teams combined for 17 points in the frame, and Harvard took a 17-14 lead into halftime.

Harvard's offense came to life in the third quarter. A 12-4 run capped by a Kira Bianchi layup stretched the lead to 31-22 heading into the fourth.

Then the Crimson delivered the knockout. Bianchi, Cara Kavanagh, and Addison Garrison keyed a 12-0 run early in the fourth that pushed Harvard's lead to 43-22. Princeton made a late push, outscoring Harvard 20-7 the rest of the way, but the Tigers never got closer than eight, and the celebration began at the final buzzer.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard wins the Ivy League Tournament and earns the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament. The Crimson improves to 23-7 and has won six straight.
- Junior Addison Garrison led Harvard with 15 points (6-of-14 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), seven rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed a team-high 11 rebounds, and senior Chloe Petrakis added seven points, eight rebounds, and two blocks.
- Sophomore Jasmine Ramsey gave Harvard five points in six minutes off the bench, including a 3-pointer.
- Harvard held Princeton to 17-of-64 shooting from the field and forced 16 turnovers while committing just 10.
- Harvard went 2-1 against Princeton this season.
