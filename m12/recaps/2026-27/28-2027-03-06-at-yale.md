Harvard women's basketball built a 17-point lead at John J. Lee Amphitheater on Saturday, then had to survive a furious Yale rally to close the regular season with a 75-73 win.

The Bulldogs scored the final eight points of the game and 33 in the fourth quarter alone, but the Crimson (21-7, 10-4 Ivy) held on to complete a season sweep of its rival and win its fourth straight. Harvard shot 58.5% from the field, forced 18 turnovers, and heads into the Ivy League Tournament as the No. 2 seed.

"That's a rivalry game, and Yale played with a lot of pride in that fourth quarter. I'm not thrilled with how we finished, but I'm proud that we found a way to win, and now we get to go play for a championship," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored the game's first basket, and then Harvard took over. Chloe Petrakis, Addison Garrison, and Jada Nkemelu keyed a 19-3 run that gave the Crimson a 19-5 lead after the first quarter.

Points were harder to come by in the second quarter. Yale used a 10-3 spurt to cut the deficit to 24-15, and Harvard took a 26-17 lead into halftime.

The Bulldogs opened the third quarter with a 10-2 run to pull within 30-29, but Harvard responded with its best stretch of the night. A 20-4 run pushed the lead to 50-33, and the Crimson led 52-40 entering the fourth.

Yale's offense caught fire in the final frame, but Harvard kept answering. The Crimson still led 73-65 with two minutes to play, and Garrison's mid-range jumper with 1:17 left made it 75-65. Yale scored the next eight points, the last with 43 seconds to play, to make it a one-possession game, but the Harvard defense got the stops it needed in the closing seconds to hold on.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard finishes the regular season 21-7 overall and 10-4 in Ivy League play, earning the No. 2 seed in the Ivy League Tournament.
- Harvard swept the season series with Yale, following a 65-57 win at Lavietes Pavilion on Jan. 9.
- Junior Addison Garrison led Harvard with 17 points (8-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 FT), four assists, and four steals.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh added 15 points (6-of-9 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT) and five assists.
- Sophomore Celeste Laurent scored a season-high 13 points off the bench on 5-of-6 shooting.
- Chloe Petrakis (12) and Kira Bianchi (11) also reached double figures, giving Harvard five double-digit scorers.
- Harvard recorded 11 steals.
