Harvard women's basketball (17-5, 6-2 Ivy) pulled away from Brown for a 71-57 victory on Saturday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

"That was a complete team effort, and that's what we're going to need as the season goes on," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before junior Addison Garrison splashed a three from the wing for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together a 19-1 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 1-0 deficit into a 19-2 lead. Harvard led 21-7 after the first quarter.

An 11-0 Brown spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 21-18. Celeste Laurent, Garrison, and Fatima Quintero keyed a 7-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 28-18. The Crimson led 33-23 at the break.

The Bears went on a 13-3 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 44-38. The Crimson strung together a 12-4 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Quintero's bucket, which stretched the lead to 56-42. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

A 7-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Laurent's three, stretched the lead to 71-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-5 overall and 6-2 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Kira Bianchi finished with 14 points (6-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 FT), 10 rebounds, and two blocks for a double-double.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis chipped in 14 points (4-of-14 FG, 6-of-6 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Addison Garrison finished with 11 points (3-of-11 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- Fatima Quintero (10) also reached double figures.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh came away with five steals.
