Harvard women's basketball (20-7, 9-4 Ivy) held on for a 70-65 win over Columbia on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

The Crimson shot 56.9% from the field and held the Lions to 44.4%. The win is Harvard's third straight.

"Those are the games that build a team. We didn't play our best, but we found a way, and that's growth," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Columbia scored first before senior Anika Pemberton banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. The Lions led 12-10 after one.

Columbia countered with an 8-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 15-14 deficit into a 22-15 lead. A 10-4 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Pemberton's bucket inside, cut the deficit to 26-25. The Crimson went into the locker room down 28-25.

Harvard put together a 16-2 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 28-25 deficit into a 41-30 lead. Columbia went on an 18-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 41-30 deficit into a 48-43 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

The game was tied at 62 in the final minutes. With 2:36 left, Jada Nkemelu scored through contact to put Harvard ahead 64-62. Pemberton finished a post move with 1:53 left to push the lead to 66-62. Columbia answered from beyond the arc with 1:47 left to get within 66-65, but Addison Garrison answered when she buried a 3-pointer to make it 69-65. Garrison made a free throw with 48 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-7 overall and 9-4 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Addison Garrison led the way for Harvard with 19 points (7-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 4-of-10 FT).
- Senior Cara Kavanagh contributed 16 points (6-of-10 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and seven assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi chipped in 12 points (6-of-11 FG) and six rebounds.
