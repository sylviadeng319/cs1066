Columbia made enough plays down the stretch to edge Harvard women's basketball (14-4, 3-1 Ivy), 71-66, on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The loss snapped Harvard's seven-game winning streak.

"We were right there. That's a tough one, but this group will respond," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Senior Cara Kavanagh got Harvard on the board when she hit a pull-up jumper with 9:13 left in the opening quarter. A 12-5 Columbia spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 12-7 lead. The Lions led 16-13 after one.

Harvard put together an 8-2 spurt midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 27-26. The Lions went on a 12-4 run midway through the quarter, which extended the lead to 41-32. The Crimson went into the locker room down 45-39.

Chloe Petrakis, Kira Bianchi, and Jasmine Ramsey keyed a 15-4 run in the quarter that turned a 49-39 deficit into a 54-53 lead. Columbia led 56-54 after three quarters.

The Lions led 67-66 in the final minutes. The Lions converted a layup with 2:34 left to stretch the lead to 69-66. Columbia scored inside with 2:18 left to stretch the lead to 71-66. Harvard did not score over the final 3:17.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 14-4 overall and 3-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led the way for Harvard with 20 points (9-of-14 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), five rebounds, and four assists.
- Junior Fatima Quintero finished with 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh dished out a team-high 10 assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi protected the rim with three blocks.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 27 field goals.
