For 38 minutes, No. 13 seed Harvard women's basketball had No. 4 seed Maryland on the ropes. In the end, the Terrapins found just enough. Maryland scored eight straight points in the final two minutes to pull out a 65-62 win over the Crimson in the NCAA Tournament First Round on Friday at Xfinity Center in College Park, Md.

Harvard (23-8) erased a 10-point third-quarter deficit with a 23-11 run and led 60-57 inside the final three minutes in front of a crowd of 9,531. But the Terrapins answered, and a late Jasmine Ramsey putback was as close as the Crimson would get. The loss ends a six-game winning streak and the collegiate careers of seniors Cara Kavanagh, Wren Okafor, Anika Pemberton, and Chloe Petrakis.

"I told our players in the locker room that one game doesn't define this season. They won an Ivy League championship, they went toe-to-toe with one of the best teams in the country, and our seniors leave this program better than they found it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Junior Addison Garrison opened the scoring with a jumper, and Harvard led 7-3 before Maryland answered with a 6-0 run. The Terrapins led 14-12 after one quarter.

Down 16-12 in the second quarter, the Crimson went on a 10-1 run capped by a Fatima Quintero layup to go up 22-17. Maryland responded with a 12-3 spurt and took a 29-25 lead into halftime.

Maryland scored the first six points of the second half to go up 35-25, but Harvard's response was one of its best stretches of the season. Garrison, Jada Nkemelu, and Petrakis keyed a 23-11 run that gave the Crimson a 48-46 lead entering the fourth quarter.

Harvard held a 60-57 lead inside the final three minutes. Maryland scored inside with 2:00 left and hit a go-ahead jumper moments later. A Terrapins layup with 1:35 left made it 63-60, and two free throws with 51 seconds to go pushed the lead to five. Ramsey's putback cut it to 65-62, and Harvard had one final look to tie in the closing seconds, but the shot wouldn't fall.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard's season ends in the NCAA Tournament First Round with a 23-8 record and an Ivy League Tournament championship.
- Junior Fatima Quintero led Harvard with a season-high 22 points (8-of-12 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- Junior Kira Bianchi added 12 points (5-of-10 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds, and junior Addison Garrison scored 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT).
- Senior Cara Kavanagh dished out a team-high eight assists in her final collegiate game.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis finished her career with eight points, seven rebounds, and two blocks.
- Harvard outshot Maryland from the field, 42.6% to 39.3%, but the Terrapins made all 11 of their free throws.
