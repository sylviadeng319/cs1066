Harvard women's basketball and Princeton needed 50 minutes to settle Saturday's rematch at Lavietes Pavilion, and the Tigers finally pulled away in the second overtime for a 75-71 win.

The Crimson (17-7, 6-4 Ivy) led by 12 in the first half, and Addison Garrison hit two 3-pointers in the final 2:21 of regulation to keep Harvard alive. But the Crimson shot 29.8% from the field over five periods, and Princeton made enough plays in the second extra session to split the season series. The loss was Harvard's second in as many nights, following Friday's 60-59 defeat against Penn.

"That was a heavyweight fight. Both teams left everything out there. It's going to hurt tonight, but there's a lot of basketball left, and this group is going to be better for having played in a game like this," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Senior Cara Kavanagh opened the scoring with two free throws, and Harvard scored the first seven points of the game, capped by a Kira Bianchi putback. Princeton answered with a 15-6 run to go in front 15-13, and the teams were tied at 15 after one quarter.

The Crimson took control in the second quarter. Kavanagh, Chloe Petrakis, and Garrison keyed a 14-2 run that broke a 17-all tie and pushed Harvard ahead 31-19. The Tigers closed the half on a 9-3 spurt to trail 34-28 at the break.

Princeton flipped the game in the third quarter. Trailing 39-28, the Tigers went on an 18-3 run to take a 46-42 lead, and they led 49-45 entering the fourth.

Princeton still led 57-54 in the final three minutes when Garrison knocked down a corner three with 2:21 left to tie it. The Tigers scored inside to go back ahead 59-57, and Jada Nkemelu made one of two free throws to make it 59-58. With 45 seconds left, Garrison drilled another three for a 61-59 Harvard lead, but Princeton answered with a layup with 28 seconds to go, and neither team scored again in regulation.

Garrison's free throw opened the scoring in the first overtime, and the teams traded baskets the rest of the way before finishing the period tied at 66. Princeton scored first in the second overtime and led 73-70 late in the period. Two Tigers free throws with 55 seconds left made it a two-possession game, and a Petrakis free throw only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-7 overall and 6-4 in Ivy League play. The teams split the regular-season series after Harvard won 84-79 at Princeton on Jan. 29.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh led Harvard with a season-high 20 points (6-of-16 FG, 6-of-13 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and added seven assists.
- Junior Addison Garrison scored 18 points (6-of-21 FG, 4-of-13 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), including two 3-pointers in the final 2:21 of regulation.
- Junior Jada Nkemelu provided a spark off the bench with 11 points (4-of-7 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Senior Chloe Petrakis grabbed 13 rebounds, seven of them on the offensive end, and junior Fatima Quintero added 11 rebounds while playing a team-high 40 minutes.
- Harvard made 13 3-pointers and finished with 54 rebounds, including 19 on the offensive glass.
