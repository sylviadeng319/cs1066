Harvard women's basketball (7-2) picked up a 60-48 win over Siena on Saturday at Alumni Recreation Center in Loudonville, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

## How it Happened

The Saints scored first before junior Jada Nkemelu drilled a three for Harvard's first points. Nkemelu, Chloe Petrakis, and Kira Bianchi keyed a 16-9 run in the quarter that turned a 4-0 deficit into a 16-13 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 14-1 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Bianchi's layup, stretched the lead to 38-24. Harvard took a 38-26 lead into halftime.

The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Addison Garrison's layup, which stretched the lead to 46-30. Harvard led 46-34 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 54-34. Harvard kept the Saints at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below six.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-2 overall.
- Junior Addison Garrison paced the Crimson with 15 points (7-of-13 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT), four assists, and three steals.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis recorded a double-double with 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and 12 rebounds.
- Junior Kira Bianchi finished with 13 points (6-of-9 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and nine rebounds.
