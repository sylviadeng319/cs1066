Harvard women's basketball sent its seniors out in style. On Senior Day at Lavietes Pavilion, the Crimson (18-7, 7-4 Ivy) rolled past Dartmouth, 84-56, on Saturday, building a lead as large as 35 behind a big day from its four-player senior class.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Cara Kavanagh, Wren Okafor, Anika Pemberton, and Chloe Petrakis, and Carrie Moore put all four in the starting lineup. They helped Harvard build a 50-32 halftime lead, and the Crimson shot 57.7% from the field on the day. The win snapped a two-game skid that included a double-overtime loss to Princeton a week earlier.

"This senior class has meant so much to our program, on the court and off it. To see all four of them out there together at the start, and to see the way the whole team played for them, that's what today was about," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Dartmouth scored first and led 8-6 in the first quarter before Harvard found its footing. The Crimson closed the frame on a 16-7 run to lead 22-15 after 10 minutes.

Harvard poured in 28 points in the second quarter, getting contributions up and down the lineup, and took a 50-32 lead into halftime.

Petrakis, Kavanagh, and Kira Bianchi keyed a 9-0 run early in the third quarter that pushed the lead to 62-35, and Harvard led 67-42 entering the fourth.

A 10-0 run early in the fourth, capped by a Petrakis free throw, stretched the margin to 77-42. With the game well in hand, every player on Harvard's roster saw the floor.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-7 overall and 7-4 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-5 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh led Harvard with 15 points (3-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 6-of-7 FT) and four assists in her final regular-season home game.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis added 12 points (5-of-8 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds, and senior Anika Pemberton scored 11 points on 4-of-6 shooting.
- Senior Wren Okafor made her first start of the season and dished out a team-high seven assists.
- Junior Addison Garrison scored 12 points, and junior Kira Bianchi went a perfect 4-of-4 from the field for 10 points off the bench.
- Five Harvard players reached double figures, the bench contributed 30 points, and all 15 players on the roster saw action.
