Harvard women's basketball (11-3) picked up a 74-56 win over Fordham on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight. The Crimson shot 46.7% from the field and held Fordham to 33.8%.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Celeste Laurent got Harvard on the board when she knocked down a jumper with 9:09 left in the opening quarter. A 25-9 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Brielle Mendoza's putback, turned a 3-2 deficit into a 27-12 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 19-8 burst in the quarter, capped by Chloe Petrakis's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 46-20. A 9-0 Fordham spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 46-29. It stayed that way at halftime.

The Rams went on a 6-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 51-37. Harvard led 61-43 heading into the fourth.

Laurent, Fatima Quintero, and Kira Bianchi keyed a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 69-50. The lead reached 26, allowing Wren Okafor, Isla Marchetti, and Juniper Pryor to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-3 overall. The Crimson is 6-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with 19 points (8-of-11 FG, 3-of-4 FT) and two blocks.
- Junior Addison Garrison contributed 17 points (6-of-12 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Fatima Quintero finished with 13 points (3-of-5 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 6-of-7 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed 12 rebounds.
