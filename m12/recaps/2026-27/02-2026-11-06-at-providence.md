Harvard women's basketball (1-1) let an 11-point lead slip away on Friday in a 64-60 loss to Providence at Alumni Hall in Providence, R.I.

Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Friars 51-39.

## How it Happened

Junior Fatima Quintero opened the scoring when she went 2-for-2 at the line. Harvard opened the game on a 7-0 run. The Friars went on an 18-3 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 7-0 deficit into an 18-10 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

Chloe Petrakis and Cara Kavanagh keyed a 10-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 21-13 deficit into a 23-21 lead. A 9-0 Providence spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 23-21 deficit into a 30-23 lead. Harvard trailed 30-27 at halftime.

A 21-8 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Addison Garrison's free throws, turned a 30-27 deficit into a 48-38 lead. Harvard led 48-41 heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 60-56 lead in the final minutes. The Friars hit a jumper with 2:43 left to get within 60-58. Providence hit a pair at the line with 1:16 left to tie it at 60. Providence scored inside with 55 seconds left to go in front 62-60. The Friars got a basket with 32 seconds left to stretch the lead to 64-60. Harvard did not score over the final 4:13.
