No. 2 seed Harvard women's basketball is one win away from the NCAA Tournament. The Crimson (22-7) beat No. 3 seed Brown, 74-67, in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals on Friday at Jadwin Gymnasium in Princeton, N.J., winning the rubber match of a season series the teams had split.

Harvard had to climb out of a 13-point second-quarter hole to do it. The Crimson closed the first half with 13 straight points to tie the game at 36, then pulled away in the fourth quarter, making 18 of its 21 free throws on the night. The win is Harvard's fifth straight.

"We were down 13 and we never blinked. That's been this team all year. Brown is a terrific team and they made us earn every bit of it, and now we get a chance to play for a championship tomorrow," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Senior Cara Kavanagh got Harvard on the board with a 3-pointer, but Brown used a 7-0 run to take a 10-4 lead. Kavanagh capped a 9-0 answer with another three to put the Crimson ahead 19-16, and Harvard led 19-18 after one quarter.

The Bears took control early in the second quarter with an 18-2 run that turned a 21-18 deficit into a 36-23 lead. Harvard responded with 13 straight points of its own, capped by a Chloe Petrakis layup, and the game was tied at 36 at halftime.

Kavanagh, Jada Nkemelu, and Anika Pemberton keyed a 12-1 run early in the third quarter that put Harvard up 48-37. Brown answered with a 13-4 burst to get within 52-50, and the Crimson carried a 54-50 lead into the fourth.

The Bears pulled within 54-53 with 8:53 left, but Harvard took over from there. A 16-4 run stretched the lead to 70-57, and the Crimson closed out the win at the free throw line.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game and improves to 22-7 on the season.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with 16 points (4-of-9 FG, 8-of-8 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh hit four 3-pointers on her way to 14 points (5-of-10 FG, 4-of-9 3-PT) and added four assists.
- Junior Addison Garrison scored 11 points (3-of-9 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT) with three assists and three steals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points, led by nine from sophomore Celeste Laurent and eight from junior Jada Nkemelu.
- Harvard made 18 of its 21 free throws.
