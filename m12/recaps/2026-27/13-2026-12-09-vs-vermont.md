Harvard women's basketball (10-3) pulled away from Vermont for a 67-54 victory on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Catamounts 35-26.

## How it Happened

Vermont scored first before junior Addison Garrison dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. A 10-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Jada Nkemelu's 3-pointer, turned a 1-0 deficit into a 10-3 lead. A 10-0 Vermont spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 10-3 deficit into a 13-10 lead. The Crimson took a 14-13 lead into the second quarter.

Garrison, Chloe Petrakis, and Nkemelu keyed an 18-6 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 32-19. The score held there at halftime.

Vermont countered with an 11-2 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 40-32. The Crimson carried a 44-34 advantage into the final frame.

Vermont got within 44-all with 7:09 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 23-4 spurt in the quarter that broke a 44-all tie and put Harvard up 67-48.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-3 overall. The Crimson is 5-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Addison Garrison finished with a team-high 21 points (9-of-14 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), six rebounds, four assists, and seven steals.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis chipped in 15 points (6-of-10 FG, 3-of-3 FT).
- Junior Jada Nkemelu hit three 3-pointers off the bench.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed nine rebounds.
- The 21 points were a season high for Garrison.
