Harvard women's basketball (6-2) erased a 16-point deficit and came away with a 59-56 win over Sacred Heart on Wednesday at William H. Pitt Center in Fairfield, Conn.

"We didn't flinch. At halftime we talked about getting back to who we are, and our players responded," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Pioneers scored first before first-year Isla Marchetti splashed a three from the wing for Harvard's first points. Sacred Heart opened the game on a 14-3 run. Sacred Heart carried a 16-8 lead into the second quarter.

Kira Bianchi, Fatima Quintero, and Chloe Petrakis keyed a 6-0 run early in the quarter that cut the deficit to 16-14. A 16-2 Sacred Heart spurt midway through the quarter extended the lead to 36-20. Harvard trailed 38-24 at halftime.

Harvard put together a 19-10 spurt in the quarter that cut the deficit to 48-43. The score held there heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 12-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Bianchi's bucket inside, which turned a 48-43 deficit into a 55-51 lead. The game was tied at 55 in the final minutes. The Pioneers made a free throw with 1:32 left to go in front 56-55, but Jada Nkemelu answered when she hit a jumper from the elbow to put Harvard ahead 57-56. Addison Garrison finished a layup in transition with 34 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-2 overall.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis paced the Crimson with 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Fatima Quintero came away with four steals.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed 11 rebounds.
- Junior Addison Garrison came away with four steals.
