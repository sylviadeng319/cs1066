Harvard women's basketball (19-7, 8-4 Ivy) picked up a 69-61 win over Cornell on Friday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before junior Fatima Quintero knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 9-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Chloe Petrakis's bucket inside, which turned a 5-0 deficit into a 9-8 lead. The Big Red carried a 20-17 lead into the second quarter.

A 14-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Kira Bianchi's layup, turned a 24-20 deficit into a 34-30 lead. Harvard took a 34-33 lead into halftime.

Harvard put together a 16-8 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 50-41. Harvard led 50-43 heading into the fourth.

Bianchi and Addison Garrison keyed a 6-0 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 56-43. Harvard kept Cornell at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below five.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-7 overall and 8-4 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh finished with a team-high 15 points (5-of-15 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Chloe Petrakis contributed 14 points (6-of-13 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Fatima Quintero added 12 points (5-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT), seven rebounds, six assists, and three steals.
- Junior Kira Bianchi recorded a double-double with 11 points (3-of-10 FG, 5-of-7 FT), 10 rebounds, and four blocks.
- Addison Garrison (10) also reached double figures.
- Junior Jada Nkemelu grabbed nine rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 27 field goals.
