Harvard women's basketball (12-3, 1-0 Ivy) cruised to a 75-35 victory over Dartmouth on Saturday at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 51-38. Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers.

## How it Happened

Junior Fatima Quintero opened the scoring when she scored on a driving layup. Harvard opened the game on a 12-3 run. The Big Green countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 12-9. That was the score after one quarter.

A 15-2 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Celeste Laurent's bucket, turned a 15-13 deficit into a 28-17 lead. The score held there at halftime.

Harvard put together a 25-4 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 53-21. The Crimson carried a 55-23 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 15-5 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Chloe Petrakis's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 75-35. With the lead as large as 40, Juniper Pryor, Niamh Hollis, and Wren Okafor saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-3 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Junior Fatima Quintero finished with 15 points (5-of-14 FG, 4-of-10 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), six rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Addison Garrison contributed 15 points (6-of-15 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh added 15 points (6-of-17 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), six assists, and five steals.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis recorded a double-double with 11 points (4-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and 12 rebounds.
- Harvard held Dartmouth to 24.0% shooting from the field.
- Harvard's 75 points were its most in a game this season.
