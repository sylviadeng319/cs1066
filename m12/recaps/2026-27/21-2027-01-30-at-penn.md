Down 14 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (16-5, 5-2 Ivy) rallied for a 58-53 win over Penn on Saturday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Kira Bianchi hit a pair of free throws. The Quakers went on a 15-3 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 4-0 deficit into a 15-7 lead. Harvard trailed 15-8 after the opening frame.

A 9-3 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Cara Kavanagh's three, cut the deficit to 18-17. Penn countered with a 15-2 run midway through the quarter that extended the lead to 33-19. The Quakers took a 35-26 lead into the break.

The Crimson strung together an 18-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Kavanagh's bucket, which turned a 35-26 deficit into a 44-37 lead. The Crimson carried a 48-43 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard held a 58-48 lead in the final minutes. The Quakers scored inside with 2:15 left to cut the deficit to 58-50. Penn made a free throw with 1:08 left to cut the deficit to 58-51. The Quakers scored with 1:01 left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-5 overall and 5-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh led Harvard with 15 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT) and five assists.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis recorded a double-double with 14 points (6-of-13 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), 10 rebounds, and two blocks.
- Junior Addison Garrison added 12 points (4-of-10 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT).
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard held Penn to 29.2% shooting from the field.
