Behind a strong third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (14-3, 3-0 Ivy) beat Cornell, 80-62, on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's seventh straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 33-24.

## How it Happened

The Big Red scored first before junior Jada Nkemelu banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. An 11-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Addison Garrison's triple, turned a 4-0 deficit into an 11-6 lead. The Crimson took a 21-16 lead into the second quarter.

Harvard put together a 15-4 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 36-23. The Crimson led 38-28 at the break.

Celeste Laurent, Anika Pemberton, and Chloe Petrakis keyed a 22-10 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 60-38. Harvard led 60-44 heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together an 18-8 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Kira Bianchi's layup, which stretched the lead to 80-62. The lead reached 22, allowing Isla Marchetti, Lauren Wakefield, and Brielle Mendoza to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-3 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis finished with a team-high 17 points (7-of-9 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Jada Nkemelu contributed 13 points (5-of-6 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT).
- Senior Anika Pemberton added 10 points (5-of-7 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT).
- Harvard's bench contributed 38 points.
- Harvard's 80 points were its most in a game this season.
