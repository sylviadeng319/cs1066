Behind a strong third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (4-1) beat Delaware, 64-47, on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Blue Hens 59-40.

## How it Happened

The Blue Hens scored first before junior Addison Garrison added a free throw for Harvard's first points. A 9-3 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Jada Nkemelu's bucket inside, turned a 3-0 deficit into a 9-6 lead. Harvard trailed 10-9 after the opening frame.

Garrison, Chloe Petrakis, and Cara Kavanagh keyed a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that turned a 19-15 deficit into a 22-19 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 26-25.

Harvard put together a 19-3 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 45-28. The Crimson carried a 48-35 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Petrakis's layup, which stretched the lead to 58-37.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-1 overall.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis finished with a team-high 15 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Anika Pemberton added 11 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Cara Kavanagh chipped in 11 points (4-of-12 FG, 1-of-8 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Addison Garrison recorded a double-double with 10 points (4-of-13 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), 11 rebounds, and four assists.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed 12 rebounds.
- Harvard held Delaware to 24.3% shooting from the field.
