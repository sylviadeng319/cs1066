Down 10 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (8-3) rallied for a 63-62 win over Boston College on Wednesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers.

"Our defense turned the game. Once we started getting stops, everything opened up," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Eagles scored first before junior Kira Bianchi converted a putback for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 7-4 deficit into a 10-7 lead. Harvard trailed 16-15 after the opening frame.

Boston College countered with a 17-3 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 22-18 deficit into a 35-25 lead. The Eagles took a 36-29 lead into the break.

The Crimson strung together a 22-6 burst in the quarter, capped by Cara Kavanagh's jumper, which turned a 36-29 deficit into a 51-42 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 62-59 lead in the final minutes. With 2:18 left, Bianchi made a free throw to make it 63-59. The Eagles converted a layup with 24 seconds left to get within 63-61. The Eagles scored with 24 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-3 overall. The Crimson is 3-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Addison Garrison led the way for Harvard with 18 points (7-of-19 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Senior Chloe Petrakis contributed 11 points (4-of-6 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Fatima Quintero added 11 points (3-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
