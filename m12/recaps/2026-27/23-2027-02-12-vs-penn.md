Harvard women's basketball (17-6, 6-3 Ivy) let a 14-point lead slip away on Friday in a 60-59 loss to Penn at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Quakers 43-34.

## How it Happened

Junior Addison Garrison opened the scoring when she buried a 3-pointer. Harvard opened the game on a 15-5 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 17-7.

A 6-0 Penn spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 19-13. Chloe Petrakis, Garrison, and Kira Bianchi keyed an 8-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 34-20. Harvard took a 34-22 lead into halftime.

The third quarter was a back-and-forth affair. Harvard led 49-39 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 58-45. Harvard held a 58-53 lead in the final minutes. With 1:33 left, Fatima Quintero made a free throw to make it 59-53. Penn converted a layup with 1:09 left to get within 59-55. The Quakers hit a three with 53 seconds left to get within 59-58. The Quakers hit a jumper with 19 seconds left to go in front 60-59. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-6 overall and 6-3 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-4 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led the way for Harvard with 14 points (6-of-12 FG, 2-of-3 FT) and 10 rebounds for a double-double.
- Junior Addison Garrison contributed 13 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT), nine rebounds, and four assists.
- Junior Fatima Quintero came away with four steals.
- Junior Kira Bianchi grabbed nine rebounds.
