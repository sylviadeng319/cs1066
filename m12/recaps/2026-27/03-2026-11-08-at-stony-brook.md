Harvard women's basketball (2-1) led from start to finish in a 71-56 win over Stony Brook on Sunday at Stony Brook Arena in Stony Brook, N.Y.

Harvard's defense forced 23 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Junior Kira Bianchi got Harvard on the board when she made a free throw with 9:33 left in the opening quarter. The Crimson strung together a 12-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Jada Nkemelu's putback, which broke a 7-all tie and put Harvard up 19-8. Harvard led 21-13 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 13-6 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 35-21. The Crimson led 35-26 at the break.

Fatima Quintero, Nkemelu, and Addison Garrison keyed a 14-5 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 49-31. The Crimson carried a 53-39 advantage into the final frame.

A 9-1 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Nkemelu's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 67-51.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-1 overall.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with 17 points (7-of-12 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), eight rebounds, and three steals.
- Junior Kira Bianchi contributed 15 points (5-of-10 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 5-of-8 FT).
- Senior Cara Kavanagh finished with 13 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-3 FT).
- Jada Nkemelu (10) also reached double figures.
