Down 13 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (5-2) rallied for a 74-72 win over Penn State on Monday at Bryce Jordan Center in University Park, Pa.

"That's a resilient group in that locker room. Being down didn't change our belief one bit," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Lady Lions scored first before junior Addison Garrison scored on a driving layup for Harvard's first points. Penn State opened the game on a 19-6 run. The Crimson strung together a 14-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Fatima Quintero's free throws, which turned a 19-6 deficit into a 20-19 lead. The Lady Lions led 21-20 after one.

A 23-11 Penn State spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 44-31. The Lady Lions took a 46-38 lead into the break.

Harvard put together a 17-3 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 47-38 deficit into a 55-50 lead. Penn State went on a 9-0 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 55-50 deficit into a 59-55 lead. The Crimson faced a 59-58 deficit going into the final frame.

Penn State led 68-67 in the final minutes. Penn State converted a layup with 2:38 left to stretch the lead to 70-67. The Lady Lions hit a jumper with 1:48 left to stretch the lead to 72-67, but Kira Bianchi answered when she converted a putback to pull Harvard within 72-69. Jada Nkemelu banked in a turnaround with 1:17 left to make it 72-71. Garrison splashed a three from the wing with 58 seconds left to put Harvard ahead 74-72, and the Crimson held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-2 overall.
- Senior Chloe Petrakis led Harvard with 17 points (6-of-12 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), eight rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Addison Garrison added 15 points (5-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT).
- Junior Fatima Quintero finished with 15 points (4-of-13 FG, 1-of-8 3-PT, 6-of-6 FT).
- Kira Bianchi (12) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's 74 points were its most in a game this season.
