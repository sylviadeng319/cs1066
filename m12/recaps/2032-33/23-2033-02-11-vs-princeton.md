Four weeks after edging Princeton by a point at Jadwin Gymnasium, Harvard women's basketball did it again, and this time it took an extra period. Junior Natalie Olsen finished a layup in transition with four seconds left in overtime to give the Crimson (22-1, 9-0 Ivy) a 74-73 win over the Tigers on Friday in front of 2,195 fans at Lavietes Pavilion.

The victory completed a regular-season sweep of Princeton and pushed Harvard's winning streak to 22 games. The Tigers won the battle on the boards 38-30, but the Crimson countered with 10 steals and shot 49.2% from the field in a game that featured lead changes in every quarter.

"Two games against Princeton, two one-point games. That's what this rivalry is. I'm proud of how we kept answering, and Natalie made a big-time play in a big moment," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton used an 11-2 run to turn a 4-3 deficit into a 14-6 lead early. Harvard answered with eight straight points, capped by an Olsen 3-pointer, and the teams were tied at 16 after one quarter.

The Crimson used an 8-0 spurt to go up 28-22 in the second quarter, but Princeton closed the half with nine straight points and led 33-32 at the break. The Tigers struck again early in the third with a 10-0 run that turned a 37-33 deficit into a 43-37 lead, and they took a 45-42 edge into the fourth.

Down 53-46, Harvard ripped off 10 unanswered points, capped by a Bella Chen 3-pointer, to go ahead 56-53. The final minutes went back and forth. Tiana Adeyemi's pull-up jumper with 2:33 left tied it at 58, and Esme Ellison scored inside twice, the second time through contact with 53 seconds remaining, for a 62-60 lead. Princeton tied it with a basket at the 30-second mark, and the game went to overtime at 62-all.

Princeton scored first in the extra period and led 73-72 in the final seconds. Harvard pushed the ball up the floor, and Olsen finished at the rim with four seconds to go, and the Crimson held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 22-1 overall and 9-0 in Ivy League play, extending its winning streak to 22 games. The Crimson is 10-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Harvard swept the regular-season series with Princeton, following a 65-64 win at Jadwin Gymnasium on Jan. 15.
- Senior Esme Ellison led Harvard with 17 points on 8-of-12 shooting.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira scored 13 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and handed out a team-high six assists.
- Sophomore Bella Chen filled the stat sheet with 11 points, six rebounds, four assists, and five steals.
- Junior Natalie Olsen scored eight points off the bench on 3-of-4 shooting, including the game-winning layup.
- Jasmine Danforth (11) and Jordan Delgado (10) also reached double figures.
