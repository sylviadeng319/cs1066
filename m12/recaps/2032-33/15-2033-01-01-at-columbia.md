Behind a strong first quarter, Harvard women's basketball (14-1, 1-0 Ivy) beat Columbia, 74-60, on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Lions 34-26. The Crimson shot 51.0% from the field and held the Lions to 41.1%.

"That was a complete team effort, and that's what we're going to need as the season goes on," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Senior Chloe Ferreira opened the scoring when she hit a pair of free throws. Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 3-2 deficit into an 11-3 lead. Harvard led 17-9 after the first quarter.

A 25-6 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's free throws, stretched the lead to 42-15. The Lions went on an 18-7 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 49-33. That was the score at halftime.

The Lions countered with a 13-3 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 59-48. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Ferreira's layup, which stretched the lead to 65-48. Harvard kept the Lions at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 11.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-1 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira finished with a team-high 19 points (7-of-10 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Jordan Delgado finished with 16 points (6-of-13 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Esme Ellison contributed 12 points (3-of-3 FG, 6-of-6 FT) and two blocks.
- Junior Jasmine Danforth grabbed 11 rebounds.
- Senior Tiana Adeyemi came away with four steals.
