Down 10 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (21-1, 8-0 Ivy) rallied for a 75-69 win over Cornell on Saturday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's 21th straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 45-31.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before sophomore Lainey Rinaldi hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. The Big Red carried a 17-14 lead into the second quarter.

A 7-0 Cornell spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 24-14. Esme Ellison, Jordan Delgado, and Chloe Ferreira keyed a 9-0 run early in the quarter that cut the deficit to 24-23. Cornell took a 41-35 lead into the break.

Cornell went on an 8-2 run midway through the quarter, which extended the lead to 49-42. The Crimson faced a 58-51 deficit going into the final frame.

The game was tied at 67 in the final minutes. Jasmine Danforth banked in a turnaround with 2:06 left to give the Crimson a 69-67 lead. Ellison scored inside with 2:01 left to push the lead to 71-67. Cornell hit a jumper with 1:56 left to get within 71-69, but Delgado answered when she finished a layup in transition to push the lead to 73-69. Tiana Adeyemi knocked down a jumper with 7 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 21-1 overall and 8-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Jordan Delgado finished with a team-high 19 points (6-of-13 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and six assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 16 points (8-of-17 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT) and 14 rebounds.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira chipped in 12 points (5-of-14 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT), seven rebounds, three assists, and two blocks.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 29 field goals.
