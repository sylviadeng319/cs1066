Six days after Columbia spoiled Senior Day in Cambridge, top-seeded Harvard women's basketball settled the score. The Crimson (26-3) beat No. 4 seed Columbia, 71-66, in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals on Friday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I., winning the rubber match of a season series the teams had split.

Harvard's seniors carried the load. Esme Ellison, Chloe Ferreira, Jordan Delgado, and Tiana Adeyemi supplied most of the Crimson's offense, and three of them hit big shots in the final 94 seconds after Columbia had grabbed a fourth-quarter lead. Harvard shot 50.9% from the field and assisted on 19 of its 27 baskets.

"We didn't like how last Saturday felt, and our players came in here with a purpose. Columbia is a terrific team, and when they made their run in the fourth, our seniors wanted the ball. Now we get to play for a championship tomorrow," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Delgado opened the scoring with a 3-pointer, and Harvard jumped out to an 18-9 lead. Columbia answered with seven straight points to get within 18-16, and the Crimson led 20-18 after one quarter.

The Lions went in front with a 9-0 run early in the second quarter for a 27-21 lead. Harvard responded with a 13-1 burst, capped by a Natalie Olsen layup, to go up 34-28, and took a 34-30 lead into halftime.

Columbia tied it at 34 to start the third quarter before the Crimson ran off 11 straight points for a 45-34 lead. The Lions chipped back to 48-46 with a 12-3 run, and Harvard led 53-48 entering the fourth.

Columbia scored nine unanswered points early in the fourth to take a 57-55 lead. Harvard had regained a 63-61 edge in the closing minutes when Ferreira drilled a 3-pointer from the wing with 1:34 left for a 66-61 lead. Columbia hit a three of its own 10 seconds later, but Adeyemi answered with a corner three to make it 69-64. Ellison's jumper from the elbow with 31 seconds left put the game out of reach.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game and improves to 26-3. The Crimson went 2-1 against Columbia this season.
- Senior Esme Ellison led Harvard with 21 points (9-of-14 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira scored 17 points (6-of-10 FG, 4-of-8 3-PT) and handed out five assists.
- Senior Jordan Delgado added 12 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Harvard's four seniors combined for 54 of the team's 71 points, and senior Tiana Adeyemi answered a Columbia 3-pointer in the final 1:24 with a corner three that pushed the lead back to 69-64.
- Harvard made nine 3-pointers and outrebounded Columbia 32-26.
