Harvard women's basketball (18-1, 5-0 Ivy) pulled away from Dartmouth for a 67-53 victory on Saturday at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

The win is Harvard's 18th straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 38-30.

"That was a complete team effort, and that's what we're going to need as the season goes on," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Junior Jasmine Danforth got Harvard on the board when she added a free throw with 9:35 left in the opening quarter. Bella Chen, Esme Ellison, and Jordan Delgado keyed a 13-2 run early in the quarter that turned a 4-1 deficit into a 14-6 lead. Harvard led 18-14 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 19-6 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 37-20. A 6-0 Dartmouth spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 37-26. Harvard went into the locker room up 39-28.

The Crimson strung together a 9-1 burst early in the quarter, capped by Tiana Adeyemi's free throw, which stretched the lead to 48-34. Dartmouth went on a 9-1 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 49-43. The score held there heading into the fourth.

An 18-8 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Adeyemi's free throws, stretched the lead to 67-53.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-1 overall and 5-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira finished with a team-high 15 points (4-of-13 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Jordan Delgado chipped in 13 points (6-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) and four assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison grabbed 12 rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 18 of its 23 field goals.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded six steals.
