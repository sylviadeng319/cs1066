The Crimson made quick work of Merrimack on Wednesday, as Harvard women's basketball (8-1) built a lead as large as 38 on the way to a 79-43 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's eighth straight. Harvard's defense forced 21 turnovers.

## How it Happened

The Warriors scored first before senior Tiana Adeyemi buried a mid-range jumper for Harvard's first points. Merrimack went on a 9-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 6-5 deficit into a 14-8 lead. The Crimson strung together a 7-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's bucket inside, which evened the score at 15. The Warriors carried a 19-18 lead into the second quarter.

Bella Chen, Adeyemi, and Simone Zamora keyed a 20-7 run in the quarter that turned a 19-18 deficit into a 38-26 lead. The score held there at halftime.

A 24-10 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Adeyemi's layup, stretched the lead to 62-36. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 17-5 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 79-41. The lead reached 38, allowing Quinn Castellanos, Reese Kearns, and Malia Moreau to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-1 overall. The Crimson is 5-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 14 points (6-of-11 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT).
- Sophomore Bella Chen finished with 12 points (5-of-10 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT).
- Junior Jasmine Danforth chipped in 11 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Chloe Ferreira (10) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 23 points.
- Harvard held Merrimack to 28.8% shooting from the field.
