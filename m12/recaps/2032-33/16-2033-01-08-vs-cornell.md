It came down to the final minutes, but Harvard women's basketball (15-1, 2-0 Ivy) made the plays it needed in a 71-66 win over Cornell on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 15th straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 42-32.

"That was a gritty win. We'll learn from it, but we'll enjoy it first," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Cornell scored first before senior Esme Ellison scored inside for Harvard's first points. Bella Chen, Chloe Ferreira, and Jordan Delgado keyed a 22-4 run midway through the quarter that turned a 9-7 deficit into a 29-13 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

A 17-11 Cornell spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 40-30. Harvard went into the locker room up 43-30.

Cornell went on a 14-4 run in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 50-47. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Cornell countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 57-56. Harvard held a 64-62 lead in the final minutes. With 2:43 left, Ellison hit a pair of free throws to push the lead to 66-62. The Big Red hit a jumper with 2:31 left to get within 66-64, but Ferreira answered when she hit a pull-up jumper to push the lead to 68-64. Cornell made two free throws with 1:58 left to cut the deficit to 68-66. Ferreira splashed a three from the wing with 1:04 left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-1 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 7-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Bella Chen led Harvard with 16 points (6-of-18 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Chloe Ferreira finished with 14 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 11 points (4-of-8 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and 15 rebounds.
- Sophomore Lainey Rinaldi hit three 3-pointers off the bench.
- Harvard forced 20 turnovers and recorded 11 steals.
