Harvard women's basketball (23-1, 10-0 Ivy) pulled away from Penn for a 60-48 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 23th straight.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before senior Esme Ellison went 2-for-2 at the line for Harvard's first points. An 11-2 Penn spurt midway through the quarter broke a 4-all tie and pushed the Quakers ahead 15-6. The Quakers led 15-10 after one.

A 15-6 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Ellison's putback, turned a 15-10 deficit into a 25-21 lead. The Crimson led 28-27 at the break.

Ellison, Jasmine Danforth, and Chloe Ferreira keyed a 16-4 run in the quarter that turned a 30-28 deficit into a 44-34 lead. Harvard took a 46-36 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Quakers got within 47-44 with 7:20 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 13-2 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 60-46.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 23-1 overall and 10-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Esme Ellison led Harvard with 12 points (5-of-14 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), nine rebounds, and two blocks.
- Sophomore Bella Chen recorded a double-double with 10 points (4-of-11 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT), 10 rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Jordan Delgado contributed 10 points (3-of-10 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and eight assists.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded seven steals.
- Harvard held Penn to 31.6% shooting from the field.
