Harvard women's basketball (4-1) cruised to a 82-61 victory over Sacred Heart on Tuesday at William H. Pitt Center in Fairfield, Conn.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pioneers 32-24. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

Senior Jordan Delgado opened the scoring when she knocked down a jumper. Harvard opened the game on a 6-0 run. The Pioneers countered with a 14-7 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 6-0 deficit into a 14-13 lead. It was 16-all after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 15-7 spurt early in the quarter that broke a 16-all tie and put Harvard up 31-23. Harvard went into the locker room up 33-28.

A 16-10 Sacred Heart spurt early in the quarter flipped a 33-28 deficit into a 44-43 lead. A 15-5 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's triple, turned a 44-43 deficit into a 58-49 lead. Harvard took a 60-51 lead into the fourth quarter.

Esme Ellison, Delgado, and Natalie Olsen keyed a 20-6 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 80-57. The lead reached 23, allowing Ciara Bergstrom, Malia Moreau, and Elise Fennimore to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-1 overall.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led the way for Harvard with 20 points (7-of-15 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
- Sophomore Bella Chen added 13 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison contributed 12 points (5-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded five steals.
