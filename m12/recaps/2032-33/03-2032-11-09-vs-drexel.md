The Crimson made quick work of Drexel on Tuesday, as Harvard women's basketball (2-1) built a lead as large as 38 on the way to a 85-47 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Dragons 34-23. The Crimson shot 57.4% from the field and held Drexel to 36.0%.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Natalie Olsen finished a layup in transition. The Dragons countered with a 7-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 3-0 deficit into a 7-3 lead. Esme Ellison, Chloe Ferreira, and Jordan Delgado keyed an 18-9 run in the quarter that turned a 7-3 deficit into a 21-16 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 12-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Olsen's three, stretched the lead to 37-24. The Crimson led 39-26 at the break.

The Crimson strung together a 16-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Delgado's triple, which stretched the lead to 55-31. A 7-1 Drexel spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 56-38. Harvard led 62-38 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 23-9 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 85-47. With the lead as large as 38, Elise Fennimore, Malia Moreau, and Simone Zamora saw time off the bench.
