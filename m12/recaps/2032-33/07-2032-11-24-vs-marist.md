Harvard women's basketball (6-1) never trailed on Wednesday, beating Marist, 82-57, at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Red Foxes 53-44. The Crimson shot 40.7% from the field and held Marist to 30.9%.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Esme Ellison scored through contact. Harvard opened the game on a 23-4 run. Marist countered with a 12-4 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 29-18. The Crimson took a 30-18 lead into the second quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's layup, which stretched the lead to 39-18. Marist went on a 14-6 run in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 45-32. It stayed that way at halftime.

An 8-2 Marist spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 47-40. The Crimson carried a 55-46 advantage into the final frame.

Bella Chen, Olsen, and Chloe Ferreira keyed a 23-4 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 80-55. With the lead as large as 25, Naomi Prescott, Malia Moreau, and Quinn Castellanos saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-1 overall. The Crimson is 3-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira paced the Crimson with 19 points (8-of-17 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT).
- Sophomore Bella Chen added 14 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Jasmine Danforth grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Senior Jordan Delgado dished out a team-high six assists.
- Harvard's bench contributed 29 points.
- The Crimson assisted on 22 of its 33 field goals.
