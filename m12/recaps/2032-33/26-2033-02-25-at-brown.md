Brown made enough plays down the stretch to edge Harvard women's basketball (24-2, 11-1 Ivy), 67-62, on Friday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

The loss snapped Harvard's 24-game winning streak. Brown shot 59.1% from the field, while Harvard was held to 41.8%.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Bella Chen got Harvard on the board when she hit a pull-up jumper with 6:33 left in the opening quarter. The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's triple, which turned a 4-2 deficit into a 9-4 lead. The Crimson took a 13-12 lead into the second quarter.

Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 16-15 deficit into a 23-16 lead. Brown went on a 14-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 23-16 deficit into a 30-25 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 32-30.

The Bears countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 33-30 deficit into a 36-33 lead. The Crimson faced a 47-45 deficit going into the final frame.

The Bears led 64-55 in the final minutes. With 1:45 left, Esme Ellison banked in a turnaround to make it 64-57. Ellison scored through contact with 1:33 left to pull Harvard within 64-59. With 40 seconds left, Chen made a free throw to pull Harvard within 64-60. With 33 seconds left, Jordan Delgado buried a mid-range jumper to pull Harvard within 64-62. Brown hit a three with 9 seconds left to stretch the lead to 67-62.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 24-2 overall and 11-1 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Esme Ellison led the way for Harvard with 18 points (4-of-8 FG, 10-of-10 FT) and five rebounds.

- Junior Jasmine Danforth chipped in 10 points (5-of-12 FG).
