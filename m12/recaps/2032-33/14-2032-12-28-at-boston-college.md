Down 8 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (13-1) rallied for a 66-60 win over Boston College on Tuesday at Conte Forum in Chestnut Hill, Mass.

Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers. The win is Harvard's 13th straight.

## How it Happened

The Eagles scored first before senior Jordan Delgado knocked down a corner three for Harvard's first points. Boston College opened the game on a 15-7 run. Harvard put together a 7-0 spurt late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 15-14. Harvard trailed 16-14 after the opening frame.

Esme Ellison, Wren Farrow, and Tiana Adeyemi keyed a 32-10 run in the quarter that turned an 18-14 deficit into a 46-28 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

Boston College countered with a 10-2 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 48-38. Harvard led 55-45 heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 64-58 lead in the final minutes. Boston College converted a layup with 2:39 left to cut the deficit to 64-60. Jasmine Danforth hit a jumper from the elbow with 46 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-1 overall.
- Senior Jordan Delgado paced the Crimson with 17 points (5-of-12 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
- Senior Esme Ellison added 14 points (6-of-14 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Junior Jasmine Danforth chipped in 11 points (5-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT) and seven rebounds.
