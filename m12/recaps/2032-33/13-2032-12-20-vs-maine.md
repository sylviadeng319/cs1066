Harvard women's basketball (12-1) picked up a 80-62 win over Maine on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Black Bears 38-28. The win is Harvard's 12th straight.

## How it Happened

The Black Bears scored first before sophomore Bella Chen dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. Simone Zamora, Chen, and Jordan Delgado keyed a 13-3 run midway through the quarter that turned a 13-8 deficit into a 21-16 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

Harvard put together an 11-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 32-19. A 10-0 Maine spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 32-29. Harvard took a 43-35 lead into halftime.

A 21-10 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Delgado's jumper, stretched the lead to 64-45. Harvard took a 64-47 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 12-3 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Natalie Olsen's three, which stretched the lead to 80-61. With the lead as large as 19, Ciara Bergstrom, Malia Moreau, and Zamora saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-1 overall. The Crimson is 6-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led the way for Harvard with 15 points (5-of-10 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Sophomore Bella Chen added 14 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT).
- Senior Esme Ellison chipped in 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Chloe Ferreira came away with five steals.
- Harvard forced 21 turnovers and recorded 14 steals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 22 points.
