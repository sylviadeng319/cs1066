Senior Day at Lavietes Pavilion did not get the ending Harvard women's basketball wanted. Columbia used a 9-0 run midway through the fourth quarter to erase a four-point deficit and held on for a 71-68 win over the Crimson (25-3, 12-2 Ivy) on Saturday, handing Harvard its first home loss since the season opener.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Tiana Adeyemi, Jordan Delgado, Esme Ellison, and Chloe Ferreira, and head coach Carrie Moore started all four, giving Adeyemi her first start of the season. The Crimson forced 20 turnovers and grabbed 18 offensive rebounds, but shot 36.1% from the field while Columbia hit nine 3-pointers and shot 49.0%.

The loss snapped a 12-game home winning streak, but it does not change the standings. Harvard finishes the regular season in first place, a game ahead of Princeton, and will open the Ivy League Tournament as the No. 1 seed in a semifinal rematch with No. 4 seed Columbia.

"I'm disappointed for our seniors, because they deserved a better finish today. They've given this program everything, and the best way to honor them is to bounce back next weekend," Moore said.

## How it Happened

Columbia came out hot and opened the game on a 15-4 run. Harvard answered with a 16-3 run, capped by an Adeyemi basket, to take a 20-18 lead after one quarter.

A 10-4 burst early in the second quarter, capped by a Jasmine Danforth 3-pointer, pushed the Crimson ahead 30-22, and Harvard led 36-33 at halftime.

Harvard was up 42-35 in the third quarter when Columbia struck with an 11-2 run to take a 46-44 lead. Ferreira, Danforth, and Wren Farrow answered with eight straight points, and the Crimson carried a 52-48 lead into the fourth.

Harvard still led 61-57 midway through the final period before the Lions scored nine unanswered points to go up 66-61. Ferreira's mid-range jumper with 2:39 left cut it to three, and after a Columbia basket, Delgado buried a 3-pointer from the wing to make it 68-66. The Lions answered with a three with 1:05 left, and Ellison's two free throws pulled Harvard within 71-68, but the Crimson's last attempt to tie came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard finishes the regular season 25-3 overall and 12-2 in Ivy League play, first in the league standings. The Crimson is 12-2 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Esme Ellison posted a double-double with 19 points (7-of-15 FG, 5-of-5 FT) and 11 rebounds in her final regular-season home game.
- Senior Jordan Delgado scored 13 points and was a perfect 3-of-3 from 3-point range.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira added 12 points, nine rebounds, and four assists.
- Senior Tiana Adeyemi made her first start of the season and had six points, seven rebounds, and a team-high five assists.
- Harvard assisted on 19 of its 26 field goals and forced 20 Columbia turnovers.
