Harvard women's basketball (17-1, 4-0 Ivy) erased a 13-point deficit and came away with a 77-69 win over Penn on Monday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

The Crimson shot 50.0% from the field and held Penn to 41.5%. The win is Harvard's 17th straight.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Jordan Delgado buried a mid-range jumper. The Quakers went on a 15-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 15-2 lead. Delgado, Esme Ellison, and Natalie Olsen keyed a 16-7 run midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 22-18. Penn led 23-18 after one.

The Crimson strung together a 21-5 burst early in the quarter, capped by Jasmine Danforth's 3-pointer, which turned a 23-18 deficit into a 39-28 lead. Harvard took a 44-34 lead into halftime.

A 12-0 Penn spurt early in the quarter flipped a 44-34 deficit into a 46-44 lead. Harvard put together a 12-4 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 46-44 deficit into a 56-50 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Penn got within 62-60 with 4:57 left before Harvard took control. A 17-7 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's triple, turned a 62-60 deficit into a 77-69 lead.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-1 overall and 4-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Esme Ellison led the way for Harvard with 19 points (8-of-10 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira chipped in 13 points (4-of-9 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Jasmine Danforth finished with 13 points (6-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT).
- Natalie Olsen (13) and Jordan Delgado (11) also reached double figures.
- The Crimson assisted on 20 of its 30 field goals.
