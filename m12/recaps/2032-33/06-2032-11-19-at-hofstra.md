Behind a strong second quarter, Harvard women's basketball (5-1) beat Hofstra, 61-52, on Friday at Mack Sports Complex in Hempstead, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's fifth straight. Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Pride scored first before senior Jordan Delgado dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Delgado's layup, which turned a 5-0 deficit into a 10-5 lead. Hofstra countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 12-7 deficit into a 14-12 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

Tiana Adeyemi, Bella Chen, and Esme Ellison keyed an 11-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 14-12 deficit into a 23-14 lead. The Pride went on an 8-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 23-22. The Crimson led 34-29 at the break.

A 7-0 Hofstra spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 39-36. Harvard put together an 8-0 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 47-36. Harvard led 47-38 heading into the fourth.

A 6-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Chen's layup, stretched the lead to 53-38. Hofstra never got closer than nine in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-1 overall.
- Senior Jordan Delgado finished with a team-high 17 points (6-of-12 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison finished with 14 points (7-of-12 FG) and five rebounds.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira chipped in 11 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT) and eight rebounds.
- Ellison surpassed 1,000 career points.
