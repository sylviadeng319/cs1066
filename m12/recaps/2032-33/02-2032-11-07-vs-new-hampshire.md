Harvard women's basketball (1-1) held on for a 58-56 win over New Hampshire on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

## How it Happened

Senior Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board when she connected from deep with 9:52 left in the opening quarter. A 10-3 New Hampshire spurt early in the quarter flipped a 4-0 deficit into a 10-7 lead. Jasmine Danforth, Tiana Adeyemi, and Bella Chen keyed a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that turned a 10-7 deficit into a 13-10 lead. The teams were knotted at 14 after one.

The Wildcats went on a 13-3 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 20-17 deficit into a 30-23 lead. The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Danforth's putback, which turned a 30-23 deficit into a 33-30 lead. The score held there at halftime.

Harvard put together a 16-6 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 49-38. The Crimson carried a 49-42 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard held a 58-52 lead in the final minutes. New Hampshire scored inside with 23 seconds left to get within 58-54. The Wildcats scored with 18 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 1-1 overall.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 17 points (6-of-12 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) and five assists.
- Sophomore Bella Chen contributed 15 points (4-of-14 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
- Junior Jasmine Danforth chipped in 12 points (4-of-7 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Esme Ellison grabbed 10 rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 14 of its 19 field goals.
- Chen's 15 points were a career high.
