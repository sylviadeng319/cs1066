Harvard women's basketball finished off a weekend sweep in emphatic fashion. One night after beating Brown by 21, the Crimson (20-1, 7-0 Ivy) pulled away from Yale for a 76-47 win on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion, its 20th straight victory.

Harvard shot 59.6% from the field, outrebounded the Bulldogs 38-21, and closed the game with a 27-8 fourth quarter. It was a lopsided first chapter of this season's rivalry after the teams split last season's two meetings, with Yale winning 58-53 in New Haven and Harvard taking the rematch in Cambridge.

"I thought we were sharp from the start, and then we got a little loose with the ball in the middle of the game. To come out in the fourth quarter on the second night of a back-to-back and play like that says a lot about our depth," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored the game's first basket before junior Natalie Olsen answered with a pull-up jumper. That started a 25-8 Harvard surge through the rest of the first quarter, and the Crimson led 25-10 after 10 minutes.

The Bulldogs made their push in the second. Yale used a 12-2 run to trim the deficit to 34-26, which was the score at halftime, and outscored Harvard 16-9 in the period.

The teams traded baskets for much of the third quarter, and Harvard took a 49-39 lead into the fourth.

Then the Crimson ran away with it. Sophomore Lainey Rinaldi, Jordan Delgado, and Esme Ellison led a fourth-quarter blitz in which Harvard outscored Yale 27-8, and the lead reached 29 by the final horn. All 16 players on the Harvard roster saw the floor.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-1 overall and 7-0 in Ivy League play, extending its winning streak to 20 games. The Crimson is 9-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 18 points on 8-of-11 shooting, including 2-of-5 from 3-point range, in 21 minutes.
- Sophomore Lainey Rinaldi was perfect off the bench, scoring 11 points (5-of-5 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) with three assists.
- Sophomore Bella Chen added 10 points, five rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Jasmine Danforth went 7-of-8 at the free throw line for nine points, and senior Esme Ellison had nine points and nine rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 23 points, and the Crimson made 15 of its 20 free throws.
