Harvard women's basketball (3-1) pulled away from Arkansas for a 71-57 victory on Sunday at Bud Walton Arena in Fayetteville, Ark.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

"We did a lot of the little things well tonight: boxing out, talking on defense, making the extra pass," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Razorbacks scored first before senior Esme Ellison hit a jumper from the elbow for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 30-4 burst in the quarter, capped by Chloe Ferreira's layup, which turned a 4-2 deficit into a 32-8 lead. Arkansas went on a 12-2 run late in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 34-20. The score held there after one quarter.

Ellison, Ferreira, and Jordan Delgado keyed a 9-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 49-28. Harvard took a 49-30 lead into halftime.

Neither team could create much separation in the third quarter. Harvard took a 58-42 lead into the fourth quarter.

A 9-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Ferreira's three, stretched the lead to 69-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-1 overall.
- Senior Esme Ellison finished with a team-high 19 points (7-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and 15 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira added 15 points (7-of-15 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT) and six rebounds.
- Sophomore Bella Chen chipped in 10 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT).
- Junior Jasmine Danforth grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard held Arkansas to 32.8% shooting from the field.
