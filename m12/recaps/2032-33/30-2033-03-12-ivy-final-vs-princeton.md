Harvard women's basketball was down 20 to Princeton early in the third quarter of the Ivy League Tournament championship game. By the final horn, the Crimson was celebrating a championship. No. 1 seed Harvard (27-3) stormed back for a 71-66 win over No. 2 seed Princeton on Saturday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I., claiming the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament.

The comeback was built on a 33-point third quarter and a 32-11 run that turned a 44-24 deficit into a lead. Sophomore Bella Chen poured in a career high, and Harvard finished 3-0 against the Tigers this season, a year after Princeton beat the Crimson three times, the last in the 2032 Ivy League Tournament semifinals. It is Harvard's first Ivy League Tournament title, and first NCAA Tournament bid, since 2029-30.

"I've never been prouder of a group. Down 20 against a team as good as Princeton, nobody in our huddle flinched. Bella was fearless, our seniors were steady, and this team just kept believing," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Princeton came out firing. The Tigers scored first, and after a Jordan Delgado free throw got Harvard on the board, Princeton built a 24-9 lead after one quarter. The second quarter was more even, but the Tigers still led 39-23 at halftime.

Princeton scored five of the first six points of the third quarter to go up 44-24. Then the Crimson caught fire. Harvard outscored the Tigers 32-11 over the rest of the quarter, and an Esme Ellison layup capped the run and gave the Crimson its first lead of the game at 56-55 heading into the fourth.

Princeton went back in front early in the final period, and the Tigers led 60-58 midway through it. Harvard answered with a 9-2 spurt, capped by a Chen 3-pointer, to take a 67-62 lead. Jasmine Danforth's putback with 2:54 left made it 69-64, and after a Princeton layup with 1:34 to go, Natalie Olsen knocked down a mid-range jumper with 27 seconds left to seal the title.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard wins the Ivy League Tournament and earns the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament. The Crimson improves to 27-3 and finishes 3-0 against Princeton.
- Sophomore Bella Chen led Harvard with a career-high 25 points (9-of-19 FG, 5-of-14 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Esme Ellison scored 17 points, going 9-of-10 at the free throw line, and grabbed eight rebounds.
- Senior Jordan Delgado added 12 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Jasmine Danforth pulled down eight rebounds, including the putback that made it a two-possession game late.
- Harvard outscored Princeton 33-16 in the third quarter and committed just seven turnovers in the game.
