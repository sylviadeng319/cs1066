Harvard women's basketball (19-1, 6-0 Ivy) cruised to a 82-61 victory over Brown on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 19th straight.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before junior Natalie Olsen added a free throw for Harvard's first points. Tiana Adeyemi, Naomi Prescott, and Jasmine Danforth keyed a 10-2 run early in the quarter that turned a 9-5 deficit into a 15-11 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 19-16.

The teams traded baskets for much of the second quarter. The Crimson led 32-27 at the break.

A 12-5 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's free throws, stretched the lead to 44-34. The Bears countered with an 8-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 46-44. The Crimson carried a 51-45 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together a 31-14 burst in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's three, which stretched the lead to 82-61. The lead reached 21, allowing Prescott, Malia Moreau, and Simone Zamora to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-1 overall and 6-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 16 points (6-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT), three assists, and three steals.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira contributed 13 points (4-of-8 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
- Senior Esme Ellison added 12 points (4-of-5 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and six rebounds.
- Tiana Adeyemi (12) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 27 points.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded 10 steals.
