Harvard women's basketball (11-1) picked up a 67-53 win over UMass Lowell on Friday at Costello Athletic Center in Lowell, Mass.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the River Hawks 38-30. The win is Harvard's 11th straight.

## How it Happened

Senior Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board when she sank two free throws with 8:56 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 14-0 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 22-13.

The River Hawks went on an 18-4 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 24-15 deficit into a 33-28 lead. The Crimson strung together a 19-1 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's free throws, which turned a 33-28 deficit into a 47-34 lead. The score held there at halftime.

The third quarter was a back-and-forth affair. The Crimson carried a 54-39 advantage into the final frame.

An 11-5 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Ellison's putback, stretched the lead to 67-51.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-1 overall.
- Senior Esme Ellison paced the Crimson with 24 points (10-of-12 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Jordan Delgado finished with 13 points (5-of-10 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and six assists.
- Sophomore Bella Chen contributed 11 points (4-of-12 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Tiana Adeyemi came away with four steals.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded 13 steals.
- Delgado surpassed 1,000 career points.
- Ellison's 24 points were a career high.
