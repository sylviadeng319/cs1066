Harvard women's basketball (7-1) picked up a 72-64 win over Albany on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's seventh straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Great Danes 34-25.

## How it Happened

The Great Danes scored first before senior Jordan Delgado scored on a driving layup for Harvard's first points. A 17-10 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Chloe Ferreira's free throws, turned a 3-0 deficit into a 17-13 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

Harvard put together a 24-7 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 41-20. It stayed that way at halftime.

Albany countered with a 13-5 run early in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 48-33. The Crimson carried a 58-39 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard kept Albany at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below six.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-1 overall. The Crimson is 4-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 20 points (6-of-12 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 7-of-11 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira contributed 12 points (5-of-6 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and five assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison chipped in 11 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded six steals.
