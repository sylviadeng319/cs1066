Harvard women's basketball opened the 2032-33 season with a late charge that came up one shot short. Villanova held off the Crimson (0-1), 59-54, on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion, knocking down a 3-pointer with 21 seconds left to stop a 7-0 Harvard run in the closing minutes.

The Wildcats, who beat Harvard 76-52 in Villanova, Pa., last December, shot 48.0% from the field and never let the Crimson settle in after a 14-2 burst in the third quarter. Harvard answered with effort on the glass, winning the rebounding battle 37-32 and grabbing 16 offensive boards, but shot 36.1% and made seven of its 12 free throws.

"I loved the fight at the end, and that's who this team is going to be. But a good team like Villanova makes you pay for every empty possession, and we had too many of them tonight. We'll learn from it and get back to work on Sunday," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Villanova scored first before senior captain Jordan Delgado buried a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points of the season. The Crimson used a 7-0 spurt to tie the game at 7, but the Wildcats closed the quarter on an 8-0 run and led 15-7 after 10 minutes.

Harvard's best stretch came early in the second quarter. Trailing 18-9, the Crimson ripped off a 15-2 run, capped by a Jasmine Danforth free throw, to take a 24-20 lead. Villanova answered with six straight points to go back in front, and Harvard went into the locker room down 27-26.

The Crimson led 30-27 early in the third quarter before the Wildcats took control with a 14-2 run that made it 41-32. Villanova carried a 45-37 lead into the fourth.

Down 56-47 in the final two minutes, Harvard made one last push. Chloe Ferreira hit a free throw with 1:43 left, senior captain Esme Ellison banked in a turnaround, and Bella Chen knocked down a mid-range jumper with 1:21 to go. Wren Farrow's post move with 55 seconds left pulled the Crimson within 56-54, but Villanova's three on the other end put the game away.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard opens the season 0-1.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with 15 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) in the season opener.
- Senior Esme Ellison added 14 points (7-of-13 FG) and nine rebounds, five of them on the offensive glass.
- Junior Jasmine Danforth scored 12 points on 5-of-7 shooting and grabbed nine rebounds.
- Sophomore Bella Chen dished out a team-high four assists.
- Harvard outrebounded Villanova 37-32 and scored the final seven points before the Wildcats' late 3-pointer.
