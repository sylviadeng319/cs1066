Harvard women's basketball finally got one back against Princeton. Trailing by nine in the third quarter at Jadwin Gymnasium on Saturday, the Crimson (16-1, 3-0 Ivy) rallied for a 65-64 win, its first victory over the Tigers after five straight losses in the series.

Princeton went 14-0 in Ivy League play last season and beat Harvard three times, including in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals. This time the Crimson flipped the script in the fourth quarter, outscoring the Tigers 18-12 and getting the stops it needed down the stretch to stay unbeaten in the league. The win is Harvard's 16th straight.

"We've talked about this game since last March. Princeton is the standard in this league, and we knew we'd have to take a punch in their building. I'm proud of how our seniors kept us together when we were down nine," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first and led 5-2 before Harvard caught fire. A 19-8 run, capped by a Jordan Delgado 3-pointer, put the Crimson up 21-13, and Harvard led 21-17 after one quarter.

The Tigers answered in the second. A 7-0 spurt turned a 22-17 deficit into a 24-22 lead, and Princeton went into halftime ahead 35-33.

Princeton opened the third quarter with seven straight points to build its largest lead at 42-33. Harvard chipped away with a 7-1 spurt to get within 43-40, but the Tigers still led 52-47 entering the fourth.

The Crimson took over from there. Down 56-51, Harvard went on a 12-2 run, capped by a Lainey Rinaldi 3-pointer off the bench, to go in front 63-58. Delgado's floater with 2:48 left made it 65-60. Princeton scored twice in the next 30 seconds to pull within a point, but Harvard's defense held the Tigers without a point over the final 2:18 to close out the win.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-1 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play, extending its winning streak to 16 games.
- Senior Jordan Delgado led Harvard with a season-high 21 points (8-of-15 FG, 4-of-9 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison posted a double-double with 10 points (5-of-8 FG) and a season-high 16 rebounds.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira added 15 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
- Sophomore Bella Chen scored 11 points and grabbed five rebounds, and junior Jasmine Danforth dished out five assists.
- Harvard assisted on 17 of its 25 field goals and won the rebounding battle 40-37.
- The win snapped a five-game losing streak against Princeton, which swept three meetings with Harvard last season.
