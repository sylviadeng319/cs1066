Harvard women's basketball (10-1) pulled away from Northeastern for a 78-68 victory on Tuesday at Cabot Center in Boston, Mass.

The win is Harvard's 10th straight.

"There's a lot to build on from tonight. I liked our pace and our poise," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Huskies scored first before sophomore Bella Chen drilled a three for Harvard's first points. A 12-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's free throw, turned an 11-6 deficit into an 18-11 lead. Northeastern went on an 8-0 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 20-13 deficit into a 21-20 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 13-4 Northeastern spurt in the quarter flipped a 25-23 deficit into a 36-29 lead. The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's triple, which evened the score at 36. Harvard trailed 43-39 at halftime.

Harvard put together a 25-3 spurt in the quarter that turned a 43-39 deficit into a 64-46 lead. Harvard led 64-51 heading into the fourth.

Northeastern never got closer than eight in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-1 overall.
- Senior Jordan Delgado finished with a team-high 20 points (8-of-14 FG, 4-of-6 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira added 14 points (5-of-12 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Esme Ellison finished with 11 points (4-of-5 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Jasmine Danforth (11) also reached double figures.
- Sophomore Bella Chen came away with four steals.
