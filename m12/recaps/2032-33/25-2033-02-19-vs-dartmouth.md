The Crimson made quick work of Dartmouth on Saturday, as Harvard women's basketball (24-1, 11-0 Ivy) built a lead as large as 29 on the way to a 76-47 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 46.8% from the field and held Dartmouth to 37.5%. Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers.

"It's fun to watch this group when the ball is moving like that. Nobody cared who scored," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Big Green scored first before first-year Ciara Bergstrom scored through contact for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Tiana Adeyemi's free throws, which turned a 3-0 deficit into a 9-3 lead. Dartmouth countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 9-3 deficit into a 10-9 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 15-10.

A 31-10 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's 3-pointer, stretched the lead to 46-20. Harvard took a 48-22 lead into halftime.

The Big Green went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 51-29. Harvard took a 57-33 lead into the fourth quarter.

Elise Fennimore, Jordan Delgado, and Esme Ellison keyed a 12-2 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 76-47. With the lead as large as 29, Naomi Prescott, Fennimore, and Quinn Castellanos saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 24-1 overall and 11-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 12-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira led Harvard with 15 points (5-of-11 FG, 1-of-7 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Jordan Delgado finished with 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT) and three assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard outrebounded Dartmouth 39-26, including 13 offensive boards.
- Harvard's bench contributed 32 points.
