Behind a strong fourth quarter, Harvard women's basketball (9-1) beat Fordham, 75-66, on Saturday at Rose Hill Gymnasium in Bronx, N.Y.

Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers. The win is Harvard's ninth straight.

"We came in with a clear game plan and our players executed it," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Bella Chen opened the scoring when she drilled a three. Fordham went on a 15-8 run in the quarter, which flipped a 5-0 deficit into a 15-13 lead. It was 15-all after the first quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Chen's three, which broke a 17-all tie and put Harvard up 24-17. The Rams countered with a 12-1 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 24-17 deficit into a 29-25 lead. The score held there at halftime.

Jasmine Danforth, Esme Ellison, and Chen keyed a 19-8 run midway through the quarter that turned a 34-29 deficit into a 48-42 lead. A 6-0 Fordham spurt late in the quarter drew level at 48. Harvard took a 50-48 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Rams countered with a 10-2 run early in the quarter that flipped a 54-50 deficit into a 60-56 lead. Harvard held a 67-64 lead in the final minutes. With 2:35 left, Chen connected from deep to make it 70-64. Fordham hit a pair at the line with 1:50 left to get within 70-66, but Danforth answered when she added a free throw to make it 71-66. With 43 seconds left, Chen buried a mid-range jumper to push the lead to 73-66. Lainey Rinaldi put the game away when she scored on a driving layup with 41 seconds left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-1 overall.
- Sophomore Bella Chen finished with 16 points (6-of-11 FG, 4-of-9 3-PT), five rebounds, and five steals.
- Senior Chloe Ferreira contributed 16 points (6-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and four assists.
- Senior Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 15 points (6-of-7 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- Harvard forced 22 turnovers and recorded 11 steals.
