Harvard women's basketball (12-4, 2-0 Ivy) led from start to finish in a 78-45 win over Cornell on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 47.5% from the field and held Cornell to 27.3%. The win is Harvard's third straight.

"We talked about being the aggressor, and our players took that to heart from the first possession," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Ciara Bergstrom hit a jumper from the elbow. Harvard outscored the Big Red 24-11 in the first quarter. Harvard led 24-11 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 12-2 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 42-19. The score held there at halftime.

Lainey Rinaldi, Elise Fennimore, and Bria Jefferson keyed an 8-2 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 52-28. The Crimson carried a 57-35 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together an 18-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's bucket, which stretched the lead to 78-45. With the lead as large as 33, Juniper Haddad, Fatima Sorensen, and Malia Moreau saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-4 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 8-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi paced the Crimson with 26 points (8-of-13 FG, 6-of-8 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT).
- Senior Bella Chen contributed 14 points (5-of-17 FG, 2-of-14 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT).
- Senior Elise Fennimore chipped in 11 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) and eight rebounds.
- Harvard outrebounded Cornell 44-31, including 13 offensive boards.
- Rinaldi's 26 points were a career high.
