Harvard women's basketball (3-1) pulled away from Delaware for a 76-62 victory on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 60.8% from the field and held Delaware to 41.2%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Blue Hens 34-20.

## How it Happened

Senior Elise Fennimore got Harvard on the board when she scored through contact with 8:22 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 7-0 run. A 10-4 Delaware spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 11-10. The Crimson took a 13-10 lead into the second quarter.

Delaware went on a 15-6 run in the quarter, which flipped a 13-10 deficit into a 25-19 lead. Lainey Rinaldi, Chloe Rousseau, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed an 11-0 run late in the quarter that turned a 25-19 deficit into a 30-25 lead. It stayed that way at halftime.

A 25-8 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Quinn Castellanos's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 55-35. The score held there heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Rousseau's bucket, which stretched the lead to 70-51. The Blue Hens never got closer than 12 in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-1 overall.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi paced the Crimson with 19 points (8-of-11 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), seven rebounds, and five assists.
- Senior Elise Fennimore added 14 points (5-of-6 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Bella Chen contributed 12 points (4-of-6 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and five assists.
- Quinn Castellanos (10) also reached double figures.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau came away with four steals.
