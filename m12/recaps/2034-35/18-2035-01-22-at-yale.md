Harvard women's basketball spotted Yale a 10-point lead in the first quarter, then answered every Bulldogs charge on Monday at John J. Lee Amphitheater. The Crimson (14-4, 4-0 Ivy) scored 13 straight points late in the fourth quarter and held on for a 68-65 win in New Haven, Conn.

Playing for the second time in three days after a win at Brown on Saturday, Harvard stayed unbeaten in Ivy League play and extended its winning streak to five games. The Crimson made 12 of its 13 free throws, and that edge at the line proved decisive in a game in which Yale shot 49.0% from the field.

"Yale is a tough place to play, and they threw a lot of punches tonight. We got down early, and then they made a big run in the fourth, but our players never lost their composure. You have to win games like this on the road in this league," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Yale scored first and opened the game on an 11-1 run, with a Lainey Rinaldi free throw accounting for the Crimson's only point in that stretch. Harvard responded with a 14-1 burst, capped by an Elise Fennimore layup, to lead 15-12 after one quarter.

Callie Winslow's bucket capped a 9-0 run early in the second quarter that pushed the lead to 24-12. The Bulldogs countered with a 14-4 run of their own and trailed just 28-26 at halftime.

The game swung again in the third quarter. Trailing 31-28, Harvard scored 20 consecutive points to build a 48-31 lead. Yale answered with 13 unanswered points late in the period, and the Crimson's lead was down to 48-44 entering the fourth.

The Bulldogs kept coming. A 14-3 run turned a 52-46 deficit into a 60-55 Yale lead, but the Bulldogs did not score again until the final two minutes. The Crimson scored nine straight to go back in front 64-60, then Bella Chen dropped in a floater with 2:45 left and Fennimore converted a putback with 2:17 remaining for a 68-60 lead. Yale hit a 3-pointer with 1:46 to play and scored again with 23 seconds left, but it could get no closer.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-4 overall and 4-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson has won five straight.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi led Harvard with 16 points (6-of-8 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Elise Fennimore added 15 points (5-of-9 FG, 5-of-5 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos scored 11 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 FT) and grabbed six rebounds.
- Sophomore Callie Winslow gave Harvard seven points (2-of-3 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and two steals off the bench.
- Harvard shot 12-of-13 (92.3%) from the free throw line and outrebounded Yale 31-26.
