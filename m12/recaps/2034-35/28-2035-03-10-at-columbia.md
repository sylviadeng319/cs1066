Harvard women's basketball (21-7, 11-3 Ivy) erased a 15-point deficit and came away with a 65-62 win over Columbia on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

Junior Quinn Castellanos got Harvard on the board when she scored through contact with 9:49 left in the opening quarter. Columbia went on a 14-3 run in the quarter, which flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 14-5 lead. The Lions carried a 16-7 lead into the second quarter.

A 10-2 Columbia spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 28-13. Bria Jefferson, Bella Chen, and Lainey Rinaldi keyed a 20-2 run midway through the quarter that turned a 28-13 deficit into a 33-30 lead. The score held there at halftime.

The Lions countered with a 9-2 run early in the quarter that flipped a 33-30 deficit into a 39-35 lead. A 12-4 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Chen's triple, turned a 41-37 deficit into a 49-45 lead. It was 49-all heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 13-2 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 56-49 deficit into a 62-58 lead. Harvard held a 62-58 lead in the final minutes. Columbia drained a 3-pointer with 2:39 left to get within 62-61, but Chen answered when she splashed a three from the wing to push the lead to 65-61. The Lions scored with 2:16 left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 21-7 overall and 11-3 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi paced the Crimson with 19 points (7-of-12 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT), seven rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Bella Chen chipped in 14 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
