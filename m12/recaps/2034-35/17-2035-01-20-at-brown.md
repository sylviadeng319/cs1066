Harvard women's basketball (13-4, 3-0 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Brown, 82-56, at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

Harvard's defense forced 21 turnovers. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

"It's fun to watch this group when the ball is moving like that. Nobody cared who scored," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as sophomore Hailey Brennan hit a pull-up jumper. Harvard opened the game on a 16-8 run. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 16-10.

A 24-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Bria Jefferson's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 40-21. The Crimson led 40-23 at the break.

Harvard put together a 15-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 57-30. The Bears countered with a 9-2 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 59-39. The Crimson carried a 61-41 advantage into the final frame.

Quinn Castellanos, Ciara Bergstrom, and Chloe Rousseau keyed a 10-1 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 80-54. With the lead as large as 27, Malia Moreau, Juniper Haddad, and Savannah Lachance saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-4 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Elise Fennimore paced the Crimson with 15 points (6-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau finished with 14 points (4-of-8 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 3-of-3 FT), five rebounds, and three steals.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos contributed 12 points (5-of-6 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT).
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
