Harvard women's basketball (4-1) cruised to a 84-60 victory over Boston University on Monday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 59.3% from the field and held Boston University to 36.2%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Terriers 39-26.

## How it Happened

The Terriers scored first before junior Ciara Bergstrom converted a putback for Harvard's first points. Harvard put together a 29-10 spurt in the quarter that turned a 4-0 deficit into a 29-14 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 27-9 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Thea Dalton's bucket inside, stretched the lead to 56-27. That was the score at halftime.

The Terriers countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 64-37. Hailey Brennan, Bergstrom, and Quinn Castellanos keyed a 7-0 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 71-37. Harvard led 71-39 heading into the fourth.

Harvard kept Boston University at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 22. With the lead as large as 34, Bria Jefferson, Malia Moreau, and Dalton saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-1 overall.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi led the way for Harvard with 21 points (8-of-11 FG, 4-of-7 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five assists.
- Junior Ciara Bergstrom chipped in 13 points (6-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Elise Fennimore added 11 points (4-of-7 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Quinn Castellanos (10) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 27 points.
- Rinaldi's 21 points were a career high.
