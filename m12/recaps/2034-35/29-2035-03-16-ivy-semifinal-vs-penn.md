For the second straight year, Harvard women's basketball will play for an Ivy League Tournament title. The No. 2 seed Crimson (22-7) climbed out of an 11-point hole in the third quarter and beat No. 3 seed Penn, 63-55, in the semifinals on Friday at Jadwin Gymnasium in Princeton, N.J.

The win settled the season series with the Quakers. Harvard won 70-61 at Lavietes Pavilion on Feb. 2, and Penn answered with a 68-57 win at The Palestra on Feb. 16. Friday's game was also a rematch of last year's semifinal, which Harvard won 56-55 in Ithaca, N.Y., on the way to the program's first Sweet 16.

Harvard's defense set the tone. Penn made just 21 of its 72 shots (29.2%), and the Crimson outrebounded the Quakers 47-42. Harvard has now won five straight and will face top-seeded Princeton on Saturday in a rematch of last season's championship game.

"We got down, and nobody in our huddle panicked. That stretch in the third quarter was about as connected as we've been all year on both ends of the floor. Now we get to play for a championship again, and that's all you can ask for this time of year," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Penn opened with seven straight points before senior Elise Fennimore scored inside for Harvard's first basket. Quinn Castellanos's putback capped a 9-1 burst that put the Crimson ahead 9-8, but the Quakers led 11-9 after one quarter.

Harvard tied it at 11 early in the second quarter, but Penn answered with a 15-7 run for a 26-18 lead and took a 26-19 advantage into halftime.

The Quakers pushed the lead to 39-28 with a 7-0 run midway through the third quarter. Then Harvard flipped the game. The Crimson scored 13 straight points, the last on a Chloe Rousseau bucket, and carried a 41-39 lead into the fourth.

Harvard kept the pressure on in the final period. Penn got within 45-42 with 8:09 left, but the Crimson outscored the Quakers 18-13 the rest of the way. From the 39-28 deficit, Harvard outscored Penn 35-9 to build a 63-48 lead before the Quakers scored the game's last seven points.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game and improves to 22-7. The Crimson has won five straight.
- Harvard won the season series with Penn, 2-1.
- Senior Bella Chen led Harvard with 15 points (6-of-11 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT) and three assists.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau added 13 points (6-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 FT), six rebounds, and three steals.
- Senior Elise Fennimore scored 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-2 FT) with eight rebounds, and junior Quinn Castellanos grabbed 10 rebounds, four of them offensive.
- Harvard held Penn to 21-of-72 shooting from the field and 10-of-33 from 3-point range.
