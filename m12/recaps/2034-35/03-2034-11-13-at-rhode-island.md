Harvard women's basketball (2-1) let an 8-point lead slip away on Monday in a 63-60 loss to Rhode Island at Ryan Center in Kingston, R.I.

The Rams shot 46.2% from the field, while Harvard was held to 34.8%.

## How it Happened

Rhode Island scored first before senior Bella Chen buried a 3-pointer for Harvard's first points. A 16-2 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Chloe Rousseau's bucket, turned a 6-0 deficit into a 16-8 lead. Rhode Island went on a 10-0 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 16-8 deficit into an 18-16 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 20-18.

Rhode Island countered with a 17-3 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 27-24 deficit into a 41-30 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 43-33.

The Crimson strung together a 14-4 burst early in the quarter, capped by Hailey Brennan's jumper, which evened the score at 47. The Crimson faced a 54-51 deficit going into the final frame.

Harvard held a 58-57 lead in the final minutes. Rhode Island drained a 3-pointer with 2:59 left to go in front 60-58. The Rams answered from beyond the arc with 2:12 left to stretch the lead to 63-58, but Elise Fennimore answered when she banked in a turnaround to pull Harvard within 63-60. Harvard did not score over the final 2:03. The Crimson's final attempt to tie it came up short.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 2-1 overall.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi finished with a team-high 19 points (7-of-20 FG, 5-of-10 3-PT), seven rebounds, and three assists.
- Senior Bella Chen contributed 18 points (7-of-16 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and four assists.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded nine steals.
