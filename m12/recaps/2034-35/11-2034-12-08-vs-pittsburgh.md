The Crimson made quick work of Pittsburgh on Friday, as Harvard women's basketball (8-3) built a lead as large as 25 on the way to a 75-52 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Panthers 41-33.

## How it Happened

Senior Lainey Rinaldi opened the scoring when she scored on a driving layup. A 7-0 Pittsburgh spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 7-2 lead. Bella Chen, Hailey Brennan, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed a 10-2 run midway through the quarter that turned a 7-2 deficit into a 12-9 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 14-13.

The Crimson strung together an 18-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Chloe Rousseau's triple, which stretched the lead to 37-20. The Panthers countered with a 10-1 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 38-30. The score held there at halftime.

Harvard put together a 12-2 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 52-39. Harvard took a 54-42 lead into the fourth quarter.

A 21-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Rousseau's free throw, stretched the lead to 75-50. The lead reached 25, allowing Malia Moreau, Savannah Lachance, and Juniper Haddad to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-3 overall. The Crimson is 5-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
