Harvard women's basketball (7-2) pulled away from Siena for a 71-62 victory on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Bella Chen scored on a driving layup. The Saints countered with an 8-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 2-0 deficit into an 8-2 lead. Harvard put together a 13-1 spurt midway through the quarter that turned an 8-2 deficit into a 15-9 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

An 11-2 Siena spurt early in the quarter flipped a 15-9 deficit into a 20-17 lead. The Crimson strung together a 9-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's layup, which turned a 20-17 deficit into a 26-20 lead. Harvard took a 30-29 lead into halftime.

Elise Fennimore, Chloe Rousseau, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed a 9-2 run early in the quarter that turned a 31-30 deficit into a 39-33 lead. The Saints went on an 11-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 41-35 deficit into a 46-43 lead. Harvard led 50-46 heading into the fourth.

A 9-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Rinaldi's three, stretched the lead to 62-51.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-2 overall. The Crimson is 4-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Bella Chen led the way for Harvard with 26 points (8-of-14 FG, 4-of-9 3-PT, 6-of-6 FT).
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi finished with 19 points (9-of-16 FG, 1-of-6 3-PT) and five assists.
- Senior Elise Fennimore contributed 11 points (5-of-8 FG, 1-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau came away with four steals.
- The Crimson assisted on 18 of its 25 field goals.
