Harvard women's basketball (17-5, 7-1 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Cornell, 64-50, at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

Harvard's defense forced 22 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Senior Bella Chen got Harvard on the board when she knocked down a corner three with 9:36 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on an 18-8 run. Cornell went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 18-15. The score held there after one quarter.

Harvard put together an 11-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 30-18. Cornell countered with a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 30-24. Harvard took a 35-24 lead into halftime.

A 16-8 Cornell spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 43-40. The Crimson strung together a 12-2 burst late in the quarter, capped by Elise Fennimore's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 55-42. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Big Red never got closer than 11 in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-5 overall and 7-1 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Bella Chen led Harvard with 23 points (7-of-19 FG, 4-of-12 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
- Senior Elise Fennimore recorded a double-double with 12 points (5-of-15 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-5 FT) and 12 rebounds.
- Junior Ciara Bergstrom grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi dished out a team-high six assists.
- Harvard held Cornell to 29.1% shooting from the field.
