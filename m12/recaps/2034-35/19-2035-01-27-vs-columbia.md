Harvard women's basketball (15-4, 5-0 Ivy) pulled away from Columbia for a 60-50 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight.

## How it Happened

The Lions scored first before sophomore Chloe Rousseau finished a layup in transition for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 15-6 burst in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's layup, which turned a 2-0 deficit into a 15-8 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

The Lions went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 16-15. Lainey Rinaldi, Malia Moreau, and Elise Fennimore keyed a 15-3 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 31-18. That was the score at halftime.

An 8-0 Columbia spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 32-26. A 9-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Rinaldi's jumper, stretched the lead to 46-31. Harvard led 46-36 heading into the fourth.

Harvard held a 52-47 lead in the final minutes. The Lions hit a three with 1:26 left to get within 52-50, but Quinn Castellanos answered when she banked in a turnaround to push the lead to 54-50. Castellanos finished a post move with 23 seconds left to make it 56-50. With 17 seconds left, Fennimore hit a jumper from the elbow to make it 58-50. Fennimore scored inside with 12 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-4 overall and 5-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Bella Chen led Harvard with 16 points (6-of-13 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), five rebounds, and three steals.
- Senior Elise Fennimore recorded a double-double with 15 points (6-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), 15 rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos chipped in 10 points (5-of-12 FG), eight rebounds, and three blocks.
- Harvard held Columbia to 26.4% shooting from the field.
