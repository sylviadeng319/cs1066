Harvard women's basketball (16-4, 6-0 Ivy) led from start to finish in a 70-61 win over Penn on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's seventh straight. Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Junior Ciara Bergstrom opened the scoring when she converted a putback. Harvard opened the game on a 27-12 run. Harvard led 27-17 after the first quarter.

The Crimson strung together an 8-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Bella Chen's layup, which stretched the lead to 35-21. Penn countered with a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 35-28. Harvard went into the locker room up 41-30.

A 13-2 Penn spurt early in the quarter drew level at 43. Lainey Rinaldi, Chen, and Elise Fennimore keyed a 10-3 run midway through the quarter that broke a 43-all tie and put Harvard up 53-46. Harvard took a 55-49 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together a 7-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 66-54.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-4 overall and 6-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Bella Chen finished with a team-high 16 points (6-of-16 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), four assists, and three steals.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi contributed 14 points (5-of-13 FG, 4-of-7 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Sophomore Hailey Brennan added 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT).
- Junior Quinn Castellanos protected the rim with three blocks.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points.
