Harvard women's basketball (7-3) came up just short on Monday, falling 66-65 to Drexel at Daskalakis Athletic Center in Philadelphia, Pa.

The Dragons shot 46.4% from the field, while Harvard was held to 37.9%.

## How it Happened

Drexel scored first before senior Lainey Rinaldi dropped in a floater for Harvard's first points. A 7-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Elise Fennimore's triple, turned a 4-0 deficit into a 7-4 lead. Drexel countered with a 13-5 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 7-4 deficit into a 17-12 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

Drexel went on a 10-1 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 27-13. Harvard trailed 29-20 at halftime.

A 23-13 Drexel spurt in the quarter extended the lead to 52-33. Harvard trailed 52-35 entering the fourth.

Drexel led 62-58 in the final minutes. With 1:11 left, Bella Chen hit a pair of free throws to make it 62-60. Callie Winslow drilled a three with 1:07 left to give the Crimson a 63-62 lead. With 55 seconds left, Chen dropped in a floater to push the lead to 65-62. The Dragons scored inside with 44 seconds left to get within 65-64. The Dragons got a basket with 14 seconds left to go in front 66-65. The Crimson's final attempt to take the lead came up short.
