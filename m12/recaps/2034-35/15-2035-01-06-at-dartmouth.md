Harvard women's basketball (11-4, 1-0 Ivy) cruised to a 73-35 victory over Dartmouth on Saturday at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

It was Harvard's Ivy League opener. The Crimson shot 38.4% from the field and held the Big Green to 24.0%.

"I loved our energy from the opening tip. We defended, we shared the ball, and we played for each other for 40 minutes," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Dartmouth scored first before senior Bella Chen knocked down a jumper for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 17-3 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Elise Fennimore's bucket inside, which turned a 6-4 deficit into a 21-9 lead. The Crimson took a 21-13 lead into the second quarter.

Chen, Fennimore, and Sage Navarro keyed a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 30-19. It stayed that way at halftime.

Harvard put together a 14-7 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 44-26. That was the score heading into the fourth.

A 29-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Fennimore's putback, stretched the lead to 73-32. The lead reached 41, allowing Malia Moreau, Navarro, and Savannah Lachance to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-4 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Bella Chen led Harvard with 24 points (9-of-20 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi added 14 points (6-of-14 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five assists.
- Senior Elise Fennimore recorded a double-double with 11 points (5-of-12 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and 10 rebounds.
- Harvard forced 18 turnovers and recorded nine steals.
