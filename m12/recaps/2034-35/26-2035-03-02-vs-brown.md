The Crimson made quick work of Brown on Friday, as Harvard women's basketball (19-7, 9-3 Ivy) built a lead as large as 21 on the way to a 73-53 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 48.2% from the field and held the Bears to 37.0%.

"That's what it looks like when we're locked in on the defensive end. Our defense created our offense tonight," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Brown scored first before junior Quinn Castellanos scored through contact for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Castellanos's free throw, which turned a 2-0 deficit into a 6-2 lead. A 15-6 Brown spurt in the quarter flipped a 6-2 deficit into a 17-12 lead. It stayed that way after one quarter.

A 13-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Juniper Haddad's bucket, turned a 20-12 deficit into a 25-20 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 31-30.

Harvard put together a 24-1 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 38-34 deficit into a 58-39 lead. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Castellanos, Bella Chen, and Chloe Rousseau keyed an 8-0 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 68-47. With the lead as large as 21, Malia Moreau, Haddad, and Thea Dalton saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-7 overall and 9-3 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 12-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Bella Chen finished with a team-high 21 points (8-of-15 FG, 4-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi contributed 13 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 3-of-5 FT).
- Junior Quinn Castellanos finished with 10 points (4-of-5 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
