Behind a strong second quarter, Harvard women's basketball (10-4) beat Albany, 71-64, on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion.

"We came in with a clear game plan and our players executed it," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Great Danes scored first before senior Lainey Rinaldi made a free throw for Harvard's first points. Albany opened the game on a 6-0 run. The Crimson strung together a 14-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Ciara Bergstrom's layup, which turned a 9-3 deficit into a 17-11 lead. The Crimson took a 17-13 lead into the second quarter.

An 8-0 Albany spurt midway through the quarter drew level at 27. Rinaldi, Quinn Castellanos, and Bergstrom keyed a 17-4 run midway through the quarter that broke a 27-all tie and put Harvard up 44-31. It stayed that way at halftime.

Albany went on an 8-2 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 56-48. Harvard took a 58-50 lead into the fourth quarter.

Albany got within 62-60 with 6:33 left before Harvard took control. A 7-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Elise Fennimore's putback, stretched the lead to 69-60.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-4 overall. The Crimson is 7-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Elise Fennimore led the way for Harvard with 17 points (6-of-14 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi added 14 points (4-of-9 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT) and three assists.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos grabbed 11 rebounds.
- Senior Bella Chen came away with six steals.
