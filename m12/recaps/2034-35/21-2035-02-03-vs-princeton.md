Harvard women's basketball came into Saturday unbeaten in Ivy League play, perfect at Lavietes Pavilion and riding a seven-game winning streak. Princeton ended all three runs. The Tigers took control in the second quarter and held the Crimson to five fourth-quarter points in a 56-43 win.

A night after beating Penn, Harvard (16-5, 6-1 Ivy) led 17-12 after one quarter but was outscored 44-26 the rest of the way. Princeton won the battle on the glass 42-36, grabbing 11 offensive rebounds, and went 13-of-18 from the free throw line while Harvard attempted only seven free throws. The loss was the Crimson's first at home this season after 10 straight wins in Cambridge.

"Princeton is a really good team, and they made us pay for our mistakes today. We had our chances in the third quarter and couldn't get over the hump. We'll learn from it, because we'll see them again at their place in two weeks," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Princeton opened a 3-0 lead before junior Quinn Castellanos banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. Ciara Bergstrom's putback capped a 10-0 run that put the Crimson in front 10-3. The Tigers answered with seven straight to tie it at 10, and Harvard closed the quarter ahead 17-12.

The second quarter belonged to Princeton. A 13-2 run early in the period turned a five-point deficit into a 25-19 Tigers lead, and Princeton took a 31-26 advantage into the break.

Harvard fell behind 32-26 early in the third quarter, then Chloe Rousseau, Elise Fennimore, and Castellanos keyed a 10-3 run that put the Crimson back on top at 36-35. Princeton responded with a 9-2 spurt and led 44-38 after three quarters.

The Crimson pulled within 46-40 with 7:28 to play, but the Tigers outscored Harvard 10-3 the rest of the way to pull away.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 16-5 overall and 6-1 in Ivy League play. The defeat snapped a seven-game winning streak and was the Crimson's first home loss of the season; Harvard is 10-1 at Lavietes Pavilion.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi led Harvard with 13 points (5-of-15 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT).
- Junior Ciara Bergstrom added eight points (4-of-9 FG) and eight rebounds off the bench.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos scored seven points and grabbed six rebounds, and senior Elise Fennimore added six points and five rebounds.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau had five points and five rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 12 of its 18 field goals.
