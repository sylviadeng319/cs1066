Harvard women's basketball (17-6, 7-2 Ivy) let a 14-point lead slip away on Friday in a 68-57 loss to Penn at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

The Quakers shot 44.4% from the field, while Harvard was held to 30.4%.

"This group is too good to dwell on it. We'll learn and respond," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Quakers scored first before junior Ciara Bergstrom scored inside for Harvard's first points. Penn opened the game on an 8-2 run. Bella Chen, Chloe Rousseau, and Hailey Brennan keyed a 15-4 run midway through the quarter that turned an 8-2 deficit into a 17-12 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 12-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's jumper, which stretched the lead to 33-19. Penn countered with a 10-4 run late in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 37-29. The score held there at halftime.

Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 48-34. Penn went on a 20-1 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 48-34 deficit into a 54-49 lead. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Harvard got within 56-50 with 8:08 left, but Penn outscored the Crimson 12-7 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-6 overall and 7-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Bella Chen finished with a team-high 16 points (5-of-16 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT).
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi finished with 14 points (4-of-16 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Elise Fennimore grabbed nine rebounds.
