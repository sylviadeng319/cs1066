One night after a loss at Penn, Harvard women's basketball ran into a Princeton team that could not miss. The Tigers shot 58.8% from the field and turned a six-point deficit into a 21-point halftime lead with a 29-2 run, beating the Crimson, 78-47, on Saturday at Jadwin Gymnasium in Princeton, N.J.

Princeton swept the regular-season series, following its 56-43 win at Lavietes Pavilion on Feb. 3. The Crimson (17-7, 7-3 Ivy) has lost two straight and three of its last four games, all against Penn and Princeton.

"We didn't have an answer for them in the second quarter, and against a team like Princeton, that's the game. It's a tough weekend for us, but we have four league games left and a lot to play for. We have to go home and get back to who we are," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Harvard started well. After Princeton scored first, Bella Chen buried a 3-pointer to begin an 8-0 run that Quinn Castellanos capped with a layup for an 8-3 lead. The Crimson led 9-7 after one quarter and stretched the margin to 15-9 early in the second.

Then Princeton took over. The Tigers outscored Harvard 29-2 over the rest of the half to turn that deficit into a 38-17 lead at the break. Princeton scored 31 points in the quarter.

Harvard showed more fight in the third quarter. Juniper Haddad's 3-pointer capped a 6-0 run that cut the deficit to 38-23, and the Crimson won the quarter 19-18, but Princeton still led 56-36 after three.

The Tigers outscored Harvard 22-9 to start the fourth quarter for a 78-45 lead, and Harvard scored the game's final two points.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-7 overall and 7-3 in Ivy League play. Princeton swept the regular-season series after a 56-43 win in Cambridge on Feb. 3.
- Senior Bella Chen led Harvard with 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- Junior Ciara Bergstrom (2-of-2 FG, 2-of-3 FT) and sophomore Hailey Brennan (3-of-6 FG) each scored six points off the bench, and Brennan added four rebounds.
- Sophomore Juniper Haddad scored five points in seven minutes, including a 3-pointer.
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points, and all 15 players on the roster saw action.
