Harvard women's basketball (6-2) let a 21-point lead slip away on Monday in a 57-54 loss to Holy Cross at Hart Center in Worcester, Mass.

Holy Cross shot 42.0% from the field, while Harvard was held to 27.8%. The loss snapped Harvard's four-game winning streak.

## How it Happened

Senior Elise Fennimore opened the scoring when she added a free throw. Harvard opened the game on a 26-5 run. A 6-0 Holy Cross spurt late in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 26-11. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crusaders countered with a 20-2 run in the quarter that drew level at 32. The score held there at halftime.

An 11-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Fennimore's bucket inside, turned a 34-32 deficit into a 43-34 lead. The Crimson carried a 45-40 advantage into the final frame.

Holy Cross led 53-48 in the final minutes. The Crusaders hit a jumper with 1:57 left to stretch the lead to 55-48. Holy Cross scored inside with 1:50 left to stretch the lead to 57-48, but Lainey Rinaldi answered when she splashed a three from the wing to pull Harvard within 57-51. With 5 seconds left, Bella Chen connected from deep to pull Harvard within 57-54. The Crimson's final attempt to tie it came up short.
