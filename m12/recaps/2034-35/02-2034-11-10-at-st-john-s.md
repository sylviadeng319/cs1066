Harvard women's basketball (2-0) picked up a 62-53 win over St. John's on Friday at Carnesecca Arena in Queens, N.Y.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Senior Elise Fennimore opened the scoring when she went 2-for-2 at the line. Harvard opened the game on an 11-2 run. St. John's went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 11-9. Harvard led 16-9 after the first quarter.

An 8-0 St. John's spurt early in the quarter flipped a 16-9 deficit into a 17-16 lead. The Crimson strung together a 14-5 burst in the quarter, capped by Fennimore's layup, which turned a 17-16 deficit into a 30-22 lead. That was the score at halftime.

The Red Storm countered with a 10-2 run early in the quarter that drew level at 32. Fennimore, Bella Chen, and Quinn Castellanos keyed a 14-2 run in the quarter that broke a 32-all tie and put Harvard up 46-34. The Crimson carried a 46-36 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together a 16-9 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 62-45. Harvard kept St. John's at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below nine.

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
