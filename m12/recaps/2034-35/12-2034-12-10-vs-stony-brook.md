Harvard women's basketball (9-3) led from start to finish in a 79-55 win over Stony Brook on Sunday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 55.1% from the field and held Stony Brook to 35.2%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Seawolves 37-28.

## How it Happened

Senior Elise Fennimore got Harvard on the board when she added a free throw with 9:31 left in the opening quarter. Hailey Brennan, Lainey Rinaldi, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed a 13-0 run early in the quarter that broke a 3-all tie and put Harvard up 16-3. Harvard led 24-12 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 17-4 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 41-19. Stony Brook went on a 6-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 41-25. Harvard took a 48-28 lead into halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 13-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Fennimore's putback, which stretched the lead to 63-31. Harvard led 63-36 heading into the fourth.

An 8-1 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Chloe Rousseau's free throw, stretched the lead to 71-41. The Seawolves never got closer than 21 in the fourth quarter. With the lead as large as 32, Malia Moreau, Thea Dalton, and Juniper Haddad saw time off the bench.
