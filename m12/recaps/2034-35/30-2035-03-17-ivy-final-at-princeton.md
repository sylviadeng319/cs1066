Harvard women's basketball's bid to repeat as Ivy League Tournament champion came down to the final two minutes at Jadwin Gymnasium on Saturday, and top-seeded Princeton made the last plays. The No. 2 seed Crimson led 51-46 in the fourth quarter before the Tigers closed on a 12-3 run for a 58-54 win in the championship game.

The loss ended Harvard's season at 22-8 and snapped a five-game winning streak, leaving the Crimson one win short of a return trip to the NCAA Tournament. Princeton, which went 13-1 in league play, beat Harvard for the third time this season, a year after Harvard won the title game between the two teams, 63-62, in Ithaca, N.Y.

It was the final game for seniors Bella Chen, Lainey Rinaldi, and Elise Fennimore, who were part of last season's run to the program's first Sweet 16.

"I'm heartbroken for our players, especially our seniors. We had a lead in the fourth quarter against a great Princeton team on its home floor, and we just couldn't make one or two more plays. Bella, Lainey, and Elise have given this program everything, and they're leaving it better than they found it," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first, and Lainey Rinaldi's driving layup got Harvard on the board. The Crimson led 3-2 early, but the Tigers answered with an 18-6 spurt for a 20-9 lead. Chloe Rousseau and Rinaldi then keyed an 8-0 run late in the quarter, and Harvard trailed only 20-17 after one.

Harvard went in front 27-25 in the second quarter, but Princeton closed the half with an 8-2 run for a 33-29 lead at the break.

The Crimson defense took over in the third quarter, holding Princeton to nine points. Down 36-30, Harvard used a 12-4 burst, capped by a Bella Chen free throw, to go ahead 42-40, and the teams were tied at 42 entering the fourth.

Elise Fennimore's putback capped a 9-0 run early in the fourth that turned a 46-42 deficit into a 51-46 Harvard lead. Princeton answered with six straight points and led 52-51 in the final minutes. After a Tigers free throw with 1:37 left, Rousseau's pull-up jumper tied it at 53. Princeton hit a 3-pointer with 1:02 to play for a 56-53 lead, Hailey Brennan's free throw cut it to 56-54, and the Tigers' layup with 27 seconds left sealed it.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard finishes the season 22-8 overall after going 11-3 in Ivy League play and earning the No. 2 seed in the Ivy League Tournament.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi led Harvard with 18 points (8-of-18 FG, 2-of-10 3-PT), five rebounds, and three steals in her final game.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau added 12 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) and three steals.
- Senior Elise Fennimore scored 11 points (5-of-13 FG, 1-of-1 FT) and grabbed seven rebounds, and junior Ciara Bergstrom pulled down seven rebounds off the bench.
- Sophomore Hailey Brennan contributed five points and three assists.
- Harvard recorded 11 steals. Princeton won all three meetings between the teams this season.
