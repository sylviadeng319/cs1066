Harvard women's basketball (5-1) picked up a 77-66 win over Northeastern on Wednesday at Cabot Center in Boston, Mass.

The win is Harvard's third straight.

"There's a lot to build on from tonight. I liked our pace and our poise," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Huskies scored first before senior Elise Fennimore scored through contact for Harvard's first points. The Huskies opened the game on a 7-0 run. The Crimson strung together an 18-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Lainey Rinaldi's jumper, which turned a 7-0 deficit into an 18-7 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 22-13.

Harvard put together a 13-2 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 39-21. The Huskies went on an 8-0 run late in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 39-29. The Crimson led 42-31 at the break.

Quinn Castellanos, Rinaldi, and Bella Chen keyed a 23-8 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 65-41. A 13-4 Northeastern spurt midway through the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 69-54. The score held there heading into the fourth.

The Huskies never got closer than nine in the fourth quarter.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-1 overall.
- Senior Bella Chen finished with a team-high 26 points (8-of-18 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT, 7-of-7 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Elise Fennimore finished with 14 points (7-of-14 FG, 0-of-5 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi chipped in 13 points (5-of-11 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Chen's 26 points were a career high.
