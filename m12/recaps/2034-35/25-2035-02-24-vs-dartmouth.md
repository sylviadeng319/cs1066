Harvard women's basketball (18-7, 8-3 Ivy) erased an 18-point deficit and came away with a 88-69 win over Dartmouth on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The victory snapped a two-game skid.

## How it Happened

Dartmouth scored first before senior Elise Fennimore banked in a turnaround for Harvard's first points. Dartmouth opened the game on a 31-13 run. Harvard trailed 31-14 after the opening frame.

A 9-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Fennimore's bucket inside, cut the deficit to 31-23. The Big Green went on a 6-0 run late in the quarter, which extended the lead to 46-32. The Crimson went into the locker room down 48-38.

Harvard put together a 19-2 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 52-40 deficit into a 59-54 lead. Dartmouth countered with a 7-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 59-54 deficit into a 61-59 lead. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Fennimore, Quinn Castellanos, and Ciara Bergstrom keyed a 26-0 run early in the quarter that turned a 61-59 deficit into a 85-61 lead. The lead reached 24, allowing Malia Moreau, Juniper Haddad, and Thea Dalton to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-7 overall and 8-3 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Elise Fennimore led the way for Harvard with 20 points (8-of-9 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT).
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi chipped in 16 points (6-of-20 FG, 4-of-11 3-PT) and three steals.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos added 11 points (4-of-6 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and five rebounds.
- Hailey Brennan (11) and Bella Chen (10) also reached double figures.
- Harvard's bench contributed 26 points.
- Harvard's 88 points were its most in a game this season.
