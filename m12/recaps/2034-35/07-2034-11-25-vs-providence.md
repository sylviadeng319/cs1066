Harvard women's basketball (6-1) never trailed on Saturday, beating Providence, 81-60, at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 56.6% from the field and held Providence to 44.9%. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Callie Winslow got Harvard on the board when she knocked down a jumper with 9:12 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 24-5 run. Harvard led 24-8 after the first quarter.

The Friars went on a 10-0 run early in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 29-21. The Crimson strung together a 10-3 burst early in the quarter, capped by Ciara Bergstrom's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 39-24. Harvard went into the locker room up 46-33.

Harvard put together a 19-4 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 67-40. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

Harvard kept the Friars at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 20. The lead reached 28, allowing Malia Moreau, Thea Dalton, and Fatima Sorensen to see time off the bench.
