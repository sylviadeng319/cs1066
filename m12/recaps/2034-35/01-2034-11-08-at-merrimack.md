Harvard women's basketball began the follow-up to its first Sweet 16 season the hard way. The Crimson (1-0) did not shoot well, but it rebounded, defended and made the plays down the stretch to beat Merrimack, 59-52, on Wednesday at Hammel Court in North Andover, Mass.

Harvard made just 30.8% of its shots, yet it outrebounded the Warriors 50-36, grabbed 20 offensive rebounds and turned 13 steals into a steady supply of extra possessions. Merrimack committed 18 turnovers. It was a winning formula for a team that graduated five seniors from last season's 28-5 squad and is led this year by senior captains Bella Chen and Lainey Rinaldi.

"Last season was special, but this is a new team, and it has to find its own identity. We didn't shoot it well tonight, and we still found a way to win on the road because we rebounded and we defended. That travels," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball.

## How it Happened

Senior Elise Fennimore put the first point of the season on the board with a free throw. After Merrimack edged ahead 2-1, Harvard ripped off a 16-6 spurt for a 17-8 lead, and the Crimson took a 17-11 advantage into the second quarter.

Quinn Castellanos capped a 9-0 run early in the second quarter with a layup, stretching the lead to 26-12. Merrimack answered with a 15-5 surge, though, and the Warriors trailed only 31-27 at halftime.

The Warriors went ahead 37-36 in the third quarter before Ciara Bergstrom, Rinaldi, and Fennimore keyed a 7-0 run that put Harvard back in front 43-37. The Crimson led 43-40 after three quarters.

Merrimack scored seven straight early in the fourth to take a 47-45 lead, but Harvard regained control and led 55-51 in the final minutes. A Warriors free throw with 2:18 left made it a three-point game. Fennimore answered with a 3-pointer for a 58-52 lead, and Chen's free throw with 1:15 to go closed the scoring.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi led Harvard with 20 points (6-of-15 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 5-of-6 FT) and three assists.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau scored a career-high 14 points (6-of-12 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT).
- Junior Quinn Castellanos pulled down 12 rebounds, and senior Elise Fennimore added nine points, 10 rebounds, and three steals.
- Junior Ciara Bergstrom grabbed nine rebounds, four on the offensive end, in 20 minutes off the bench.
- Harvard outrebounded Merrimack 50-36, including 20 offensive boards, and forced 18 turnovers with 13 steals.
