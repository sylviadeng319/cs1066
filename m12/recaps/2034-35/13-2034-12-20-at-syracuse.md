"Games like this show you where you need to grow. We'll take the lessons and move forward," said head coach Carrie Moore.

Syracuse countered with a 20-4 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 29-12. Harvard put together a 6-0 spurt late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 33-22. That was the score at halftime.

Syracuse went on an 18-9 run in the quarter, which extended the lead to 51-31. The Orange led 51-34 after three quarters.

A 19-4 Syracuse spurt early in the quarter extended the lead to 70-40. The Crimson kept fighting, but Syracuse held firm.
