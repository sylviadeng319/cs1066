Harvard women's basketball gave its seniors a fitting send-off from Lavietes Pavilion. Trailing 59-55 with 1:31 to play, the Crimson scored the game's final nine points to beat Yale, 64-59, on Senior Day on Saturday.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Bella Chen, Lainey Rinaldi, and Elise Fennimore in their final regular-season home game, and two of them delivered at the finish. Chen, a captain, led the Crimson in scoring without missing from beyond the arc, and she tied the game with a jumper in the final minute. Rinaldi, the other captain, sealed it with a 3-pointer with 13 seconds left.

The Crimson (20-7, 10-3 Ivy) won its third straight, swept the season series with Yale and finished its home schedule 13-1. Harvard forced 20 turnovers and scored 24 points in the fourth quarter, a day after beating Brown in Cambridge.

"It's fitting that our seniors made the plays at the end. Bella, Lainey, and Elise have meant so much to this program, and to see them go out like that in their last game here, I'm just really happy for them," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

Fennimore opened the scoring with a free throw. After Yale moved in front 8-6, Harvard answered with a 6-0 burst capped by a Rinaldi jumper for a 12-8 lead, and the Crimson led 14-10 after one quarter.

Neither team could pull away in the second quarter, which Harvard won 13-11 for a 27-21 halftime lead.

Yale came out of the break strong. The Bulldogs used a 13-6 run midway through the third quarter to draw even at 40, and the teams entered the fourth tied.

Yale led 57-55 in the final minutes and scored inside with 1:31 left to go up 59-55. Quinn Castellanos answered with a post move, and Chen's jumper with 52 seconds to play tied it at 59. With 27 seconds left, Ciara Bergstrom knocked down a jumper from the elbow to give Harvard the lead for good, and Rinaldi's 3-pointer put the game away.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-7 overall and 10-3 in Ivy League play, and the Crimson finishes 13-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Harvard swept the season series with Yale, following a 68-65 win at John J. Lee Amphitheater on Jan. 22.
- Senior Bella Chen led Harvard with 25 points (10-of-14 FG, 4-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), four rebounds, and two steals.
- Senior Lainey Rinaldi added nine points and a team-high four assists, and senior Elise Fennimore had seven points (3-of-3 FT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Quinn Castellanos scored nine points and blocked three shots.
- Sophomore Chloe Rousseau made all three of her free throws and came away with three steals.
- Harvard forced 20 turnovers while committing 13.
