Harvard women's basketball (10-0) erased an 8-point deficit and came away with a 75-70 win over Marist on Tuesday at McCann Arena in Poughkeepsie, N.Y.

The win is Harvard's 10th straight. The Crimson shot 60.8% from the field and held Marist to 50.9%.

## How it Happened

Senior Niamh Hollis got Harvard on the board when she finished a layup in transition with 9:37 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 21-13 run. Harvard led 21-17 after the first quarter.

Harvard put together a 10-2 spurt midway through the quarter that turned a 25-24 deficit into a 34-27 lead. The Red Foxes went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which drew level at 34. The Crimson led 36-34 at the break.

The Red Foxes countered with a 22-12 run in the quarter that flipped a 42-37 deficit into a 59-54 lead. Marist led 59-55 after three quarters.

A 9-0 Marist spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 62-61 deficit into a 70-62 lead. Marist led 70-66 in the final minutes. With 2:53 left, Jordan Delgado buried a mid-range jumper to pull Harvard within 70-68. Isla Marchetti dropped in a floater with 2:01 left to tie the game at 70. With 1:28 left, Mackenzie Takahashi scored on a driving layup to give the Crimson a 72-70 lead. With 1:15 left, Marchetti added a free throw to push the lead to 73-70. Marchetti hit a pull-up jumper with 42 seconds left to seal it.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 10-0 overall.

- Senior Brielle Mendoza contributed 13 points (6-of-9 FG, 1-of-1 FT).
- First-year Esme Ellison added 12 points (5-of-5 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and eight rebounds.
