Harvard women's basketball (3-0) cruised to a 77-47 victory over St. John's on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Red Storm 50-41. The Crimson shot 43.9% from the field and held St. John's to 27.3%.

"We talked about being the aggressor, and our players took that to heart from the first possession," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

The Red Storm scored first before senior Brielle Mendoza hit a jumper from the elbow for Harvard's first points. St. John's opened the game on a 7-0 run. Mendoza, Emeka Rhodes, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 20-8 run in the quarter that turned a 7-0 deficit into a 20-15 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

The Red Storm countered with a 10-0 run early in the quarter that flipped a 23-15 deficit into a 25-23 lead. A 7-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's putback, turned a 28-26 deficit into a 33-28 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 33-30.

Harvard put together a 17-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 50-30. The Red Storm went on an 8-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 50-38. Harvard led 57-38 heading into the fourth.

The Crimson strung together a 15-0 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Takahashi's jumper, which stretched the lead to 77-47. The lead reached 30, allowing Lauren Wakefield, Tiana Adeyemi, and Mira Hightower to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 3-0 overall.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with 16 points (7-of-12 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-4 FT) and six rebounds.
- First-year Esme Ellison added 14 points (6-of-11 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Isla Marchetti contributed 12 points (4-of-11 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-6 FT), six rebounds, and seven assists.
