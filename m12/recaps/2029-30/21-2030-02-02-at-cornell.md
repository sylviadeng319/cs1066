Down 10 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (19-2, 5-2 Ivy) rallied for a 68-58 win over Cornell on Saturday at Newman Arena in Ithaca, N.Y.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 46-38.

"We dug ourselves a hole, but I loved how we climbed out of it, one stop at a time," said head coach Carrie Moore.

## How it Happened

First-year Esme Ellison got Harvard on the board when she knocked down a corner three with 9:19 left in the opening quarter. Cornell went on a 23-11 run in the quarter, which flipped a 3-0 deficit into a 23-14 lead. The Big Red led 23-16 after one.

The Crimson strung together a 10-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's layup, which evened the score at 26. Harvard trailed 34-31 at halftime.

Ellison, Isla Marchetti, and Thea Sutherland keyed a 17-5 run in the quarter that turned a 36-33 deficit into a 50-41 lead. The Crimson carried a 52-44 advantage into the final frame.

The Big Red got within 54-51 with 9:10 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 14-7 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 68-58.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 19-2 overall and 5-2 in Ivy League play.
- First-year Esme Ellison led Harvard with 21 points (8-of-10 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Isla Marchetti came away with five steals.
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and recorded 13 steals.
