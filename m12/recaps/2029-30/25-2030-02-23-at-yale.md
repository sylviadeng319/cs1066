Harvard women's basketball finished off a season sweep of its archrival on Saturday, beating Yale, 67-53, at John J. Lee Amphitheater in New Haven, Conn. The Crimson (23-2, 9-2 Ivy) has won all eight meetings with the Bulldogs since the start of the 2026-27 season.

A 17-0 run in the second quarter turned a one-point deficit into a 16-point halftime lead, and Harvard's work on the boards did the rest. The Crimson outrebounded Yale 54-45, grabbed 14 offensive rebounds, and held the Bulldogs to 29.2% shooting. The win is Harvard's fifth straight, following a 79-58 home win over Yale in the league opener on Jan. 5.

"Every game in this building is a fight, and our players knew Yale was going to come out swinging," said head coach Carrie Moore. "We didn't shoot it great, but we defended, we rebounded, and we found a way to answer every time they made a push."

## How it Happened

Yale scored first, and senior Niamh Hollis answered with a 3-pointer. Hollis, Kendall Lindqvist, and Thea Sutherland keyed a 16-6 run that put Harvard ahead 16-8, but the Bulldogs closed the first quarter with nine straight points to lead 17-16.

Harvard went back in front early in the second quarter, but a 6-0 Yale run flipped a 25-20 Harvard lead into a 26-25 Bulldogs edge. Then the Crimson took over with a 17-0 run. A 3-pointer from first-year Tiana Adeyemi capped the burst and sent Harvard to halftime up 42-26.

Points were scarce for Harvard after the break. Yale scored nine straight to cut the deficit to 47-37 in the third quarter, and the Crimson's lead was 49-39 heading into the fourth.

Harvard steadied itself in the final frame. A 7-0 run capped by a Mackenzie Takahashi 3-pointer made it 62-48, and Yale never got closer than seven the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 23-2 overall and 9-2 in Ivy League play and completes a season sweep of Yale.
- Senior Isla Marchetti led Harvard with 16 points (7-of-18 FG, 2-of-8 3-PT), a team-high seven assists, and three steals.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza recorded a double-double with 10 points (3-of-8 FG, 4-of-4 FT) and 11 rebounds.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi added 10 points (3-of-10 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo pulled down eight rebounds off the bench, and first-year Esme Ellison blocked three shots.
- Senior Niamh Hollis went 2-of-2 from the field for five points, and first-year Tiana Adeyemi scored five points in five minutes.
- Harvard went 8-of-8 at the free throw line, and the bench contributed 23 points.
