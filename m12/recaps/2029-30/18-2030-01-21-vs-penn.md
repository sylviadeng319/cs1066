Harvard women's basketball (17-1, 3-1 Ivy) let a 10-point lead slip away on Monday in a 79-75 loss to Penn at Lavietes Pavilion.

The loss snapped Harvard's 17-game winning streak.

"We have to be more consistent for 40 minutes. We showed flashes, but flashes aren't enough," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi opened the scoring when she scored on a driving layup. An 11-3 Penn spurt early in the quarter flipped a 2-0 deficit into an 11-5 lead. Valentina Okonkwo, Thea Sutherland, and Esme Ellison keyed a 16-4 run early in the quarter that turned an 11-5 deficit into a 21-15 lead. Harvard led 37-31 after the first quarter.

Penn countered with a 22-5 run in the quarter that flipped a 41-31 deficit into a 53-46 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 53-48.

A 19-3 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Okonkwo's putback, turned a 55-48 deficit into a 67-58 lead. The Crimson carried a 67-63 advantage into the final frame.

The Crimson strung together an 8-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Sutherland's layup, which broke a 67-all tie and put Harvard up 75-67. Harvard held a 75-69 lead in the final minutes. The Quakers made a free throw with 2:29 left to get within 75-70. The Quakers converted a layup with 1:23 left to get within 75-72. The Quakers hit a jumper with 1:11 left to cut the deficit to 75-74. Penn answered from beyond the arc with 15 seconds left to go in front 77-75. The Quakers got a basket with 6 seconds left to stretch the lead to 79-75. Harvard did not score over the final 3:46.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 17-1 overall and 3-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 10-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo led Harvard with 15 points (6-of-6 FG, 3-of-4 FT).
- Junior Thea Sutherland added 14 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT).
- First-year Esme Ellison chipped in 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), seven rebounds, and two blocks.

- Okonkwo's 15 points were a career high.
