Harvard women's basketball (20-2, 6-2 Ivy) picked up a 84-71 win over Brown on Saturday at Pizzitola Sports Center in Providence, R.I.

## How it Happened

The Bears scored first before sophomore Valentina Okonkwo scored inside for Harvard's first points. A 9-0 Brown spurt early in the quarter broke a 2-all tie and pushed the Bears ahead 11-2. An 11-5 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Kendall Lindqvist's layup, cut the deficit to 16-13. Harvard trailed 24-15 after the opening frame.

The Crimson strung together a 22-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's putback, which turned a 24-15 deficit into a 37-24 lead. Brown went on a 12-2 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 39-36. Harvard took a 43-38 lead into halftime.

Thea Sutherland, Isla Marchetti, and Okonkwo keyed a 16-3 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 61-45. Brown countered with an 8-1 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 62-53. Harvard led 66-55 heading into the fourth.

Brown got within 68-65 with 7:31 left before Harvard took control. Harvard put together a 16-4 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 84-69.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 20-2 overall and 6-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Isla Marchetti paced the Crimson with 16 points (5-of-9 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 4-of-6 FT), six assists, and five steals.
- First-year Jordan Delgado contributed 12 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Thea Sutherland chipped in 11 points (4-of-5 FG, 1-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Mackenzie Takahashi (11), Valentina Okonkwo (10), and Esme Ellison (10) also reached double figures.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 30 points.
- Harvard's 84 points were its most in a game this season.
