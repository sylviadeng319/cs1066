Harvard women's basketball (18-1, 4-1 Ivy) pulled away from Brown for a 65-47 victory on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 41.7% from the field and held Brown to 28.8%.

## How it Happened

Brown scored first before senior Brielle Mendoza scored inside for Harvard's first points. The Crimson strung together a 14-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Chloe Ferreira's 3-pointer, which turned a 4-0 deficit into a 14-4 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 21-13.

Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 33-16. The Crimson led 36-21 at the break.

Niamh Hollis, Jordan Delgado, and Esme Ellison keyed a 14-5 run in the quarter that stretched the lead to 50-31. Harvard took a 54-35 lead into the fourth quarter.

A 9-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Ferreira's three, stretched the lead to 63-41. The lead reached 22, allowing Mira Hightower, Ferreira, and Lauren Wakefield to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 18-1 overall and 4-1 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 11-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Mira Hightower finished with 8 points (4-of-4 FG).
- First-year Esme Ellison grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 37 points.
