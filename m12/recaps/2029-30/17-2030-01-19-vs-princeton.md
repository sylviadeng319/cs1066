Princeton came to Lavietes Pavilion on Saturday having won the last five meetings between the teams, and it scored the first 14 points of the afternoon. Harvard women's basketball spent the rest of the game taking the series back. The Crimson (17-0, 3-0 Ivy) rallied for a 67-65 win over the Tigers, its first victory over Princeton since the 2027 Ivy League Tournament final.

Harvard stayed unbeaten by shooting 53.8% from the field and outlasting a Princeton team that knocked down 10 3-pointers. The win was far from tidy. The Crimson turned it over 19 times and went 6-of-13 at the line. Senior Isla Marchetti and first-year Esme Ellison carried the offense, and Harvard made just enough plays down the stretch for its 17th straight win.

"Down 14 to a team like Princeton, it would have been easy to let the game get away from us. Nobody in our locker room flinched," said head coach Carrie Moore. "We weren't clean, and we'll learn from that, but I love the fight in this group."

## How it Happened

Princeton opened the game on a 14-0 run before Niamh Hollis got Harvard on the board with a 3-pointer. That started a 14-0 answer of the Crimson's own, capped by an Ellison putback, that tied the game at 14. The Tigers led 22-21 after one quarter.

Princeton pushed back in front 27-21 early in the second quarter, but Harvard ripped off an 11-1 run, capped by another Ellison putback, to go ahead 32-28. The Crimson took a 35-33 lead into halftime.

Harvard stretched its lead to 40-35 in the third quarter before Princeton answered with a 13-6 spurt to go up 48-46. Harvard tied it, and the teams were level at 48 after three quarters.

The Crimson broke the tie with a 16-7 run early in the fourth and led 64-55 in the final minutes. Princeton hit a 3-pointer with 2:58 left to cut it to 64-58, but Marchetti answered from deep to restore a nine-point cushion at 67-58. The Tigers would not go quietly, scoring on a three with 1:34 left, a jumper with 1:22 left, and a basket with 27 seconds left to make it 67-65, but Harvard held on from there.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 17-0 overall and 3-0 in Ivy League play, extending its winning streak to 17 games. The Crimson is 10-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Isla Marchetti led Harvard with 20 points (7-of-14 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 3-of-8 FT), including the 3-pointer that put the Crimson up 67-58.
- First-year Esme Ellison added 17 points (8-of-13 FG, 1-of-3 FT), seven rebounds, and two steals.
- Junior Thea Sutherland scored 13 points on 5-of-7 shooting and grabbed seven rebounds.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi dished out a team-high six assists, and senior Brielle Mendoza pulled down eight rebounds.
- Harvard erased a 14-0 deficit to start the game and snapped a five-game losing streak against Princeton.
