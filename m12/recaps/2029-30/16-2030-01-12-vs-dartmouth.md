The Crimson made quick work of Dartmouth on Saturday, as Harvard women's basketball (16-0, 2-0 Ivy) built a lead as large as 24 on the way to a 66-44 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 16th straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 55-40.

## How it Happened

First-year Esme Ellison got Harvard on the board when she added a free throw with 9:33 left in the opening quarter. The Big Green went on an 11-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 1-0 deficit into an 11-1 lead. An 11-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Thea Sutherland's layup, turned an 11-1 deficit into a 12-11 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 27-10 burst in the quarter, capped by Brielle Mendoza's putback, which stretched the lead to 39-21. It stayed that way at halftime.

Dartmouth countered with a 14-2 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 41-35. Mackenzie Takahashi, Isla Marchetti, and Sutherland keyed an 8-0 run late in the quarter that stretched the lead to 49-35. The score held there heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 17-7 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 66-42. With the lead as large as 24, Lauren Wakefield, Emeka Rhodes, and Tiana Adeyemi saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 16-0 overall and 2-0 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 9-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi finished with a team-high 12 points (4-of-12 FG, 2-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 11 rebounds.
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo grabbed nine rebounds.
- First-year Esme Ellison grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard held Dartmouth to 27.4% shooting from the field.
- Harvard's bench contributed 28 points.
