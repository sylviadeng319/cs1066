Down 10 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (24-2, 10-2 Ivy) rallied for a 74-66 win over Penn on Friday at The Palestra in Philadelphia, Pa.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight.

"That's a resilient group in that locker room. Being down didn't change our belief one bit," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

Penn scored first before junior Kendall Lindqvist scored inside for Harvard's first points. The Quakers opened the game on a 16-6 run. Harvard put together a 16-9 spurt midway through the quarter that cut the deficit to 25-22. It stayed that way after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 22-8 burst early in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's free throws, which turned a 25-22 deficit into a 44-33 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 48-38.

The Quakers went on a 10-2 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 59-54. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Isla Marchetti and Esme Ellison keyed a 9-2 run early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 70-58.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 24-2 overall and 10-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Isla Marchetti paced the Crimson with 15 points (6-of-13 FG, 3-of-6 3-PT) and four steals.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi contributed 12 points (3-of-8 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and five rebounds.
- First-year Esme Ellison finished with 12 points (5-of-6 FG, 2-of-3 FT).
- Thea Sutherland (11) and Jordan Delgado (10) also reached double figures.
