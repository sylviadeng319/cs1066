Harvard women's basketball (6-0) erased a 10-point deficit and came away with a 79-73 win over Hofstra on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's sixth straight.

"We didn't flinch. At halftime we talked about getting back to who we are, and our players responded," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as sophomore Valentina Okonkwo added a free throw. Harvard opened the game on a 13-0 run. The Pride went on a 16-2 run midway through the quarter, which flipped a 15-2 deficit into an 18-17 lead. The Crimson took a 23-22 lead into the second quarter.

A 15-0 Hofstra spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 36-31 deficit into a 46-36 lead. A 6-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Thea Sutherland's layup, cut the deficit to 46-42. It stayed that way at halftime.

Hofstra countered with a 6-0 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 54-47. The Crimson strung together a 15-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's layup, which turned a 54-47 deficit into a 62-54 lead. Harvard took a 62-56 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together a 12-3 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 77-65. Harvard held a 77-67 lead in the final minutes. Hofstra scored inside with 2:46 left to cut the deficit to 77-69. The Pride made two free throws with 2:02 left to get within 77-71. Hofstra got a basket with 1:53 left to cut the deficit to 77-73. Brielle Mendoza put the game away when she banked in a turnaround with 1:28 left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 6-0 overall. The Crimson is 4-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- First-year Esme Ellison finished with 14 points (4-of-5 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 5-of-5 FT).
- Senior Isla Marchetti added 14 points (6-of-9 FG, 2-of-3 3-PT) and eight assists.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi chipped in 12 points (4-of-9 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Brielle Mendoza (12) also reached double figures.
