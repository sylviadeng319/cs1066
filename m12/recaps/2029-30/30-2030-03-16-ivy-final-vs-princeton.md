Two weeks after a 23-point loss at Princeton, Harvard women's basketball met the Tigers with a title on the line and left no doubt. The No. 2 seed Crimson (27-3) beat top-seeded Princeton, 75-57, on Saturday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y., to win the Ivy League Tournament and claim the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament.

It is Harvard's first Ivy League Tournament title since 2027, and it came against the team that beat the Crimson in the 2028 championship game. The Crimson shot 46.6% from the field, went 14-for-14 at the free throw line, and held Princeton, the regular-season champion at 12-2, to 34.4% shooting and 10-of-32 from beyond the arc. Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi was unstoppable in limited minutes, and Harvard won the season series against the Tigers, 2-1.

"I'm so happy for these players. Two weeks ago we got embarrassed by a great Princeton team, and they could have let that define them. Instead they went back to work," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball. "To win a championship with this group, and to watch our seniors cut down the nets, is something I'll never forget."

## How it Happened

Senior Brielle Mendoza opened the scoring with a jumper from the elbow, and Harvard raced out to a 13-3 lead. Princeton scored the last five points of the quarter, and the Crimson led 13-8 after one.

Takahashi, Mendoza, and Isla Marchetti keyed a 13-2 run early in the second quarter that pushed the lead to 26-10. The Tigers answered with a 12-2 run of their own to get within 28-22, but Harvard closed the half strong and took a 31-22 lead into the locker room.

A 7-0 burst early in the third quarter, capped by a Mira Hightower free throw, stretched the margin to 40-24. Princeton made its push from there, using a 17-6 run to pull within 46-41. Harvard steadied itself and led 52-43 entering the fourth.

Then the Crimson ran away with it. Harvard opened the final quarter with a 23-7 run that made it 75-50, and the lead reached 25. With the title in hand, Moore emptied her bench, and all 14 players on the roster got to share in the win.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard wins the Ivy League Tournament and earns the league's automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament. The Crimson improves to 27-3 and has won three straight.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with 21 points (7-of-12 FG, 1-of-5 3-PT, 6-of-6 FT) in 17 minutes.
- Senior Isla Marchetti added 13 points (4-of-11 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT).
- Junior Thea Sutherland scored 10 points (3-of-6 FG, 4-of-4 FT) with six rebounds and three assists.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza finished with eight points on 4-of-7 shooting, and senior Niamh Hollis knocked down two 3-pointers.
- First-year Jordan Delgado handed out a team-high four assists off the bench.
- Harvard went 14-of-14 at the free throw line and held Princeton to 34.4% shooting from the field.
