Harvard women's basketball (18-2, 4-2 Ivy) dropped a 63-56 decision to Columbia on Friday at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Brielle Mendoza scored through contact. The Lions went on a 14-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 2-0 deficit into a 14-2 lead. A 6-0 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's bucket, cut the deficit to 14-8. Columbia carried a 16-8 lead into the second quarter.

The Lions countered with a 9-3 run early in the quarter that extended the lead to 25-13. Mendoza, Thea Sutherland, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed an 8-0 run late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 27-23. It stayed that way at halftime.

Harvard put together a 15-4 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 27-23 deficit into a 38-31 lead. A 10-2 Columbia spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 38-31 deficit into a 41-40 lead. Harvard took a 42-41 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard got within 43-42 with 9:32 left, but Columbia outscored the Crimson 20-14 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard moves to 18-2 overall and 4-2 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza paced the Crimson with 13 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-2 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Isla Marchetti finished with 10 points (4-of-11 FG, 0-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-5 FT), four assists, and three steals.
