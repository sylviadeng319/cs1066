Harvard women's basketball (9-0) held on for a 70-65 win over Maine on Saturday at Memorial Gym in Orono, Maine.

The win is Harvard's ninth straight. Harvard's defense forced 20 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Brielle Mendoza scored inside. Harvard opened the game on a 14-0 run. Maine went on a 7-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 14-7. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 20-13.

A 20-11 Maine spurt in the quarter flipped a 20-13 deficit into a 33-31 lead. An 8-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's 3-pointer, turned a 33-31 deficit into a 39-33 lead. That was the score at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 7-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's putback, which stretched the lead to 46-37. The Black Bears countered with a 9-0 run in the quarter that drew level at 46. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

A 10-3 Maine spurt early in the quarter flipped a 49-46 deficit into a 56-52 lead. Harvard held a 67-56 lead in the final minutes. Maine hit a three with 1:35 left to cut the deficit to 67-59. The Black Bears got a basket with 1:32 left to cut the deficit to 67-61, but Isla Marchetti answered when she connected from deep to make it 70-61. The Black Bears converted a layup with 26 seconds left to get within 70-63. The Black Bears scored with 7 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 9-0 overall.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi finished with a team-high 22 points (8-of-13 FG, 4-of-9 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), three assists, and four steals.
- Junior Thea Sutherland contributed 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), four assists, and three steals.
- Senior Isla Marchetti chipped in 10 points (4-of-10 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT), five assists, and three steals.
- Takahashi's 22 points were a career high.
