Harvard women's basketball (5-0) picked up a 71-57 win over Sacred Heart on Tuesday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 46.3% from the field and held Sacred Heart to 36.2%. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Pioneers 36-25.

## How it Happened

Sacred Heart scored first before junior Kendall Lindqvist finished a post move for Harvard's first points. Lindqvist, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Valentina Okonkwo keyed a 22-12 run in the quarter that turned a 2-0 deficit into a 22-14 lead. That was the score after one quarter.

A 9-1 Sacred Heart spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 27-23. The Crimson led 32-24 at the break.

The Crimson strung together a 15-7 burst in the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's 3-pointer, which stretched the lead to 49-34. Harvard led 51-39 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together an 18-11 spurt midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 71-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 5-0 overall.
- Senior Isla Marchetti led Harvard with 13 points (4-of-9 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three steals.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza contributed 11 points (3-of-6 FG, 5-of-5 FT), five rebounds, and five assists.
- First-year Esme Ellison finished with 10 points (5-of-8 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT) and nine rebounds.
- Kendall Lindqvist (10) also reached double figures.
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo grabbed 10 rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
