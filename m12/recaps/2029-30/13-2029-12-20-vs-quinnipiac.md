Harvard women's basketball (13-0) pulled away from Quinnipiac for a 60-52 victory on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 13th straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Bobcats 53-40.

## How it Happened

Quinnipiac scored first before sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi hit a pull-up jumper for Harvard's first points. Takahashi, Brielle Mendoza, and Isla Marchetti keyed a 12-2 run early in the quarter that turned a 5-0 deficit into a 12-7 lead. Harvard led 14-10 after the first quarter.

The Crimson strung together an 11-5 burst early in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's putback, which stretched the lead to 25-17. Harvard went into the locker room up 25-21.

The Bobcats went on a 9-2 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 28-22 deficit into a 31-30 lead. Harvard led 37-35 heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together an 18-9 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 55-44.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 13-0 overall. The Crimson is 6-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi paced the Crimson with 14 points (6-of-13 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and five assists.
- First-year Jordan Delgado hit three 3-pointers off the bench.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 17 rebounds.
- First-year Esme Ellison grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard held Quinnipiac to 30.0% shooting from the field.
- Harvard's bench contributed 24 points.
