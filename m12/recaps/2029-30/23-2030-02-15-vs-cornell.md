The Crimson made quick work of Cornell on Friday, as Harvard women's basketball (21-2, 7-2 Ivy) built a lead as large as 26 on the way to a 78-52 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's third straight. Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Red 43-30.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as junior Mira Hightower scored inside. Harvard opened the game on a 10-3 run. Cornell countered with a 12-0 run late in the quarter that flipped a 12-5 deficit into a 17-12 lead. The Big Red carried a 17-14 lead into the second quarter.

A 15-4 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's 3-pointer, turned a 17-14 deficit into a 29-21 lead. The Crimson led 33-29 at the break.

The Crimson strung together an 18-4 burst in the quarter, capped by Esme Ellison's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 55-39. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 23-13 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 78-52. The lead reached 26, allowing Lauren Wakefield, Hightower, and Emeka Rhodes to see time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 21-2 overall and 7-2 in Ivy League play. The Crimson is 12-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza finished with a team-high 17 points (7-of-11 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- First-year Esme Ellison finished with 14 points (6-of-9 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Junior Thea Sutherland contributed 10 points (4-of-8 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT) and five rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 25 points.
