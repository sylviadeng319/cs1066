Princeton evened the season series with Harvard women's basketball in emphatic fashion on Saturday, rolling to a 79-56 win at Jadwin Gymnasium in Princeton, N.J., one night after the Crimson's win at Penn.

The Tigers answered Harvard's 67-65 victory in Cambridge on Jan. 19 by shooting 56.6% from the field and building a 30-point lead by the end of the third quarter. Princeton outrebounded the Crimson (24-3, 10-3 Ivy) 33-22 and made 14 of its 15 free throws. The loss snapped Harvard's six-game winning streak and was its most lopsided of the season.

"Princeton was terrific tonight, and we didn't have an answer for them. That one is on all of us, starting with me," said head coach Carrie Moore. "We'll learn from it. This group has responded every time it's been knocked down, and we have a lot left to play for."

## How it Happened

Princeton scored first, and junior Thea Sutherland got Harvard on the board with a corner three. The Tigers controlled the rest of the opening quarter and led 24-12 after 10 minutes.

The second quarter went no better. Princeton opened it with a 14-1 run to go ahead 38-13, and the Tigers took a 42-21 lead into the break after holding Harvard to nine points in the frame.

A 15-2 Princeton run in the third quarter made it 60-30, and the Tigers led 65-35 heading into the fourth.

The Crimson didn't stop competing. Harvard outscored Princeton 21-14 in the final quarter, with 14 players getting into the game, but the deficit was far too large to overcome.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the loss, Harvard falls to 24-3 overall and 10-3 in Ivy League play. Harvard and Princeton split their regular-season meetings.
- First-year Esme Ellison led Harvard with 15 points (5-of-7 FG, 5-of-7 FT) in 22 minutes.
- First-year Jordan Delgado added nine points on 3-of-4 shooting off the bench.
- Junior Thea Sutherland and senior Isla Marchetti scored seven points apiece, and Sutherland added three steals.
- Senior Niamh Hollis made two 3-pointers for six points.
- Harvard's bench contributed 21 points.
