A year ago, Columbia ended Harvard women's basketball's season with a one-point win in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals at Lavietes Pavilion. On Friday, on the Lions' home floor, the No. 2 seed Crimson (26-3) returned the favor. Harvard beat No. 3 seed Columbia, 69-59, at Levien Gymnasium in New York, N.Y., to advance to the Ivy League Tournament championship game.

The rubber match of a season series the teams had split was tied at 59 with two and a half minutes to play. Harvard scored the final 10 points of the game. Senior Isla Marchetti hit the go-ahead 3-pointer, and junior Thea Sutherland did the rest. The Crimson won the battle on the boards 55-46, grabbed 18 offensive rebounds, and went a perfect 9-for-9 at the line.

"Our players have thought about that game in our gym all year. They never talked about revenge, they just went to work," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball. "Columbia is a terrific team and this is a tough place to play. When it got tight at the end, our seniors and Thea made winning plays, and now we get to play for a championship."

## How it Happened

Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo opened the scoring with a basket through contact, and Harvard came out on fire. The Crimson poured in 31 points in the first quarter and led 31-16 after 10 minutes.

Columbia found its footing in the second quarter. A 12-4 Lions run cut the margin to 35-28, and Harvard took a 41-34 lead into halftime.

The teams traded baskets through most of the third quarter, each scoring 10 points in the frame, and the Crimson carried a 51-44 lead into the fourth.

Columbia kept coming. Harvard still led 59-56 in the final minutes when the Lions hit a 3-pointer with 2:31 left to tie the game at 59. Marchetti answered with a three of her own to put the Crimson back in front 62-59. Sutherland then took over, hitting two free throws with 43 seconds left, a 3-pointer from the wing with 41 seconds to go, and a putback with 21 seconds remaining to put the game away.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the Ivy League Tournament championship game and improves to 26-3 on the season. The Crimson went 2-1 against Columbia this season.
- Junior Thea Sutherland led Harvard with a career-high 19 points (7-of-17 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT) and 11 rebounds for a double-double, scoring the final seven points of the game.
- First-year Esme Ellison added 11 points (4-of-9 FG, 3-of-3 FT) and nine rebounds, six of them offensive.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 11 rebounds, and sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi added nine points and eight rebounds.
- Senior Isla Marchetti scored nine points, including the go-ahead 3-pointer in the final minutes, and dished out a team-high five assists.
- Harvard outrebounded Columbia 55-46 and held the Lions to 32.9% shooting from the field.
