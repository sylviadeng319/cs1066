Harvard women's basketball (7-0) erased an 8-point deficit and came away with a 72-70 win over Rutgers on Sunday at Jersey Mike's Arena in Piscataway, N.J.

The win is Harvard's seventh straight.

## How it Happened

The Scarlet Knights scored first before first-year Jordan Delgado finished a layup in transition for Harvard's first points. The Scarlet Knights opened the game on a 15-7 run. Isla Marchetti and Thea Sutherland keyed a 6-0 run late in the quarter that cut the deficit to 15-13. The Scarlet Knights led 17-13 after one.

A 24-6 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Brielle Mendoza's layup, turned a 17-13 deficit into a 37-23 lead. Harvard took a 37-26 lead into halftime.

Rutgers countered with a 22-8 run in the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 51-50. That was the score heading into the fourth.

Harvard put together a 12-0 spurt early in the quarter that turned a 56-53 deficit into a 65-56 lead. Harvard held a 67-65 lead in the final minutes. Rutgers hit a jumper with 2:46 left to tie it at 67. The Scarlet Knights scored inside with 2:17 left to go in front 69-67, but Mackenzie Takahashi answered when she dropped in a floater to tie the game at 69. Mendoza hit a triple with 8 seconds left to give the Crimson a 72-69 lead. Rutgers scored with less than a second left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 7-0 overall.
- Junior Thea Sutherland paced the Crimson with 17 points (7-of-16 FG, 3-of-10 3-PT).
- Senior Brielle Mendoza chipped in 14 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 3-of-5 FT) and seven rebounds.
- Senior Isla Marchetti added 11 points (4-of-11 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and five assists.
- Jordan Delgado (10) also reached double figures.
- Sutherland's 17 points were a career high.
