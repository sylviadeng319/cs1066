Down 9 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (11-0) rallied for a 68-57 win over Penn State on Thursday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The win is Harvard's 11th straight.

## How it Happened

First-year Esme Ellison opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Harvard opened the game on a 10-2 run. Penn State went on a 10-2 run midway through the quarter, which drew level at 12. The Crimson took a 21-14 lead into the second quarter.

A 21-3 Penn State spurt in the quarter flipped a 25-16 deficit into a 37-28 lead. The Crimson went into the locker room down 37-30.

Isla Marchetti, Thea Sutherland, and Mackenzie Takahashi keyed a 13-2 run early in the quarter that turned a 37-30 deficit into a 43-39 lead. Penn State countered with a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that flipped a 43-39 deficit into a 45-43 lead. The game was tied at 47 after three quarters.

A 13-2 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Marchetti's three, broke a 47-all tie and put Harvard up 60-49.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 11-0 overall. The Crimson is 5-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Thea Sutherland paced the Crimson with 15 points (7-of-17 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT) and five assists.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi finished with 12 points (5-of-11 FG, 2-of-4 3-PT) and seven rebounds.
- First-year Esme Ellison added 10 points (5-of-8 FG).
- Isla Marchetti (10) also reached double figures.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 12 rebounds.
