Eight months after a one-point loss to Columbia in the Ivy League Tournament semifinals ended its 2028-29 season, Harvard women's basketball got back to work with an 80-72 win at New Hampshire on Thursday at Lundholm Gym in Durham, N.H.

The Crimson (1-0) made the second quarter its own, outscoring the Wildcats 32-13 to turn a three-point game into a 44-22 halftime lead. New Hampshire shot 54.0% from the field and poured in 31 points in the fourth quarter, but Harvard's defense had already done its damage. The Crimson came up with 14 steals, forced 17 turnovers while committing only nine, and went a perfect 13-for-13 at the free throw line.

"I loved our pace and our hands in that first half. We turned defense into offense, and that's who we want to be," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball. "We have to finish better than we did tonight, but there's a lot to build on, and our first-years handled a road opener like veterans."

## How it Happened

New Hampshire scored first, and first-year Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board in her collegiate debut by going 2-for-2 at the line. The Wildcats led 7-2 before Esme Ellison, Isla Marchetti, and Brielle Mendoza keyed an 8-0 run that put the Crimson ahead 10-7, and Harvard led 12-9 after one quarter.

Then the Crimson took off, putting up 32 second-quarter points. A 32-11 run capped by a Mackenzie Takahashi basket put Harvard up 44-20, and the Crimson carried a 44-22 lead into the locker room.

The Crimson kept the pressure on after the break. A 19-10 spurt in the third quarter stretched the margin to 63-37, and Harvard led 65-41 heading into the fourth.

New Hampshire made a game of it late, outscoring Harvard 31-15 in the final frame, but the Wildcats never got closer than the final margin of eight.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard opens the season 1-0.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi led Harvard with a career-high 17 points (7-of-11 FG, 2-of-5 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT), seven steals, and three assists.
- Senior Isla Marchetti added 13 points (5-of-11 FG, 3-of-5 3-PT) and three steals.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza scored 12 points (5-of-14 FG, 1-of-3 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT) and grabbed seven rebounds.
- Junior Thea Sutherland finished with 10 points on 5-of-8 shooting.
- First-year Esme Ellison made her collegiate debut in the starting lineup and posted nine points and 10 rebounds, six of them on the offensive glass.
- First-year Jordan Delgado scored six points and handed out three assists off the bench in her debut.
- Harvard's 32 second-quarter points came on the strength of a 32-11 run, and the Crimson finished with 14 steals.
