Harvard women's basketball carried a perfect record into Ivy League play, and the rivalry didn't slow it down. The Crimson (15-0, 1-0 Ivy) never trailed in a 79-58 win over Yale in the league opener on Saturday at Lavietes Pavilion, stretching its winning streak to 15 games.

It was a clinic in ball movement. Harvard assisted on 26 of its 31 field goals, turned the ball over just seven times, and went 10-for-10 at the free throw line. The bench chipped in 36 points. First-year Jordan Delgado led the way with a career high in her first Ivy League game. Harvard has now won all seven meetings with Yale since the start of the 2026-27 season.

"That's the way we want to play. The ball didn't stick, everybody touched it, and everybody was looking for the next pass," said head coach Carrie Moore. "Yale is always a battle, and I'm proud of how we started league play."

## How it Happened

Delgado opened the scoring with a 3-pointer, and Harvard jumped out to a 14-5 lead. Yale answered with a 10-4 run to pull within 18-15, but the Crimson closed the quarter ahead 20-15.

The second quarter broke it open. Harvard ripped off a 26-9 run, capped by an Esme Ellison layup, to go up 50-30. The Crimson scored 30 points in the frame and took a 50-32 lead into halftime.

Mackenzie Takahashi, Delgado, and Kendall Lindqvist keyed an 11-5 run early in the third quarter that pushed the margin to 61-37. The Bulldogs strung together seven straight points to get within 61-44, but Harvard answered again and led 68-45 heading into the fourth.

A 7-0 spurt early in the final frame made it 75-47, and the lead reached 28. Yale never got closer than 17 the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 15-0 overall and 1-0 in Ivy League play, extending its winning streak to 15 games. The Crimson is 8-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- First-year Jordan Delgado scored a career-high 15 points (5-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and added four assists off the bench.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi added 13 points (4-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT), a team-high seven assists, and six rebounds.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza scored 10 points on 4-of-5 shooting.
- Senior Isla Marchetti dished out five assists, and sophomore Valentina Okonkwo added seven points on 3-of-4 shooting.
- Harvard assisted on 26 of its 31 field goals and committed just seven turnovers.
- Harvard's bench contributed 36 points, and every player on the roster got into the game.
