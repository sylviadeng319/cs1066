The Crimson made quick work of Albany on Monday, as Harvard women's basketball (2-0) built a lead as large as 21 on the way to a 83-63 win at Lavietes Pavilion.

Harvard's defense forced 23 turnovers.

## How it Happened

Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo opened the scoring when she banked in a turnaround. Harvard opened the game on an 11-3 run. A 15-3 Albany spurt midway through the quarter flipped an 11-3 deficit into an 18-14 lead. The teams were knotted at 19 after one.

An 11-3 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Okonkwo's bucket inside, turned a 25-19 deficit into a 30-28 lead. The Great Danes went on a 7-0 run late in the quarter, which flipped a 30-28 deficit into a 35-30 lead. Harvard trailed 39-34 at halftime.

The Crimson strung together a 13-1 burst early in the quarter, capped by Isla Marchetti's 3-pointer, which turned a 41-36 deficit into a 49-42 lead. The Crimson carried a 53-51 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard put together a 26-7 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 79-58. With the lead as large as 21, Emeka Rhodes, Chloe Ferreira, and Lauren Wakefield saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 2-0 overall.
