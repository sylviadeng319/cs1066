Harvard women's basketball (25-3, 11-3 Ivy) never trailed on Saturday, beating Dartmouth, 70-59, at Leede Arena in Hanover, N.H.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Big Green 46-33. The Crimson shot 44.6% from the field and held Dartmouth to 36.1%.

"Everybody who checked in made an impact. That kind of depth is something we've been building toward," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

First-year Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board when she splashed a three from the wing with 9:53 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on a 13-0 run. The Crimson took a 20-8 lead into the second quarter.

An 11-3 Harvard run in the quarter, capped by Thea Sutherland's layup, stretched the lead to 35-20. Harvard took a 37-22 lead into halftime.

Harvard put together a 9-0 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 48-27. Dartmouth went on an 8-0 run midway through the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 48-35. Harvard took a 58-37 lead into the fourth quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 6-0 burst late in the quarter, capped by Brielle Mendoza's free throws, which stretched the lead to 68-55.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 25-3 overall and 11-3 in Ivy League play.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza led Harvard with 16 points (7-of-10 FG, 2-of-2 FT), 10 rebounds, and two blocks for a double-double.
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo added 11 points (5-of-7 FG, 1-of-2 FT) and five rebounds.
- First-year Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 10 points (4-of-10 FG, 0-of-2 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and 13 rebounds.
- Jordan Delgado (10) also reached double figures.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi dished out a team-high six assists.
- Junior Thea Sutherland grabbed nine rebounds.
- The Crimson assisted on 19 of its 29 field goals.
- Harvard's bench contributed 26 points.
