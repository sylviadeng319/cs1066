Harvard women's basketball (12-0) cruised to a 83-59 victory over UMass Lowell on Monday at Costello Athletic Center in Lowell, Mass.

The win is Harvard's 12th straight. Harvard knocked down 10 3-pointers.

## How it Happened

Harvard struck first as senior Brielle Mendoza added a free throw. The River Hawks went on an 11-0 run early in the quarter, which flipped a 1-0 deficit into an 11-1 lead. Esme Ellison, Mackenzie Takahashi, and Isla Marchetti keyed a 24-3 run midway through the quarter that turned an 11-1 deficit into a 25-14 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 25-16.

Harvard put together a 17-1 spurt early in the quarter that stretched the lead to 42-19. The River Hawks countered with a 6-0 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 44-27. The Crimson led 47-27 at the break.

The Crimson strung together a 12-0 burst early in the quarter, capped by Ellison's bucket inside, which stretched the lead to 59-27. A 19-9 UMass Lowell spurt in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 70-48. The Crimson carried a 72-49 advantage into the final frame.

Harvard kept UMass Lowell at arm's length throughout the fourth, never letting the lead dip below 21. With the lead as large as 32, Lauren Wakefield, Mira Hightower, and Tiana Adeyemi saw time off the bench.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 12-0 overall.
- First-year Esme Ellison led Harvard with 23 points (7-of-10 FG, 9-of-9 FT) and eight rebounds.
- Senior Isla Marchetti contributed 15 points (5-of-7 FG, 4-of-6 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT).
- Junior Thea Sutherland chipped in 15 points (6-of-13 FG, 3-of-9 3-PT, 0-of-2 FT).
- Mackenzie Takahashi (15) and Brielle Mendoza (11) also reached double figures.
- Ellison's 23 points were a career high.
