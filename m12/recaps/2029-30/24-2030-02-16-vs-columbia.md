For a half, Senior Day at Lavietes Pavilion looked like it might belong to Columbia. Then Harvard women's basketball's defense took over. The Crimson (22-2, 8-2 Ivy) erased a 17-point third-quarter deficit and held the Lions to 14 second-half points in a 54-46 win on Saturday in front of a sold-out crowd of 2,195.

Before tip-off, Harvard honored seniors Niamh Hollis, Brielle Mendoza, Isla Marchetti, and Lauren Wakefield, and head coach Carrie Moore put all four in the starting lineup alongside first-year Esme Ellison. It was the first start of the season for both Hollis and Wakefield. The win avenged a 63-56 loss at Columbia on Feb. 1 and was Harvard's fourth straight.

"This senior class has been through a lot together, and I told them at halftime that they weren't going to let their last regular-season game in this building go like that," Moore said. "Our defense in the third quarter was as good as it's been all year. I'm so proud of these four, and of the way the whole team played for them."

## How it Happened

Columbia opened the game on a 7-0 run before Ellison got Harvard on the board at the free throw line. A 9-2 Harvard spurt, capped by a Mendoza basket inside, tied it at 9, but the Lions closed the quarter ahead 13-9.

The second quarter belonged to Columbia. The Lions outscored Harvard 19-8 in the frame, and the Crimson went to the locker room down 32-17.

Columbia scored the first basket of the second half to go up 34-17. From there, Harvard flipped the game. The Crimson clamped down on defense, went on a 23-3 run capped by a Jordan Delgado layup, and took a 40-37 lead into the fourth quarter. Columbia managed just five points in the third.

The seniors took it home. Mendoza, Marchetti, and Hollis keyed a 12-4 run early in the fourth that pushed the lead to 52-41, and the Lions never got closer than five the rest of the way.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 22-2 overall and 8-2 in Ivy League play, splitting the season series with Columbia. The Crimson is 13-1 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Senior Isla Marchetti led Harvard with 13 points (5-of-10 FG, 3-of-7 3-PT) and a team-high six steals.
- First-year Esme Ellison added 10 points (4-of-7 FG, 2-of-2 FT) and six rebounds.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed nine rebounds, and senior Lauren Wakefield matched her with nine rebounds and three assists in her first start of the season.
- Senior Niamh Hollis scored seven points with three steals in 32 minutes.
- Harvard outscored Columbia 23-5 in the third quarter and 37-14 in the second half.
- The Crimson recorded 13 steals and outrebounded the Lions 40-35.
