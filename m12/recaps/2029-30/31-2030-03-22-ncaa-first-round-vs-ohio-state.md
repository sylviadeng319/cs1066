Thirty-two years after Harvard women's basketball stunned No. 1 seed Stanford, the Crimson has another NCAA Tournament win to celebrate. No. 10 seed Harvard (28-3) closed the game on an 8-0 run to beat No. 7 seed Ohio State, 71-62, in the First Round on Friday at Bramlage Coliseum in Manhattan, Kan.

It is the program's first NCAA Tournament victory since its 1998 upset of Stanford, and it came the hard way. The teams traded runs all game, and the Buckeyes took a 57-56 lead early in the fourth quarter. Harvard answered, then shut Ohio State out over the final stretch while sophomore Valentina Okonkwo scored three baskets in the last two and a half minutes. The Crimson forced 19 turnovers, shot 46.6% from the field, and got double-figure scoring from four players, led by first-year Esme Ellison.

"I don't have the words right now. I'm so proud of this team," said Carrie Moore, The Kathy Delaney-Smith Head Coach for Harvard Women's Basketball. "Everybody who has ever played in this program shares in a win like this. Ohio State is a great team, and our players kept answering. Every time they made a run, we got a stop, we got a big shot, and we stayed together. Now we get to keep playing."

The win is Harvard's fourth straight, its 28th of the season, and sends the Crimson to the Second Round on Sunday.

## How it Happened

Ellison opened the scoring with a putback. After Ohio State went up 5-2, Brielle Mendoza, Isla Marchetti, and Kendall Lindqvist keyed a 17-5 run that gave the Crimson a 19-10 lead, and Harvard led 19-14 after one quarter.

Harvard pushed the margin to 24-16 early in the second quarter before Ohio State answered with 13 straight points to go up 29-24. Down 31-26 late in the half, the Crimson responded with a 9-0 run and took a 35-31 lead into the locker room.

The Buckeyes scored eight unanswered early in the third quarter to turn a 37-33 deficit into a 41-37 lead. Harvard had an answer again. A 14-3 run, capped by a 3-pointer from first-year Jordan Delgado, put the Crimson back in front 51-44, and Harvard led 51-46 entering the fourth.

Ohio State used a 7-0 run to take a 57-56 lead early in the final frame, but Harvard clawed back in front and led 63-62 in the final minutes. Then Okonkwo took over. She scored inside with 2:20 left to make it 65-62 and scored through contact moments later to make it 67-62. Ellison added a free throw with 1:35 left, Okonkwo hit a jumper from the elbow with 1:32 left, and Delgado's free throw with 1:12 to go capped the scoring. Ohio State did not score again.

## Harvard Highlights

- Harvard advances to the NCAA Tournament Second Round and improves to 28-3. It is the program's first NCAA Tournament victory since its 1998 upset of No. 1 seed Stanford.
- First-year Esme Ellison led Harvard with 15 points (5-of-11 FG, 1-of-1 3-PT, 4-of-5 FT) and nine rebounds.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza went a perfect 5-of-5 from the field for 13 points (1-of-1 3-PT, 2-of-3 FT) and added three assists.
- First-year Jordan Delgado scored 12 points off the bench (4-of-10 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 1-of-2 FT) and handed out three assists.
- Senior Isla Marchetti added 10 points and three steals.
- Sophomore Valentina Okonkwo scored seven points off the bench, including six of Harvard's final eight points.
- Harvard forced 19 turnovers and committed 12, and its bench contributed 23 points.
