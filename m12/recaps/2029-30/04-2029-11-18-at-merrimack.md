Down 9 in the third quarter, Harvard women's basketball (4-0) rallied for a 82-74 win over Merrimack on Sunday at Hammel Court in North Andover, Mass.

Harvard controlled the glass, outrebounding the Warriors 40-29. The win is Harvard's fourth straight.

"That's a resilient group in that locker room. Being down didn't change our belief one bit," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

First-year Jordan Delgado got Harvard on the board when she splashed a three from the wing with 9:23 left in the opening quarter. Harvard opened the game on an 8-0 run. The Warriors countered with a 10-0 run early in the quarter that flipped an 8-0 deficit into a 10-8 lead. After 10 minutes, Harvard was in front 19-13.

A 6-0 Harvard run early in the quarter, capped by Mackenzie Takahashi's jumper, stretched the lead to 25-17. A 14-0 Merrimack spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 27-19 deficit into a 33-27 lead. Merrimack took a 37-32 lead into the break.

The Warriors went on a 7-0 run early in the quarter, which extended the lead to 46-37. Isla Marchetti, Valentina Okonkwo, and Brielle Mendoza keyed a 19-2 run midway through the quarter that turned a 46-37 deficit into a 56-48 lead. It stayed that way heading into the fourth.

The Warriors countered with a 19-5 run midway through the quarter that cut Harvard's lead to 71-70. Harvard held a 75-70 lead in the final minutes. Merrimack drained a 3-pointer with 1:33 left to get within 75-73, but Thea Sutherland answered when she hit a pair of free throws to push the lead to 77-73. Esme Ellison finished a post move with 23 seconds left to push the lead to 79-73. Sutherland connected from deep with 14 seconds left to make it 82-73. The Warriors scored with 12 seconds left, but it only trimmed the final margin.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 4-0 overall.

- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi finished with 13 points (5-of-14 FG, 0-of-4 3-PT, 3-of-4 FT) and five assists.
