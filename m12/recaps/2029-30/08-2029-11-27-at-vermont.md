Down 9 in the first half, Harvard women's basketball (8-0) rallied for a 72-62 win over Vermont on Tuesday at Patrick Gym in Burlington, Vt.

The win is Harvard's eighth straight.

"Our defense turned the game. Once we started getting stops, everything opened up," Carrie Moore said after the game.

## How it Happened

The Catamounts scored first before senior Isla Marchetti buried a mid-range jumper for Harvard's first points. A 10-0 Vermont spurt midway through the quarter flipped a 7-6 deficit into a 16-7 lead. A 6-0 Harvard run late in the quarter, capped by Valentina Okonkwo's bucket inside, cut the deficit to 16-13. The score held there after one quarter.

The Crimson strung together a 12-2 burst early in the quarter, capped by Mira Hightower's bucket inside, which turned a 16-13 deficit into a 25-18 lead. Harvard went into the locker room up 29-24.

Marchetti, Brielle Mendoza, and Jordan Delgado keyed a 14-4 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 45-34. The Catamounts went on a 9-1 run late in the quarter, which cut Harvard's lead to 46-43. The Crimson carried a 48-43 advantage into the final frame.

The Catamounts countered with a 19-7 run early in the quarter that flipped a 50-43 deficit into a 62-57 lead. The Catamounts led 62-61 in the final minutes. With 2:40 left, Thea Sutherland scored through contact to give the Crimson a 63-62 lead. Esme Ellison converted a putback with 1:59 left to make it 65-62. With 1:57 left, Ellison finished a post move to push the lead to 67-62. With 11 seconds left, Mackenzie Takahashi finished a layup in transition to push the lead to 69-62. Marchetti put the game away when she buried a 3-pointer with 8 seconds left.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 8-0 overall.
- Senior Isla Marchetti finished with a team-high 16 points (5-of-14 FG, 2-of-6 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), five rebounds, and three assists.
- Junior Thea Sutherland finished with 14 points (5-of-11 FG, 3-of-4 3-PT, 1-of-1 FT).
- First-year Esme Ellison recorded a double-double with 10 points (5-of-7 FG, 0-of-1 3-PT) and 11 rebounds.
- Sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi dished out a team-high six assists.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed 13 rebounds.
