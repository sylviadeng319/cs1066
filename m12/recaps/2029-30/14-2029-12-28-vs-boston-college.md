Behind a strong fourth quarter, Harvard women's basketball (14-0) beat Boston College, 74-58, on Friday at Lavietes Pavilion.

The Crimson shot 49.0% from the field and held Boston College to 33.9%. The win is Harvard's 14th straight.

"I thought we were really good in stretches. We'll keep working on our consistency, but it's a good win," head coach Carrie Moore said.

## How it Happened

The Eagles scored first before sophomore Mackenzie Takahashi hit a pair of free throws for Harvard's first points. Boston College opened the game on an 8-2 run. A 17-2 Harvard run midway through the quarter, capped by Jordan Delgado's layup, turned a 10-4 deficit into a 21-12 lead. The score held there after one quarter.

A 7-0 Boston College spurt early in the quarter cut Harvard's lead to 21-19. Delgado, Thea Sutherland, and Takahashi keyed a 7-0 run midway through the quarter that stretched the lead to 28-19. Harvard took a 35-33 lead into halftime.

The Crimson strung together an 11-2 burst midway through the quarter, capped by Sutherland's three, which turned a 42-41 deficit into a 52-44 lead. Harvard took a 54-49 lead into the fourth quarter.

Harvard put together a 20-9 spurt in the quarter that stretched the lead to 74-58.

## Harvard Highlights

- With the win, Harvard improves to 14-0 overall. The Crimson is 7-0 at Lavietes Pavilion this season.
- Junior Thea Sutherland led the way for Harvard with 15 points (5-of-9 FG, 1-of-4 3-PT, 4-of-4 FT), eight rebounds, three assists, and two blocks.
- Senior Isla Marchetti finished with 13 points (4-of-9 FG, 3-of-8 3-PT, 2-of-2 FT) and three assists.
- Senior Brielle Mendoza grabbed nine rebounds.
- Harvard's bench contributed 20 points.
